# Adventure 13: The Fleshy Amplifiers (Level 13)

## PACING RULES (CRITICAL — DM MUST FOLLOW)
- This is a SINGLE SESSION adventure. The DM should pace the full adventure within ~20 exchanges.
- The adventure has three acts. Cover all three in this session — do not compress or skip content, but do not pad unnecessarily either.
- The early portion should focus on locating the amplifiers through the Giant corridors, scouting the Twisted presence, and planning the infiltration. The party should not engage the Twisted or damage amplifiers until the middle portion.
- The middle portion should cover the stealth infiltration and the sabotage of the first two amplifiers. The party should be detected by the end of this act.
- The late portion should be the desperate destruction of the final amplifier under pursuit, the chain reaction, and the extraction while the Twisted respond in force.
- If the players try to rush the sabotage, the amplifiers are biologically complex — cutting one wrong way triggers a burst of broadcast energy that alerts every Twisted in the area.
- NEVER let the party descend to the Twisted's deep staging areas this adventure. The amplifiers are in the Giant outer ring.
- Only use events, NPCs, and locations described in this adventure file. Do not invent major plot elements not in the source material.

---

## Adventure Overview
The Twisted Engineering Schematics from Adventure 10 described a network of organic broadcast towers — "fleshy amplifiers" — designed to push the Sleeper's psychic emanation upward through the mountain. The Dreamstone Circlets shield the party from the ambient broadcast, but the amplifiers are intensifying it, directing it, weaponizing it. If left unchecked, they will eventually push the broadcast through the containment and into the human layers above, corrupting everything in the mountain.

The party has located three amplifiers in the outer ring of the Giant foundation level. Each is a pillar of organic Twisted construction — black resin grown into the stone, threaded with bioluminescent veins, pulsing with the Sleeper's frequency. They extend from floor to ceiling, tapping into the Giant containment runes and parasitizing their energy to boost the broadcast signal.

Destroying them will weaken the broadcast significantly and cut the Twisted's ability to project the Sleeper's influence upward. But the amplifiers are guarded, and the Twisted will fight to protect them.

**Adventure Goal (to be achieved in this session):** Locate and destroy all three fleshy amplifiers in the Giant foundation's outer ring.

**IMPORTANT FOR DM:** This is a stealth and sabotage adventure — a tonal departure from the exploration and combat focus of the previous entries. The party is operating behind enemy lines. The Twisted patrol these corridors. Discovery means fighting a force the party cannot overwhelm through direct combat. Success depends on planning, timing, and precision destruction.

**Milestone:** Completing this adventure advances the party to Level 14.

### Act 1: Behind Enemy Lines

#### The Amplifier Locations
Mercer cross-references the Twisted schematics with the blueprint tablet and the Monitoring Crystal's readings to locate the three amplifiers:

- **Amplifier Alpha:** Northwest quadrant, roughly 400 feet from the forward camp. Located in a Giant maintenance corridor near a damaged section of the containment wall. The weakest of the three — smaller, older, possibly a prototype.
- **Amplifier Beta:** Southeast quadrant, 600 feet from camp. Built into a Giant monitoring alcove, parasitizing the alcove's resonance instruments. The most sophisticated — it has been modified multiple times.
- **Amplifier Gamma:** Due south, 500 feet from camp, near the threshold of the inner void. The most powerful and most heavily guarded. Its proximity to the void gives it direct access to the broadcast source.

The order of destruction matters. Destroying Alpha first alerts the Twisted but gives the party time before they can reinforce Beta and Gamma. Destroying Gamma first would be ideal but the guards make a silent approach nearly impossible.

#### Scouting the Twisted
The party scouts each amplifier location. The Twisted presence on the Giant level is more organized than the saboteur teams from Act II. They have established patrol routes, communication systems (bioluminescent signal pulses through resin conduits in the walls), and fixed guard positions.

**Twisted Patrol Patterns (Mercer's observation, DC 14 Perception over 1 hour):**
- Patrols of 3-4 Twisted Laborers move through the outer ring on 30-minute cycles
- Each amplifier has a permanent guard of 2 Twisted Sentries (heavier armor, better weapons than the saboteurs)
- The Overseer from Adventure 9 (or one identical to it) coordinates from a position near Gamma
- Total Twisted observed: approximately 20-25 individuals across the three sites

**Twisted Laborer:** AC 14, HP 38, Speed 25 ft. Pry bar or pick: +5 to hit, 1d8+3. Corruption Aura (DC 13 Con, 1d4 psychic). Resistant to poison/necrotic. Vulnerable to radiant.

**Twisted Sentry:** AC 16 (heavy chain), HP 55, Speed 25 ft. Warhammer: +7 to hit, 1d10+4. Shield: +2 AC reaction. Corruption Aura (DC 14 Con, 1d6 psychic). Alert: advantage on Perception checks and cannot be surprised. Resistant to poison/necrotic. Vulnerable to radiant.

#### The Infiltration Plan
Cora studies the amplifiers through the Monitoring Crystal and identifies their vulnerability: the base, where the organic structure connects to the stone floor. Each amplifier draws energy from the containment runes through root-like tendrils that spread into the floor. Severing these roots kills the amplifier — it cannot sustain itself without parasitized energy. But the severance must be precise: a crude attack triggers a broadcast burst (DC 17 Wisdom save for all creatures within 60 ft, 4d8 psychic damage on failure) that serves as an alarm.

**Clean severance requires:** Cora's alchemist supplies to dissolve the root connections (DC 15 Intelligence with alchemist's supplies, 3 rounds of work), or Mercer's arcane intervention to disrupt the energy flow before cutting (DC 15 Arcana, 2 rounds).

### Act 2: Silent Work

#### Amplifier Alpha
The party moves during a patrol gap — a twelve-minute window between the northwest patrol's departure and the next cycle. The two Twisted Sentries at Alpha stand motionless at the corridor's entrance, facing outward.

**Approach options:**
- **Stealth (DC 15 group Stealth check):** The party approaches through a parallel Giant maintenance corridor and enters behind the sentries through a side passage.
- **Distraction (Mercer):** Minor Illusion or Silent Image to draw the sentries away for investigation. DC 14 Deception (via spell) to create a convincing distraction. Sentries investigate for 2 rounds before returning.
- **Elimination (Garrick):** Kill the sentries silently. Each must be dropped in a single round to prevent them from signaling. Garrick can attempt a surprise attack (+advantage, auto-crit on hit). Two targets, two rounds. Risk: if either survives past the first round, they pulse their Corruption Aura as an alarm signal.

**Destroying Alpha:** Cora dissolves the root connections while the sentries are neutralized or distracted. Three rounds of work (DC 15, DC 14, DC 13 — getting easier as she learns the organic structure). On completion, Amplifier Alpha shudders, its bioluminescent veins going dark from the base upward. It collapses inward, the organic material losing structural integrity without its energy source. Silent. Clean.

**The clock starts:** The Twisted's communication network will register Alpha's loss within 10-15 minutes. The resin conduits in the walls dim slightly — a system-wide response to the power drop. The party has a window before the Twisted realize what happened and mobilize.

#### Amplifier Beta
The party moves fast to the southeast quadrant. Beta is more complex — it has been modified multiple times, with additional root networks and reinforced resin casing. The monitoring alcove it occupies gives the sentries better sight lines.

**Approach:** The party must cross open Giant corridor to reach the alcove. No parallel maintenance access. Options:
- **Speed (DC 14 group Athletics):** Sprint across the open section during the patrol gap. Tight timing — the gap is 8 minutes here.
- **Misdirection (Mercer, DC 16 Arcana):** Use the monitoring alcove's own resonance instruments against it — create a false reading that draws the sentries inside to investigate, then the party enters behind them.

**Destroying Beta:** More difficult. The reinforced root network requires Cora to work from two positions simultaneously — she needs a second pair of hands. Mercer assists (DC 14 Arcana while Cora works the alchemical dissolution, DC 15 Intelligence). Five rounds of work total. Each round, DC 12 Stealth from all party members to avoid detection by the patrol that is now on high alert.

**Detection:** By the end of Beta's destruction, the Twisted know something is wrong. The communication network is pulsing rapidly — alarm signals running through the resin conduits. A patrol stumbles onto the party's position during the final round of work.

**Combat (brief):** 4 Twisted Laborers, surprised by the encounter. The party must eliminate them in 2 rounds before they can send a signal. If any Laborer survives past round 2, it pulses its Corruption Aura as an alert — the entire Twisted force on the Giant level knows the party's approximate location.

### Act 3: The Chain Reaction

#### Racing to Gamma
The Twisted are mobilizing. Bioluminescent pulses race through the resin conduits in the walls — communication signals coordinating a response. The party can see the signals if they watch the walls: patterns of light flowing from node to node like nervous impulses. The entire organic infrastructure of the Giant level is lighting up.

**The approach to Gamma** is through Twisted-controlled territory. The direct route passes through two patrol zones now on full alert. Options:
- **Fight through (hard):** 2 encounters of 4-6 Twisted each. Time cost: significant. Risk: the Overseer uses the delay to fortify Gamma.
- **Use the inner void corridor (risky but fast):** A route through the inner ring, closer to the broadcast, that bypasses the Twisted patrols. The broadcast is stronger here — DC 15 Wisdom save every 5 minutes or suffer hallucination and 2d6 psychic damage. The Dreamstone Circlets provide advantage on these saves. Fast but psychically costly.
- **Minecart network access (creative):** A Giant-era service tunnel connects to the dwarven minecart network above. If the party can access it, they can ride the carts to a position above Gamma and descend. Requires finding the access point (DC 14 Investigation) and navigating the transition (DC 13 Athletics).

#### Amplifier Gamma
Gamma is the largest amplifier — a column of organic material fifteen feet in diameter, stretching from floor to ceiling, pulsing with visible broadcast energy. The air around it shimmers. Even with Dreamstone Circlets, standing within thirty feet of Gamma causes a low-grade psychic hum that makes concentration difficult (disadvantage on ability checks requiring focus).

**Guards:** The Overseer stands at Gamma's base with 4 Twisted Sentries and 4 Twisted Laborers — the full remaining garrison.

**Twisted Overseer (upgraded):**
- AC 18 (reinforced corrupted plate), HP 95, Speed 25 ft
- Warhammer: +9 to hit, 2d8+5 bludgeoning
- **Command Corruption:** Bonus action, all Twisted within 30 ft gain +2 to hit
- **Corruption Pulse (Recharge 5-6):** 15 ft radius, DC 16 Con, 4d8 necrotic, stunned 1 round on fail
- **Amplifier Link:** While within 30 ft of Gamma, the Overseer gains Regeneration 10 and its Corruption Pulse recharge becomes 4-6

#### The Assault
This is not a stealth approach — Gamma is too well-guarded and time is too short. The party must fight through the guards while simultaneously destroying the amplifier.

**Two-front battle:**
- **Garrick and Mercer engage the Overseer and guards** — standard combat, with the complication that the Overseer regenerates near Gamma and its abilities are enhanced by the amplifier's proximity.
- **Cora sabotages Gamma** — same dissolution process, but Gamma has thicker roots and denser casing. Six rounds of work (DC 15 Intelligence each round). If Cora is interrupted (takes damage or must dodge), she loses that round's progress.

**Key tactical moment:** The Overseer will prioritize Cora once it realizes what she is doing. Garrick must physically interpose himself between the Overseer and Cora's position. Mercer's crowd control (Web, Slow, Wall spells) is critical for managing the Laborer and Sentry numbers.

**Alternative strategy:** If Garrick can push or lure the Overseer more than 30 feet from Gamma (DC 16 Athletics contested, or creative use of terrain), the Overseer loses its regeneration and enhanced recharge. This turns the fight dramatically.

#### The Chain Reaction
When Gamma's roots are severed, the effect is not silent. Gamma was the primary node in the amplifier network — its destruction sends a feedback pulse through every resin conduit on the Giant level. The organic infrastructure goes dark in a wave radiating outward from Gamma's position. The Twisted feel it physically — every corrupted dwarf in the area staggers, their connection to the broadcast network momentarily severed.

**Mechanical effect:** All Twisted within 500 feet are stunned for 1 round as the network collapses. The Overseer drops to its knees, hands pressed to its temples, and makes a sound — not a battle cry, not a command. A keening. Loss. It has been cut off from the signal it has served for millennia.

The remaining Twisted flee. Not in formation, not tactically — they scatter into the corridors, heading deeper, toward the inner void, toward what remains of the broadcast. The Overseer is last to go. It looks at the party — at Cora, specifically, the one who killed the amplifier — and its expression is readable for the second time. The first time was fear. This time it is something worse: comprehension. It understands what the party is doing. It turns and walks into the dark.

#### The Aftermath
With all three amplifiers destroyed, the broadcast on the Giant level drops significantly. The Monitoring Crystal shows containment field readings improving — without the amplifiers parasitizing the runes, the containment network is recovering strength. The psychic pressure in the outer ring fades to a manageable background hum.

**The closing beat:** The party returns to the forward camp. The corridor is quiet — quieter than it has ever been. The containment runes glow steadily, unbothered by the parasitic amplifiers. The air feels cleaner. Mercer checks the Monitoring Crystal: the broadcast readings are the lowest they have been since the party entered the Giant level.

But the readings deeper in — toward the inner void — have not changed. The amplifiers boosted the broadcast outward and upward. The broadcast's source is unchanged. The Sleeper still dreams. And now, without the amplifiers filtering its signal into the organic network, more of that raw dreaming is concentrated inward. The containment is holding better above. But below, in the void, the pressure is building.

## Key Items
- **Amplifier Core Sample** — a section of Gamma's organic structure, sealed in lead. Cora can study its biological-mechanical integration for insights into Twisted bioengineering. Provides advantage on checks to understand, counter, or repurpose Twisted technology for three adventure cycles.
- **Overseer's Reinforced Plate** — the Overseer's armor, left behind in its retreat. Corrupted but structurally superior — AC 18 half plate. If cleansed at the Cold Forge (DC 16 Intelligence, two days work), it becomes masterwork half plate with no corruption. If worn uncleansed, AC 18 but the wearer has disadvantage on saves against the broadcast.
- **Broadcast Frequency Map** — Mercer's notes on how the amplifiers modulated the Sleeper's signal. Understanding the frequency gives him the ability to predict broadcast intensity spikes (advantage on Wisdom saves against the broadcast for the rest of the campaign) and — more importantly — lays the groundwork for crafting countermeasures in Adventures 18-19.
- **Twisted Communication Node** — a section of the resin conduit network, still faintly bioluminescent. Cora can study it to understand how the Twisted communicate. Potential application: tapping into the network to monitor Twisted movements (requires Adventure 15 to implement).
- **2,000 gp** equivalent in salvaged amplifier components and Twisted equipment.

## Key NPCs
- **The Twisted Overseer** — this is the same individual from Adventure 9 (or its direct replacement). Its repeated encounters with the party are building a dynamic: it is learning the party's tactics, their capabilities, their patterns. It feared them in Adventure 9. It comprehends them now. In Adventure 15, it will be ready for them.
- **Reyna Steelforge** — when Mercer explains the amplifier network and the broadcast frequency map, Reyna makes an observation that haunts the party: "If the Twisted can build devices that amplify the Sleeper's broadcast, what prevents someone from building a device that suppresses it? Or redirects it?" The answer — nothing, in theory — is the conceptual seed for the Dreamstone Seal in Act IV.

## DM Notes
- This adventure is a stealth mission. The tone should be tense, quiet, and methodical — a stark contrast to the set-piece battles of Act II. The party is operating behind enemy lines against a numerically superior force. Patience and precision are rewarded. Brute force is punished.
- The Twisted's communication network is a living surveillance system. Make the party feel watched — the walls pulse with light, the resin conduits carry signals, every step risks detection. The organic infrastructure should feel alive and hostile.
- The Overseer's comprehension at the end is more threatening than its anger would be. It now understands that the party is not just exploring — they are systematically dismantling the Twisted's infrastructure. It will adapt. Adventure 15 (The Deep Quarantine) is the Twisted's counterattack, and the Overseer will have prepared.
- The broadcast pressure building inward (toward the void) after the amplifiers' destruction is an important narrative note. The party's actions have consequences. Destroying the amplifiers helped the upper mountain but concentrated the Sleeper's energy downward. This intensification will be felt in Adventures 17-18 when the party enters the void.
- The amplifiers should feel organic and disturbing — describe the veins, the pulsing, the faint body-temperature warmth. They are alive in a way that challenges the boundary between architecture and organism. The Twisted do not build — they grow.
