# Adventure 12: The Dreamstone Vein (Level 12)

## PACING RULES (CRITICAL — DM MUST FOLLOW)
- This is a SINGLE SESSION adventure. The DM should pace the full adventure within ~20 exchanges.
- The adventure has three acts. Cover all three in this session — do not compress or skip content, but do not pad unnecessarily either.
- The early portion should focus on locating the raw Dreamstone vein through the Giant corridors and the environmental hazards of approaching it. The party should not begin mining until the middle portion of the adventure.
- The middle portion should cover the mining operation itself — the hallucinogenic auras, the gravity warps, and the challenge of extracting Dreamstone safely while managing psychic exposure.
- The late portion should be the crisis — something defends the vein, and the party must complete extraction under duress while dealing with the most intense Dreamstone effects yet.
- If the players try to rush the mining, the Dreamstone's hallucinogenic aura makes hasty work dangerous — cuts release concentrated radiation bursts.
- NEVER let the party reach the Anomaly's chamber this adventure. The vein is in the outer ring of the Giant foundation, not the inner void.
- Only use events, NPCs, and locations described in this adventure file. Do not invent major plot elements not in the source material.

## Adventure Overview
The Giant Monitoring Crystal from Adventure 11 has revealed something unexpected. During routine containment readings, Mercer detected an anomalous spike — not in the direction of the Anomaly's void, but laterally, in the outer ring of the Giant foundation. The spike corresponds to a location on the blueprint tablet marked as a "Resonance Seam" — a natural geological formation where the Anomaly's radiation has crystallized the bedrock into raw Dreamstone over millions of years.

The Dreamstone Fragment from Adventure 6 gave the party their first taste of the material's properties. A raw vein would provide enough Dreamstone to forge items of genuine power — psychic shielding, containment tools, weapons that can harm the Anomaly's manifestations. Cora has been theorizing about Dreamstone-forged equipment since they found the fragment. Now she can build it.

But Dreamstone in its raw, unshielded state is dangerous. The vein radiates concentrated psychic energy that causes hallucinations, perception distortions, and localized gravity anomalies. The closer the party gets, the more reality frays at the edges.

**Adventure Goal (to be achieved in this session):** Locate the Dreamstone vein. Extract enough raw Dreamstone for crafting (minimum 20 lbs). Return to the forward camp intact.

**IMPORTANT FOR DM:** The Dreamstone effects are the dungeon in this adventure. The vein is not guarded by monsters (initially) — it is guarded by its own radiation. The hallucinations should be disorienting and atmospheric, not combat encounters. The gravity warps should feel physically wrong. The party is mining in a reality distortion zone, and the challenge is maintaining coherence long enough to do the work.

**Milestone:** Completing this adventure advances the party to Level 13.

### Act 1: The Resonance Seam

#### Following the Signal
The Giant Monitoring Crystal pulses more intensely as the party moves through the Inspection Circuit toward the anomalous reading. Mercer maps the signal's source — roughly half a circuit away from their forward camp, in a section of the outer ring they have not yet explored.

The journey reveals new sections of the Giant architecture: maintenance corridors sized for Giant workers, tool storage alcoves containing implements the size of small trees, and a Giant-era workshop with a forge hearth large enough to park a house inside. The Giants' domestic infrastructure is staggering in its casual enormity.

**DC 14 Perception (any character):** The containment runes in this section are brighter than elsewhere — pulsing in complex, shifting patterns rather than the steady glow near the capstone. The containment is working harder here, compensating for something that generates more radiation than the baseline.

#### The Approach Zone
Two hundred feet from the vein's location, the environment begins to change:

- **Visual distortion:** The walls appear to breathe — expanding and contracting by fractions of an inch in rhythm with the containment runes. This is not an illusion. The Dreamstone radiation is warping space at a micro level.
- **Auditory distortion:** A low hum, below the range of normal hearing but felt in the jaw and teeth. It modulates unpredictably — rising, falling, occasionally spiking into a high whine that makes concentration difficult.
- **Gravitational distortion:** Weight fluctuates. The party feels lighter for a step, heavier for the next. Garrick's armor pulls to the left. Cora's pack tugs upward. Nothing dramatic — a few percent variation — but deeply disorienting over time.

**DC 13 Constitution save (all characters, upon entering the approach zone):** Failure: nausea from the gravity fluctuations. Disadvantage on Dexterity checks for 10 minutes. A mild effect — a preview of what is to come.

#### The Vein
The Dreamstone vein is visible from fifty feet away. It runs through the floor, wall, and ceiling of a section of the outer ring like a frozen lightning bolt — a seam of crystallized stone roughly three feet wide that glows with its own inner light. The light shifts through the spectrum: blues, purples, greens, golds. It is beautiful. It is very, very wrong.

The vein is not simply embedded in stone. It has grown into the Giant architecture, threading through the containment runes and — in places — disrupting them. Where the Dreamstone crosses a rune sequence, the rune's glow is warped, redirected, absorbed. The vein is feeding on the containment energy.

**Mercer's analysis (DC 15 Arcana):** The Dreamstone is crystallized Anomaly radiation. It forms where the Sleeper's psychic emanation has been concentrated against rock for millions of years. The vein is, in a sense, the Sleeper's dream made solid. Mining it will release stored psychic energy — the deeper they cut, the more concentrated the release.

**Cora's assessment:** The vein contains hundreds of pounds of raw Dreamstone. They need twenty pounds minimum for meaningful crafting. The challenge is extraction without destabilizing the surrounding containment runes and without overexposing the party to the radiation.

### Act 2: Mining the Dream

#### Extraction Process
Cora designs a systematic extraction protocol:
1. **Score the vein** along natural fracture lines using precision tools (DC 14 Dexterity with tinker's tools per section)
2. **Extract the section** by driving iron wedges into scored lines (DC 13 Strength from Garrick)
3. **Shield immediately** by wrapping extracted Dreamstone in lead sheeting (Cora, no check required but must be done within 1 round of extraction)
4. **Clear the radiation burst** — each extraction releases a psychic pulse that affects everyone within 30 feet

**Radiation Burst (per extraction):** DC 14 Wisdom save. Failure: hallucination lasting 1d4 minutes. The hallucinations are vivid, personalized, and non-hostile — they are fragments of the Sleeper's dream intruding on the character's perception.

**Hallucination table (roll or choose):**
1. The character sees the corridor as it was when the Giants built it — new stone, bright runes, Giant workers moving through the space in calm procession. The Giants are aware of the character. They look down with expressions of immense sadness.
2. The character perceives the walls as transparent. They can see through the stone in every direction — the containment vessel, the Dwarven Layer above, the human layers, the surface, the sky. They are simultaneously inside the mountain and above it. The vertigo is severe.
3. The character hears music. Not human music — a composition of frequencies that the human ear interprets as melody but is actually the mathematical structure of the containment field. It is achingly beautiful. They want to listen forever.
4. The character sees themselves from outside their own body, standing next to themselves, watching themselves mine. The double makes eye contact. It mouths a word the character cannot hear.

#### Gravity Complications
As the mining progresses and more Dreamstone is exposed, the gravity distortions intensify:

- **Hour 1 (extractions 1-3):** Mild fluctuations. Equipment floats briefly. Garrick's hammer drifts on the backswing.
- **Hour 2 (extractions 4-6):** Significant warps. A ten-foot zone around the exposed vein experiences directional gravity shifts — "down" rotates 15 degrees, causing everything to slide slowly toward one wall. The party must brace and compensate.
- **Hour 3 (extractions 7-9):** Dangerous. A gravity inversion pocket forms — a five-foot sphere where "down" is "up." Anyone stepping into it falls upward, hits the forty-foot ceiling, and takes 4d6 bludgeoning damage. The pocket is visible as a shimmer in the air. It drifts slowly, unpredictably.

**Cora's countermeasure:** By studying the gravity fluctuation patterns, Cora can predict pocket formation 1 round in advance (DC 15 Intelligence after observing for 30 minutes). She calls out warnings, allowing the party to avoid the worst effects.

#### Progress and Strain
By the midpoint of the mining operation, the party has extracted roughly 15 lbs of Dreamstone — short of the 20 lb target. The work is grueling: the hallucinations are cumulative (each successive save is at -1 to the DC modifier, making later saves easier as the mind acclimates), but the physical toll of mining in a gravity distortion zone is exhausting.

**DC 13 Constitution save (all characters):** Failure: one level of exhaustion from the sustained psychic and physical strain.

### Act 3: The Vein Defends Itself

> **HOSTILE-ONLY ENCOUNTER — THE SENTINEL ATTACKS INSTANTLY**
> THE SENTINEL MATERIALIZES AND ATTACKS INSTANTLY. It responds to proximity like a pressure plate responds to weight — it has no consciousness, no awareness, no reaction to words or intent. It is crystallized energy responding to a stimulus. Dialogue directed at the Sentinel produces no response. The party fights the Sentinel while mining.

#### The Dreamstone Sentinel
A new sound has begun from deeper in the Giant corridors — from the direction of the inner void — a low, resonant pulse, like a drum struck underwater. The Sleeper has noticed the mining. Something is coming.

The pulse from the inner void is not the Sleeper directly — it is a resonance effect. The mining has disturbed the vein's equilibrium, and the stored psychic energy is coalescing. As the party prepares for the final extractions, the Dreamstone itself responds.

A shape forms from the exposed vein face. Not a creature — a crystalline construct, roughly humanoid, ten feet tall, made of raw Dreamstone that has organized itself into a moving, glowing form. It is not alive. It is a defense mechanism — the vein's stored energy manifesting as a physical obstacle, like an immune response.

**Dreamstone Sentinel:**
- AC 16 (crystalline), HP 120
- Speed 30 ft, Hover 15 ft (gravity does not affect it normally)
- **Crystal Slam:** +8 to hit, 2d10+5 force damage + DC 15 Wisdom save or 2d8 psychic damage
- **Gravity Pulse (Recharge 5-6):** 20 ft radius. DC 16 Strength save. Failure: gravity reverses for the affected creature for 1 round (falls upward, 4d6 bludgeoning from ceiling impact, then falls back down for another 4d6 unless arrested).
- **Psychic Burst (Recharge 6):** 30 ft radius. DC 16 Wisdom save. Failure: hallucination (as extraction table above) plus stunned for 1 round. Success: hallucination only.
- **Regeneration:** Regains 10 HP at the start of its turn while within 30 ft of the exposed vein. If the vein is fully shielded (covered in lead), regeneration stops.
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Thunder damage (shatters crystalline structure, deals double). Radiant damage bypasses regeneration.
- **Damage Resistances:** Resistant to fire, cold, lightning, nonmagical weapons.

#### Fighting While Mining
The party must complete the final 5 lbs of extraction while dealing with the Sentinel. This is a two-front challenge:

- **Garrick engages the Sentinel** — keeping it away from Cora's mining position. The gravity distortions make melee combat unpredictable (each round, roll d4: 1-2 gravity is normal, 3 gravity is light — Garrick's attacks have disadvantage but he can jump 20 ft, 4 gravity is heavy — his attacks have advantage but his speed is halved).

- **Cora continues extraction** — she needs 2 more successful extraction rounds (DC 14 Dexterity) while under combat stress. Each extraction releases a radiation burst as before.

- **Mercer manages the environment** — he must shield the party from the worst gravity effects and psychic bursts while providing combat support. His Monitoring Crystal gives him advance warning of gravity shifts (one round).

**Key tactical insight:** The Sentinel regenerates near the vein. If the party shields the exposed vein face with lead (Cora can do this in 2 rounds instead of mining), the regeneration stops — but so does the mining. The optimal strategy is to have Garrick lure the Sentinel away from the vein (using himself as a target), allowing Cora to mine and then shield, while Mercer provides cover.

#### Completion
When the 20 lb threshold is reached and the remaining exposed vein is shielded with lead, the Sentinel loses its energy source. It staggers, its crystalline form losing cohesion, and collapses into a pile of inert Dreamstone fragments — adding another 5 lbs to the haul.

The gravity distortions settle as the exposed Dreamstone is covered. The hallucinations fade. The corridor returns to its baseline strangeness — merely alien, not actively hostile.

**The closing beat:** The party carries 25 lbs of raw Dreamstone back to the forward camp, each piece wrapped in lead and packed in iron-bound cases Cora brought from the forge. The haul is enough for multiple items of real power — psychic shielding, reality-anchoring tools, weapons that can interact with the Anomaly's manifestations.

That night, for the first time since entering the Giant level, none of them dream. The Dreamstone, even shielded, absorbs the ambient broadcast energy around the camp like a psychic sponge. The silence in their sleep is the most restful thing any of them have experienced in weeks.

Mercer stares at the lead-wrapped bundles and writes in his journal: "We have stolen the Sleeper's dream and wrapped it in lead. I wonder what it will dream about now."

## Key Items
- **Raw Dreamstone (25 lbs)** — enough for significant crafting. Cora can produce the following at the Cold Forge over the course of Act III:
  - **Dreamstone Circlet (1 per party member, 5 lbs each):** Grants advantage on Wisdom saves against the Sleeper's broadcast and resistance to psychic damage. Essential equipment for deeper exploration.
  - **Dreamstone-Forged Weapon (10 lbs):** A weapon that deals an additional 1d6 psychic damage and counts as magical for purposes of overcoming resistances. Can harm Anomaly manifestations.
  - **Reality Anchor (5 lbs):** A device that stabilizes local gravity in a 15 ft radius. Prevents gravity distortions within its area. One use per long rest.
  - Player choice which items to craft — the 25 lbs allows for 3 circlets plus one other item, or 2 circlets plus a weapon and an anchor.
- **Sentinel Fragments** — crystallized Dreamstone from the collapsed Sentinel. Unlike raw vein Dreamstone, these fragments are pre-structured — they have a lattice pattern that makes them easier to work with but limits their applications. Cora can use them as focuses for existing magic items, granting +1 to saving throw DCs for artificer infusions.
- **Dreamstone Vein Survey** — Mercer's detailed notes on the vein's location, extent, and radiation properties. Identifies two more veins on the Giant level that could be mined in future (not this adventure). Also maps the gravity distortion patterns, providing advantage on future navigation checks in the Giant foundation.
- **1,800 gp** equivalent in Dreamstone fragments too small for crafting but valuable as trade goods or material components.

## Key NPCs
- **Reyna Steelforge** — fascinated by the Dreamstone samples. She wants to send fragments to scholars across Laternia for analysis. The party must decide whether sharing Dreamstone samples with the outside world is wise — the material is powerful and potentially dangerous.
- **Ignathar (Fire Elemental)** — the forge will need to reach extreme temperatures to work Dreamstone. Ignathar can do this, but it requires effort: "The crystal of dreams does not yield to ordinary fire. I must burn as I burned in the deep places. It has been a long time." Working Dreamstone at the forge is harder on the Elemental — Cora should check on its condition after each Dreamstone crafting session.

## DM Notes
- The hallucinations are the heart of this adventure. Make each one vivid, personal, and unsettling without being hostile. The Sleeper's dreams are not nightmares — they are alien beauty that the human mind cannot process. The horror is not in what the characters see but in how it makes them feel: small, temporary, and infinitely curious.
- The gravity distortions should be mechanically disruptive but narratively wondrous. Describe objects floating in slow arcs, dust motes spiraling upward, Garrick's hair drifting as if underwater. This is not a combat hazard — it is a physics-defying environment that happens to be dangerous.
- The Sentinel is an immune response — the vein protecting itself the way a body forms a scab. Its collapse is a mining hazard resolved. Narrate the Sentinel's destruction like a cave-in or a rockslide — dangerous debris, crystalline shrapnel, dust settling.
- The crafting options from the Dreamstone are the Act III equipment upgrade, replacing the Act II forge crafting. Dreamstone Circlets are essentially required for the deeper Giant level exploration — without psychic shielding, the broadcast will prevent progress. The party should craft at least 2-3 circlets before Adventure 13.
- The peaceful, dreamless sleep at the end is significant. The Dreamstone absorbs ambient psychic energy. This property will be relevant in Act IV when the party must craft the final Seal.
