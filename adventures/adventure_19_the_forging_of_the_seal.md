# Adventure 19: The Forging of the Seal (Level 19)

## PACING RULES (CRITICAL — DM MUST FOLLOW)
- This is a SINGLE SESSION adventure. The DM should pace the full adventure across ~20 exchanges.
- Cover gathering the final components and the Fire Gate decision in the early part, the forging skill challenge in the middle, and the final forging rounds under maximum duress in the late part.
- If the players try to rush the forging, the Dreamstone's crystalline structure shatters if heated too quickly or inscribed with runes that are not precisely calibrated. Precision is required.
- NEVER let the party deploy the Seal this adventure. It is built here. It is used in Adventure 20.
- A D&D session should feel like a chapter of a book, not a summary of one.
- Only use events, NPCs, and locations described in this adventure file. Do not invent major plot elements not in the source material.

---

## Adventure Overview
The party has the knowledge, the materials, and the position. The Architect's Codex contains the containment theory. The scaling runes provide the technical framework. The Dreamstone provides the resonance medium. Korathan's Chisel provides the inscription tool. The staging position puts them within deployment range of the sphere.

The Dreamstone Seal is not a weapon. It is a containment upgrade — a device that reinforces the boundary between the Sleeper's reality and the physical universe, extending the containment's effective lifespan by another hundred thousand years. Its design is based on Korathan's original work, scaled for human crafting by the scaling runes, powered by Dreamstone's natural resonance with the Anomaly's frequency.

Building it inside the Caldera — within the broadcast zone, in zero gravity, fifty feet from the boundary between worlds — is necessary because the Seal must be calibrated to the sphere's exact frequency. The frequency shifts constantly, in patterns too subtle to measure from a distance. Cora must build the Seal while listening to the sphere, adjusting her work in real time to match its song.

**Adventure Goal (to be achieved in this session):** Forge the Dreamstone Seal. Survive the Caldera's response to its construction.

**IMPORTANT FOR DM:** This is a crafting adventure — the climax is not a fight but a creation. Cora is the primary actor, with Garrick and Mercer in critical support roles. The skill challenge should feel like the most important thing the party has ever done, because it is. Every piece of knowledge, every tool, every item they have accumulated across nineteen adventures feeds into this moment.

**Milestone:** Completing this adventure advances the party to Level 20.

### Act 1: The Components

#### Gathering in the Caldera
The Seal requires components that can only be found or finalized within the Caldera itself:

**Component 1 — Calibrated Dreamstone:**
The raw Dreamstone from Adventure 12 must be re-crystallized within the broadcast zone to attune it to the sphere's frequency. Cora heats the Dreamstone using a portable forge assembly (brought from the Cold Forge, assembled at the staging position) and allows the broadcast's resonance to imprint on the cooling crystal.

**DC 15 Intelligence (Cora):** Monitor the cooling rate and broadcast frequency simultaneously, adjusting the crystal's growth to match. If successful, the resulting Dreamstone blank is perfectly attuned — it vibrates at the sphere's exact frequency, producing a sound just below hearing. If failed, the crystal is slightly off-frequency — still usable but the final Seal will be less effective (Adventure 20 deployment DCs increase by 2).

**Component 2 — Containment Rune Matrix:**
Mercer must inscribe a complete rune matrix onto the Dreamstone blank using Korathan's Chisel replica and the scaling rune techniques. The matrix is the Seal's "programming" — the instructions that tell the Dreamstone how to interact with the sphere's boundary.

**DC 16 Arcana (Mercer, advantage from Architect's Codex):** Four hours of continuous inscription work. The runes must be precise — each one hand-carved at human scale, reproducing Giant containment patterns that were originally carved with tools the size of trees. This is the most technically demanding work Mercer has ever attempted.

**Component 3 — Structural Casing:**
The Dreamstone blank and rune matrix must be housed in a structural casing that protects them during deployment and channels the Seal's energy. Garrick is the hands: under Cora's direction, he shapes adamantine plates (from the unworked ore or the Juggernaut's chassis, worked at the Cold Forge during Intermission III) into a precisely fitted shell.

**DC 14 Athletics + DC 13 Intelligence (Garrick, with Cora's guidance):** The casing is simple in concept — a sphere of adamantine plates bolted around the Dreamstone core. The challenge is doing it in zero gravity, where every hammer blow sends both Garrick and the workpiece spinning unless properly anchored.

#### The Fire Gate Decision
If the party bargained with Ignathar and has not yet opened the Fire Gate, this is the moment. The portable forge at the staging position is adequate but limited — it cannot reach the temperatures needed for the most precise Dreamstone work. Opening the Fire Gate would provide a direct connection to the Elemental Plane of Fire, giving Cora access to unlimited elemental heat.

**Opening the Fire Gate:**
- **Risk:** A portal to the Plane of Fire inside the containment vessel could disrupt the containment field. The anchor points might fluctuate. The Deep Things might react to the alien energy.
- **Benefit:** Cora gains advantage on all forging checks. The Seal's final quality is improved (Adventure 20 deployment DCs decrease by 2). Ignathar's bargain is fulfilled — the Elemental is free.
- **Mechanics:** Mercer uses the scaling runes to unseal the conduit (DC 15 Arcana). The Fire Gate opens — not a roaring inferno but a focused stream of elemental fire, channeled by the conduit's Giant-era architecture into a controlled flow. The heat is immense but directed. The containment runes nearest the Gate dim slightly — the field is strained but holds.

If opened, Ignathar arrives within minutes — drawn through the conduit from the Cold Forge above, flowing through the mountain's geothermal network into the Caldera. The Elemental enters the Caldera as a being of pure fire, undiminished for the first time in millennia. It is magnificent — a pillar of white flame, twenty feet tall, blazing with joy.

"You kept your word," it says. "The small ones kept their word."

Ignathar assists with the forging willingly. Its control of temperature is supernatural — it can heat the Dreamstone to exact specifications and hold the temperature indefinitely. This is the partnership that the bargain was always meant to produce: the small ones build, the fire provides.

All three components are prepared (or nearly so). The Dreamstone blank is calibrated. The rune matrix is inscribed. The adamantine casing is shaped. The Fire Gate is opened or remains sealed, depending on the party's choice. The forging site at the staging position is ready. The sphere pulses fifty feet away, its boundary rippling. The Deep Things drift closer, curious about the fire and the work. The forging itself begins.

### Act 2: The Forging

#### The Skill Challenge
The forging of the Dreamstone Seal is an extended skill challenge — the most complex and consequential the party has faced. It requires all three party members working in concert, each contributing their expertise simultaneously.

**Structure:** 12 rounds of forging, each requiring a check from each party member. The party needs 8 or more total successes (out of 36 possible checks) to complete the Seal. But quality matters — the more successes, the more effective the Seal:
- **8-15 successes:** The Seal functions. Containment extended by 50,000 years. Adequate.
- **16-24 successes:** The Seal is strong. Containment extended by 100,000 years (matching the original). Excellent.
- **25-36 successes:** The Seal is masterwork. Containment extended by 200,000 years. The party has exceeded Korathan's original design. Legendary.

**Per-round checks:**

**Cora (primary forger):**
- DC 15 Intelligence with tinker's tools (advantage if Builder's Certainty, advantage if Ignathar assists)
- She heats, shapes, and assembles the Dreamstone core, adjusting crystalline grain in real time to match the sphere's shifting frequency

**Mercer (rune inscriber):**
- DC 15 Arcana (advantage if Scholar's Clarity, advantage with Architect's Codex)
- He inscribes the containment runes onto the Dreamstone as Cora shapes it, layering the matrix in precise sequence

**Garrick (structural support):**
- DC 13 Athletics or Intelligence (advantage if Guardian's Peace)
- He anchors the workpiece in zero gravity, holds components in position during assembly, and manages the adamantine casing's fit as the inner components change shape during forging

#### The Anomalies
The sphere responds to the Seal's construction — not deliberately, but reactively. As the Seal takes shape and begins to resonate with the sphere's frequency, the broadcast fluctuates. The Caldera becomes unstable.

**Anomaly escalation (one per round, cumulative):**

**Rounds 1-3 — Tremors:**
The sphere's surface ripples more intensely. The staging position vibrates. Equipment drifts. Minor — DC 12 Dexterity to maintain grip on tools and components. Failure adds -1 to that round's forging check.

**Rounds 4-6 — Gravity Fluctuations:**
The zero gravity wavers — brief gravity surges in random directions, lasting 1-2 seconds each. Enough to disrupt fine work. DC 13 Dexterity to compensate. Failure adds -2 to forging check. The Reality Anchor helps (reduces DC to 11 within its radius).

**Rounds 7-9 — Deep Thing Swarms:**
The Deep Things converge on the staging position — not attacking but investigating. 2d4 Deep Things per round, drifting through the workspace, passing through components and party members. Their Void Touch is incidental, not aggressive, but still damaging on contact. DC 14 Dexterity to avoid contact while working. Failure: 2d8 force damage + -2 to forging check.

Garrick's role shifts from structural support to defender — he must keep the Deep Things away from Cora and Mercer's workspace. Each round he spends defending reduces his own forging check but prevents Deep Thing interference with the others.

**Rounds 10-12 — The Broadcast Surge:**
The sphere's broadcast spikes to its highest intensity. The echoes return — each character's echo manifesting in the workspace, speaking, distracting, asking the unanswered questions from Adventure 18.

- Characters with Broadcast Immunity (resolved echoes) can ignore the surge. Their forging checks are unaffected.
- Characters with Broadcast Resistance (unresolved echoes) must make DC 16 Wisdom saves each round. Failure: the echo's presence imposes disadvantage on that round's forging check. Success: the echo provides insight, granting +2 to the check (the unresolved doubt becomes a creative force).

The party has completed 8-10 rounds of forging (of 12 total). The Seal is taking shape — a sphere of adamantine the size of a human skull, its interior a lattice of inscribed Dreamstone that hums with the sphere's frequency. It is not finished. The final rounds — the most critical, where the resonance must be locked and the casing sealed — remain. The anomalies are at their peak. The Deep Things swirl around the staging position. The echoes speak. The broadcast surges. Everything builds toward the final act.

### Act 3: The Final Rounds

#### Rounds 11-12 (or 10-12 if Act 2 ended early)
The final forging rounds are the hardest. The Seal is almost complete — it sings with its own frequency now, resonating with the sphere in a rising harmony that makes the air shimmer. The broadcast fights the resonance, not maliciously but instinctively — two incompatible frequencies interfering with each other.

**The Caldera's response reaches its peak:**
- Deep Things swarm — 4d4 per round, pressing closer, their Void Touch more intense
- Gravity oscillates wildly — the Reality Anchor strains at its limits
- The sphere's surface extends pseudopods of boundary material toward the staging position — reaching, probing, not attacking but testing the new resonance source
- The echoes (if unresolved) speak continuously — a stream of questions, observations, truths, and fears

**Garrick's final stand:** He must hold the perimeter alone while Cora and Mercer complete the work. This is a combat-endurance test — not against a single enemy but against an environment that is actively rejecting the Seal's presence. He fights Deep Things (standard combat), deflects pseudopods (DC 16 Athletics to push them away from the workspace), and anchors the staging position against gravity surges (DC 15 Athletics to prevent the entire workspace from drifting toward the sphere).

**Cora's final act:** The last step is sealing the adamantine casing while the Dreamstone core is resonating. The timing must be exact — the casing must close at the moment the core's frequency locks onto the sphere's pattern. One chance. One check.

**DC 17 Intelligence with tinker's tools (Cora):** All bonuses and penalties from the session apply. Ignathar's assistance, Builder's Certainty, the echo's influence — everything converges on this single roll.

**On success:** The casing closes. The Seal locks. The Dreamstone core's frequency harmonizes with the sphere's boundary — a clean, pure note that cuts through the broadcast like a bell through fog. The Deep Things stop. The gravity stabilizes. The echoes fall silent. For one moment, the Caldera is still.

The Seal is complete.

**On failure:** The casing closes imperfectly. The Seal functions but with a flaw — a hairline crack in the adamantine, a misaligned rune, a frequency offset. It will work, but the deployment in Adventure 20 will be harder (DCs increase by 3) and the containment extension will be reduced by one tier.

#### The Completed Seal
The Dreamstone Seal sits in Cora's hands — a sphere of adamantine and Dreamstone, roughly the size of a human skull, warm to the touch, humming with a frequency that matches the boundary between worlds. Its surface is covered in scaled-down Giant containment runes, inscribed by Mercer, powered by Dreamstone calibrated to the Sleeper's exact resonance frequency.

It is the most important object in the mountain. Possibly the most important object on the continent.

**What it does:** When deployed at the sphere's boundary, the Seal integrates with the existing containment architecture — adding a new layer to the translation field, reinforcing the barrier between realities, extending the containment's effective lifespan by decades of thousands of years. It is Korathan's work, continued. The long watch, extended.

**The closing beat:** Cora holds the Seal and looks at the sphere. It pulses steadily, its boundary rippling, the First World visible through its surface in moments of transparency. The Deep Things have resumed their slow orbits. The echoes are quiet — resolved or unresolved, they have said what they needed to say.

Garrick floats beside her, battered from the defense, his Runic Maul still crackling with residual void energy. Mercer hovers nearby, the Codex open in his lap, recording the Seal's specifications with a hand that does not quite shake.

"It is done," Cora says.

"It is built," Mercer corrects. "Done is what happens when we place it."

## Key Items
- **The Dreamstone Seal** — the campaign's ultimate objective, now crafted. Its quality (adequate/excellent/masterwork) is determined by the skill challenge's total successes. It must be deployed in Adventure 20.
- **Fire Gate Status** — if opened, the conduit between the Caldera and the Plane of Fire remains active. Ignathar has departed through it, free at last, but the conduit can be used by the party for emergency access to elemental fire. If not opened, the Gate remains sealed.
- **Forging Notes** — Cora's detailed record of the forging process. If the party ever needs to build a second Seal (or repair this one), these notes provide the blueprint.

## Key NPCs
- **Ignathar (Fire Elemental)** — if freed, Ignathar's departure is a moment of catharsis. The ancient Elemental has served, been imprisoned, been freed, been rebargained with, and now — finally — goes home. Its last words to the party: "The fire remembers the small ones who kept their word. If you need flame, you know where to find it." Ignathar is not gone forever — it can be called through the Fire Gate in dire need.
- **The Deep Things** — during the forging, their behavior shifts. They begin as curious, become agitated during the anomalies, and at the moment of the Seal's completion, they do something unprecedented: they stop. All of them. Every Deep Thing in the Caldera freezes in place for one heartbeat. Then they resume. Whatever the Seal's completion meant to the Sleeper's dreaming, the Deep Things registered it.
- **The Echoes** — their final appearance during the forging is their last major role. Whether resolved or persistent, they have served their narrative purpose: humanizing the cosmic, connecting the campaign's thematic arc to the characters' personal arcs.

## DM Notes
- The forging skill challenge is the campaign's mechanical climax. It should feel epic, collaborative, and earned. Every item, every technique, every piece of knowledge the party has gathered feeds into the checks. Reference the sources: "Cora uses the Dwarven Forge Tools from Adventure 7," "Mercer references the scaling runes from Adventure 14," "Garrick anchors with techniques learned navigating the Gravity Well."
- The quality tiers (adequate/excellent/masterwork) affect Adventure 20 but should not feel like pass/fail. Even an adequate Seal is a triumph — the party has built a containment device that extends the seal by fifty thousand years. The tiers affect deployment difficulty and the narrative epilogue, not whether the campaign can be completed.
- Ignathar's departure (if freed) is one of the campaign's most emotionally resonant moments. An ancient being, imprisoned for millennia, kept alive by a bargain with three small people who kept their word. Let it breathe. Let it matter.
- The Deep Things freezing at the Seal's completion is the closest the campaign comes to showing the Sleeper's "reaction." The Sleeper does not know the party exists. But it felt the Seal's frequency lock onto its boundary, and for one moment, its dreaming changed. That single heartbeat of silence is the most communication the party will ever have with the entity they have spent twenty adventures learning to contain.
