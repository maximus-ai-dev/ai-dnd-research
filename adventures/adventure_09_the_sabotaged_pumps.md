# Adventure 9: The Sabotaged Pumps (Level 9)

## PACING RULES (CRITICAL — DM MUST FOLLOW)
- This is a SINGLE SESSION adventure. Pace the full adventure within ~20 exchanges.
- Cover all three acts in this session. Do not compress excessively — each act should receive meaningful screen time — but do not stretch or pad either.
- The early part of the adventure should focus on reaching Pump Station East via the minecart network, discovering the sabotage, and encountering the first direct evidence of the Twisted.
- The middle part should cover establishing defensive positions, repairing what they can, and the first wave of Twisted saboteurs.
- The late part should be the full defensive engagement — multiple waves, escalating pressure, and the final pump activation under fire. The pumps come online. The water begins to recede.
- If the players try to rush the pump repairs, the sabotage is layered — fixing one problem reveals another. The Twisted have been methodical.
- NEVER let the party pursue the Twisted into the deep tunnels this adventure. The Twisted retreat into flooded passages the party cannot follow.
- A D&D session should feel like a chapter of a book, not a summary of one.
- Only use events, NPCs, and locations described in this adventure file. Do not invent major plot elements not in the source material.

## Adventure Overview
The minecart network is operational. The forge is hot. The party has accomplished more in weeks than the dwarves' successors managed in centuries. But the transit map shows a critical gap: the northern branch to Pump Station East is partially flooded, and the pump station itself is offline. Without the pumps, the lower Dwarven Layer remains submerged — hundreds of feet of infrastructure, mining operations, and the primary access routes to the Giant foundation level are underwater.

The party takes the northern rail line as far as it goes. The flooding begins gradually — ankle-deep water on the tracks, then knee-deep, then the tunnel slopes upward to a dry platform above the waterline. Pump Station East is here: a massive dwarven engineering installation designed to drain the lower levels using the mountain's own geothermal pressure differentials. It should be running. It is not.

It has been sabotaged. Deliberately, systematically, and recently. Pipes have been severed. Valves have been fused shut with an organic resin that Cora has never seen before. Control mechanisms have been dismantled with surgical precision. Someone — something — does not want these pumps running.

The Twisted have arrived.

**Adventure Goal (to be achieved in this session):** Repair Pump Station East and defend it against Twisted saboteurs until the pumps are fully operational.

**IMPORTANT FOR DM:** This is the party's first direct combat encounter with the Twisted. They are not mindless. They are corrupted dwarves — preserved by the Black Slumber's radiation, their engineering skills intact, their bodies suffused with black fluid and iridescent veins. They do not speak. They do not negotiate. They work in coordinated teams with clear tactical objectives: destroy the pumps, prevent drainage, protect whatever is in the flooded depths below. They are tragic — and they are lethal.

**Milestone:** Completing this adventure advances the party to Level 10.

### Act 1: Pump Station East

#### The Northern Line
The minecart ride north from the transit hub takes twenty minutes. The tunnel follows a steady decline, well-built and well-maintained — the dwarves valued their water infrastructure. Rail conditions are good for the first half. Then the water appears.

It starts as moisture on the rails — a thin film that makes the cart's wheels hiss. Then puddles between the sleepers. Then standing water, rising steadily. The maintenance cart begins to drag. At six inches of water on the tracks, Cora calls a halt — the cart will derail if they continue.

The party proceeds on foot. The water rises to knee level before the tunnel slopes upward to a dry platform — an elevated staging area designed to remain above the waterline during normal pump operations. The platform holds the entrance to Pump Station East.

**Garrick's observation:** The water is not stagnant. It circulates slowly, drawn by currents from below. And it is warm — warmer than the upper level water should be. Whatever is heating it comes from deep.

**Cora's assessment:** The water level is wrong. According to the transit map and the engineering principles of the pump system, the normal waterline should be thirty feet below this platform. The pumps have been off long enough for the lower levels to completely flood and the water to rise into the transit tunnels.

#### The Station
Pump Station East is an engineering marvel. A circular chamber sixty feet in diameter, dominated by three massive pump assemblies arranged in a triangle. Each pump is a fifteen-foot-tall mechanism of iron pistons, stone channels, and precisely machined valve systems. They use geothermal pressure differentials — heat from below pushes water upward through intake channels, then the pumps redirect it through drainage conduits that carry it out of the mountain entirely, emerging as streams on the Starmetal Hills' eastern slopes.

The pumps are dead. And they have been murdered.

**The sabotage (Cora's investigation, DC 14 Intelligence):**
- **Pump One:** Intake valve fused shut with a black organic resin — hard as stone, resistant to tools. The resin is biological, grown rather than applied. It has the iridescent sheen of Dreamstone radiation.
- **Pump Two:** Three critical connecting pipes severed cleanly. The cuts are precise — not broken by force but sliced through by something sharp and deliberate. The pipe sections have been removed entirely, taken away.
- **Pump Three:** The control mechanism — a complex arrangement of gears, levers, and pressure gauges — has been disassembled piece by piece. The components are scattered across the floor in a pattern that looks almost organized. Whoever did this understood exactly how the mechanism worked.

**Mercer's analysis:** This is not animal damage. This is engineering. The sabotage was performed by something that understood dwarven pump technology at a professional level. And the organic resin — he has read about it in the Runesmith's Journal from Adventure 7. The last runesmith mentioned "the black growth that sealed the lower corridors." This is Twisted bioengineering.

#### First Evidence
Near Pump Three's scattered components, Garrick finds a footprint in the mineral residue on the floor. It is a dwarven boot print — broad, heavy-soled, the tread pattern matching the ceremonial armor from the mausoleum. But this print is fresh. Days old, not centuries.

More prints lead from the pump station to a side passage that descends into the flooded section. The passage is half-submerged — two feet of warm, dark water with no visible bottom. In the water, faint bioluminescence: blue-green light from organisms Mercer cannot identify, pulsing in slow rhythm.

Something moves in the water. A ripple, thirty feet in. Then stillness.

The Twisted are watching.

### Act 2: Holding the Line

#### Triage and Repair
The party commits to repairing the pumps. Cora prioritizes:

**Pump Three (control mechanism)** is the fastest to repair — the components are present, just disassembled. Cora can reassemble the mechanism (three DC 14 Intelligence checks with tinker's tools, one per major subsystem).

**Pump One (resin-sealed valve)** requires removing the organic resin. Cora's alchemist supplies can dissolve it, but the process is slow and the resin resists chemical treatment. DC 15 Intelligence with alchemist's supplies to create an effective solvent. The resin fights the solvent — it regrows at the edges as fast as Cora can dissolve the center. She needs someone to scrape the regrowing edges while she works the center. Two-person job.

**Pump Two (severed pipes)** is the hardest. The missing pipe sections must be replaced. The dwarves kept spare parts — Cora finds a maintenance closet with replacement components, but they need to be cut to length and fitted. This requires forge-quality tools and heat for welding. Either someone makes a round trip to the Cold Forge for portable welding equipment, or Cora improvises with her tinker's tools (DC 16 Intelligence, disadvantage without proper welding tools).

#### Establishing Defenses
While Cora works, Garrick and Mercer fortify the station:

- **The main entrance** from the northern tunnel is the primary approach. Garrick barricades it with pump components and overturned carts from the staging platform.
- **The flooded side passage** is the secondary approach — and the more dangerous one. It cannot be effectively barricaded because the water would simply flow around any obstacle. Mercer rigs an alarm using Minor Illusion and tripwires fashioned from dwarven wire found in the maintenance closet.
- **The drainage conduits** are a potential third approach — large enough for a dwarven body to crawl through, they connect the pumps to the mountain's exterior. Currently dry (the pumps are off). If the Twisted can access the conduit system from outside the station, they could bypass all defenses.

#### The First Wave
Midway through Cora's repair of Pump Three, Mercer's alarm triggers. Ripples in the flooded passage. Then silence. Then a shape rises from the water in the side passage — a dwarf, standing waist-deep, perfectly still.

It is wrong. The proportions are dwarven — stocky, broad-shouldered, heavy-limbed. But the skin has the iridescent Dreamstone sheen the party saw on the mausoleum dead. The veins are black and visibly pulsing. The eyes are open and aware — not the hollow gaze of the echoes. These eyes track. These eyes plan.

It is carrying tools. Not weapons — tools. A pry bar, a stone chisel, a coil of the black organic resin.

Three more rise behind it.

**Twisted Saboteurs (4):**
- AC 15 (corrupted dwarven chain), HP 45 each, Speed 25 ft (30 ft swimming)
- Pry Bar: +6 to hit, 1d8+4 bludgeoning
- Chisel Strike: +6 to hit, 1d6+4 piercing, ignores 2 points of AC (armor penetration)
- **Coordinated Sabotage:** If two or more Twisted reach a pump mechanism in the same round, they can perform a sabotage action that undoes one hour of Cora's repair work
- **Corruption Aura (passive):** Creatures within 5 ft of a Twisted make Constitution saves (DC 13) at the start of their turn. Failure: 1d4 psychic damage and brief disorientation (disadvantage on next ability check)
- **Resistant:** Resistant to poison and necrotic damage. Vulnerable to radiant damage.

**Combat objective:** The Twisted do not prioritize killing the party. They prioritize reaching the pumps. Two will engage Garrick at the barricade as a distraction. Two will attempt to circle through the flooded passage to reach the pumps from behind. If any reach a pump, they begin sabotaging immediately.

The combat is a holding action. The party must kill or repel the Twisted while Cora continues repairs (she loses one round of work for every round she is forced to stop and fight or dodge).

### Act 3: The Siege

#### Final Repairs Under Fire
The final act opens with urgency. Cora needs sustained uninterrupted work to finish Pumps One and Two. The party will not get uninterrupted time.

**The repair sequence:**
- **Pump One (remaining work):** Cora finishes dissolving the resin while Garrick scrapes the regrowing edges. DC 13 Intelligence (Cora) and DC 12 Strength (Garrick) working simultaneously. On completion, the intake valve is clear.
- **Pump Two:** Pipe replacement. If the party retrieved welding tools from the forge, DC 14 Intelligence. If improvising, DC 16 Intelligence. On completion, the water system is connected.
- **Final activation:** All three pumps must be started in sequence. Pump Three first (already operational — one lever pull). Pump One second (valve opens, water rushes in, the whole station shakes). Pump Two last (completes the circuit). Each activation is a DC 12 Intelligence check.

#### The Second Wave
As repairs progress, the second wave arrives. This is not a sabotage team — this is an assault.

**Twisted Assault Force:**
- 6 Twisted Saboteurs (same stats as Act 2)
- 1 Twisted Overseer

**Twisted Overseer:**
- AC 17 (heavy corrupted plate), HP 78, Speed 25 ft
- Warhammer: +8 to hit, 2d8+5 bludgeoning
- **Command Corruption:** As a bonus action, directs all Twisted within 30 ft to focus on a single target or objective. Affected Twisted gain +2 to hit and advantage on their next sabotage action.
- **Corruption Pulse (Recharge 5-6):** 15 ft radius burst. DC 15 Constitution save. Failure: 3d8 necrotic damage and stunned for 1 round. Success: half damage, no stun.
- **Resistant:** Resistant to poison and necrotic. Vulnerable to radiant.

The Overseer is visibly different from the saboteurs. It wears heavier armor. Its movements are more deliberate, more purposeful. It assesses the party's defenses before committing its forces. This is a commander.

**The assault pattern:**
- Round 1-2: Saboteurs probe defenses from multiple entry points (main entrance, flooded passage, drainage conduits — they found the third approach)
- Round 3-4: Overseer commits the main force through the primary entrance while a flanking pair enters through the conduits behind Cora
- Round 5+: If the pumps are still being repaired, the Overseer personally advances to destroy them

**Key tactical moments:**
- Garrick must hold the main entrance against four saboteurs and the Overseer. His Rune Knight abilities (Giant's Might) are critical here — growing to Large size in the twelve-foot doorway creates a living barricade.
- Mercer must cover the drainage conduit approach — two saboteurs emerging from a crawlspace directly behind the pump Cora is repairing. Spell selection matters: area denial (Web, Flaming Sphere) is more effective than direct damage.
- Cora must keep working. Every round she spends fighting is a round the pumps stay offline. The party must protect her, not rely on her combat contribution.

#### The Third Wave (Conditional)
If the pumps are not activated within 10 rounds of the second wave's arrival, a third group appears: 4 more saboteurs from the flooded passage, moving fast and directly for the pumps with no pretense of engagement. This is the Twisted's last-ditch effort — pure sabotage, ignoring the party entirely.

#### Activation
When Cora completes the final pump and pulls the activation sequence, the effect is dramatic. The geothermal pressure differential engages — a deep shudder runs through the entire station. The pumps roar to life, pistons driving with a force that vibrates the floor. Water in the flooded passage begins to recede visibly. The warm current reverses — water is now being pulled down and out through the drainage conduits.

Any Twisted still in the station when the pumps activate are caught in the current shift. The water in the flooded passage drops rapidly, stranding them on wet stone. Without water, they are slower and less coordinated. Mopping up is straightforward.

The Overseer, if still alive, does something unexpected: it stops fighting. It stands at the entrance to the flooded passage, watching the water recede, and for one moment its expression is readable. It is not anger. It is fear. Then it turns and retreats into the draining passage, moving faster than should be possible, disappearing into the dark.

#### The Water Recedes
Over the next several hours, the pump station does its work. The water level in the lower Dwarven Layer drops steadily. Tunnels that have been submerged for centuries begin to drain. The minecart tracks on the lower levels emerge from the receding waterline, corroded but intact.

From below, as the water drains, sounds emerge: the groaning of stressed stone, the drip of centuries of mineral deposits, and — distant but unmistakable — the heavy industrial sounds the party first heard from the Human layers above. The Twisted's operations are being exposed. Their flooded hiding places are draining. They are losing their cover.

**The closing beat:** Mercer stands at the edge of the flooded passage, watching the waterline drop. In the receding water, something catches the torchlight — a glint of metal, massive, partially visible. As the water drops another foot, the outline becomes clearer: a construct. Mechanical. Dwarven. Enormous — at least fifteen feet tall, partially embedded in the tunnel floor. It is corroded and still, coated in the black organic resin of the Twisted. It is not moving.

"What is that?" Garrick asks.

Mercer consults the blueprint tablet. His face goes white. "It is a mining mech. The dwarves built autonomous excavation machines. And the Twisted have been... modifying it."

## Key Items
- **Twisted Resin Sample** — a jar of the black organic resin, sealed in lead. Cora can study its properties: biological, radiation-responsive, grows in the presence of Dreamstone energy. Understanding it gives advantage on future checks to counter Twisted engineering (two adventure cycles).
- **Overseer's Warhammer** — a +1 warhammer of dwarven make, corrupted by prolonged Twisted contact. The handle is warm to the touch and the head has a faint iridescent sheen. If Cora cleanses it at the Cold Forge (one day of work, DC 14 Intelligence), it becomes a standard +1 warhammer with no corruption effects. If kept uncleansed, it deals an additional 1d4 necrotic damage but the wielder takes 1 necrotic damage per hit dealt.
- **Pump Station Operations Manual** — found in the maintenance closet, partially damaged by moisture. Contains detailed schematics of the entire pump network, including Pump Station West (which services the other half of the Dwarven Layer) and the master drainage controls at the Drowned Chokepoint — the intersection where all water systems converge. Seeds Adventure 10.
- **Twisted Engineering Samples** — tools and components left behind by the saboteurs. Dwarven manufacture, modified with organic components. Cora's analysis reveals the Twisted have been integrating biological materials into mechanical systems — a fusion of dwarven engineering and the Anomaly's corruption.
- **800 gp** in salvageable pump components, dwarven plumbing fixtures, and tool sets.

## Key NPCs
- **The Twisted Overseer** — the first Twisted the party has encountered who demonstrated tactical intelligence and emotional response. Its fear at the water receding suggests the Twisted have something to protect in the flooded depths. Its retreat means it will report back. The party has been identified as a threat.
- **Ignathar (Fire Elemental)** — the forge's heat is now connected to the pump system via the cooling channels. Ignathar's fire powers the geothermal differentials that drive the pumps. If the party bargained with Ignathar, it comments: "The small ones drain the deep places. The deep things will not be pleased." If bound, the forge fire flickers nervously when the pumps activate.
- **Reyna Steelforge** — when news reaches Grimhold that the party has activated ancient dwarven infrastructure, Reyna's response is to begin organizing the Grimhold Specialists — engineers, miners, and smiths willing to work deep. This is the seed for Intermission II.

## DM Notes
- The Twisted should feel tragic, not just dangerous. Describe their dwarven features — the braided beards now matted with black fluid, the ceremonial armor corroded but maintained, the professional precision of their sabotage. These were craftsmen once. They are still craftsmen. They just serve something else now.
- The defense-of-the-pump-station format is a deliberate tonal shift from exploration to tactical combat. The party is no longer discovering — they are holding territory. This is the campaign's transition from "explorers" to "defenders."
- The Overseer's fear is the most important narrative beat. The Twisted have been operating in secret for millennia. The party has just drained their hiding place. The Overseer knows what comes next: the party will find what is in the flooded depths. This terrifies the Twisted because their operations — the drills, the amplifiers, the slow destruction of the Giant containment — will be exposed.
- The mining mech glimpsed at the end is the Drowned Juggernaut — the Act II boss. Do not name it yet. Let it be a shape in the dark, a promise of something worse. Adventure 10 will reveal it fully.
- The pump activation permanently changes the campaign geography. The lower Dwarven Layer is now accessible. The transit map's flooded sections are draining. New routes open. The party's operational range has expanded dramatically — and so has their exposure to the Twisted.
