# Adventure 16: The Saboteur's Drill (Level 16, Act III Finale)

## PACING RULES (CRITICAL — DM MUST FOLLOW)
- This is a SINGLE SESSION adventure. The DM should pace the full adventure across ~20 exchanges.
- Cover the crisis and Warden discovery in the early part, the analysis and Resonator construction in the middle, and the boss fight and breach sealing in the late part.
- If the players try to rush the Warden, it is far too powerful for direct engagement. The containment technology is the equalizer.
- NEVER let the party enter the void through the breach this adventure. The breach must be sealed, not explored.
- A D&D session should feel like a chapter of a book, not a summary of one.
- Only use events, NPCs, and locations described in this adventure file. Do not invent major plot elements not in the source material.

---

## Adventure Overview
The staging ground is destroyed. The organic drill is rubble. The party sealed the bore hole. Victory — brief, fragile, and incomplete.

Three days after the party returned from Adventure 15, the Monitoring Crystal screams. Not a reading — a scream, the crystal's resonance frequency spiking to a pitch that shatters a beaker on Cora's workbench. The containment field readings are in freefall. A breach has occurred.

Not at the bore hole they sealed. Not at the Juggernaut's scar. A third site — on the opposite side of the Giant Capstone, in a section the party has never explored. The Twisted had a backup plan. A third drill, hidden deeper, operated by a smaller team, boring from a direction the party's monitoring did not cover.

And something has come through.

The breach released a pulse of void energy that reached the Giant foundation's deepest structural supports. There, in a chamber the blueprint tablet labels as "The Warden's Post," the energy contacted something the Giants left behind: the skeletal remains of a Giant guardian, entombed in a containment sarcophagus as a last-resort defender. The void energy has reanimated it — infected it — transformed it into something neither alive nor dead. The Reanimated Warden: a Giant skeleton forty feet tall, suffused with the Sleeper's corruption, standing at the breach like a sentinel of the thing below.

**Adventure Goal (to be achieved in this session):** Reach the breach site. Defeat the Reanimated Warden. Seal the breach before the void energy destabilizes the entire capstone.

**IMPORTANT FOR DM:** This is the Act III boss fight. The Warden is a CR 17+ encounter against a level 16 party — it is meant to be fought with the containment technology the party has developed across Act III, not through raw combat power. The scaling runes, the Dreamstone equipment, the containment field manipulation — all of it comes together here.

**Milestone:** Completing this adventure advances the party to Level 17 and triggers Intermission III.

### Act 1: The Third Breach

#### The Alarm
The Monitoring Crystal's spike is accompanied by physical effects throughout the Giant level: the containment runes flicker, the forward camp's psychic shielding wavers for a terrifying moment, and from below, a sound like a mountain exhaling — a rush of warm, sweet-smelling air that flows through every corridor, carrying the concentrated essence of the Anomaly's radiation.

**DC 13 Constitution save (all characters at the forward camp):** Failure: 2d6 psychic damage and the character sees a flash of the void — the darkness below the capstone, the shapes within it, closer than before. The Dreamstone Circlets provide advantage.

**Cora's immediate assessment:** The Monitoring Crystal shows the breach location — southwest quadrant, roughly 600 feet from the forward camp, in unexplored territory. The containment field readings at the breach are zero. Whatever happened, it was catastrophic and sudden.

#### Racing to the Breach
The party moves fast. The corridors toward the breach site show escalating damage: containment runes cracked and dark, stone walls fractured by the energy pulse, and — most disturbing — organic resin in the walls, long dormant since the amplifiers' destruction, now glowing faintly. The void energy is feeding the remnant Twisted infrastructure.

**Environmental hazards on approach:**
- **Radiation surge zones:** Pockets where the void energy has pooled. DC 16 Constitution save or 3d6 psychic + necrotic damage. The Reality Anchor creates safe passage through these zones.
- **Gravity fractures:** Localized gravity failures where the containment field has collapsed. Random direction shifts, floating debris, sections of corridor where "down" is sideways. DC 14 Dexterity save to navigate without injury (2d6 bludgeoning on failure).
- **Echo manifestations:** The void energy has triggered echo responses in the stone itself — momentary apparitions of Giants, dwarves, and humans who once walked these corridors. They are not hostile — they are residual psychic imprints activated by the energy surge. But they are disorienting.

#### The Warden's Post
The corridor opens into a vast chamber — the "Warden's Post" from the blueprint tablet. It was designed as a guardian station: a chamber where a Giant sentry could be stationed permanently, embedded in the containment architecture, monitoring the seal below. The chamber is roughly 80 feet in diameter with a domed ceiling sixty feet high.

At the center of the chamber: a sarcophagus. Open. Empty.

At the far wall: the breach. A ragged hole forty feet across in the capstone floor, edges glowing with void energy. The containment runes around the breach are shattered — not cracked, shattered, as if struck by an immense force from below.

And standing at the edge of the breach, facing the party, is the Reanimated Warden.

#### The Warden
It is the largest creature the party has ever seen. A Giant skeleton, forty feet tall, held together not by ligaments but by threads of void energy that lace through the bones like dark tendons. Its skull is crowned with corrupted Dreamstone growths — crystalline formations that glow with the same iridescent light as the void below. Its hands are the size of minecarts. Its eye sockets burn with the broadcast's frequency.

It does not attack immediately. It turns its massive skull toward the party and regards them. Then it speaks — not in Giant, not in any language, but in the broadcast frequency itself. The words form directly in the party's minds:

*THE SEAL IS BROKEN. THE WATCHER WAKES. THE DEEP THINGS RISE.*

Then it raises one enormous fist and brings it down on the chamber floor with enough force to crack the stone for thirty feet in every direction.

> **HOSTILE-ONLY ENCOUNTER — THE BROADCAST IS A RECORDING, NOT COMMUNICATION**
> The Warden's telepathic message is residual psychic static playing through its dead nervous system — like a bell still ringing after the clapper has stopped. The Warden cannot hear the party, cannot respond to words, cannot be reasoned with. It is corrupted — the original guardian overwritten by void energy. THE WARDEN ATTACKS IMMEDIATELY AFTER THE BROADCAST. Its first action is a Colossal Slam targeting the nearest party member.

The party has reached the Warden's Post, seen the breach and the Reanimated Warden, and survived the initial demonstration of its power. The Warden does not pursue — it is tethered to the breach, drawing power from the void energy flowing through it. The party retreats to assess and plan. The containment field readings continue to drop. Time is critical.

### Act 2: The Containment Fails

#### Analyzing the Warden
From a safe distance, Mercer studies the Warden using the Monitoring Crystal and his accumulated knowledge:

**DC 15 Arcana (advantage from Architect's Codex):** The Warden was a Giant guardian — one of several entombed in sarcophagi throughout the foundation level as emergency defenders. The containment system was designed to reanimate them if the seal was breached, turning them into autonomous combat units that would fight whatever came through. The problem: the void energy that reanimated the Warden also corrupted it. It is defending the breach, but it is also feeding on it. It will not allow anyone to seal the breach because sealing it would cut off its power source.

**Key vulnerabilities:**
1. **The void tether:** The Warden draws power through the breach. Its strength is proportional to its proximity to the void. At the breach's edge, it is at full power. Forty feet away, it weakens. Eighty feet away, it becomes manageable.
2. **The containment runes:** The chamber's containment runes are damaged but not destroyed. If reactivated, they would create a suppression field that weakens the void energy — and therefore the Warden. Mercer can reactivate them using the scaling runes and the Monitoring Crystal, but he needs time and proximity to the rune arrays on the chamber walls.
3. **The Dreamstone resonance:** The Warden's corrupted Dreamstone crown is its control node — similar to the Juggernaut's biological brain. Dreamstone-forged weapons deal double damage to it. If the crown is destroyed, the Warden loses coherence.
4. **The Capstone Resonator:** A device Mercer can construct from the Dreamstone Power Cell (from Adventure 10), the scaling runes, and the Monitoring Crystal. It would create a focused containment pulse that could stun the Warden and temporarily suppress the breach. But building it takes time — and components.

#### Building the Capstone Resonator
Mercer and Cora work together to construct the Resonator during the middle part of the adventure:

**Components required:**
- Dreamstone Power Cell (from Adventure 10) — provides the energy source
- Monitoring Crystal — provides the frequency template
- Korathan's Chisel (replica) — provides the rune-inscription capability
- Raw Dreamstone (5 lbs from Adventure 12 reserves) — provides the resonance medium

**Construction:** DC 16 Arcana (Mercer) and DC 15 Intelligence (Cora) working simultaneously, 4 hours of work. The Resonator is a device roughly the size of a lantern, humming with contained power. It has one charge. When activated, it produces a containment pulse in a 60-foot radius that:
- Suppresses void energy for 3 rounds
- Deals 6d8 radiant damage to void-corrupted creatures
- Stuns void-corrupted creatures for 1 round (DC 18 Constitution save negates stun)
- Temporarily seals any breach within the radius (3 rounds)

#### First Engagement
The party returns to the Warden's Post to test the Warden's capabilities and set up for the final assault.

**The Reanimated Warden:**
- AC 20 (Giant bone + void energy reinforcement)
- HP 280
- Speed 40 ft
- **Colossal Slam:** +13 to hit, reach 15 ft, 4d10+8 bludgeoning + 2d8 necrotic. Target is pushed 15 ft and knocked prone (DC 18 Strength save negates push/prone).
- **Void Stomp:** All creatures within 20 ft: DC 18 Dexterity save, 3d10 bludgeoning + knocked prone. The ground cracks and difficult terrain is created in the area.
- **Broadcast Scream (Recharge 5-6):** 60 ft cone. DC 18 Wisdom save. Failure: 6d8 psychic damage, frightened 1 minute, and broadcast hallucination (the character perceives the void as a welcoming place and must use movement to approach the breach unless they succeed on the save at end of each turn). Success: half damage, not frightened.
- **Void Tether:** Regains 20 HP at the start of each turn while within 40 ft of the breach. At 80+ ft from breach, regeneration stops. At 120+ ft, loses 10 max HP per round.
- **Legendary Resistance (2/day):** Automatically succeeds on a failed saving throw.
- **Damage Resistances:** Resistant to nonmagical weapons, fire, cold, lightning. Immune to poison, psychic, necrotic.
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Radiant. Dreamstone-forged weapons deal double damage to the corrupted crown (AC 16, HP 60, can be targeted separately).

**The test engagement should establish:** The Warden's devastating melee power (one Colossal Slam can nearly kill a level 16 character), its Broadcast Scream's terrifying crowd control, and its void tether regeneration. The party should deal some damage, learn its patterns, and retreat when the Warden demonstrates that a direct fight at the breach is suicide.

The party has built the Capstone Resonator, tested the Warden's capabilities, and retreated to plan the final assault. They know the Warden's weaknesses: distance from the breach, the containment runes, and the corrupted crown. They have one shot with the Resonator. They need to use it perfectly.

### Act 3: Sealing the Breach

#### The Final Assault
The party enters the Warden's Post with a coordinated plan:

**Phase 1 — Rune Reactivation:**
Mercer moves along the chamber walls, reactivating damaged containment runes using the scaling techniques from Korathan's tomb. Each rune array takes 1 round and a DC 15 Arcana check. There are four arrays — north, south, east, west. Each reactivated array reduces the Warden's void tether regeneration by 5 HP/round and creates a 20-foot zone of suppressed void energy. Reactivating all four creates a partial containment field across the entire chamber.

The Warden will not ignore Mercer. It recognizes what he is doing. After the first array is reactivated, it prioritizes him — moving to crush him against the wall. Garrick must intercept.

**Phase 2 — The Lure:**
Garrick's objective: draw the Warden away from the breach. Every 10 feet of distance reduces its regeneration. At 80 feet, the regeneration stops entirely. Garrick cannot match the Warden's damage output in a fair fight, but he can survive it — adamantine armor negates critical hits, Rune Knight Giant's Might gives him the size to withstand the pushback, and his Dreamstone-forged weapon (if crafted) deals meaningful damage to the crown.

The Warden does not want to leave the breach. Drawing it away requires either:
- Garrick dealing enough damage to trigger its aggression (30+ damage in a round provokes a pursuit response)
- Cora creating a perceived threat to the breach itself (a distraction — alchemist's fire near the breach edge, making the Warden think the party is trying to collapse the breach rather than seal it)

**Phase 3 — The Resonator:**
When the Warden is at least 60 feet from the breach and 3+ containment arrays are active, Cora deploys the Capstone Resonator. She must reach the breach's edge and activate the device directly above the void opening.

**Resonator activation:** DC 16 Dexterity (Cora) to plant the device while the breach's void energy pushes against her. On success:
- 60-foot radius containment pulse: 6d8 radiant damage to all void-corrupted creatures
- Warden: DC 18 Constitution save or stunned for 1 round
- Breach: temporarily sealed for 3 rounds (void energy flow stops)
- Chamber: all containment runes in the radius flare to full power, creating a sustained suppression field

**Phase 4 — The Kill:**
With the Warden stunned (or at least weakened by the suppression field) and the breach sealed (cutting off its regeneration), the party has a 3-round window to inflict maximum damage.

- **Garrick:** Full melee assault on the Warden's body. Without regeneration and with the suppression field reducing its resistance, each hit counts. Target the crown when possible (double damage from Dreamstone weapons).
- **Mercer:** Highest-level damage spells. The suppression field removes the Warden's psychic immunity — Mercer's spells now deal full damage. Concentrate fire on the crown.
- **Cora:** Crossbow bolts and alchemist's fire on the crown. Her Dreamstone-forged bolts (if crafted) deal bonus psychic damage to the crown's crystalline structure.

**Crown Destruction:** When the corrupted Dreamstone crown reaches 0 HP, it shatters in a burst of iridescent light. The Warden loses coherence — its void-energy tendons unravel, its bones disconnect, and the forty-foot skeleton collapses in a cascade of ancient bone that shakes the entire chamber.

**If the Resonator fails or the party cannot deploy it:** The fight becomes a war of attrition. The party must reactivate all four rune arrays (reducing regeneration to 0) and then batter the Warden down through sustained damage. This is possible but extremely costly — the Warden's Broadcast Scream becomes more frequent under sustained assault (recharge becomes 4-6, then 3-6), and the void energy from the open breach creates environmental hazards (DC 15 Con save each round or 2d6 necrotic from ambient exposure).

#### Sealing the Breach
With the Warden destroyed, the breach must be sealed permanently. The Resonator's temporary seal buys time, but a permanent solution requires:

1. **Mercer activates all four containment rune arrays** at full power (DC 14 Arcana per array, already partially done during the fight)
2. **Cora uses the scaling runes to inscribe new containment runes** around the breach's edges (DC 15 Intelligence with Korathan's Chisel, 2 hours of work)
3. **The Dreamstone Power Cell's remaining energy** is channeled into the new runes, providing the power source for the permanent seal

The seal closes. The void energy recedes. The breach is covered by a network of new runes that glow with the same steady light as the Giant originals — smaller, human-scale, but functional. Korathan's technology transfer, put to its intended use.

#### The Closing Beat
The party stands in the Warden's Post, surrounded by the bones of a Giant and the glow of new and ancient runes. The chamber is quiet. The containment field readings on the Monitoring Crystal are climbing — the breach seal is holding, and the reactivated rune arrays are strengthening the surrounding network.

From the void below, through the newly sealed breach, the broadcast is muted. The Sleeper's note — the sustained tone from Adventure 15 — is softer now. Not gone. Never gone. But contained, for the moment, by the work of small hands using tools a Giant left for them.

Garrick picks up a fragment of the Warden's skull — a piece of Giant bone the size of his forearm — and turns it in his hands. "It was a guardian. Like the one in the tomb. Just... broken."

Mercer nods. "It was doing what it was built to do. The corruption just pointed it in the wrong direction."

Cora looks at the new runes she inscribed, comparing them to the ancient Giant originals beside them. "Ours are smaller. But they hold."

## Intermission III: The Dreamstone Citadel
With Act III complete, the party enters a three-month downtime period:
- **Transcontinental Experts (Tier 3 Workforce):** Reyna's contacts bring in specialists from across Laternia — scholars from Battlecrypt, engineers from Forgehold, and a small contingent of Wardstone Wardens who establish a permanent security presence. The fortress is now a significant installation, not a frontier outpost.
- **Arcane Defenses:** Mercer and the Battlecrypt scholars install a containment monitoring network throughout the mountain, using the scaling runes and Dreamstone components. The fortress now has an early warning system for any future containment failures.
- **Teleportation Circle:** The scholars establish a teleportation circle connecting the fortress to Battlecrypt. Supply lines are no longer dependent on overland travel. The fortress can receive reinforcements within minutes.
- **Lead-Lined Forge:** Cora upgrades the Cold Forge with lead-lined containment chambers for working with Dreamstone. The forge can now safely process raw Dreamstone into finished components without radiation exposure.
- **Capstone Restoration:** The Grimhold engineers and Battlecrypt scholars perform a comprehensive restoration of the Giant Capstone, using the scaling runes to fill every crack, seal every bore hole, and reinforce every weak point. The capstone is stronger now than it has been in centuries.

## Key Items
- **+3 Runic Maul** — forged from the Warden's arm bones and inscribed with Giant containment runes using the scaling techniques. The weapon hums with residual void energy, channeled and purified through the rune work. Deals an additional 2d6 radiant damage to void-corrupted creatures. Garrick's signature weapon for Act IV.
- **Architect's Codex (complete)** — with the data from the Warden's Post, the bore hole survey, and the breach seal, Mercer's Codex is now comprehensive. It contains: the full containment architecture map, all known weak points, the scaling runes, the resonance frequencies, and the theoretical framework for building the Dreamstone Seal. This is the campaign's most important document.
- **Capstone Resonator (depleted)** — the Resonator's charge is spent, but the device itself is intact. During Intermission III, Cora and Mercer can recharge it using Dreamstone and the lead-lined forge. It becomes a reusable tool for Act IV (1 charge per long rest, effects scaled down to 4d8 radiant and 15-foot radius for repeated use).
- **Warden's Bones** — Giant bone infused with void energy, then purified by the containment field. Material for 2 magic items of the party's choice, crafted at the lead-lined forge during Intermission III. Suggestions: a shield of void resistance, a helm of broadcast immunity, or a set of containment anchors for the Act IV descent.
- **10,000 gp** equivalent in Giant-era materials, purified void crystals, and the Warden's sarcophagus components.

## Key NPCs
- **Reyna Steelforge** — the Act III finale cements Reyna as the party's most important ally. Her resource mobilization during Intermission III transforms the fortress into a genuine stronghold. She tells the party plainly: "What you have done in this mountain will be remembered for as long as people remember anything. But the work is not done. It is time to go below."
- **The Wardstone Wardens** — a squad of six Wardens arrives during Intermission III, led by a senior officer who assesses the mountain's situation with professional gravity. The Wardens' presence means the fortress has official recognition from the region's military authority. They will hold the upper levels while the party descends into the void.
- **Ignathar (Fire Elemental)** — if the party bargained with Ignathar, the Fire Gate promise becomes relevant. The Giant level has been partially explored, and the Fire Gate's approximate location is known (from the blueprint tablet). Finding and opening it becomes a priority for Act IV — and fulfilling the bargain may provide a powerful ally for the final confrontation.

## DM Notes
- The Warden fight should feel like a culmination. Every tool, technique, and piece of knowledge the party has gathered across Act III is relevant. The scaling runes, the Dreamstone equipment, the containment field manipulation, the Resonator — all of it was building toward this moment. If the party uses their full toolkit, the fight is challenging but winnable. If they try to brute-force it, it is nearly impossible.
- The Warden's voice speaking the broadcast frequency is the first time the Sleeper's influence has produced coherent language directed at the party. "The seal is broken. The watcher wakes. The deep things rise." This is not the Sleeper speaking to the party — it is the Sleeper's dreaming state expressing itself through the Warden's dead nervous system — like a bell still ringing after the clapper has stopped. The Sleeper does not know the party exists as individuals. It knows something is happening in its cage.
- Intermission III is the biggest power spike in the campaign. The party goes from "three adventurers and some miners" to "the command team of a military-academic fortress with teleportation access and a forge capable of working Dreamstone." Their resources are now institutional. This matches the scale of what they face in Act IV.
- The Wardstone Wardens' arrival should feel earned. The party has been operating alone for fifteen adventures. Outside help arriving now is not cavalry — it is recognition that what the party has uncovered is too important for three people to handle alone.
- Act IV begins below the capstone, in the void itself. The party has secured everything above. Now they enter the Sleeper's domain — where reality is negotiable, physics are suggestions, and the deepest, oldest thing in the mountain is very slightly awake.
