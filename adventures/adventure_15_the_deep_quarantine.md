# Adventure 15: The Deep Quarantine (Level 15)

## PACING RULES (CRITICAL — DM MUST FOLLOW)
- This is a SINGLE SESSION adventure. The DM should pace the full adventure within ~20 exchanges.
- The adventure has three acts. Cover all three in this session — do not compress or skip content, but do not pad unnecessarily either.
- The early portion should focus on the intelligence-gathering phase — tapping the Twisted communication network, locating the staging ground, and planning the assault. The party should not attack during this act.
- The middle portion should cover the approach through heavily irradiated territory, the first combat encounters with elite Twisted defenders, and the discovery of the drill.
- The late portion should be the assault on the staging ground — fighting through the Twisted command structure, destroying their infrastructure, and the revelation that the drill is bigger than expected.
- If the players try to rush the staging ground, the Twisted have had time to prepare defenses since Adventure 13. They are ready.
- NEVER let the party access the area below the Giant Capstone through the staging ground this adventure. The drill has not yet breached the capstone from this angle.
- Only use events, NPCs, and locations described in this adventure file. Do not invent major plot elements not in the source material.

---

## Adventure Overview
The amplifiers are down. The broadcast is weakened. But the Twisted are not defeated — they have retreated deeper, regrouped, and intensified their primary objective: breaching the Giant Capstone.

The Twisted Communication Node from Adventure 13 has given Cora the ability to monitor the organic signal network. She has spent weeks studying the pulse patterns, mapping communication routes, and decoding basic signal structures. The intelligence is alarming: the Twisted have a staging ground in the deep outer ring, below the party's forward camp, where they have concentrated their remaining forces and a second drilling operation — larger and more advanced than the Juggernaut from Adventure 10.

This is not an exploration mission. This is a preemptive strike.

**Adventure Goal (to be achieved in this session):** Locate the Twisted staging ground. Destroy their infrastructure. Discover the extent of the drilling operation.

**IMPORTANT FOR DM:** This is a military operation. The party is attacking a fortified position held by an enemy that has been preparing for them. The Overseer from Adventures 9 and 13 has studied the party's tactics and planned countermeasures. The fights should be harder, the Twisted should be smarter, and the party should feel that their previous encounters have taught both sides.

**Milestone:** Completing this adventure advances the party to Level 16.

### Act 1: Listening to the Dark

#### Tapping the Network
Cora has modified the Twisted Communication Node into a monitoring station. Installed at the forward camp and connected to the resin conduits in the walls (which still carry faint residual signals despite the amplifiers' destruction), it provides a window into the Twisted's coordination.

**Key intelligence gathered over two weeks of monitoring:**

- **The staging ground location:** Deep outer ring, roughly 800 feet below the forward camp, in a section of the Giant foundation where the containment wall meets the structural bedrock. The Twisted have established a permanent installation here.
- **Force composition:** Approximately 30-40 Twisted, including a command structure. Multiple Overseers. Specialized workers operating heavy equipment. This is not a patrol force — it is a garrison.
- **The drill:** Signal traffic refers to it repeatedly — "the bore," "the deep work," "the machine." It is large enough to require its own communication node. Operational status: active and accelerating.
- **Defenses:** The staging ground's approaches are trapped and patrolled. The Twisted have established perimeter alarms using their organic signal network — any breach of the perimeter triggers an immediate alert.

**DC 16 Intelligence (Cora):** She identifies a blind spot in the communication network — a section of the approach route where a damaged resin conduit creates a gap in the signal coverage. The party can approach through this gap without triggering the perimeter alarm. The gap is narrow (a thirty-foot section of corridor) and time-limited (the Twisted repair their conduits on a regular cycle — the gap will close within 24 hours of the party's observation).

#### Planning the Strike
The party plans with the resources they have accumulated across Act III:

- **Dreamstone Circlets** for psychic shielding
- **Monitoring Crystal** for radiation tracking
- **Korathan's scaling runes** for containment field manipulation
- **Broadcast Frequency Map** for predicting psychic hazards
- **Giant-scale supplies** for tactical equipment

Mercer proposes a three-phase operation:
1. **Infiltrate** through the communication gap
2. **Sabotage** the staging ground's power supply (the organic network feeding the drill)
3. **Strike** the command structure while the defenses are disrupted

Garrick's assessment is blunter: "We go in fast, break what we can, and get out before they can coordinate a response."

Cora's compromise: "Both. We sabotage first, then strike while they are confused. The communication gap gives us one shot at surprise. After that, it is a fight."

### Act 2: The Irradiated Zone

#### The Approach
The route to the staging ground descends through sections of the Giant foundation the party has not previously explored. The containment runes here are sparser — the outer edge of the network, where the field is weakest. The Dreamstone radiation is stronger than anything the party has encountered outside the Dreamstone Vein.

**Environmental hazard — Radiation Zone:**
- **Light exposure (first 200 feet):** DC 13 Constitution save per hour. Failure: 1d6 psychic damage, brief disorientation. Dreamstone Circlets negate.
- **Moderate exposure (next 200 feet):** DC 15 Constitution save per 30 minutes. Failure: 2d6 psychic damage, hallucination (1 round). Circlets provide advantage.
- **Heavy exposure (staging ground perimeter):** DC 16 Constitution save per 10 minutes. Failure: 3d6 psychic damage, hallucination (1d4 rounds), one level of exhaustion. Circlets provide advantage and resistance.

**Cora's countermeasure:** The Reality Anchor (if crafted in Adventure 12) creates a mobile zone of stability. The party can travel in the Anchor's radius with significantly reduced exposure (treat heavy as moderate, moderate as light).

#### The Communication Gap
The party reaches the blind spot — a thirty-foot section of corridor where the resin conduit is cracked and dark. Beyond it, the Twisted perimeter begins. The timing is tight: they must pass through, navigate two hundred feet of patrolled corridor, and reach the staging ground before the gap is repaired.

**DC 14 group Stealth check** to pass through without alerting the patrols on either side of the gap. On failure: a Twisted patrol of 4 Laborers and 1 Sentry investigates. Combat must be resolved in 3 rounds to prevent an alarm.

#### The Outer Staging Ground
Past the perimeter, the Giant corridor opens into a complex of modified chambers that the Twisted have converted into a military installation:

- **The Barracks:** Organic alcoves grown into the walls, each containing a resting Twisted. Approximately 15 are present — the others are on patrol or at the drill site. The sleeping Twisted can be bypassed if the party is quiet (DC 15 group Stealth) or neutralized systematically (risky — each combat risks alerting others).

- **The Armory:** A chamber filled with Twisted-modified weapons and equipment. Warhammers with organic grips, chain armor with resin reinforcement, and tools designed for the drill operation. One item stands out: a set of lead-lined containers holding Dreamstone fragments, carefully sorted by size and purity. The Twisted are collecting Dreamstone for their own purposes.

- **The Organic Power Hub:** A nexus of resin conduits where the communication and power networks converge. This is the staging ground's nervous system. Destroying it would blind and disorient the Twisted for critical minutes.

#### First Combat
The party encounters an elite Twisted patrol: 2 Twisted Sentries and 1 Twisted Engineer — a new variant.

**Twisted Engineer:**
- AC 15 (light corrupted armor), HP 65, Speed 25 ft
- Wrench-pick: +7 to hit, 1d8+4 bludgeoning
- **Repair:** As an action, restores 15 HP to a Twisted ally or repairs a damaged mechanism within 5 ft
- **Sabotage Device (1/day):** Places a timed explosive resin charge. Detonates after 3 rounds: 15 ft radius, DC 15 Dexterity save, 4d8 fire + necrotic damage
- **Corruption Aura:** DC 14 Con, 1d6 psychic within 5 ft
- **Resistant:** Poison, necrotic. Vulnerable: radiant.

The Engineer is a priority target — its Repair ability extends Twisted combatants significantly, and its Sabotage Device can create dangerous area denial.

#### The Drill Discovery
Past the outer installations, the corridor opens into a massive chamber — a natural cavern that the Twisted have expanded into a construction bay. The drill is here.

It is not a machine like the Juggernaut. It is grown. A biological construct fifty feet long, shaped like an enormous boring worm, with a rotating head of crystallized Dreamstone teeth and a body of layered organic resin reinforced with salvaged dwarven iron. It sits in a cradle of organic infrastructure, connected to the power hub by dozens of resin conduits. It pulses with the Sleeper's rhythm.

The drill is aimed at the floor — at a section of the Giant Capstone that Mercer's blueprint identifies as a structural weak point. The Twisted have been boring into the capstone from below the forward camp's position. They are thirty feet into the capstone's fifty-foot thickness.

**Mercer's horror:** "They are not just drilling. They are growing. The drill is alive — it adds mass from the rock it consumes. Every foot it bores, it gets bigger. In another month, it will be through."

### Act 3: The Strike

#### Destroying the Power Hub
The party's first objective is the Organic Power Hub. Without it, the drill loses power, the communication network goes dark, and the Twisted defenders lose coordination.

**The hub is guarded by:** 2 Twisted Sentries, 2 Twisted Engineers, and the Twisted Overseer.

**Twisted Overseer (final form):**
- AC 19 (master-crafted corrupted plate), HP 120, Speed 30 ft
- Warhammer: +10 to hit, 2d8+6 bludgeoning + 1d6 necrotic
- **Command Corruption:** Bonus action, all Twisted within 30 ft gain +2 to hit and +1d6 necrotic damage
- **Corruption Pulse (Recharge 4-6):** 20 ft radius, DC 17 Con, 4d8 necrotic, stunned 1 round on fail
- **Tactical Adaptation:** The Overseer has studied the party. It knows Cora is the priority saboteur and will direct attacks at her. It knows Garrick is the primary combatant and will attempt to flank rather than engage head-on. It knows Mercer is the spellcaster and will use Corruption Pulse to break his concentration.
- **Desperate Rally (1/day, below half HP):** All Twisted within 60 ft gain temporary HP equal to the Overseer's remaining HP and advantage on attacks for 2 rounds.

**The hub destruction:** While the party fights the Overseer and its guards, Cora must reach the hub's central node — the nexus where all conduits meet — and destroy it. She can use:
- Alchemist's fire (3 rounds of sustained application, DC 14 Dexterity each round)
- Physical destruction (the node is AC 12, HP 60, but each round it is attacked it releases a Corruption Pulse — DC 15 Con, 2d8 necrotic — as a defensive response)
- Cora's amplifier dissolution technique from Adventure 13 (DC 15 Intelligence with alchemist's supplies, 4 rounds but clean and safe)

When the hub is destroyed, the effect cascades: resin conduits go dark throughout the staging ground. The communication network dies. The drill shudders and stops. Every Twisted in the installation staggers as their connection to the organic infrastructure is severed.

#### The Drill
With the power hub down, the drill is vulnerable but not destroyed. It is a living construct — even without external power, it has residual energy. It pulses weakly, its Dreamstone teeth still rotating at a fraction of their operational speed.

**Destroying the drill:**
- The Dreamstone teeth can be shattered with sustained radiant or thunder damage (AC 15, HP 80 for the drill head)
- The organic body can be burned (fire vulnerability — takes double damage from fire, but the body is large enough that localized fire takes 5 rounds to spread)
- Cora can reverse the drill's own power against it — if she accesses the control mechanisms and overloads them (DC 16 Intelligence, 2 rounds), the drill tears itself apart from within

**While the party destroys the drill,** surviving Twisted attempt a counterattack — 8-10 disoriented but still dangerous Laborers and Sentries converging on the construction bay. Without the Overseer's coordination (if defeated) or the communication network, they fight individually rather than tactically. This makes them less effective but unpredictable.

#### The Revelation
As the drill is destroyed and the party examines the bore hole it created, Mercer makes a discovery that changes the campaign's calculus:

The bore hole extends thirty feet into the capstone. At the bottom, visible by magical light, the stone is different — translucent, warm, pulsing with inner light. This is not the capstone itself. It is what lies immediately beneath it: the upper surface of the Anomaly's domain. The barrier between the containment and the void is twenty feet thick at this point, not fifty as previously believed.

And in the translucent stone, shapes move. Not Twisted. Something else. Something larger, slower, and infinitely more patient. The Sleeper's domain is not empty. It is inhabited.

**Mercer's conclusion:** The drill was closer than anyone realized. Twenty more feet — a week of boring — and the capstone would have been breached from a direction the containment network was not designed to defend. The Juggernaut was the frontal assault. The drill was the flanking maneuver. The Twisted have been attacking the containment from multiple angles simultaneously.

#### The Withdrawal
The staging ground is destroyed. The drill is rubble. The power hub is dark. But the party is deep in enemy territory, irradiated, exhausted, and now aware that the threat is larger than they knew.

**The closing beat:** On the ascent back to the forward camp, the party passes through the communication gap — still open, the repair cycle disrupted by the hub's destruction. The corridors are dark and silent. The organic infrastructure is dead. For the first time on the Giant level, the walls are just walls.

But from below — from the direction of the bore hole, from the translucent stone at the bottom of the shaft — a sound follows them up. Not the Twisted's industrial rhythm. Not the Sleeper's ambient broadcast. A new sound: a single, sustained note, deep and resonant, that vibrates the containment runes as it passes through the stone. The Sleeper has felt the drill's destruction. It is not grateful. It is not angry. It is awake. Slightly more awake than it was yesterday.

Mercer writes in his journal with a hand that does not shake, because he will not let it: "We have won a battle. The war is below us, and it has been going on for fifty thousand years. We are the newest soldiers. We will not be the last."

## Key Items
- **Twisted Command Signal Decoder** — recovered from the Overseer's equipment. Allows Cora to decode the Twisted's encrypted communications, providing detailed intelligence on any remaining Twisted operations in the mountain. Advantage on all checks to anticipate or counter Twisted activity for the rest of the campaign.
- **Dreamstone Drill Fragments** — crystallized Dreamstone from the drill's teeth, pre-shaped by the boring process into regular geometric forms. Cora can use these as pre-fabricated components for the Dreamstone Seal (reduces the crafting DC by 2 in Adventure 19).
- **Bore Hole Survey** — Mercer's measurements and observations of the bore hole and the translucent stone at its base. Provides critical data for Adventure 16: the capstone's true thickness profile, weak points, and the nature of the barrier between the containment and the void.
- **Twisted Bioengineering Catalogue** — comprehensive documentation of the staging ground's organic technology: resin composition, growth patterns, power distribution. Cora's understanding of Twisted bioengineering is now expert-level. She can improvise countermeasures to any Twisted technology she encounters.
- **3,500 gp** equivalent in salvaged Twisted equipment, Dreamstone fragments, and dwarven components.

## Key NPCs
- **The Twisted Overseer (final encounter)** — whether killed or driven off, this is the Overseer's last appearance as a recurring antagonist. Its arc across Adventures 9, 13, and 15 represents the Twisted's transition from hidden saboteurs to open combatants to defeated remnants. If killed, its death should feel significant — not triumphant but solemn. It served the Sleeper for millennia. It lost.
- **Reyna Steelforge** — the revelation about the capstone's true thickness and the shapes in the translucent stone shakes her deeply. She begins mobilizing resources beyond Grimhold: "We need people who understand what we are dealing with. Not miners. Not historians. We need the Wardens. We need the Gatekeepers." This seeds the Tier 3 workforce from Intermission III.

## DM Notes
- This adventure should feel like a turning point. The party has gone from explorers to defenders to attackers. They are taking the fight to the Twisted rather than reacting. This shift in agency should be empowering — and sobering.
- The Overseer's Tactical Adaptation is the most important combat mechanic. It means the Twisted are learning from previous encounters. The party's standard tactics should be countered — forcing them to innovate. This rewards creative play and punishes routine.
- The translucent stone and the shapes within it are the first direct evidence of what lives in the Sleeper's domain. Do not describe the shapes clearly. They are "large," "slow," and "patient." Let the party's imagination fill in the horror.
- The single sustained note at the end is the Sleeper becoming slightly more aware. Not fully awake — the containment still holds — but the drilling and its destruction have registered. This increased awareness will intensify the broadcast in Adventures 17-18.
- The bore hole should be sealed before Adventure 16. Cora and the Grimhold engineers can seal it using the scaling runes and containment techniques from Korathan's tomb. This is a downtime task, not an adventure.
