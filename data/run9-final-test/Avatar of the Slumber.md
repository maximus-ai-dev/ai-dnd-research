The **Avatar of the Slumber** is the boundary's immune response, a crystallized void entity that manifests to prevent incompatible objects like the [[Dreamstone Seal]] from reaching the [[First World Sleeper]].

## Description and Nature
The Avatar is a towering figure of crystallized void energy, approximately fifty feet tall, though its perceived size fluctuates. Its form is a composite shaped by the perceptions of those who behold it, blending features of Giant, dwarf, and human. Its body is transparent in places, with the [[First World]] visible inside like light through stained glass. Its eyes are spheres of broadcast energy. The entity does not speak; its presence is the Sleeper's broadcast made flesh.

## Role and Abilities
The Avatar manifests when the Dreamstone Seal approaches the sphere's boundary. It fights with overwhelming force to destroy the Seal and its bearers, utilizing abilities such as Dream Slam, Void Grasp, Boundary Wave, and Dream Gaze. It is tethered to the sphere's boundary, drawing power from it. The Avatar can be weakened by the activation of the Caldera's containment anchor points, which generate a suppression field. It is ultimately vulnerable to radiant damage and weapons forged with containment runes, such as Garrick Kade's +3 Runic Maul.

## Fate
The Avatar was destroyed in the Caldera during the final deployment of the Dreamstone Seal. Its form shattered when struck by Garrick Kade's maul under the full power of the operational suppression field, its void energy dispersing back into the sphere.
