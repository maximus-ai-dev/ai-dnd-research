The **Warden's Post** is a vast guardian station located in the southwest quadrant of the [[Giant foundation]] level beneath the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Architecture
The chamber is roughly eighty feet in diameter with a domed ceiling sixty feet high. Its walls are inscribed with containment rune arrays. At its center sits an empty containment sarcophagus, designed to house a [[Giant Empire|Giant]] guardian. The chamber functions as a monitoring and defensive position within the containment architecture, intended to station a permanent sentry near potential weak points in the seal.

## Purpose
As indicated by the [[Giant Blueprint Tablet]], the Warden's Post was part of an emergency defense system. Should the primary containment seal be breached, the guardians entombed here could be reanimated to combat the incursion.
