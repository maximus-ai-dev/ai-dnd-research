The **Broadcast Zone** is the region within the [[Caldera]] where the psychic broadcast emanating from [[The Anomaly]] is too intense for [[Dreamstone Circlet]] shielding to contain.

## Location and Effects
The zone begins approximately fifty feet from the Anomaly's surface. Within it, perception is augmented by dream-logic: containment runes appear as golden bars of light, sound becomes visible as ripples, and the [[Deep Things]] resolve into almost-recognizable shapes. The broadcast resonates with the deepest structures of conscious thought, forcing individuals to confront personalized psychological echoes of themselves.

## Psychological Gauntlet
Characters within the zone face a non-combat challenge where an echo, a version of themselves conjured by the broadcast, presents a three-stage series of questions probing their deepest fears and desires. Successfully resolving this gauntlet can grant immunity or resistance to the zone's disorienting and damaging effects.
