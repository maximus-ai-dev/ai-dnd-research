The **Threshold** is the initial transition zone leading from the [[Giant Capstone]] down into the [[Caldera]].

## Description
The Threshold is a fifty-foot descent where the cylindrical shape of the Caldera becomes apparent. The walls curve gently and are densely packed with overlapping [[Giant Empire|Giant]] containment runes that burn with bright golden light—the strongest point of the containment barrier.

## Environmental Effects
The primary characteristic of the Threshold is progressively weakening gravity. At the top, gravity is normal, but with each step downward, weight diminishes. By the bottom of the Threshold, objects and beings weigh roughly half their normal weight.

## Features
The walls are carved with Giant-sized handholds and grip points, which at human scale serve as ledges and platforms for resting and anchoring during the descent.
