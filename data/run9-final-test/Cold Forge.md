The **Cold Forge** is a major dwarven industrial complex and ancient workshop located within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
Constructed as the primary smelting and metalworking facility for the ancient dwarven hold, the forge chamber is centered on a great stone hearth, surrounded by an advanced network of cooling channels that regulate its extreme temperatures. It serves as a secure base of operations and crafting station.

## Power Source and History
The forge was historically powered by the ancient fire elemental [[Ignathar]], who was bound to its hearth. After the party reactivated the forge, Ignathar maintained a voluntary partnership, providing the immense and controlled heat necessary for advanced metallurgy. The forge's unique thermal regulation allows it to reach the extreme temperatures required to work specialized materials such as [[Dreamstone]] and adamantine.

## Role in the Containment Effort
The Cold Forge was the preparation site for the construction of the [[Dreamstone Seal]]. Components were assembled here, and the party's bargain with Ignathar was finalized at this location. The forge's restored elemental fire was later channeled through the opened [[Fire Gate]] to provide the precise heat needed for the Seal's forging within the [[Caldera]].
