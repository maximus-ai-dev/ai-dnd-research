The **First World** is the original, living reality from which [[The Anomaly]], also known as the [[First World Sleeper]], originates.

## Nature of Reality
It is a reality governed by different fundamental laws of physics than the known world. Within the First World, consciousness is a fundamental force, stone can sing, and light has tangible weight. It is a realm of impossible beauty and alien logic.

## Connection to the Caldera
The First World exists on the other side of the boundary represented by the Anomaly's spherical form in the [[Caldera]]. The Giant containment did not capture the Sleeper but built a wall between its native reality and the physical universe. Glimpses through the sphere's surface reveal the living First World, not a dead remnant, with the Sleeper simply present within it.
