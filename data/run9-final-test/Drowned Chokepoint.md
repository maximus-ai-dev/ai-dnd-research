The **Drowned Chokepoint** is a critical infrastructure junction located at the deepest navigable level of the [[Dwarven Layer]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
It is a vast, domed cavern where all primary water management systems—drainage pipes, pump circuits, and overflow channels—converge. The floor is a network of drainage channels radiating from a central elevated platform. The chokepoint sits directly above the [[Giant Capstone]], the seal separating dwarven architecture from the Giant foundation below.

## Significance
For centuries, the intersection was completely submerged. Its recent drainage, facilitated by the reactivated pump stations, exposed it as a major staging area and workshop for the [[Twisted]]. The location is strategically vital, as breaching the capstone from this point would allow corruption to flood upward through the entire mountain. It was here that the [[Drowned Juggernaut]] was constructed and deployed.
