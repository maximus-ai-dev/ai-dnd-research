# Session 13: Surgical Strike

*The following account was compiled from field notes, arcane recordings, and firsthand observation by the chronicler attached to the expedition. It documents the critical sabotage operation against the Twisted amplification network in the Giant foundation layer.*

---

The air in the [[Cold Forge]] chamber carried the scent of cooling metal and ozone—a familiar, comforting smell after weeks of work in the [[Dwarven Layer]]. [[Ignathar]]'s fire burned steadily in the central forge pit, casting dancing orange light across the massive stone anvils where the party had spent days preparing. Before them lay the fruits of their research: the [[Dreamstone]] fragments, the Twisted schematics, and [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]]'s analysis spread across a worktable.

[[Cora Flint]] had been cross-referencing everything. The organic broadcast towers—"fleshy amplifiers"—were not just passive structures. They were active weapons, parasitizing the [[Giant Empire]]'s containment runes to push the [[Sleeper]]'s psychic emanation upward through the mountain. [[Mercer]] spread his notes, his voice echoing in the vast chamber.

"Three amplifiers," he said, tapping the [[Giant Blueprint Tablet]] where three pulsing red markers glowed. "Alpha in the northwest quadrant—a prototype. Beta in the southeast—more sophisticated. Gamma due south, near the threshold of the inner void—the most powerful." He looked up, his expression grim. "They're tapping into the containment runes. Destroy them, and we cut the Twisted's ability to project the Sleeper's influence upward."

[[Garrick Kade]] hefted his maul, the runes along its shaft glowing faintly. "Guarded?"

"Twenty to twenty-five individuals across the three sites," [[Mercer]] continued. "This isn't exploration. This is a sabotage mission behind enemy lines."

[[Cora]] examined the schematics showing the amplifiers' root systems. "The vulnerability is here. Sever the root connections, and the amplifier collapses. But it has to be precise—a crude attack triggers a broadcast burst that alerts every [[Twisted]] in the area."

---

## The Alpha Gambit

They decided to begin with [[Amplifier Alpha]]—the prototype, smaller and presumably less guarded. [[Mercer]]'s analysis revealed a critical insight: the amplifiers weren't just drawing power from the containment runes—they were using them as a resonant medium. A feedback loop that, if severed improperly, could cascade through the entire containment system.

"Three simultaneous disruptions," [[Mercer]] explained, conjuring his spectral mind—the glowing manifestation of his spellbook. "We isolate the amplifier from the runic network first, then sever the physical roots."

[[Garrick]] nodded, his grip tightening on his maul. "You handle the magic. I'll handle anything that tries to stop you."

Their approach through the maintenance corridor was anything but stealthy. [[Cora]]'s boot scraped loudly against loose stone, [[Garrick]]'s chain mail clanked with every step, and even [[Mercer]]'s attempt at quiet movement proved insufficient. The two [[Twisted Sentries]] at Alpha's entrance snapped to attention, moving with silent, coordinated efficiency to flank their position.

"Quiet's over," [[Garrick]] growled, hefting his maul as he activated [[Giant's Might]], his frame expanding as magic took hold.

[[Cora]] cast [[Stinking Cloud]], filling the corridor with nauseating vapors that incapacitated one Sentry. [[Mercer]] paralyzed the other with [[Hold Person]], and [[Garrick]] made quick work of both with brutal efficiency. The fight was swift, violent, and loud—but successful.

With the guards eliminated, they moved to the amplifier itself: a fifteen-foot-diameter column of organic material stretching from floor to ceiling, its bioluminescent veins pulsing with rhythmic light. [[Mercer]] placed a timed scroll of [[Dispel Magic]] at one interface point while positioning himself at another, his spectral mind covering the third.

"Positions," [[Mercer]] said, his hands tracing precise patterns. "The energy trough occurs in forty-three seconds. We disrupt on my mark."

The countdown felt eternal. [[Garrick]] stood guard at the chamber entrance, his maul resting on his shoulder, eyes scanning the corridor. [[Cora]] had her alchemical compounds ready—specific acids and solvents calibrated for organic dissolution.

"Five... four... three... two... one..."

Three waves of anti-magic energy hit simultaneously. The amplifier shuddered, its glow flickering erratically as the parasitic connections severed cleanly.

"Now, [[Cora]]!" [[Mercer]] said, maintaining his concentration.

[[Cora]] worked with surgical precision. First the outer resin layer dissolved under her acidic solvent, then the secondary connective tissue yielded to an alkaline mixture, and finally the core vascular bundle collapsed under a destabilization compound. The amplifier crumbled inward, becoming inert biomass without so much as a whisper of broadcast energy.

"Alpha neutralized," [[Cora]] reported, already gathering her supplies.

But through the stone, they felt a vibration—a pulse traveling through the organic infrastructure. The resin conduits in the walls dimmed slightly, then pulsed with alarm signals. The Twisted communication network had registered the loss.

---

## Beta's Complexity

They moved quickly to [[Amplifier Beta]] in the southeast quadrant, healing [[Garrick]]'s wounds on the move. [[Mercer]]'s spectral mind scouted ahead, reporting changed defenses: two [[Twisted Sentries]] now reinforced by four [[Twisted Laborers]], with patrol patterns tightened to five-minute intervals.

The approach was exposed—forty feet of open corridor with no cover. [[Mercer]] attempted misdirection, manipulating the Giant monitoring instruments in the alcove to create a false energy spike reading. It worked partially, drawing four Laborers inside to investigate, but two remained at the entrance, now suspicious and alert.

"Eliminate them quickly," [[Cora]] decided.

[[Garrick]] charged across the open space, his maul connecting with a critical hit that instantly killed the first Laborer. [[Mercer]] paralyzed the second with [[Hold Person]], and [[Garrick]] finished it with another brutal swing.

But Beta was more complex than Alpha. [[Mercer]]' analysis revealed nine connection nodes—three primary, three secondary, three tertiary—with the tertiary nodes serving as emergency broadcast channels that required perfectly timed simultaneous disruption.

They were still identifying the nodes when the four Laborers emerged from investigating the false alarm. [[Cora]] cast [[Web]], trapping three immediately. [[Garrick]] engaged the fourth, but the sounds of combat drew attention.

"Patrol incoming!" [[Mercer]] warned. "Sixty seconds out!"

[[Cora]] summoned a [[Flaming Sphere]] that ignited the webbing, creating a wall of fire. [[Mercer]] made a desperate decision—instead of attacking the Twisted, he cast [[Thunderwave]] at the stone archway of the alcove entrance, targeting a structural weak point. The entrance collapsed in a shower of rubble, buying them precious minutes.

Working frantically, they implemented a phased approach: primary nodes first, then secondary, then the critical tertiary emergency channels. [[Garrick]] held the cracking rubble barrier as digging sounds grew louder from the other side.

"Thirty seconds is all we need," [[Garrick]] growled, planting himself before the weakening stone.

[[Mercer]] coordinated the final phase with academic precision. "Three... two... one... now!" Three disruptions hit simultaneously. The amplifier's violent shuddering ceased abruptly, replaced by a deep groaning collapse as the entire structure lost integrity.

"Beta neutralized," [[Mercer]] reported. "But we have fifteen seconds before that rubble fails."

They retreated through an emergency maintenance tunnel, collapsing sections behind them as they went. The controlled barriers bought them distance and time, leading to a junction chamber with access to the primary ventilation network.

---

## The Approach to Gamma

The ventilation shaft offered a stealthy approach to [[Amplifier Gamma]]—the final and most powerful amplifier. [[Cora]] dissolved the rusted grate with alchemical compounds, and they entered single file, [[Mercer]] using [[Minor Illusion]] to mask their sounds.

After fifty feet of cautious movement, they reached a monitoring chamber directly above Gamma's position. Through the mesh floor, they looked down upon a sight that made even [[Garrick]]'s battle-hardened composure waver.

Amplifier Gamma was massive—a column twenty feet in diameter, stretching from floor to ceiling in a chamber that must be part of the inner void threshold. Bioluminescent veins pulsed with intense, rhythmic light, casting the entire space in an eerie glow. The air shimmered visibly with broadcast energy, and even through their [[Dreamstone Circlets]], they felt the psychic pressure like a physical weight.

Below, the Twisted had concentrated their remaining forces. The [[Twisted Overseer]] stood at Gamma's base—the same corrupted dwarf from their previous encounters, now clad in reinforced plate armor that glowed with the same bioluminescent patterns as the amplifier. Around it stood four [[Twisted Sentries]] and four [[Twisted Laborers]]—the full remaining garrison. They weren't patrolling or working. They were waiting. Alert. Ready.

The resin conduits in the walls pulsed with frantic light patterns—the communication network screaming alarms. The Twisted knew Alpha and Beta were gone. They knew the saboteurs were coming.

And they were prepared.

[[Mercer]] whispered, his voice barely audible over the amplifier's hum. "Gamma is the primary node. It's connected directly to the inner void—closest to the Sleeper itself."

[[Cora]] examined the amplifier through the mesh. "The root network is enormous. Multiple primary connections, secondary redundancies, tertiary emergency channels. This will require our full methodology, amplified."

[[Garrick]]'s grip tightened on his maul. "And they're waiting for us."

Below, the [[Twisted Overseer]] looked up—not at their hiding place, but as if sensing something. Its corrupted face showed no fear this time. No confusion. Only cold, focused comprehension.

It understood what they were doing.

It was ready for them.
