The **Reality Anchor** is a portable arcane device designed to stabilize local gravitational and spatial fields.

## Function
When activated, the Reality Anchor creates a spherical field with a radius of approximately thirty feet. Within this field, it enforces a consistent direction of "down" and normalizes physics, counteracting external gravitational distortions or null-gravity environments.

## Usage in the Campaign
The device was essential for navigating the disorienting [[Threshold]] zone and for operating within the zero-gravity environment of the [[Caldera]]. During the forging of the [[Dreamstone Seal]] at the [[Observation Platform]], the Reality Anchor was deployed to create a stable workspace. However, as the Caldera's anomalies escalated in response to the forging, the Anchor strained under the intense gravitational fluctuations and the surging power of the [[First World Sleeper]]'s broadcast, though it continued to provide critical stabilization.
