**Twisted Sentries** are a type of [[Twisted]] combatant specializing in guard duties.

## Description
Twisted Sentries are heavier and better armored than [[Twisted Laborers]]. They wear heavy chain armor and are armed with warhammers and shields. They possess enhanced alertness, making them difficult to surprise.

## Role
Sentries are deployed as permanent guards at critical [[Twisted]] installations, such as the [[Fleshy Amplifiers]]. They form the core of the Twisted's defensive garrisons.
