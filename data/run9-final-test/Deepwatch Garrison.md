The **Deepwatch Garrison** was a military unit of the dwarven hold [[Kazad-Khrom]], responsible for security and threat response within the deeper mining and industrial regions of the mountain.

## Known References
The garrison is mentioned in operational logs recovered from the [[Eastern Transit Hub]]. The final log entry, dated from the time of the hub's abandonment, records a request for hunter teams from the Deepwatch Garrison to deal with an "apex predator"—the behir [[Kellashen]]—that had occupied the area. The request appears to have gone unanswered, suggesting the garrison was already compromised or unable to respond by that time.

The garrison is also referenced in connection with other subterranean threats; logs note that the garrison reported "similar phenomena" of [[Thermal Anomalies]] in the lower levels, indicating it served as a frontline monitoring and defense force against dangers emerging from the mountain's depths.

## Status
The current status of the Deepwatch Garrison is unknown. Its absence during the crisis at the Eastern Transit Hub implies it was likely overwhelmed or disbanded during the final days of Kazad-Khrom's occupation.
