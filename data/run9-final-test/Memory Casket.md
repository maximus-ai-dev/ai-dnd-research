The **Memory Casket** is a sealed brass cylinder containing the operational records and key artifacts of the [[Secretive Brotherhood]].

## Contents
The casket holds the Brotherhood's operations log, several memory crystals, and three brass insignias identical to those carried by their wardens.

## Discovery and Authentication
The casket was discovered within the [[Salt-Vaults]] and its authenticity was verified by [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]]. The recordings within detail the critical events that led to the Brotherhood's decision to seal the vaults and enter stasis, serving as a primary historical source for their final actions.
