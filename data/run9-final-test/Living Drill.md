The **Living Drill** was a massive biological construct created by the [[Twisted]] within a staging ground deep in the [[Giant foundation]]. Shaped like an enormous boring worm approximately fifty feet long, it featured a rotating head of crystallized [[Dreamstone]] teeth and a body of layered organic resin reinforced with salvaged dwarven iron. The drill was a living entity that grew as it bored, connected to the Twisted's organic power network and aimed at a structural weak point in the [[Giant Capstone]]. Its purpose was to breach the capstone from below.
