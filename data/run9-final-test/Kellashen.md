**Kellashen** was a behir, a twelve-legged serpentine predator with the ability to generate and discharge lightning, that occupied the eastern transit hub of the dwarven minecart network for decades. Its name, derived from the dwarven term "Kellashen" meaning "lightning coil," was recorded in maintenance logs found at the hub.

## Description
Kellashen was approximately twenty-five feet long, with a wedge-shaped head lined with needle-sharp teeth and eyes that glowed with predatory intelligence. Its twelve powerful legs allowed it to climb walls and ceilings with ease, and blue-white lightning constantly crackled along its scaled hide. It possessed a lightning breath weapon capable of projecting a twenty-foot line of electrical energy.

## Territory and Behavior
The behir established its lair in the [[Eastern Transit Hub]], a natural cavern incorporated into the [[Minecart Network]] east of the [[Cold Forge]]. It was an apex territorial predator, clever enough to memorize the tunnel layout and utilize narrow side passages for ambush and retreat. It avoided sections of conductive iron rail when possible, understanding they could channel its own lightning breath. Kellashen's presence effectively sealed the eastern branch of the minecart network, as noted in a final dwarven log entry that requested a hunter team from the [[Deepwatch Garrison]].

## Demise
Kellashen was slain in a confrontation within the minecart network tunnels. Its death cleared the eastern transit hub, allowing for the potential reactivation of the minecart network's critical eastern branch.
