The **Dreamstone Seal** is a masterwork containment device forged to reinforce the failing Giant architecture that contains the [[First World Sleeper]] within the [[Caldera]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Composition
The Seal is a sphere roughly the size of a human skull, composed of a [[Dreamstone]] core housed within a structural casing of adamantine plates. Its surface is inscribed with a complete containment rune matrix—scaled-down versions of the original Giant patterns—using [[Korathan's Chisel]].

## Purpose and Function
The Seal is a containment upgrade, not a weapon. When deployed at the Sleeper's boundary, it integrates with the existing architecture to reinforce the barrier between realities, significantly extending the containment's effective lifespan. Its design is based on [[Korathan]]'s original work, scaled for human crafting via the scaling runes, and powered by the Dreamstone's natural resonance with the Sleeper.

## Creation
The Seal was forged in a single, intense session within the Caldera itself. The construction required the coordinated expertise of [[Cora Flint]] (forging and assembly), [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] (rune inscription), and [[Garrick Kade]] (structural support and defense), with critical assistance from the elemental heat of [[Ignathar]] via the opened [[Fire Gate]]. The forging process provoked escalating environmental anomalies from the Caldera, including tremors, gravity fluctuations, and a surge of the Sleeper's broadcast.
