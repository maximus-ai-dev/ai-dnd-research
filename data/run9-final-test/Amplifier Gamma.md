**Amplifier Gamma** is one of three [[Fleshy Amplifiers]] constructed by the [[Twisted]] in the [[Giant foundation]] level.

## Location
It is located due south in the Giant foundation's outer ring, approximately 500 feet from the forward camp established at the [[Bastion of the Asymmetrical]], near the threshold of the inner void.

## Description
Amplifier Gamma is the largest, most powerful, and most heavily guarded of the amplifiers. Its proximity to the inner void gives it more direct access to the [[First World Sleeper]]'s broadcast source. The air around it shimmers with visible broadcast energy, and standing within thirty feet causes a disruptive psychic hum.

## Status
As of the latest reconnaissance, Amplifier Gamma remains operational and is defended by the full remaining [[Twisted]] garrison, including the [[Twisted Overseer]].
