**Ignathar** is an ancient fire elemental bound to the hearth of the [[Cold Forge]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Nature
Ignathar manifests as an intelligent, articulate entity of heat and flame. Its form is that of a pillar of white flame, approximately twenty feet tall when fully manifested. Originally bound by dwarven runesmiths to power the forge, it was later released from compulsory service and maintains a voluntary partnership with those who have reactivated the forge.

## History and Role
After being freed by the party, Ignathar entered into a bargain: in exchange for assistance in reigniting the Cold Forge, the party would consider opening the sealed [[Fire Gate]]. This bargain was fulfilled during the forging of the [[Dreamstone Seal]], where Ignathar provided precise, supernatural control of elemental heat. Upon the Seal's completion, Ignathar departed through the open Fire Gate to the [[Elemental Plane of Fire]].

## Significance
Ignathar's controlled fire was essential for advanced metallurgy within the mountain, particularly for working exotic materials like [[Dreamstone]]. Its assistance was a critical factor in the successful creation of the Dreamstone Seal.
