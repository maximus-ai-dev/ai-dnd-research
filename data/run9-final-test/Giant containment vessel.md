The **Giant containment vessel** is a massive, ancient structure built by the [[Giant Empire]] to contain the [[First World Sleeper]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Design and Construction
Designed by Master Runecarver [[Korathan]] of the [[Architect's Council]], the vessel represents three centuries of construction effort—the longest single project in Giant history. It operates on principles of nested reality layers, dimensional folding, and harmonic resonance.

## Current Status and Purpose
The vessel was engineered to contain the Sleeper for one hundred thousand years. It is currently at the halfway point of its designed lifespan and is weakening on schedule. The vessel's integrity is critical to preventing the Sleeper's dream radiation from rendering the mountain and surrounding regions uninhabitable. Knowledge from Korathan's tomb, particularly the [[Korathan's scaling runes|Scaling Runes]], is essential for understanding and reinforcing the failing structure.
