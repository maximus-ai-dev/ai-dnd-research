**Commander Cassen** was a high-ranking member of the [[Secretive Brotherhood]]. His preserved body was discovered in the Central Hall of the [[Grand Archive]], wearing Commander-rank insignia and showing no visible wounds.

## Final Actions and Beliefs
The body was oriented toward the [[Restricted Vault]], suggesting he perished while attempting to reach countermeasures during a crisis. Records indicate he believed the [[deep resonance]] was "climbing," marking it as the most significant discovery of the age.
