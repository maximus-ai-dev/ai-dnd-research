The **Giant Blueprint Tablet** is a magical artifact containing the original architectural plans for the Giant-era containment facility within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. Engineers from [[Grimhold]] used the tablet as a guide to cut a controlled, magically-sealed breach through the capstone, allowing access to the [[Giant Empire]] foundation level without compromising the overall containment field. The tablet provides detailed structural information, including the identification of weak points in the [[Giant Capstone]].
