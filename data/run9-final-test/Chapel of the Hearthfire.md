The **Chapel of the Hearthfire** is a simple timber chapel in the frontier town of [[Grimhold]].

## Function and Atmosphere
It serves as a place of worship, healing, and community gathering for the town's residents. The chapel provides a sense of solace and stability in the harsh frontier environment.

## Caretaker and Services
The chapel is tended by the gentle healer [[Brother Aldwin]]. It is a known and reliable source for medical supplies and local information, particularly regarding strange occurrences associated with the nearby [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].
