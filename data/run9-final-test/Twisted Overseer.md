The **Twisted Overseer** is a commanding entity within the [[Twisted]] forces operating in the [[Giant foundation]] level. It is a corrupted dwarf clad in master-crafted, corrupted plate armor, with black veins pulsing beneath the metal. The Overseer coordinates Twisted activities and defenses, having studied the tactics of those opposing it. It wields a warhammer imbued with necrotic energy and can unleash a Corruption Pulse that damages and stuns foes. The entity represents the Twisted's transition from hidden saboteurs to organized military opposition.
