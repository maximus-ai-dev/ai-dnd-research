# Session 3: The Sleeping Mountain's Dream

*The following account chronicles the third week of occupation at the Asymmetrical Mountain fortress, as recorded by an observer of the events. The fortress, once a crumbling relic of the [[Iron Warlords]], has begun to transform under the stewardship of [[Cora Flint]], [[Garrick Kade]], and [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]]. But as they discovered, some secrets are better left buried—and some sleepers should never be awakened.*

---

## The Breach in the Wall

The air in the [[Salt-Vaults]] preparation kitchen hung thick with the scent of wet mortar and salt—the smell of progress. For three days, [[Rolf]] and his workers had followed the precise instructions of the [[Stone-Tenders]], their clicking binary pulses guiding the repair of cracked support pillars. [[Cora Flint]] reviewed supply tallies with [[Brenna]], while [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] documented the constructs' behavior from his perch on an overturned barrel. [[Garrick Kade]] stood watch at the corridor junction, his eyes occasionally drifting to the boarded-up stairwell marked "[[Vault of the Earth-Shaper]]"—where three workers had disappeared weeks before.

The mood was better than it had been since their arrival. Thirty people were eating again, thanks to preserved food from the vaults below. The winter howled outside, but inside there was warmth, purpose, and the steady rhythm of reconstruction.

Then Rolf's mattock punched through the eastern wall with a hollow *crack*.

Cold air whistled through the four-inch hole, carrying a scent both stale and sweet—impossibly old. "Hit hollow," Rolf reported quietly. "Four inches of stone where there should be six feet. Something's on the other side."

As he spoke, the two [[Stone-Tenders]] near the wall began clicking rapidly—not approval or disapproval, but something urgent. They positioned themselves between the hole and the food silo, forming a stone barrier. Through the breach, a low rhythmic sound echoed from the darkness beyond. Like breathing. Like something waking up.

## The Preserved Wardens

"Everyone back!" Cora commanded, her ledger snapping shut. "Garrick, between the workers and that hole. Mercer, translate that clicking."

[[Garrick Kade]] moved immediately, his maul raised. "Professor, if you're going to translate, do it fast."

[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] approached the Stone-Tenders, his academic excitement giving way to alarm. "That's a containment alert! They're establishing a perimeter against something that breached protocols."

Cora examined the mortar around the breach. "This isn't Iron Warlord work. The aggregate has too much crushed quartz. This is older. Much older." Her trained eye caught details others missed—concentric reinforcement patterns, hairline cracks showing where something had pressed against the wall from the other side. Recently.

Rolf carefully widened the hole with his chisel. As torchlight spilled into the space beyond, it revealed a narrow corridor with three alcoves. In each stood a humanoid figure clad in unfamiliar ring mail, perfectly preserved, chests rising and falling with slow, rhythmic breathing. Their skin was pale and waxy, dark veins visible beneath the surface.

The Stone-Tenders' clicking intensified to a frantic staccato.

The figure in the nearest alcove stirred. Its head lifted, eyes opening—human eyes clouded with confusion. It spoke in archaic Common with an accent Mercer had only heard in ancient texts: *"Identify... yourself... to the [[Secretive Brotherhood]]... or stand... aside."*

## Negotiation and Betrayal

Cora stepped forward, hands visible. "We are the current occupants. You've been in stasis for... some time. The Iron Warlords are gone."

Mercer invoked formal protocols in the same archaic tongue. "By the authority of the University of Laternia, I invoke the Accord of Mutual Scholarly Respect! Your Brotherhood entered stasis during the 'Great Sealing' after the incident with the [[Earth-Shaper]]."

The warden—[[Warden Aldric]]—processed this with visible difficulty. "Three... centuries?" He looked at his preserved hands, then at the hole. "The stasis... held that long?"

But the second figure—[[Warden Petra]]—was already moving. Her eyes were sharp, alert. "Aldric. They're not Brotherhood. Look at their equipment. That's preservation mortar from *our* side. Someone broke our seal." She raised her hand crossbow, aiming not at the party but at Rolf, who was mixing mortar to reseal the breach. "You're sealing us back in. Standard protocol: neutralize the threat."

The bolt flew true, catching Rolf in the shoulder. He grunted, staggered back, blood soaking his tunic. "Handle this," he said through gritted teeth.

## Breach and Truce

"That's enough!" Garrick roared, swinging his maul against the wall. Stone and mortar exploded outward as he activated his Giant's Might, widening the breach to three feet. "Cora, seal it after me! I'm going in!"

"Garrick, NO!" Mercer shouted. "You'll collapse the stasis field entirely!" He turned desperately to Aldric. "Article Seven—'Violence during parley voids all protections!' She just forfeited your Brotherhood's authority!"

Petra lowered her crossbow a fraction as Aldric stepped between them. "Stand down! Article Seven, Petra! You know the penalty!"

The third warden—[[Warden Cassen]]—spoke for the first time, his voice measured. "The stone guardians... they're not attacking. They're observing. If this were a Brotherhood test, they would recognize us."

Cora's sharp analysis cut through the tension. "Your preservation is degrading. Cellular crystallization. You have maybe six months before the stasis field collapses entirely." She pointed to Petra's crossbow. "Your bolt supply—six left, fletching delaminating. Your ring mail has stress fractures. You're all dehydrated."

The reality settled on Aldric like a physical weight. "Six months," he repeated softly.

Petra finally set her crossbow down. "The sanctum... we were guarding the passage to the [[Grand Archive]]. The [[Amalgamation]] watches over it."

Cassen added, "The Earth-Shaper... that's something else. Something older. The Brotherhood was studying it when... when we went into stasis."

## The Memory Casket

Aldric made his decision. "Professor Mercer. By the authority vested in me as Senior Warden... I am transferring temporary oversight of the Grand Archive passage to your institution." He produced a brass cylinder—a [[Memory Casket]] sealed with the Brotherhood's closed-eye symbol. "The operations log. It contains our standing orders, command codes... and the dismissal phrase."

Mercer authenticated it with a drop of blood. The wax seal glowed blue, then cracked open, revealing not just parchment but shimmering crystal plates. "Memory crystals," he breathed. "First Age recording technology."

As Garrick departed for [[Grimhold]] with the wounded Rolf—accompanied by workers [[Tomas]] and [[Elara Stormwind]] to seek healing from [[Brother Aldwin]] at the [[Chapel of the Hearthfire]]—the remaining party studied the crystals.

The projections showed fragments of the Brotherhood's final days: Archmage Valerius speaking urgently, glimpses of the Amalgamation door, and—most disturbingly—the Earth-Shaper level. Walls that pulsed with light, stone that flowed like water, crystals that grew like frozen waterfalls.

And then, the crucial sequence: three figures in workman's clothing—the missing workers—standing before the Earth-Shaper door. One touched the symbol, and the stone flowed open like water. They stepped through into darkness.

## Descent into the Dream

Four days of preparation passed. Mercer discovered the Stone-Tenders' clicking created a cognitive synchronization rhythm—three pulses, then a pause—that could provide limited protection against the Earth-Shaper's influence. Cora prepared sensory-shock vials to disrupt cognitive connections. Petra helped Brenna establish proper security protocols.

On the fifth day, Garrick returned with a healed Rolf. But as they prepared for a rescue mission, the Stone-Tenders began clicking in a new, urgent pattern.

"Proximity alert," Aldric said, going pale. "Something's moving. On the other side of one of the doors."

The party descended the Earth-Shaper stairwell in synchronized three-step movements, matching the Stone-Tenders' rhythm. What they found was no natural cavern.

The walls were covered in intricate crystalline formations that pulsed with soft blue light, the patterns moving in geometric sequences that felt like mathematics made visible. In the center stood the three missing workers—[[Harlan]], Jorn, and Liana—alive and physically unharmed, but staring at the crystals with rapt wonder.

"You came," Harlan said, his voice echoing strangely. "We knew you would. The patterns predicted it." He gestured to the crystals. "They're not just pretty. They're... thoughts. The mountain's thoughts."

Garrick kept his rhythm. "Harlan. We're here to take you back."

"Back? To what? To counting supplies and fearing the winter?" Harlan touched a crystal, smiling as the light pulsed brighter. "Here, we understand everything. The patterns show us how everything connects."

## The Cognitive Wave

They moved in synchronized retreat, grabbing the workers as the Stone-Tenders maintained their protective rhythm. But as they reached the stairwell, the massive central crystal at the far end of the cavern pulsed violently.

A wave of *meaning* washed through the chamber—not heat or cold, but patterns trying to force themselves into their minds. For a terrifying moment, each of them felt the seductive conviction that the crystals revealed universal truths, that everything connected in beautiful, inevitable patterns.

The Stone-Tenders' clicking became frantic. They took three steps forward, placing themselves between the party and the crystal. Their stone bodies vibrated with effort as they absorbed the brunt of the cognitive wave.

Then silence.

The wave receded. The crystal's light dimmed. The Stone-Tenders stood motionless, now covered in fine crystalline dust that hadn't been there before. They had contained the assault. At a cost.

## Aftermath and Assessment

Back in the fortress, the rescued workers were wrapped in blankets, given water, and put to work—structured labor to rebuild their mental anchors. Harlan counted inventory, Jorn chopped wood, Liana helped in the kitchen. Slowly, the crystal patterns faded from their minds, replaced by the simple rhythms of daily work.

A week later, the remaining Stone-Tenders summoned Mercer. The [[Psychic Guardian]]'s consciousness reached out with an assessment: images of the repaired Salt-Vaults, the sealed Earth-Shaper stairwell, the Archive corridor with the Wardens standing guard by choice, the recovering workers.

*The contract is fulfilled. The balance is restored. The mountain sleeps deeper now. You have done well.*

A pause. Then:

*The Stone-Tenders who remain will guard what you build. They will guard what you build. The dreamer below... it sleeps. For now. Do not wake it again.*

The connection faded. The fortress was secure, the immediate threats contained. The three brass insignias—keys to the Archive door—gleamed on the worktable. The stone door at the end of the Archive corridor waited. The Earth-Shaper slept below, its dreams temporarily quieted.

They had time now. To prepare properly. To study. To ensure that when they opened one of those ancient doors again, they would be ready for what waited on the other side.

But as Cora made her final ledger entry and Garrick maintained his watchful patrol, a question lingered in the cold mountain air: How long does a mountain dream? And what happens when it finally wakes?
