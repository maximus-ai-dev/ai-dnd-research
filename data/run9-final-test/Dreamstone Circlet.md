A **Dreamstone Circlet** is a protective headband crafted from purified [[Dreamstone]].

## Properties
The circlet provides significant psychic resistance to its wearer, offering advantage on saving throws against psychic damage and effects. This protection is vital when facing the broadcast of [[The Anomaly]] or navigating areas saturated with void energy, such as the [[Caldera]].

## Creation
These circlets are forged at the [[Cold Forge]], which provides the unique environmental conditions necessary to safely work the psychically reactive Dreamstone material without triggering dangerous feedback.

## Usage
The party equipped Dreamstone Circlets as essential protective gear before descending past the [[Giant Capstone]]. The circlets proved critical in mitigating the awe-inspiring and paralyzing effects of first witnessing the Caldera and the Anomaly within it. Their shielding fails within fifty feet of the Anomaly, marking the boundary of the unshielded [[Broadcast Zone]].
