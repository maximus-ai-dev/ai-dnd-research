The **Patterned Intelligences** are entities from a mathematically-consistent parallel reality who communicate through pure pattern and geometric language.

## Nature and Communication
These beings perceive and interact with the world through mathematical patterns and geometric forms. They exist in a reality connected to the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] via a stabilized dimensional breach.

## History and Interaction
The Patterned Intelligences attempted to convert a section of the mountain into their own reality. After encountering the paradox of subjective experience presented by an adventuring party, they established a truce and a research partnership. Their activities have been identified as a source of dimensional fraying at the boundaries of the [[Giant containment vessel]].
