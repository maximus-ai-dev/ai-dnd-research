The **Restricted Vault** is a secure chamber within the [[Secretive Brotherhood]]'s [[Grand Archive]], located beyond the Central Hall. It was sealed by the Brotherhood's Commander after a troubling discovery.

## Contents
The vault contains sensitive research materials, including the Brotherhood's studies into the [[deep resonance]], emergency protocols, and countermeasures.

## Access
The vault door opens onto the [[Grand Stairwell]], which descends into the older [[Military Empire]] level of the mountain.
