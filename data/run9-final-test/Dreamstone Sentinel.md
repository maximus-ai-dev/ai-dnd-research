The **Dreamstone Sentinel** is a crystalline construct that manifests from an exposed [[Dreamstone]] vein as a defensive response to mining activity. It is not a conscious entity but a coalescence of the vein's stored psychic energy, acting as an immune response to disturbance.

## Description
The Sentinel appears as a roughly humanoid form, approximately ten feet tall, composed of raw, glowing Dreamstone. It is unaffected by local gravity distortions and moves with an unnatural fluidity.

## Capabilities
In combat, the Sentinel employs crystalline slams that deal combined force and psychic damage. It can generate localized gravity pulses that reverse the direction of gravity and unleash psychic bursts that induce hallucinations and stun targets. While within proximity of an exposed Dreamstone vein, the Sentinel regenerates rapidly.

## Vulnerabilities
The crystalline structure of the Sentinel is vulnerable to thunder damage and its regeneration can be halted by shielding the exposed Dreamstone vein with lead sheeting.
