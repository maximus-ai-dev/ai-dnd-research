The **Minecart Network** is an extensive dwarven transportation system built by the hold of [[Kazad-Khrom]] to move ore and materials through the [[Dwarven Layer]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Design and Infrastructure
The network consists of iron rails laid into carved stone channels, spanning hundreds of feet through branching tunnels. It features loading platforms, switch junctions, passing bays, brake mechanisms, and maintenance bays. The rails are made of treated dwarven iron resistant to corrosion, and the system includes both large ore carts and smaller hand-cranked maintenance carts. A major junction is controlled by a massive iron turntable set into the floor.

## Routes
The network connects key industrial sites:
- **West:** To the [[Cold Forge]] complex.
- **East:** To the primary mining operations and the [[Eastern Transit Hub]].
- **North:** Toward a location labeled "Pump Station East."
- **South:** A branch marked "QUARANTINE — DO NOT ACCESS" on dwarven schematics.

## History and Current Status
The network fell into disuse following the retreat of Kazad-Khrom. The eastern branch was blocked for decades by the behir [[Kellashen]], which laired in the Eastern Transit Hub. With the creature's defeat, the eastern branch has been partially cleared, making the network operational again for the first time in centuries. Reactivating the network allows rapid transport of materials and provides access to deeper regions of the mountain, including the flooded lower levels and areas connected to Giant architecture.
