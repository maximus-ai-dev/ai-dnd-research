The **Grand Archive** is a sealed repository constructed by the [[Secretive Brotherhood]], located at the terminus of an interstitial corridor behind the [[Salt-Vaults]].

## Entrance and Security
The archive is accessed via a magically sealed stone door marked with the Brotherhood's closed-eye symbol and an inscription in archaic Common. This entrance is guarded by a bound construct consciousness known as the [[Amalgamation]], which tests entrants for cognitive flexibility.

## Access Requirements
Gaining entry requires presenting three brass Brotherhood insignias to satisfy the arcane lock, followed by passing the Amalgamation's cognitive evaluation. The interior of the archive remains unexplored, its contents unknown.
