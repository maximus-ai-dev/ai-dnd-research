# Session 10: The Drowned Juggernaut

*Chronicled from the recovered field notes of the expedition to the Dwarven Layer, with additional observations from the spectral consciousness of Professor Thaddeus Mercer*

---

The pumps had been running for a week.

That steady industrial thrum had become the mountain's new heartbeat, a rhythmic pulse echoing through every corridor of the [[Dwarven Layer]]. In the [[Cold Forge]] chamber, [[Ignathar]]'s elemental fire burned steadily in the central pit, casting flickering orange light across newly cleaned stonework. The cooling system—fed by the restored [[Pump Station East]]—circulated water through ancient channels, creating a constant background hiss of steam and flowing liquid.

[[Cora Flint]] stood at the forge's control panel, her hands tracing the newly installed master forge key. For the first time in over a millennium, the Cold Forge operated at capacity. The air was warm enough to make the underground complex habitable without heavy winter gear—a testament to Ignathar's elemental presence.

[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] sat nearby, the Giant blueprint tablet open before him. His spectral mind—a shimmering, book-shaped apparition—floated nearby, its pages turning silently as it cross-referenced runes with recovered schematics. "The drainage is proceeding exactly as predicted," he murmured. "The lower Dwarven Layer should be accessible by now."

[[Garrick Kade]] leaned against the entrance archway, his newly repaired chain mail gleaming in the forge light. He'd been watching the minecart tunnel that led deeper into the mountain—the same tunnel where they'd heard industrial sounds before their encounter with [[Kellashen]]. Those sounds had changed. The rhythmic hammering had been replaced by something else: a deep, resonant vibration that traveled through the stone itself, felt in the bones rather than heard.

"The water's gone from down there," he said, his voice low. "Something's moving in the empty space."

Cora looked up from the controls. "The Pump Station Operations Manual mentioned a convergence point—the [[Drowned Chokepoint]]. Every drainage pipe meets there. It's the deepest navigable level before the [[Giant Capstone]]." She tapped a schematic showing pipes converging on a single junction. "If [[The Twisted]] were using the flooded levels as a staging area... now that the water's draining, whatever they've been building down there is exposed."

The vibration through the stone intensified momentarily—a deep *thrum* that made forge tools rattle on their racks.

Then the careful preparations shattered.

From the minecart tunnel, something massive was coming up. The vibration became violent shaking. Rails began to hum, then shriek as something ground against them. Cora's ledger fell from her hands as the entire forge chamber trembled. Garrick's Giant's Might form seemed suddenly less imposing as the tunnel entrance exploded outward in a shower of stone and shattered steel ribbing.

[[The Drowned Juggernaut]] emerged.

Eighteen feet of dwarven iron and stone, humanoid in shape but monstrous in execution. Its arms ended in spinning drill assemblies that screamed against the air, throwing sparks that sizzled where they hit damp stone. Its torso was a furnace glowing with sickly iridescent heat that smelled of [[Dreamstone]] and decay. Where its head should be, a pulsing mass of corrupted tissue pulsed with black fluid through transparent resin tubes.

The machine didn't pause. It didn't assess. It charged.

**Drill Charge.** The Juggernaut barreled forward in a straight line, hydraulic legs pistoning with brutal force. Garrick, Cora, and Mercer were directly in its path. The drills screamed as they tore through the air.

Garrick took the impact full-force—28 points of bludgeoning damage that slammed him to the stone floor. Cora failed her dodge, taking 23 damage and knocked prone. Mercer twisted aside at the last moment, taking only half damage but still staggering back, robes torn and bloodied.

The Juggernaut skidded to a halt, drills still spinning, furnace pulsing with sickly heat. It turned slowly—the motion of immense weight on hydraulic joints—and faced them again. Six thick organic resin cables trailed from its back into the ruined tunnel, pulsing with black fluid. They were still connected, feeding it power from somewhere deep below.

The forge chamber was now a battlefield. The entrance tunnel was partially collapsed behind the Juggernaut. They were trapped in here with it.

"**CALCULATING!**" Cora shouted, scrambling to her feet. "Those resin cables are feeding it—they're healing 10 HP per turn! That's an unsustainable attrition rate!"

Mercer's spectral mind floated above the chaos. "The furnace core—it's Dreamstone-powered! That's why the radiation signature was so strong!" He pointed his staff and cast Fireball through his Awakened Spellbook, swapping the damage type to cold—if it was running hot, cold might disrupt its systems.

The sphere of intense cold energy blossomed around the Juggernaut's torso. Frost formed across iron plating, but the organic resin glowed with internal heat, resisting the worst of the effect. Still, the furnace's sickly glow dimmed momentarily before flaring back to life.

Garrick charged back in, maul swinging with Great Weapon Master's brutal efficiency. His strike connected with the Juggernaut's leg plating in a shower of sparks—15 points of damage that dented dwarven iron. But even as they watched, the organic resin beneath flowed to fill the dent. The machine barely seemed to notice.

Then the Juggernaut's torso vents opened.

**Corruption Furnace.** A 30-foot cone of iridescent fire and necrotic energy erupted toward Mercer and Cora. The heat was wrong—it burned cold and hot simultaneously, carrying the psychic taint of the Sleeper's dreams.

Cora used her **Flash of Genius** as a reaction, adding her intelligence to Mercer's Constitution save. "Professor! Resist the corruption—focus on the cold equations!"

Both succeeded, taking only half damage and avoiding being poisoned. But the forge chamber was filling with toxic fumes. Ignathar's elemental fire roared in protest at this corrupted heat invading its domain. The control panel Cora had been using moments before was now partially melted, dripping molten metal.

"The cables!" Mercer shouted through the flames. "Sever the power supply! Cora, can you create an acid bath to dissolve the resin connections?"

Cora saw what Mercer had noticed: Mercer's earlier Telekinesis spell had stretched one cable taut. The stretched resin was thinner, more vulnerable. And she could see where it connected to the Juggernaut's back—a socket of fused organic material that looked less armored than the rest of the machine.

But the Juggernaut was already turning, drills spinning up again. The furnace in its torso was building heat once more. It was targeting Garrick this time, who stood between it and the wounded Mercer.

Cora made a decision. "Garrick, buy me ten seconds! I'm going to try to override the coolant system—maybe we can freeze the cables or short its furnace!"

She reached the damaged control panel. The interface was partially melted, but the underlying dwarven mechanisms were intact. She could see the coolant system controls—a series of stone levers and crystal indicators showing water flow through channels beneath the forge floor. One lever was labeled in Dwarvish: "EMERGENCY COOLANT FLOOD—FORGE CHAMBER ONLY."

Mercer collapsed against the panel beside her, his breathing shallow. "The coolant... it's ice-cold mountain water... pumped from deep aquifers..." he gasped. "If you flood the chamber... the resin will become brittle... the cables might freeze and shatter..."

But there was a problem. The emergency flood lever was locked behind a safety cage—a wrought-iron grate that required a key or a DC 15 Strength check to break open. And the Juggernaut was thirty feet away and closing.

Garrick saw the scroll at his feet—Mercer's Web scroll. He snatched it up, his blood-slick fingers nearly dropping it. The Juggernaut was between him and his companions, its back to him. The stretched cable from Mercer's Telekinesis attempt was still taut, thinner than the others.

Cora wedged a dwarven pry bar into the safety cage and heaved with all her strength. The metal groaned, bent, then snapped with a sharp crack. The cage door swung open.

Simultaneously, Garrick read the Web scroll. Thick, sticky webbing erupted from his hands, shooting across the chamber to envelop the resin cables where they connected to the Juggernaut's back. The webbing pinned three of the six cables against the stone wall, sticky strands wrapping around organic tubes and anchoring them in place.

The Juggernaut strained against the restraint, hydraulic legs pistoning, but the Web spell held. **The machine was restrained.**

Cora's hand was on the emergency flood lever. The label warned: "FLOOD CHAMBER—EXTREME COLD HAZARD—EVACUATE PERSONNEL."

"Garrick, hit the webbed cables now! They're vulnerable while restrained!" she shouted. "Mercer, brace for thermal shock—this water is ice-cold!"

She looked at Ignathar's elemental fire roaring in the forge pit. "Ignathar! Withdraw to minimal form! Emergency coolant flood incoming!"

She pulled the lever.

Ice-cold mountain water erupted from vents in the walls and floor. The temperature plummeted instantly. Frost formed on every surface. The water rose fast—ankle-deep in seconds, then knee-deep. It was painfully cold, numbing limbs and stealing breath.

The effect on the Juggernaut was immediate. The organic resin covering its plating turned milky white as it froze. The resin cables stiffened, becoming brittle. The black fluid inside them slowed to a crawl, then stopped entirely as it froze solid. **The Juggernaut's regeneration stopped.**

But the machine was still functional. Its dwarven iron core was unaffected by the cold. Its furnace still glowed, though the water rising around its legs was starting to produce clouds of steam. The biological control node pulsed frantically, as if trying to adapt to freezing conditions.

The water was now waist-deep and rising. Mercer's Feather Fall spell activated, making them buoyant. Ignathar had contracted to a small, intense ball of fire deep in the forge pit—the elemental was surviving, but barely.

Garrick's maul slammed into a webbed cable just as the Juggernaut's drill hit him in return. The impact was brutal—22 points of bludgeoning and 4 necrotic that nearly dropped him. But his strike landed true. One of the webbed resin tubes cracked under the blow, black fluid spraying across the chamber like arterial blood.

**One cable down.**

Cora's Ray of Frost hit another frozen cable, coating it in another layer of ice. The resin turned crystalline. Garrick's follow-up strike shattered it explosively.

**Two cables down.**

The battle became a brutal war of attrition in freezing water. Cora's Acid Splash weakened connections. Garrick's maul shattered brittle resin. Mercer, unconscious and floating, was kept alive only by Feather Fall and Cora's emergency healing.

**Three cables down. Four cables down.**

The Juggernaut was slowing, movements becoming jerky and uncoordinated as power failed. Steam poured continuously from its submerged furnace. The frozen resin covering its plating cracked everywhere—great spiderweb fractures spreading across its surface.

**Five cables down.**

Garrick's final attack met the Juggernaut's last desperate defense. The drill caught his maul mid-swing. Metal screamed against metal. For a moment, they were locked in a brutal stalemate—Garrick's immense strength against the dying machine's last reserves of hydraulic power.

Then the last cable, strained beyond its limits, snapped.

The final resin tube whipped free. The Juggernaut went completely still. The biological control node dimmed to a faint, irregular pulse. The drill arm stopped spinning.

The machine stood motionless in the freezing water, a dead monument to corrupted dwarven engineering.

Then the cracks began. Great chunks of milky-white resin broke off and floated to the water's surface. The biological control node gave one final, weak pulse, then went dark completely.

**The Drowned Juggernaut was defeated.**

---

*Aftermath and Salvage*

The emergency drainage system worked. Water swirled down hidden drains, revealing the chamber floor inch by inch. Ignathar's elemental fire expanded again as the forge pit dried.

Salvage operations began immediately. Using Garrick's maul as a pry bar, they extracted sections of adamantine plating from the Juggernaut's torso—thick, dark metal that gleamed with a faint blue sheen. **[[Adamantine Chassis Plates]]: enough for one suit of half plate.**

Mercer, with his spectral mind's assistance, carefully extracted the biological control node. The pulsing mass of corrupted tissue came free with a wet tearing sound. He wrapped it in lead-lined cloth—the same material they'd used for the [[Dreamstone Fragment]]. **[[Dreamstone Power Cell]]: a fist-sized sphere of crystallized Dreamstone encased in lead, still faintly warm to the touch.**

Cora collected samples of the black fluid from the severed cables. The fluid was thick, oily, and smelled of the same metallic sweetness as Dreamstone radiation, but with an organic undertone of decay.

As they worked, the rhythmic vibration from below continued. Then a new sound joined it: the distant, echoing clang of metal on stone. Many tools. And a smell wafted up—decaying flesh, fungal growth, and black resin.

The Twisted knew their Juggernaut was dead.

Mercer examined the control panel. "The emergency seal mechanism is intact but requires manual activation from both this panel and a secondary control in the transit hub. I can lock the tunnel door temporarily, but it won't hold against sustained assault."

He had a better idea. "Use the furnace core as an improvised explosive. The Dreamstone power cell is unstable—if we overload it with remaining coolant, we can trigger a thermal collapse."

They positioned the power cell against a structural weak point in the tunnel arch. Cora set alchemical traps in a chain leading back to it. From the tunnel, a wet slithering noise joined the clanging. Then a psychic impression slithered into their minds: *"The dream is broken. The guardian falls. The sleepers wake."*

Cora nodded. "Now."

Freezing water flooded onto the Dreamstone power cell. For a moment, nothing happened. Then the cell began to glow—not with light, but with a darkness that seemed to drink the light around it. The temperature plummeted. Frost raced across tunnel walls.

Then the explosion. Not fire, but cold. A wave of freezing energy erupted, flash-freezing everything in the tunnel. Stone cracked with sounds like gunshots. The arch collapsed in a roar of falling rock and shattering ice.

When dust cleared, the tunnel entrance was gone. A solid wall of collapsed stone and ice blocked it completely.

But from deep, deep below, through tons of rock, the rhythmic vibration continued. Slower now. More deliberate. As if whatever was down there had just been informed of their presence, and was considering its response.

The forge chamber was secure. The tunnel was sealed. The Juggernaut was destroyed. The salvage was theirs.

But the Drowned Chokepoint—the Twisted's main workshop, the convergence of all drainage systems, the place above the Giant Capstone—remained below them. And whatever the Twisted were building down there, they now knew they had enemies above.

Garrick hefted the salvage. "Let's go. They'll find another way up eventually. We need to be ready."

They retreated from the forge chamber, leaving the dead Juggernaut and the collapsed tunnel behind.

The battle was won, but the war was just beginning.
