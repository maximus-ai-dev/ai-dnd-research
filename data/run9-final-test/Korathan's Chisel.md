**Korathan's Chisel** is a replica of the ancient tool used by the Giant architect [[Korathan]]. It is a critical instrument for inscribing precise containment runes.

## Description and Function
The chisel is designed to work on a human scale, allowing for the precise carving of Giant containment rune patterns that were originally inscribed with tools the size of trees. It is essential for reproducing the technical framework provided by the scaling runes found in Korathan's tomb.

## Use in the Campaign
[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] used Korathan's Chisel to inscribe the complete rune matrix onto the [[Dreamstone]] core of the [[Dreamstone Seal]]. This process represented the most technically demanding work of his career, requiring hours of continuous, precise inscription to program the Seal's containment instructions.
