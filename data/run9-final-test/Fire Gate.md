The **Fire Gate** is a sealed planar conduit connecting the [[Cold Forge]] and the [[Caldera]] to the [[Elemental Plane of Fire]].

## Description
The Gate is marked with ancient Giant runes and was originally constructed by Giants during the creation of the mountain's containment architecture. It functions not as a roaring inferno but as a focused stream of elemental fire, channeled by Giant-era architecture into a controlled flow.

## Function and Significance
The Fire Gate provides a direct source of unlimited elemental heat. Opening it was part of a bargain with the fire elemental [[Ignathar]]. During the forging of the [[Dreamstone Seal]], the party opened the Gate using [[Korathan's scaling runes]]. This provided [[Cora Flint]] with advantage on all forging checks by granting access to supernatural temperature control. After the Seal's completion, the conduit remained active, allowing Ignathar to depart and providing potential future access to elemental fire.
