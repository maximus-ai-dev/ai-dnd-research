**Fleshy Amplifiers** are a network of organic broadcast towers constructed by the [[Twisted]] within the [[Giant foundation]] level of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
These amplifiers are pillars of organic Twisted construction—black resin grown into the stone, threaded with bioluminescent veins that pulse with the [[First World Sleeper]]'s psychic frequency. They extend from floor to ceiling, parasitizing the [[Giant Empire]]'s containment runes to tap their energy.

## Function
The amplifiers are designed to boost and direct the Sleeper's psychic broadcast upward through the mountain, weaponizing the emanation to corrupt the upper layers. They represent a critical component of the Twisted's infrastructure to breach the ancient containment seals.

## Known Amplifiers
Three amplifiers were identified in the Giant foundation's outer ring: [[Amplifier Alpha]] (a prototype in the northwest quadrant), [[Amplifier Beta]] (a modified and sophisticated unit in the southeast), and [[Amplifier Gamma]] (the largest and most powerful, located near the threshold of the inner void).
