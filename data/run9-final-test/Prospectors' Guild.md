The **Prospectors' Guild** is the primary governing and economic organization in the frontier town of [[Grimhold]]. Led by [[Reyna Steelforge]], the guild regulates mining claims, organizes security, and represents the town's interests. Guild prospectors were the first to report the anomalous phenomena associated with the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. Its operations have expanded from pure resource extraction to supporting the study and reinforcement of the ancient [[Giant containment vessel]].
