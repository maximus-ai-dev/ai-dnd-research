**Rolf** is a quiet, competent scout and supervisor within [[Brenna]]'s workforce of former [[Emberfell Rejects]].

## Demeanor and Skills
Rolf is stoic, highly observant, and not prone to panic, making him a reliable figure in the workforce. His skills in scouting and assessment are valued.

## Key Actions and Injury
While working in the preparation kitchen of the [[Salt-Vaults]], Rolf accidentally breached a false wall, discovering the hidden interstitial corridor that led to the [[Grand Archive]]. During the subsequent confrontation with the [[Secretive Brotherhood]] wardens who emerged to investigate the breach, he was wounded by a crossbow bolt. He survived the injury and was transported to [[Grimhold]] for proper healing at the [[Chapel of the Hearthfire]].
