**Amplifier Alpha** was one of three [[Fleshy Amplifiers]] constructed by the [[Twisted]] in the [[Giant foundation]] level.

## Location
It was located in the northwest quadrant of the Giant foundation's outer ring, approximately 400 feet from the forward camp established at the [[Bastion of the Asymmetrical]].

## Description
Amplifier Alpha was the smallest and oldest of the amplifiers, believed to be a prototype. It was situated in a Giant maintenance corridor near a damaged section of the containment wall.

## Status
The amplifier was successfully sabotaged and destroyed, severing its parasitic connection to the containment runes and causing its organic structure to collapse.
