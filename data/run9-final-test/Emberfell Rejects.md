The **Emberfell Rejects** are a band of desperate outlaws originally composed of exiles and refugees from the distant land of Emberfell.

## History and Operations
They operated in the [[Starmetal Hills]] near [[Grimhold]], surviving by raiding supply caravans. The group was initially led by the cruel [[Kregg]], who ruled through fear and intimidation.

## Current Status
After their defeat at the fortress on the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], the surviving members, under the leadership of [[Brenna]], surrendered. They were offered clemency in exchange for becoming a legitimate workforce for the adventuring party that secured the mountain fortress, trading banditry for structured labor.
