The **Giant foundation** is the deepest known architectural layer within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], a colossal subterranean structure built by an ancient race of [[Giant Empire|Giants]].

## Purpose
Its primary function is to contain the [[First World Sleeper]], a reality-warping entity imprisoned below. The foundation is engineered with intricate containment runes etched into the stone that glow with magical energy, forming a barrier against the Sleeper's psychic radiation.

## Architecture
The foundation consists of massive corridors, chambers, and infrastructure scaled for Giant workers. It features maintenance corridors, monitoring alcoves, tool storage areas, and workshop forges of staggering scale. The area is divided into an outer ring and an inner void threshold.

## Current Status
The foundation has been partially infiltrated by the [[Twisted]], who parasitize its containment runes with their organic infrastructure, such as the [[Fleshy Amplifiers]]. The outer ring is a contested zone where the party operates to sabotage Twisted operations.
