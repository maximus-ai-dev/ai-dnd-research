The **Asymmetrical Mountain** is a large, geologically anomalous peak within the [[Starmetal Hills]], known for its unsettling, lopsided profile visible for miles.

## Structure
The mountain's interior contains a deep, multi-layered subterranean complex built by successive civilizations. Beneath surface ruins and a fallen [[Military Empire]] layer lies the [[Dwarven Layer]] constructed by [[Kazad-Khrom]], which itself sits atop ancient Giant foundations. These foundations house a massive containment vessel built to imprison a powerful entity.

## Current Status
The mountain is the site of ongoing exploration and fortification by forces from [[Grimhold]].
