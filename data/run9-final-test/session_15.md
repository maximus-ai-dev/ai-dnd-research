# Session 15: The Deep Quarantine

**Session Date:** 15th of Stone-Deep, 998 YK

*Chronicler's Note: What follows is an account of the expedition into the irradiated depths below the Giant foundation. The party descended into darkness to confront a new Twisted threat, only to uncover a truth more horrifying than any breach attempt. This record documents not just a military operation, but a fundamental shift in our understanding of the containment's nature.*

---

The [[Cold Forge]] chamber had become a sanctuary of warmth and light in the deep dark of the mountain. For two weeks, the party had rested there—[[Ignathar]]'s elemental fire providing steady heat, the dwarven stonework offering solid shelter, and the minecart network secured against further incursions. Wounds had healed, supplies had been replenished from [[Grimhold]], and the constant tension of the amplifiers campaign had given way to a different kind of vigilance: the patient, methodical work of intelligence gathering.

[[Cora]] had transformed the Twisted communication node recovered from the outer ring into a sophisticated monitoring station. Connected to the residual resin conduits that still threaded through the [[Giant foundation]]'s walls, it hummed softly in a corner of the forge, its organic crystals pulsing with faint, rhythmic light. For days, she had sat before it, mapping signal patterns, decoding pulse structures, and building a picture of what the [[Twisted]] were doing now that their amplifiers were silent.

This morning, Cora's monitoring crystal displayed something new: a concentrated signal cluster deep in the outer ring, approximately eight hundred feet below their forward camp. It wasn't a patrol. It was a garrison. And at its heart, something large, alive, and growing—a second drilling operation, more advanced than the [[Drowned Juggernaut]], aimed at a structural weak point in the [[Giant Capstone]].

"They have a staging ground," Cora reported, her face pale in the forge-light. "A fortified installation. And a drill that's alive—it grows as it bores. In another month, it will be through the capstone from a direction the containment wasn't designed to defend."

She pointed to a section of her signal map—a narrow gap in the Twisted perimeter coverage, created by a damaged resin conduit. "There's a blind spot. Thirty feet wide. It gives us one shot at surprise. But the Twisted repair their conduits on a cycle. The gap closes in twenty-four hours."

The forge fire crackled. The decision hung in the warm air.

---

## The Descent Into Darkness

Six hours later, preparations were complete. Cora had brewed experimental elixirs using [[Restorative Reagents]], each vial glowing with protective amber light. [[Garrick]] had inscribed his runes—the Cloud Rune on his maul for tactical redirection, the Stone Rune on his armor for enhanced resilience. [[Mercer]] had created a scroll of *pass without trace* and prepared a spell selection for the specific threats they anticipated: *globe of invulnerability*, *telekinesis*, *chain lightning*, and *feeblemind* as a precision strike against command structures.

They descended through the [[Giant foundation]]'s corridors, the air growing colder and heavier with each hundred feet. The [[Dreamstone]] radiation manifested as a visible haze—a shimmering purple distortion that warped light and sound. Garrick felt it first: a psychic weight that made every step feel like wading through deep water.

"Radiation's already affecting me," he grunted, shaking his head to clear a brief moment of dizziness. "Nothing I can't push through, but keep an eye on me."

Mercer's spectral mind drifted ahead, unaffected by the radiation. "The corridor descends at a fifteen-degree angle," he reported through their mental link. "Structural integrity is stable, but I'm detecting organic residue on the walls—recent Twisted passage."

At a junction, they faced a choice: the main corridor leading toward the blind spot with the sound of drilling growing clearer, or a side passage that didn't appear on Cora's signal maps. Garrick argued for the direct route. "Side passage is unknown, could be a trap or waste time. That drilling means they're working right now. Every minute we waste, they get closer to breaking through."

Cora distributed the elixirs. "Before we proceed, everyone should take one of these. The temporary hit points will help buffer against psychic damage." The liquid burned going down, then spread a warm, steadying energy through their bodies.

Mercer unrolled the *pass without trace* scroll. "I'll activate this now—it lasts an hour, which should cover our approach through the blind spot and initial infiltration. Everyone stay within thirty feet of me."

The magic wrapped around them like a cloak of shadows, muffling footsteps, dampening sounds, and bending light around their forms. Combined with the radiation haze, they became near-invisible phantoms moving through the distorted corridor.

---

## Masterful Infiltration

They reached the blind spot—a thirty-foot section where the resin conduit was shattered, its organic material dried and dead. Beyond it, the Twisted perimeter began: intact conduits pulsed with sickly green light, and two [[Twisted Laborers]] moved slowly along the patrol circuit eighty feet ahead.

"Wait," Cora whispered. "Let me time their patrol pattern. Eliminating them risks noise and leaves evidence—dead bodies that get discovered on the next patrol cycle."

They moved with ghostly precision, slipping past the Laborers at the exact moment the patrols were at maximum distance, using the drilling's constant thrum as acoustic cover. The party reached the end of the two-hundred-foot corridor without a single misstep—a masterful infiltration worthy of legends.

Beyond lay the outer staging ground—a complex of modified chambers the Twisted had converted into a military installation. To the left, organic alcoves served as barracks with approximately fifteen resting Twisted. Straight ahead, an armory held Twisted-modified weapons and equipment, including lead-lined containers of sorted [[Dreamstone Fragment]]s. To the right, their primary objective: the organic power hub, a nexus of resin conduits where communication and power networks converged.

"The power hub is connected to everything," Mercer reported through his spectral mind. "Destroying it will disrupt communications, power distribution, and possibly the drill itself. But there are motion sensors—organic filaments on the floor around it."

Cora studied the layout. "Risk assessment: bypassing the barracks leaves fifteen potential combatants at our backs when we engage the power hub. That's unacceptable risk. We neutralize the barracks first, but quietly."

Garrick moved toward the nearest sleeping Twisted, but his boot scuffed loudly against loose stone. The creature stirred, beginning to wake.

"Damn," Garrick whispered, already shifting his grip on his maul. "They're waking up. Change of plan—we hit them hard and fast before they can alert the others."

Mercer gestured, whispering an incantation. "I'm casting *hold person* on the waking Twisted. Paralysis prevents both movement and vocalization." Arcane energy wrapped around the creature as it began to rise, freezing it in mid-motion.

But the damage was done. The noise had stirred others.

---

## The Overseer's Wrath

From the corridor leading to the power hub, a massive form emerged—the [[Twisted Overseer]] in its final form. Master-crafted corrupted plate armor covered its body, black veins pulsing beneath the metal. Its warhammer glowed with necrotic energy as it charged directly at Cora.

The warhammer slammed into Cora's side with crushing force. She gasped, the wind knocked from her lungs, pain radiating through her ribs. Simultaneously, two [[Twisted Engineers]] emerged from the armory chamber, their wrench-picks gleaming as they flanked Garrick.

The Overseer's voice echoed through the chamber, distorted and layered with corruption: "You have studied our methods. We have studied yours. The saboteur dies first."

Cora raised her hand, focusing arcane energy. "Priority target: the Overseer. I'll use *ray of sickness*." Green necrotic energy wrapped around the creature, but its corrupted physiology resisted the worst effects.

Garrick activated Giant's Might, his body swelling to Large size as he swung his maul at an Engineer with devastating force. The Great Weapon Master strike combined with Giant's Might created a crushing impact that left the Engineer stumbling, black fluid leaking from cracks in its armor.

Mercer cast *slow*, enveloping the Overseer and both Engineers in temporal energy. "Wisdom save DC 18 for all three. On a failure: speed halved, can only use one action or bonus action per turn, can't use reactions, and has a 50% chance of their action failing."

The Overseer and the injured Engineer were slowed, but the Overseer still raised its warhammer again, moving with painful slowness but undeniable power toward Cora. The weapon connected with her already-injured side. She cried out as ribs cracked, the necrotic energy burning through her.

Around them, the barracks was fully awakening. Four Twisted Laborers had risen and were grabbing weapons. Eight more were stirring. The party was about to be surrounded.

---

## Turning the Tide

Cora retreated behind Garrick's enlarged form, drinking a healing potion and casting *healing word* on herself. "Medical triage required. I need to disengage and heal."

Garrick saw the Overseer advancing on Cora. "Get behind me!" he roared. Using his Sentinel feat, he swung his maul at the Overseer as it attacked within reach. "You want her? You go through me first!" Then he used Action Surge, unleashing three devastating attacks that cracked the Overseer's armor and sprayed black fluid from the impacts.

Mercer cast *hypnotic pattern*, creating a twisting pattern of colors that filled the barracks area. "This should neutralize the reinforcements before they can join the fight." Eight stirring Twisted Laborers froze, their eyes glazing over as they became charmed and incapacitated.

But the Overseer was not done. Despite its wounds, it swung its warhammer at Cora once more, catching her with brutal precision. **Critical hit.** The weapon crunched into her already-injured chest with bone-breaking force. Cora cried out as she was thrown back against the wall, ribs cracking, blood spraying from her mouth. She slumped to the floor—critically wounded and barely conscious.

The Overseer then pulsed with Command Corruption energy, reinforcing the buff on remaining Twisted as black energy washed through the chamber. All nearby Twisted glowed with enhanced corruption, their weapons crackling with necrotic energy.

Mercer acted. With a sharp gesture, he used *telekinesis* to hurl the restrained Overseer through the air in a precise arc. The massive corrupted form slammed into the central organic node of the power hub with devastating force.

The impact damage was substantial, but more importantly, the power hub collapsed in on itself. Resin conduits ruptured in sprays of black fluid and green energy. A cascade failure rippled through the staging ground's nervous system.

The effect was immediate. Throughout the installation, resin conduits went dark. The communication network died. The drilling sound from the construction bay shuddered and stopped. Every Twisted in the staging ground staggered as their connection to the organic infrastructure was severed.

Mercer didn't stop there. As the Overseer struggled to rise from the wreckage, electricity crackled around his hands. ***Chain lightning*** erupted, a blinding blue-white bolt that struck the Overseer before arcing to the remaining Engineer and two Laborers. The electricity sizzled through corrupted flesh and organic modifications.

The Overseer was electrocuted where it lay, blackened and smoking. The Engineer convulsed as lightning coursed through it. Two Laborers were fried where they stood.

---

## The Horrifying Revelation

With the immediate threats neutralized, the party turned their attention to the construction bay where the [[Living Drill]] awaited. What they found there would change everything.

The drill was destroyed through careful overload—Cora reversing its power flow to make it consume itself. But as the dust settled, a new problem emerged. The bore hole—thirty feet deep into the capstone—was now exposed. And at its bottom, visible by the drill's residual glow, the stone was different: translucent, warm, pulsing with inner light.

This wasn't the capstone itself. It was what lay immediately beneath it—the upper surface of the [[First World Sleeper]]'s domain.

And in the translucent stone, shapes moved. Not Twisted. Something else. Something larger, slower, and infinitely more patient. The Sleeper's domain was not empty. It was inhabited.

The deep hum from below became a single, sustained note that vibrated the containment runes in the walls. The Sleeper had felt the drill's destruction. It was not grateful. It was not angry. It was awake. Slightly more awake than it was yesterday.

Cora's monitoring crystal recorded the shapes—not as individual creatures, but as manifestations of a single consciousness. "They're not trying to breach," she reported, her voice clinical despite the horror. "They're... existing. The Sleeper's reality has crystallized against the capstone's underside. It's growing upward, transforming the stone from beneath."

Mercer's arcane analysis confirmed it. "The Twisted weren't drilling to break through—they were drilling to interface. The living drill was designed to bond with that crystallized reality, creating a permanent connection between the Sleeper's domain and ours. It wasn't a breach tool; it was an integration tool."

The implications settled over them like a physical weight. The containment wasn't being attacked from outside—it was being consumed from within. The Sleeper's reality was slowly transforming the capstone itself, turning solid stone into crystallized dreamstuff. The twenty-foot thickness wasn't a safety margin; it was the remaining buffer before complete integration.

Mercer sealed the bore hole with *wall of force*, creating an invisible, impermeable barrier. "It lasts one hour—enough time for us to retreat and for Grimhold's engineers to create a permanent seal using [[Korathan's scaling runes]]."

But as the wall completed, something changed in the translucent stone below. The shapes stopped their slow drifting. They coalesced. They pressed upward against the barrier, not with force, but with presence. The stone itself seemed to warm, the inner light intensifying.

Cora's monitoring crystal spiked. "Reality distortion increasing. The Sleeper's awareness is focusing on this location. The wall of force will hold, but... it knows we're here now. It will remember this place."

---

## The Ascent and Aftermath

The ascent back to the forward camp was a silent, tense climb. They emerged into familiar space that now felt like a fragile sanctuary rather than a forward operating base. The containment runes on the walls glowed with their usual steady light, but now the party saw them differently: not as protective barriers, but as a language being studied and inverted by the entity below.

Cora administered medical treatment, setting her cracked ribs and running diagnostic scans for psychic contamination. The sustained note from below had stopped vibrating through the stone, but its memory lingered in their bones.

Garrick secured the access shaft with salvaged dwarven iron. "It won't hold against a determined assault, but it'll slow anything down. And we'll hear it coming."

Mercer transcribed his final observations, his journal filling with diagrams of the crystallized reality and tactical assessments. "We need to get this to [[Reyna Steelforge]] immediately. The [[The Wardens|Wardens]]' entire defensive paradigm is based on containing a breach. But there is no breach coming. There is transformation happening."

As they prepared to ascend further to the Cold Forge and then to Grimhold, Cora looked at the monitoring crystal's final recording—the shapes moving in translucent stone, the reality distortion field expanding, the Sleeper's attention focusing.

"We accomplished the mission parameters," she said, her voice clinical but with an undercurrent of something deeper. "Staging ground destroyed. Drill eliminated. Intelligence gathered. But we've also changed the equation. The Sleeper knows we exist now. It knows we can interfere. It will adapt."

Garrick hefted his maul, the runes still glowing faintly. "Let it adapt. We'll adapt faster."

Mercer closed his journal, the sound definitive in the quiet chamber. "We have won a battle. The war is below us, and it has been going on for fifty thousand years. We are the newest soldiers. We will not be the last."

*Chronicler's Epilogue: The party returned to Grimhold with intelligence that fundamentally alters our understanding of the containment threat. The Sleeper does not seek to break through—it seeks to transform what contains it. This revelation demands a complete reevaluation of defensive strategies, resource allocation, and perhaps even our understanding of reality itself. The mountain's secret is no longer just a mining town's problem. It is a threat to existence as we know it.*
