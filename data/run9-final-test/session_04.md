# Session 4: The Amalgamation's Testament

*From the Chronicles of the Salt-Vaults Expedition*

**The Weight of Two Centuries**

The air in the mountain fortress had changed in the three days of rest. Where once there had been only the silence of abandonment, now there was the organized hum of activity—workers hauling preserved stores to the surface kitchens, the [[Stone-Tenders]] maintaining their vigil over the sealed [[Earth-Shaper chamber]], and the three [[Brotherhood]] [[Warden Aldric|Wardens]] standing their eternal watch in the interstitial corridor. But beneath this new order, a deeper tension simmered.

In the preparation kitchen that served as their command center, [[Brenna]] stood over maps spread across a makeshift table. "The workforce is stable," she said, her voice carrying the weariness of command. "We have food, shelter, patched walls. But we have two sealed doors and thirty people who need to know whether we're staying or going deeper."

She tapped two locations marked in charcoal: the [[Earth-Shaper chamber]], already breached and contained but still humming with cognitive hazard, and the [[Grand Archive]], forty feet down the corridor behind a door inscribed with a closed eye and the words *The Grand Archive. Entry by rank only. The Amalgamation watches.*

[[Warden Aldric]] stepped forward, his armor creaking with preserved leather. "The Grand Archive contains centuries of Brotherhood scholarship," he said, his voice formal and distant. "Military doctrine, giant runes, fragments of the deep aura. Brother Cassen believed it was the most significant discovery of the age." He paused, the weight of two centuries in his hesitation. "The Amalgamation has guarded it since the Brotherhood left. It follows its last orders: destroy unauthorized intruders. The insignias open the door. They do not grant permission."

From the corridor, the other two Wardens watched silently. [[Warden Petra]]'s hand rested on her sword hilt. [[Warden Cassen]] stared at nothing, his mind still partly in the preservation field that had held him for two hundred years.

The air from the Archive corridor carried a faint scent—old ink, old leather, and something else beneath it. A warm, animal presence. Something that had been sleeping in the dark for a very, very long time.

---

**The Ritual of Three as One**

[[Cora Flint]] pulled out her ledger, her mind already calculating variables. "What constitutes 'authorization'?" she asked [[Warden Aldric]]. "Is there a test? A password? Or does it just attack anything that walks through that door?"

[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] raised a hand, his magical quill already conjured and hovering. "The inscription reads 'Entry by rank only.' This suggests a hierarchical system, not mere destruction. The Brotherhood was a military order—they would have protocols."

But [[Garrick Kade]] hefted his maul, his patience for analysis wearing thin. "Enough talk. We've got the keys, we know what's inside, and it's going to attack us. That's all I need to know."

The debate continued until Mercer pieced together the critical insight. "Three Scholars could act with the authority of a Commander if they agreed," he said, his eyes distant with historical recall. "That's the test. Three as one."

[[Warden Aldric]] confirmed it with the first crack in his two-century stoicism. "Three Scholars would stand before the door, each holding their insignia. They would speak in unison: *'By the authority of the Brotherhood, we convene. Three as one, we seek entry.'* Then they would place their insignias simultaneously." He looked at each of them, his ancient eyes assessing. "But it wasn't just the words. It was the intent. The Amalgamation... it senses more than sound. It senses purpose."

From the corridor, [[Warden Petra]] added, her voice sharp: "If your purpose is to loot, to destroy, to claim what isn't yours—it will know. And it will destroy you."

The warm animal scent from under the door seemed to pulse now, rhythmic, like breathing. Something was definitely aware of them on the other side. Waiting. Listening.

---

**The Guardian Awakens**

They stood before the door, three brass insignias in hand. [[Garrick Kade]] counted down: "One... two... three."

They spoke together: "By the authority of the Brotherhood, we convene. Three as one, we seek entry."

The insignias clicked into place. The arcane lock released with a sound like a held breath finally exhaled. The door swung inward.

Cold, preserved air rushed out. [[Garrick Kade]] charged through, maul raised, crossing into an antechamber thirty feet by twenty. [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]]'s Light spell illuminated the space, but its effect was immediately halved by magical darkness woven into the stone itself.

From the archway at the far end, something moved.

It was massive, wrong-proportioned, a shape made of too many bodies arranged into one form. [[The Amalgamation]] filled the archway, its face featureless, its limbs thick with preserved flesh and sinew. It moved with shocking speed for something so large, closing the distance to [[Garrick Kade]] in two heavy steps that made the stone floor tremble.

Its arm—thick as a tree trunk, ending in a blunt, heavy fist—swung in a wide arc. [[Garrick Kade]] ducked under the blow at the last moment, feeling the air rush past his head. The creature's fist struck stone instead of chain mail, sending chips of ancient masonry flying.

"Good work with the webbing!" [[Garrick Kade]] grunted as [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]]'s spell erupted around the creature. Sticky white strands wrapped around its legs, torso, arms—anchoring it to the stone floor. The Amalgamation struggled, but the magical webbing held fast. It was restrained, trapped in place.

[[Cora Flint]]'s eyes scanned the restrained creature. "The joints!" she shouted, hurling a sensory-shock vial. "Aim for the fusion points between bodies!" The glass shattered against the creature's torso where two preserved bodies met, crackling energy disrupting the necromantic bindings.

[[Garrick Kade]] didn't hesitate. He saw his opening—the creature restrained, Cora's vial disrupting its structure. He roared and charged forward, his maul swinging in a massive overhead arc aimed precisely for one of those fused joints.

---

**A Living Archive Unraveling**

But even as they attacked, [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] saw something more. "Remarkable preservation!" he called out, maintaining concentration on the Web spell. "The bodies are arranged with ritual symmetry—this isn't just a guardian, it's a reliquary given form!"

His analysis revealed the truth: [[The Amalgamation]] was a memory construct. The preserved bodies weren't haphazardly fused—they were arranged in a pattern, a ritual symmetry. The right side showed Brotherhood soldiers (military knowledge), the left side scholars (arcane theory), and the center historians (records). A central control nexus pulsed with geometric energy, coordinating all three knowledge streams.

"We're not just fighting a guardian," [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] shouted, his voice rising with academic excitement despite the danger. "We're fighting the Brotherhood's last will and testament, given form and purpose!"

[[Cora Flint]] saw it too. "There!" she pointed to the central torso. "That's the control node! Mercer's right—it's a memory lattice!" She hurled another sensory-shock vial, aiming for that pulsing geometric pattern.

[[Garrick Kade]]'s maul followed an instant later. He didn't aim for maximum destruction—he aimed for precision, striking exactly where Cora pointed. The blow connected with a sickening *crunch* of preserved flesh and something harder beneath, like bone or crystal.

The effect was immediate. The central nexus flickered erratically. The three knowledge streams—visible now as faint glowing lines—began to destabilize. The military stream glowed brighter, then dimmed. The arcane stream crackled with uncontrolled energy. The historical stream pulsed irregularly.

From the doorway, [[Warden Aldric]] shouted, "It's destabilizing! The balance is breaking!"

[[The Amalgamation]] let out a sound—not a roar, not a scream, but a deep, resonant *hum* that vibrated through the stone floor. The sound was mechanical, ancient, and filled with something that might be pain if the creature could feel pain.

---

**Cascade Failure**

The cascade failure reached its climax. The central nexus went completely dark. The three knowledge streams flared with intense light, then began to separate physically as well as magically. The military stream bodies glowed red-hot, then pulled away from the main mass. The arcane stream bodies crackled with blue energy as they separated. The historical stream bodies pulsed with soft golden light, then began to drift apart.

[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] released his concentration on both spells—the Web holding the creature and the Feather Fall he'd cast to cushion the falling bodies. The preserved Brotherhood members settled gently to the stone floor, seventeen in total: six military, six arcane, five historical.

[[The Amalgamation]]'s main structure let out that final, strained *hum*, which rose in pitch until it became a high-frequency whine. Then silence.

The creature stopped moving. The magical darkness in the room began to fade—slowly, from the edges inward, as if whatever was generating it had finally stopped.

From the doorway, [[Warden Aldric]] stepped forward. "It's over," he said quietly. "The Amalgamation has fallen."

At the center of where the creature stood, something pulsed with faint blue light: a geometric crystal the size of a fist, warm to the touch even from a distance. The [[Amalgamation Core]].

---

**The Archive Revealed**

As the magical darkness faded, the Central Hall of the [[Grand Archive]] was revealed—and with it, a story written in damage and decay.

The Hall showed extensive destruction: shelving units ripped from walls, stone benches overturned, personal items scattered and ground under something heavy. Massive claw marks, four inches deep, gouged the stone floor at regular intervals. And in the center of the Hall, a dark stain spread in a wide radius—not blood, but something older, with a viscous, sticky texture and faint metallic smell.

[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] analyzed the damage patterns. "This wasn't combat," he said quietly. "Look at the ink pots—scattered but not shattered. The quills—broken as if stepped on. This was panic. Sudden, overwhelming panic."

One more detail: a single Brotherhood body lay in the middle of the Hall, partially preserved like the others. This one wore Commander-rank insignia. It showed no visible wounds. It simply lay there, as if it had sat down and did not get up. And one finger pointed subtly toward a heavier, more secure door at the far end of the Hall—the [[Restricted Vault]].

[[Warden Aldric]] confirmed their suspicions. "The Vault contained sensitive materials. Research into the [[deep resonance]]. Emergency protocols. And countermeasures. If there was a containment breach, the Commander would have been trying to reach the countermeasures. Not to flee. To contain."

As he spoke, the preservation fields failed completely. The [[Amalgamation Core]] flickered wildly, then went dark. With a soft *pop* of releasing energy, the preservation that had held the Archive for two centuries collapsed.

The effect was immediate: the seventeen preserved bodies began to decay visibly, reduced to skeletons in rotting robes within moments. From the East Wing came a cascade of crumbling sounds—shelves collapsing, books turning to dust. The air grew warm and humid.

And the dark stain on the Central Hall floor began to shimmer. To move.

---

**The Door Unlocks**

[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]]'s hands moved over the combination dial on the [[Restricted Vault]] door. "The glyphs aren't just dates—they're event markers! The 'Breach Containment' glyph!" He aligned the symbols with practiced precision.

The final glyph clicked into place with a definitive *thunk*. The lock mechanism disengaged.

The Vault door released with a deep, resonant *whoosh* of air equalizing. But as it unlocked, the dark stain on the floor reacted. The shimmering intensified into visible rippling. The faint hum grew louder, rising to a high-frequency whine that set teeth on edge. The stain began to spread more quickly, tendrils of darkness reaching toward the party.

[[Garrick Kade]] didn't hesitate. As the dark stain spread toward them, he planted his feet and shoved the Vault door open with his shoulder. The heavy stone ground inward, revealing darkness beyond.

[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] tossed a Light coin through the doorway. The illumination revealed:

Workbenches covered with alchemical apparatus, all perfectly preserved. Sealed cabinets labeled *"Cognitive Stabilizers," "Memory Fortification Elixirs," "Resonance Dampeners."* A central containment unit—a crystalline cylinder now shattered, with dark, viscous substance spilled and dried around it. The same substance as the stain outside.

Three Brotherhood bodies, preserved but seated at workbenches, hands still on instruments as if working when they... stopped.

A logbook open on the central workbench, the final entry unfinished.

And on the far wall, another door. Different—not Brotherhood construction. Older. Heavier. With proportions that felt wrong to human eyes. And slightly ajar.

Outside, the dark stain continued to spread, its hum growing louder. Tendrils of darkness reached toward the open Vault door, as if drawn to it. As if whatever was contained in the shattered cylinder wanted to return to its source.

The cold, damp draft from the West Wing grew stronger, carrying with it the unmistakable sound of dripping water. And something else. A soft, rhythmic scraping. Like stone on stone.

---

**What Waits in the Dark**

The party stood at a critical juncture. The countermeasures were here, labeled and preserved. The source of the containment breach lay broken on the floor. The dark stain reached for the open doorway. The West Wing breathed cold, damp air with strange sounds.

And on the far wall of the Vault, an older, heavier door stood slightly ajar, leading to something the Brotherhood had been studying before everything went wrong.

[[Warden Aldric]] looked at the shattered cylinder, then at the spreading stain. "The containment breach," he said quietly. "They were studying it. Something went wrong."

[[Cora Flint]] watched the tendrils of darkness approaching. "We have countermeasures. But that stain is getting closer. We need to decide: use them on whatever that is, or seal this door and retreat."

The hum rose to a piercing whine. The Archive decayed around them. Time, as always in the depths of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], was running out.

*What the Brotherhood contained has waited two hundred years for release. What they feared has outlasted them. And now it reaches for those who would claim their legacy.*
