The **Earth-Shaper** is a cognitively active, crystalline phenomenon located deep beneath the [[Salt-Vaults]] that alters perception and reality through resonant patterns.

## Nature and Effects
This phenomenon manifests in a chamber where crystalline structures emit patterns that warp sensory input and cognitive processes. It is classified as a significant psychic and perceptual hazard.

## History and Containment
The chamber housing the Earth-Shaper was discovered and subsequently sealed by the [[Secretive Brotherhood]], who deemed it 'cognitively hazardous.' Three missing workers were later rescued from its influence, confirming the active and dangerous nature of the phenomenon.
