**The Anomaly** is a vast, ancient source of void energy and reality-warping power contained deep beneath the [[Giant Capstone]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. It is the source of the corrupting psychic broadcast and the void energy that threatens the mountain's containment architecture. Its physical manifestation within the containment vessel is a roughly spherical form, approximately fifty feet in diameter, located at the center of the [[Caldera]].

## Nature
The energy emitted by the Anomaly is a concentrated, psychically active radiation. Its presence warps local physics, creating gravity distortions and zero-gravity zones. The Anomaly is not malevolent in intent; its mere existence in physical reality is incompatible with local physical laws, causing automatic, unintentional reality distortion.

## Containment
The primary purpose of the [[Giant Empire|Giant]]-built containment vessel is to seal and suppress the Anomaly. The containment system functions as a translation or compatibility layer, converting the Anomaly's raw, reality-warping emissions into manageable frequencies such as [[Dreamstone]] radiation and psychic broadcasts. Breaches in this containment allow its energy to flood into upper layers, causing corruption and structural instability.

## Physical Location
The Anomaly resides at the center of the [[Caldera]], a massive cylindrical void beneath the capstone. It is monitored from the [[Observation Platform]] and is surrounded by a zero-gravity environment. The containment field is concentrated at four [[Anchor Points]] set into the Caldera walls.
