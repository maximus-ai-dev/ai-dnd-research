The **Suppression Field** is a containment lattice generated within the [[Caldera]] by activating its four anchor points.

## Function and Activation
The field is created when the massive iron anchor points on the Caldera wall are activated using scaling runes and the [[Monitoring Crystal]]. When active, golden beams of containment energy extend from each anchor to converge on the Sleeper's sphere, forming a lattice of suppression energy.

## Effects
Within the suppression field, void-corrupted entities, including the [[Avatar of the Slumber]], are weakened. The field imposes disadvantage on such entities' attack rolls and saving throws, partially suppresses the Sleeper's broadcast, and slows the boundary's defensive responses. When at full strength, it significantly hinders the Avatar's capabilities, including its ability to heal by drawing energy from the sphere.
