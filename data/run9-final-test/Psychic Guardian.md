The **Psychic Guardian** is an ancient, contract-bound entity concerned with maintaining balance and enforcing containment protocols within the depths of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Nature and Communication
The Guardian does not manifest physically but communicates indirectly through the stone-shaping drones known as the [[Stone-Tenders]], using binary clicking pulses to convey its will. It is aware of the surrounding structures, including the [[Secretive Brotherhood]]'s sealed corridor.

## Objectives and Behavior
The entity is not overtly hostile but is inflexible and literal regarding the terms of its ancient agreements. Its primary concern is the preservation of containment and the prevention of uncontrolled releases from the mountain's depths.
