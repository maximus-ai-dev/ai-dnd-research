The **Eastern Transit Hub** is a major junction and transfer point within the dwarven [[Minecart Network]], located east of the [[Cold Forge]] complex.

## Description
The hub is a large, natural cavern that was engineered by the dwarves of [[Kazad-Khrom]] to facilitate ore transfer. It features four parallel rail lines leading to loading docks carved into the eastern wall, along with loading cranes and a control room that overlooks the chamber.

## Condition and History
The hub was severely damaged and effectively sealed for decades after becoming the territory of the behir [[Kellashen]]. Loading cranes were toppled, minecarts were overturned, and debris blocked the rail lines. Dwarven operational logs found in the control room indicate the hub was sealed by order of Foreman Kellak after the "apex predator" was confirmed, and a request was sent to the [[Deepwatch Garrison]] for a hunter team—a request that evidently went unanswered.

Scattered throughout the hub are signs of violent conflict, including bones of cave fauna, a section of partially melted dwarven armor, and widespread scorch marks from lightning strikes. The arrangement of some debris suggests deliberate defensive constructions, possibly erected by dwarves before the hub's fall.

## Current Status
Following the defeat of Kellashen, the Eastern Transit Hub has been secured. One rail line has been cleared, revealing a functional switching mechanism. The hub's reactivation is critical for restoring material transport from the deeper mining operations to the Cold Forge and for accessing routes that lead toward other key locations, such as the [[Pump Station East]].
