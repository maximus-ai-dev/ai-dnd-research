**Kregg** is the cruel former leader of the [[Emberfell Rejects]], a bandit group that operated in the [[Starmetal Hills]].

## Background and Demeanor
A heavyset, brutal man originally from the distant land of Emberfell, Kregg ruled his band through fear and intimidation. He shares a violent past with the adventurer [[Garrick Kade]], both having been part of the same criminal network in their earlier years.

## Fate
Kregg was decisively defeated and captured at his fortified camp on the peak of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. He was bound and prepared for transport back to [[Grimhold]] to face justice for his banditry.
