The **Secretive Brotherhood** was a faction of human mages and scholars that occupied the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] complex after the fall of the [[Iron Warlords]], approximately 150-200 years ago.

## Practices and Legacy
The Brotherhood is known for its expertise in preservation magic and complex arcane locks. They constructed a concealed interstitial corridor and the sealed [[Grand Archive]] within the mountain.

## Disappearance and Guardians
For unknown reasons, the Brotherhood abandoned the site. They left behind three of their members placed in magical stasis to guard the archive corridor. The current status and whereabouts of the Brotherhood as a whole are unknown.
