**Kregg's +1 Dagger** is a magically enhanced weapon of fine craftsmanship. The dagger is a well-made blade that glows with a faint, persistent magical light. It was the personal weapon of [[Kregg]], the cruel leader of the [[Emberfell Rejects]]. Following his defeat and capture at the fortress on the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], the dagger was claimed as loot by the adventuring party.
