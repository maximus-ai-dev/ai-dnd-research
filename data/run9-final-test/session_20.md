# Session 20: The Boundary's Immune Response

*From the personal chronicles of Thaddeus Mercer, recovered from the Caldera deployment site with psychic scorch marks and bloodstains still visible on the parchment. The handwriting grows increasingly erratic as the account progresses, culminating in a final entry written with a spectral quill while the author lay at death's door.*

---

The [[Dreamstone Seal]] rested on the [[Cold Forge]]'s anvil, a perfect sphere of crystalline containment the size of a human head. For three days it had sat there—finished, complete, masterwork. The culmination of everything we had learned, fought for, and built across nineteen adventures. The mountain was quiet in a way it hadn't been since we first descended. The industrial sounds from the deep minecart network had ceased. The [[Twisted]] had withdrawn. Even the broadcast from below felt... expectant. Waiting.

We stood gathered around the anvil—[[Cora Flint]]'s hands still calloused from the final hours of forging, [[Garrick Kade]] standing watch at the chamber entrance with his +3 Runic Maul leaning against the stone doorframe, and myself with my Codex lying open on a nearby table, its final pages waiting to be filled. The Seal was ready. The staging position in the [[Caldera]] was secured. The anchor points were mapped. There was one task remaining: carry the Dreamstone Seal to the sphere's boundary and deploy it.

The party successfully transported the Dreamstone Seal to the Caldera deployment point.

The air itself shattered before we could fully comprehend what was happening. A wave of boundary energy—force and necrotic power blended into a single, devastating pulse—exploded outward from a coalescing form. The [[Avatar of the Slumber]] manifested in the center of the chamber, fifty feet of crystallized void energy, its form shifting between Giant, dwarf, and human features, its eyes burning spheres of broadcast energy. It didn't speak. It didn't need to. Its presence was the broadcast made flesh, and it had one purpose: prevent the Seal from reaching the boundary.

The Boundary Wave hit all three of us simultaneously, slamming us against the stone walls with bone-crushing force. I took the full brunt—40 damage. and searing into my very being. Cora and Garrick fared little better. Tools clattered from workbenches. The Dreamstone Seal on the anvil glowed brighter, its containment runes flaring in response to the boundary energy.

"Garrick, front line! Mercer, shield the Seal!" Cora barked, already calculating. Her artificer's mind worked with terrifying efficiency even under assault.

I pushed myself off the wall, my hands going to my spellbook. "Structural damage assessment! The Seal appears intact—the containment runes are still pulsing at the correct frequency!" My academic training took over despite the danger. "This entity isn't biological—it's a localized reality breach! We need to disrupt its connection to the boundary, not just attack its form!"

Garrick didn't need theory. He grabbed his maul, activated Giant's Might, and charged. His body surged with power as he grew to Large size, his +3 Runic Maul becoming a blur of motion. Four devastating strikes landed with thunderous force. The entity's form shuddered, cracks appearing in its surface.

I followed with arcane precision. "**Disintegrate!**" The green ray lanced out, dealing significant force damage. The Avatar staggered, visibly wounded now, but far from defeated.

The battle became a desperate dance of violence and strategy. Cora cast **Haste** on Garrick, enhancing his speed and reflexes. I attempted containment with **Forcecage**, only to watch in horror as the Avatar used **Legendary Resistance** to shatter the invisible prison on contact. This was no ordinary enemy—it was the boundary's immune response given form, and it operated by rules we were only beginning to understand.

Then it turned its burning broadcast eyes on Cora.

A beam of pure dream energy lanced out—Dream Gaze. Cora's Wisdom save failed. She vanished, pulled into the Sleeper's dreamscape. The Dreamstone Seal clattered to the stone floor.

The Avatar immediately used **Boundary Repair**, drawing energy from the sphere itself to heal thirty points of damage. Its form grew more solid even as we damaged it.

Garrick reacted with fury, landing multiple crushing blows. But the entity was adapting, learning from our attacks even as we fought it.

I scooped up the fallen Seal. "**Misty Step!**" I teleported toward the chamber exit, then cast **Wall of Force** to block the Avatar's path. The invisible barrier snapped into existence between the entity and our escape route. "This should buy us time to retreat to the Caldera!"

The Avatar flexed—and the Wall of Force shattered like glass. Another Legendary Resistance used. It was between us and the exit, and its eyes now fixed on me.

I cast a spell to transport us to the Caldera.

Reality bent. The arcane energy of the ninth-level spell surged through the chamber, wrapping around me, Garrick, and the Dreamstone Seal. The world dissolved into shimmering light. We arrived at the Caldera deployment point.

The zero-gravity expanse of the Giant containment vessel surrounded us. We were at the deployment point—fifty feet from the Sleeper's sphere, at our established staging position on the [[Observation Platform]]. The sphere pulsed before us, its boundary smooth and undisturbed. The [[Deep Things]] drifted in peaceful orbits. The containment anchor points—four massive iron rings on the Caldera wall—awaited activation.

But we weren't alone.

The air shimmered fifty feet away. The Avatar of the Slumber manifested in the Caldera. It was here. The boundary's immune response had followed us—or rather, it could manifest anywhere the boundary existed.

The battle resumed in the zero-gravity expanse, now with the added complication of navigating the Caldera's vast space. Garrick charged, his maul connecting with devastating force. I attempted to activate the northern anchor point, placing the [[Monitoring Crystal]] into the mechanism. The northern anchor point began to activate.

The Avatar saw what was happening and adapted its tactics. It turned its Dream Gaze on me.

My Wisdom save failed. I was pulled into the dreamscape. I was trapped in the dreamscape, just as Cora had been. The Seal slipped from my hands, floating free in the zero-gravity expanse.

From within the dreamscape, I was aware of Cora's presence. She attempted to understand the dreamscape patterns., her artificer's mind working even in this alien reality. "If we disrupt them from here, we weaken it for Garrick!"

We focused on the dream-patterns, attempting to interfere with the energy channels feeding the Avatar from the boundary. We attempted to perceive patterns within the dreamscape.

Meanwhile, in the physical realm, Garrick fought alone. He grabbed the floating Seal, dashed to the northern anchor point, and began the activation sequence. The northern anchor point activation progressed.

The Avatar turned its full attention on him. Void tendrils wrapped around the fighter—Void Grasp. Garrick was grappled, blinded, and deafened to the physical world, taking psychic damage each moment he remained trapped.

Cora returned from the dreamscape just in time to see the crisis. "Healing Word!" she shouted, magical energy surging into Garrick. Then she used **Flash of Genius**, her artificer's brilliance cutting through the psychic fog. "Use your strength! Now!"

Garrick channeled all his might into breaking free. With a roar that echoed through the Caldera, he shattered the void tendrils.

I returned next, appearing just as Cora went down from a Dream Slam—unconscious, dying at 1 HP. The situation was catastrophic. Garrick was the only one standing against an entity that had taken over 500 points of damage and was still functional.

I cast a powerful spell. Cora stabilized at 1 HP, and all three of us—Cora, myself, and the Seal—were instantly transported to the northern anchor point. I began the activation sequence, my fingers tracing the ancient Giant runes. The northern anchor point activation was completed.

The Avatar struck me with a Dream Slam. I was thrown back through the zero-gravity expanse, reduced to 11 out of 102 HP. Critically wounded, blood floating around me in crimson droplets.

Garrick returned from his brief imprisonment in the dreamscape. He charged, his maul swinging. I teleported to the eastern anchor point. "Garrick, keep it occupied! I'm activating the eastern anchor point!"

The Avatar flew toward me, ignoring Garrick's challenge. It was thirty feet away and closing. I was at 11 HP—one solid hit would kill me. The eastern anchor point activation was in progress but not yet complete.

I raised trembling hands. "**Shield!**" My AC increased to 20. Then I turned to the activation mechanism. "Academic observation: The anchor points are designed for redundancy! Manual activation is possible with sufficient arcane knowledge!"

I successfully activated the eastern anchor point.

The Avatar was upon me. Void tendrils wrapped around me—Void Grasp. I was grappled, taking psychic damage, blinded and deafened. But I invoked **One with the Word**, sacrificing a fifth-level spell slot to remain at 1 HP instead of falling unconscious.

The psychic agony was beyond anything I had ever experienced—a white-hot lance of pure dream-energy tearing through my consciousness. But I held on. "**Telekinesis!**" I cast the spell, my mind gripping the crystallized void form with invisible force.

Then I did something profoundly reckless. Instead of trying to break free, I reversed the Telekinesis—I pulled the Avatar toward me, wrapping the void tendrils tighter around myself while anchoring us both in place with pure force of will. "Academic observation!" I shouted through gritted teeth. "The entity is now grappled as well! It cannot pursue you!"

I telekinetically hurled the Monitoring Crystal toward Garrick. "Take it to the southern anchor point! Complete the suppression field!"

Garrick activated the southern anchor point.

The Avatar and I were locked together—wizard and void entity, scholar and immune response. Each round, it dealt psychic damage that would kill me. Each round, I sacrificed another spell slot to remain at 1 HP. Fourth-level slot. Third-level slot. The architecture of my mind was being dismantled piece by piece to pay this terrible toll.

The suppression field reached maximum strength. The entire Caldera shimmered with containment energy. The Avatar's healing was nullified. Disadvantage on all checks. Boundary Wave unavailable.

I had one second-level spell slot remaining. One more sacrifice. After that... no more One with the Word.

"Garrick!" I gasped, blood bubbling on my lips. "The suppression field is at 100%! The entity cannot heal! Strike now—not at the tendrils, but at its core! Shatter its crystalline form!"

I intensified the Telekinesis, holding the Avatar perfectly still, presenting its cracked and flickering form as a stationary target. "Now, Garrick! For Laternia! For the university! For everything we came here to prove!"

The Avatar's void tendrils tightened. Psychic damage—14 points. I invoked One with the Word for the last time, sacrificing my final second-level spell slot. Blood flowed freely now, a crimson nebula around my head. I had no spell slots left. The next psychic assault would kill me.

Garrick charged with everything he had. His +3 Runic Maul blazed with golden containment energy as he swung with all his might.

Garrick landed a critical hit.

The maul connected with thunderous force, striking the Avatar's cracked crystalline form at its weakest point. The containment runes blazed brighter than ever before, channeling the full power of the 100% suppression field.

The Avatar's form shattered.

Crystalline void energy exploded outward in a silent cascade of fading light. The void tendrils holding me dissolved into motes of darkness that drifted apart and vanished. The burning broadcast eyes dimmed, then extinguished. For one moment, the outline of the Giant it was shaped from was visible—[[Korathan]]'s silhouette, the architect, watching from within the dream. Then it was gone.

The Avatar of the Slumber was destroyed.

I floated free, unconscious but alive at 1 HP, stabilized by my final sacrifice. Garrick caught me, holding my limp form. The suppression field was fully active.

Cora administered Experimental Elixirs—Restorative Reagents giving us temporary hit points, clearing the last psychic residue. She retrieved the Dreamstone Seal from the northern anchor point. It was warm in her hands, the containment runes pulsing in perfect harmony with the suppression field's golden lattice.

She moved toward the deployment point. The Deep Things drifted around her, their forms shimmering peacefully. Garrick stood guard, his maul still glowing with residual containment energy.

Cora reached the deployment point. The sphere's boundary was before her—a smooth, translucent membrane through which the [[First World]] was visible, a reality of crystalline structures and shifting light. The suppression field's golden beams connected to it at three points, holding it steady, weakened, ready.

She raised the Dreamstone Seal. The containment runes blazed brighter, responding to the proximity of the boundary they were forged to reinforce. The entire Caldera seemed to hold its breath.

This was the moment. The culmination of everything—from a fifty-gold bounty in [[Grimhold]] to Korathan's tomb, from the Cold Forge to this zero-gravity expanse. The Seal was ready. The boundary awaited.

Cora pressed the Dreamstone Seal against the sphere's boundary.
