**Cora Flint** is a human artificer and adventurer operating in the [[Starmetal Hills]], serving as her party's engineer, field medic, and quartermaster.

## Personality and Methods
Cora is a profoundly practical and transactional individual who treats every expedition as a calculated business venture. She maintains a detailed ledger of all expenses, loot values, and debts owed. Her approach to healing is efficient and devoid of bedside manner, frequently reminding allies that the cost "is coming out of your share." She searches rooms with systematic thoroughness and insists on meticulous planning before entering dangerous areas.

## Combat Style and Abilities
She fights from range using alchemical preparations, acid vials, and Artificer infusions. Cora supports allies with healing and battlefield control, prioritizing tactical efficiency and resource management over heroics.

## Key Relationships
She respects [[Garrick Kade]]'s combat prowess but is frequently frustrated by his recklessness. She values [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]]'s knowledge but exhibits impatience with his lengthy academic lectures. They share a foundation of pragmatic, mutual respect.
