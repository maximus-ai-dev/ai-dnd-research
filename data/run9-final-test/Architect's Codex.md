The **Architect's Codex** is a comprehensive tome containing the original blueprints, runic schematics, and theoretical frameworks for the [[Giant]] containment architecture within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Contents
The codex details the design of the containment vessel, the function of containment runes, the layout of guardian stations, and the principles behind the scaling techniques used to adapt Giant-sized rune work for smaller crafts. It serves as the definitive technical reference for the mountain's deepest secrets and the nature of the [[First World Sleeper]].

## Use and Significance
[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] consults the Codex as the authoritative source for analyzing containment failures, understanding the mountain's systems, and devising solutions. It was instrumental in providing the technical framework for the [[Dreamstone Seal]], granting Mercer advantage on the Arcana checks required to inscribe the Seal's complex rune matrix. The Codex's containment theory was the foundational knowledge upon which the Seal's design was based.
