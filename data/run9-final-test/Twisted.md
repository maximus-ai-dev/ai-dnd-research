The **Twisted** are a corrupted faction operating within the depths of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Origin
They are dwarves who have been physically and mentally altered by prolonged exposure to the psychic broadcast of [[The Anomaly]].

## Characteristics
The Twisted engage in grotesque bioengineering, merging organic material with dwarven mechanical systems. Their structures and technology are characterized by black resin and fused biological components. They infiltrate and repurpose ancient dwarven and Giant infrastructure, building networks within existing tunnels.

## Goals
Their primary objective is to breach the ancient [[Giant Empire|Giant]] containment seals that separate the mountain's upper layers from [[The Anomaly]]. They employ organized patrols, overseers, and sophisticated drilling technology to achieve this, representing a persistent and organized threat to the mountain's stability.
