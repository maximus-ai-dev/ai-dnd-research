**Reyna Steelforge** is the leader of the [[Prospectors' Guild]] and the de facto authority in the frontier mining town of [[Grimhold]]. She is a pragmatic academic who views the exploration of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] as both a strategic opportunity and a profound historical study. As head of the guild, Reyna Steelforge regulates mining claims, organizes security, and represents the town's interests. After learning the true nature and timescale of the [[Giant containment vessel]], her focus shifted from pure research to active support for reinforcing the ancient structure, recognizing it as an inherited obligation.
