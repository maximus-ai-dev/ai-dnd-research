**Harlan** is a grizzled, melancholic prospector and guide with extensive familiarity of the [[Starmetal Hills]].

## Demeanor and Knowledge
Harlan has spent his life traversing the hills and possesses deep knowledge of their trails and moods. He is often found in a drunken state and carries a profound, deep-seated unease about recent changes and disturbances in the region.

## Role as a Guide
He guides travelers to the first marker stone on the trail leading to the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. Harlan confirms having seen campfire smoke near the peak and consistently warns of the mountain's reputation for bad luck, strongly advising against venturing there.
