The **Dwarven Radiation Survey Notes** are a set of technical records written in Dwarvish, recovered from a sealed iron box within a mausoleum in the upper [[Dwarven Layer]].

## Contents
The notes contain a systematic survey of [[Dreamstone]] radiation levels throughout the delve and a partial map of the complex. They identify key landmarks, including the [[Cold Forge]], and note the status of various containment crypts. The notes provided the expedition from [[Grimhold]] with critical navigational data for descending to the forge level.
