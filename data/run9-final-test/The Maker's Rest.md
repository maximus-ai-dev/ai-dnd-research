**The Maker's Rest** is the tomb and vigil chamber of [[Korathan]], Master Runecarver of the [[Architect's Council]], located in the deepest section of the [[Giant foundation]]'s outer ring.

## Structure and Purpose
The tomb is a monumental structure featuring a forty-foot-tall obsidian door inscribed with Korathan's epitaph. Its interior is a descending corridor and chamber complex filled with traps and tests designed for Giant-scale intruders, emphasizing the alien scale of the space. The burial chamber contains Korathan in stasis and the walls are covered with his personal [[Korathan's scaling runes|Scaling Runes]].

## Function
More than a simple tomb, The Maker's Rest serves as a watchpost and a deliberate repository of knowledge. It was built as a technology transfer point, preserving the critical engineering principles needed to maintain the [[Giant containment vessel]] for future, smaller races.
