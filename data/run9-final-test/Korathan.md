**Korathan** is the Master Runecarver of the [[Architect's Council]] who designed the [[Giant containment vessel]] that contains the [[First World Sleeper]].

## History and Vigil
After spending three centuries constructing the containment vessel, Korathan voluntarily entered a state of perfect temporal stasis within his tomb, [[The Maker's Rest]], to maintain a vigil over his creation. This state, referred to in Giant epitaphs as "the long watch," represents a form of suspended animation maintained by the tomb's runes.

## Legacy and Gift
Korathan anticipated that future caretakers of the containment would be smaller than Giants. He deliberately placed his masterwork [[Korathan's scaling runes|Scaling Runes]] within his tomb as a technology transfer, providing the principles necessary to scale containment magic to human-portable size and continue the work of maintaining the vessel.
