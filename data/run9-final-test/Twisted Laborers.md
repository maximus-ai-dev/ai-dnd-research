**Twisted Laborers** are a common type of [[Twisted]] combatant and worker.

## Description
Twisted Laborers are corrupted dwarves armed with tools like pry bars or picks that serve as weapons. They maintain the Twisted's organic infrastructure and act as basic soldiers.

## Role
Laborers perform construction, maintenance, and patrol duties. They form the bulk of Twisted patrols and work crews, and are often the first line of defense when installations are threatened.
