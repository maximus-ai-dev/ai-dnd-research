The **Architect's Council** was a council of Giant master builders who oversaw the creation of the [[Giant containment vessel]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Role and Decisions
The council debated the fate of the [[First World Sleeper]], ultimately choosing containment over destruction or abandonment. [[Korathan]], the Master Runecarver who designed the vessel, was a member of this council and volunteered to oversee the project and its long-term vigil.

## Legacy
The council's work represents the pinnacle of Giant engineering and a profound long-term responsibility. Their decision to contain the Sleeper and plan for its maintenance across a hundred-thousand-year timeline has directly shaped the mountain's history and the obligations inherited by later races.
