# Session 5: The Weight of Empires

*Spring, 5th Year of the Reclamation*

Three months of peace had settled over the fortress in the [[Starmetal Hills]]. Winter's harsh grip had relented, revealing the thawing landscape below our mountain perch. The [[Grand Stairwell]] now boasted a new door of my own design — heavy timber banded with iron, fitted with a lock that clicked with satisfying precision. Thirty souls now called this peak home, their lives secured by patched walls and the steady bounty of the [[Salt-Vaults]].

Yesterday, a trader from [[Grimhold]] arrived with supplies and a sealed letter from [[Reyna Steelforge]]. Her words were characteristically efficient: *"The [[Prospectors' Guild]] has records suggesting the [[Military Empire]] layer beneath your position contains trophy halls with artifacts from every civilization they conquered — including the Giant era. If any Giant artifacts remain, they could provide critical information about what lies deeper. I request a detailed map of the lower levels."*

[[Warden Aldric]], when consulted, looked uneasy. "The [[Secretive Brotherhood]] never went deeper than the Archives," he said, his voice low. "They said the lower levels were someone else's problem. But if you're going down... be careful. The Empire built heavy. And whatever's below them has been there longer."

Now we stood before the Grand Stairwell door, gear packed, spells prepared. The air from below smelled of dust and old stone — and something else, faint and metallic, that hadn't been there three months ago.

---

**The Descent**

[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] completed his ritual, the shimmer of *Detect Magic* settling around his eyes as he peered into the darkness. "The metallic scent — sweet, coppery, with iron undertones. Not blood. Preserved metal, perhaps treated with alchemical compounds."

[[Garrick Kade]] took point, his chain mail clinking as he positioned himself before the heavy door. "Three months of peace was nice, but my maul's been collecting dust. Whatever's waiting down there, it's about to learn why the [[Emberfell Rejects]] feared me."

I made a new ledger entry: *Session 5 - Military Empire Expedition. Objective: Map lower levels, locate Giant artifacts. Payment: Guild gold/supplies.*

The descent revealed architecture on a monumental scale. The steps became wider, ceilings higher, stonework more elaborate. Carved relief panels showed soldiers in parade formation, officials in ceremonial dress, maps of territories long vanished. At the base, a landing with an iron-banded door bore the inscription: *THE IMPERIAL LOWER PALACE. LET ALL WHO ENTER SERVE THE EMPIRE'S MEMORY.*

My engineering eye noted hairline cracks radiating from the landing's center — ancient, but with signs of recent micro-movement. The metallic scent was stronger here, wafting up through the cracks.

Beyond the door lay a corridor twelve feet across, built for formations, not individuals. Brass brackets held guttered torches long burned out. To the right opened the Garrison Hall with rows of stone sleeping platforms. Ahead, the corridor stretched into shadow.

Twenty feet down, another doorway revealed a dining hall. On one table: a tray of stone cups and a sealed ceramic flask. But more concerning — just beyond, a side corridor branched off where the floor had partially collapsed. A ten-foot section had dropped two feet, stone cracked and tilted. Below the crack: darkness and the sound of moving air.

My assessment was grim: the collapse wasn't random. Dwarven vaults below this section were particularly stressed. Walking there was risky.

Then they attacked.

---

**Structural Warfare**

Three figures emerged from the side corridor — gray-skinned, black-veined, moving in jerky mechanical patterns. [[Twisted Scouts]], corrupted dwarves from the layer below. Their eyes were milky white, veins bulging with black fluid. They wielded rusty pry bars and did not negotiate.

Garrick roared, charging forward as his body swelled with Giant's Might. "Hostiles! Side corridor!"

The first pry bar clanged harmlessly off his chain mail, but the fighting took its toll on the already compromised floor. With each impact, more stone dust drifted down. The crack widened with an audible *CRACK*. From somewhere above, four floors up, came a groan of stressed masonry.

"Garrick, fall back!" Mercer shouted from behind the Garrison Hall doorway, *Mage Armor* shimmering around him. "If that floor collapses further, we could all fall through!"

But Garrick fought on, his maul crushing one scout's chest with a sickening crunch. The remaining two pressed their advantage, pry bars finding gaps in his armor. Blood soaked through his chain mail as he staggered.

Then the floor gave way.

With a deafening **CRACK**, a three-foot-wide chunk of stone near the collapse zone broke free and plummeted into darkness. The hole revealed a vertical shaft dropping into blackness — and the rhythmic scraping sound, louder now. Below, black-veined limbs climbed upward.

More were coming.

---

**The Brink of Death**

Garrick took another brutal hit, collapsing to the stone floor. "Nine left..." he gasped before darkness took him.

I cast *Healing Word*, the golden light knitting some of his wounds closed. He rose, swinging his maul weakly, but the scouts drove him back down. Pry bars slammed into his chest, cracking ribs. He collapsed again, blood pooling around him.

"Garrick's at negative nine!" I shouted. "That's fatal damage!"

Mercer's academic composure shattered. "He'll die if he takes one more hit!" He scrambled back, mind racing through spell options. *Web* was already used. *Fireball* would collapse the ceiling. *Hypnotic Pattern* required line of sight through the chaotic melee.

Then his eyes fell on the patched section of wall behind where the stone table had stood — crude Military Empire stone mortared hastily over a much older opening. His *Detect Magic* detected a faint presence behind it, the same deep aura that permeated the [[Earth-Shaper chamber]].

"Whatever's behind that seal can't be worse than what's in front of us!" he shouted, beginning the somatic components for *Shatter*.

The spell erupted with a deafening **BOOM**. The crude mortar shattered, Military Empire stonework collapsing to reveal a dark opening — dwarven construction, sealed centuries ago.

And from within came a **deep, resonant hum** that vibrated in our bones rather than our ears.

The Twisted Scouts froze. Their milky white eyes widened. The black fluid in their veins pulsed in time with the hum. They stood perfectly still, as if listening.

---

**The Resonant Chamber**

We stumbled through the newly opened passage, Garrick leaning heavily on his maul, each step sending fire through his cracked ribs. The passage was narrow, dwarven scale, walls polished smooth with intricate geometric patterns that pulsed with blue crystalline light.

Thirty feet in, the passage opened into a small chamber. On the far wall: **a control node**, identical in design to the one in the Hall of the Conquered Peoples but made of blue crystalline stone that glowed from within. One of three control nodes for the pressure valve mechanism.

Carved beside it: a vertical cross-section of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] showing all five strata, with annotations in Giant script. A structural blueprint identical to the one in the Hall of the Giants.

"This is a monitoring station," Mercer breathed, tracing the Giant runes with trembling fingers. "'When the Sleeper stirs, the mountain remembers.' The Empire sealed this chamber because they couldn't understand the technology... or because they feared what it monitored."

Garrick collapsed against the chamber wall, blood still seeping from multiple wounds. "Node... activate it," he rasped. "Complete the circuit... might stop the collapse."

I cast my last *Cure Wounds*, the magic pulling him back from death's edge. He rose, barely conscious, positioning himself at the chamber entrance. "If they come back... I'll hold the doorway."

Mercer placed his hands on the cool crystalline surface. The blue light intensified, pulsing in time with his heartbeat. The resonant hum grew louder, vibrating through stone, through bone, through air.

**The node activated.**

A deep, resonant **CLANG** echoed through the mountain — felt in the teeth rather than heard. From somewhere deep in the Giant architecture, a response hum answered. The containment network still had functioning components.

**The structural collapse stopped.** The grinding of stone ceased. Dust settled. The cascade had been arrested.

Back in the archive chamber, the Twisted Scouts recovered from their stasis — but the strengthened resonance agitated them. They let out guttural, scraping cries and **fled** back through the passage, away from the frequency.

---

**The Ascent**

Following the [[Architectural Survey of the Military Empire Layer]] we'd found preserved in the archives, we navigated to a maintenance corridor branching northeast. Eighty feet down: the **service stairwell**, Iron Warlords construction, spiraling upward.

The ascent was slow. Garrick leaned heavily on his maul, taking each step with gritted teeth. Mercer supported his other side, the architectural survey tucked under his arm. I led, checking each turn.

We emerged into familiar territory: the Iron Warlords' layer near the Salt-Vaults. The air smelled of dust and old stone, not metal and corruption. The resonant hum from below was faint here, a distant vibration.

We were safe.

Mercer unrolled the survey again, his hands trembling with academic excitement and residual fear. "We have what Reyna wanted — and more. The Giant blueprint in that chamber shows the mountain's full structure. All five strata. The containment architecture around the anomaly."

He looked at us — Garrick battered and bleeding, myself exhausted, he trembling. "We mapped the Military Empire layer. We survived a structural collapse. We activated part of the Giant containment network. And we discovered evidence that the Empire knew exactly what they were building atop."

He rolled the survey carefully. "When Garrick brings this to Grimhold on the next supply run and Reyna sees what it contains... she'll understand. This changes everything."

---

*Chronicler's Note: The expedition achieved its primary objective but at terrible cost. [[Garrick Kade]] nearly died twice, saved only by desperate healing and the discovery of ancient technology. The activation of the second control node has temporarily stabilized the mountain, but the clock is ticking. Three nodes must sing as one to quiet the dreaming stone. Two are now active. The third awaits in the Hall of the Conquered Peoples — through territory now swarming with Twisted who have felt the resonance and fled from it. What they flee toward may be worse than what they flee from.*
