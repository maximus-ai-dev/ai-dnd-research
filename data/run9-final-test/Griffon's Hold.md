**Griffon's Hold** is a sturdy and well-supplied shop in the frontier town of [[Grimhold]].

## Inventory and Clientele
The store sells weapons, armor, adventuring gear, and general supplies. It is a trusted establishment for prospectors and adventurers preparing for journeys into the [[Starmetal Hills]].

## Proprietor and Atmosphere
The store is owned and operated by the retired mercenary [[Elara Stormwind]]. Her experience, fair dealing, and knowledgeable chatter create a welcoming and reliable environment. Her shop is the primary source in Grimhold for essential cold-weather gear and expedition supplies bound for locations like the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].
