The **Grand Stairwell** is a 120-foot descent connecting the [[Secretive Brotherhood]] sanctum to the [[Military Empire]] layer within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. The upper section is of [[Iron Warlords]] construction, while the lower, more elaborate stairs and landing are Imperial work. The stairwell was secured with a new timber-and-iron door built by the surface workforce and serves as the primary access route to the deeper mountain strata.
