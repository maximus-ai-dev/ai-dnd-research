**Amplifier Beta** was one of three [[Fleshy Amplifiers]] constructed by the [[Twisted]] in the [[Giant foundation]] level.

## Location
It was located in the southeast quadrant of the Giant foundation's outer ring, approximately 600 feet from the forward camp established at the [[Bastion of the Asymmetrical]].

## Description
Amplifier Beta was built into a Giant monitoring alcove, parasitizing the alcove's resonance instruments. It was the most sophisticated of the amplifiers, having been modified multiple times with additional root networks and reinforced resin casing.

## Status
The amplifier was successfully sabotaged and destroyed, though the process was more complex due to its reinforced structure and redundant connections.
