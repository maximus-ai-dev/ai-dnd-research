**Brenna** is the pragmatic leader and spokesperson for the workforce of former [[Emberfell Rejects]] occupying the fortress on the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Appearance and Demeanor
Brenna is a scarred and direct woman who focuses on logistics, resource management, and the practical survival of her people. She exhibits a no-nonsense attitude and holds those in charge accountable for the safety of her workforce.

## Role and Actions
Following the defeat of [[Kregg]], Brenna negotiated the bandits' surrender and now organizes the legitimate workforce. During the breach in the [[Salt-Vaults]], she efficiently managed the evacuation of workers and maintained order within the fortress. Her primary concerns are the security and well-being of the people under her care.
