**Grimhold** is a frontier mining town located at the base of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] within the [[Starmetal Hills]]. It serves as the primary logistical and economic hub for expeditions into the mountain. The town's economy is driven by prospecting and mining, and it is governed de facto by the [[Prospectors' Guild]] under [[Reyna Steelforge]]. Grimhold provides essential services and its engineers and resources support operations at the [[Bastion of the Asymmetrical]] and the ongoing investigation of the [[Giant containment vessel]].
