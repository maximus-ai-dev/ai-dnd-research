A **Dreamstone Power Cell** is a concentrated energy source containing crystallized [[Dreamstone]].

## Description
The cell is a fist-sized sphere of crystallized Dreamstone, typically encased in a lead shell to contain its radiation. It emits a faint warmth and pulses with psychic energy.

## Function
These cells are used by the [[Twisted]] to power their corrupted machinery and bio-mechanical hybrids, converting the [[First World Sleeper]]'s psychic radiation into usable power. They represent a direct harnessing of the Sleeper's influence.

## Known Examples
One such power cell was recovered from the wreckage of the defeated [[Drowned Juggernaut]] at the [[Drowned Chokepoint]].
