The **Monitoring Crystal** is a magical device used to monitor the integrity of the ancient [[Giant Empire|Giant]] containment field beneath the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Function
The crystal resonates with the containment field's energy signature. It provides real-time readings on field integrity and can detect breaches, spikes in void energy, and fluctuations in the containment architecture. A severe breach causes the crystal to emit a piercing psychic scream and glow with intense light.

## Significance
The device is a crucial tool for the party, allowing them to respond rapidly to containment failures. It is also used in the activation sequence for the containment anchor points within the [[Caldera]].
