The **Observation Platform** is a [[Giant Empire|Giant]]-era structure anchored to the wall of the [[Caldera]].

## Description and Function
Constructed as a long-term monitoring station, the platform is equipped with crystalline viewing panels and ancient equipment designed to study the [[First World Sleeper]]. It is positioned within a dense array of containment runes, providing a measure of protection from the chamber's environmental hazards and the Sleeper's broadcast.

## Current Use
The party secured the Observation Platform as their forward operating base and staging position within the Caldera. It served as the workspace for the final assembly and forging of the [[Dreamstone Seal]]. The platform's fixed position and relative stability, aided by devices like the [[Reality Anchor]], made it the only viable location from which to conduct the delicate, extended crafting process required to build the Seal.
