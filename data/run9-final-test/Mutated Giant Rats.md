**Mutated Giant Rats** are oversized rodents that have been physically and behaviorally warped by long-term exposure to residual magical radiation within the sealed environment of the [[Salt-Vaults]].

## Physical Characteristics
These rats possess iridescent fur and glowing eyes, clear signs of magical mutation. They exhibit heightened aggression and size compared to their normal counterparts.

## Behavior and History
Despite their mutations, they remain simple animals driven by instinct to defend their food sources and nesting grounds. A colony of these rats, led by a large alpha, infested sections of the Salt-Vaults until the colony was exterminated.
