# Session 16: The Saboteur's Drill

**Three days after the destruction of the Twisted staging ground**

*The Cold Forge* chamber hummed with a fragile peace. [[Ignathar]]'s eternal fire cast flickering orange light across dwarven stonework, illuminating [[Garrick Kade]] as he ran a whetstone along his maul's edge, the newly-fitted [[Adamantine Chassis Plates]] from the [[Drowned Juggernaut]] gleaming in the firelight. [[Cora Flint]] organized her alchemical supplies with methodical precision, checking seals on vials of acid and fire, preparing another batch of [[Dreamstone]]-inscribed bolts. [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] pored over the [[Architect's Codex]], cross-referencing [[Korathan's scaling runes]] with containment architecture maps.

Three days of relative quiet. Three days of maintenance, preparation, and the quiet dread that comes when you know something ancient has noticed you.

Then the [[Monitoring Crystal]] screamed.

Not a reading—a scream. The crystal's resonance frequency spiked to a pitch that shattered a beaker on Cora's workbench. Glass shards skittered across stone as the crystal itself glowed with iridescent light so intense it hurt to look at. Simultaneously, throughout the [[Giant foundation]] below, containment runes flickered. The forward camp's psychic shielding wavered. And from the depths, a sound like a mountain exhaling—a rush of warm, sweet-smelling air carrying the concentrated essence of [[The Anomaly]]'s radiation.

The psychic assault hit all three simultaneously. Garrick gritted his teeth, the [[Dreamstone Circlet]] on his brow glowing hot against the assault. Cora staggered, catching herself on her workbench. Mercer clutched the edge of his table, his vision swimming with images of the void below—darkness that moved, shapes that shouldn't exist.

"Southwest quadrant," Cora reported, her voice tight as her hands moved across the crystal's surface. "Roughly six hundred feet from camp. Containment readings at the breach are zero. Not low. *Zero.*"

Mercer's eyes widened. "A third drill. They had a backup."

"What came through?" Garrick growled, already standing.

The crystal showed only the void energy signature—massive, catastrophic, and something else. Something moving. Something forty feet tall, standing at the breach's edge.

---

**THE RACE TO THE BREACH**

The journey toward the southwest quadrant was treacherous. Radiation surge zones shimmered with iridescent air where void energy had pooled. Gravity fractures created disorienting sections where "down" shifted sideways, stone debris floating in mid-air. Echo manifestations flickered at the edges of vision—momentary apparitions of Giants, dwarves, and humans who once walked these corridors, their silent, repeating motions deeply unsettling.

As they rounded the final corner toward the [[Warden's Post]], the corridor opened into the vast chamber described in the [[Giant Blueprint Tablet]]. Eighty feet in diameter, sixty-foot domed ceiling, containment rune arrays on all four walls—most of them shattered.

At the center: an open, empty sarcophagus.

At the far wall: the breach. A ragged hole forty feet across in the [[Giant Capstone]] floor, edges glowing with void energy. The containment runes around it weren't just cracked—they were shattered, as if struck by an immense force from below.

And standing at the edge of the breach, facing them, was the [[Reanimated Warden]].

Forty feet of Giant skeleton held together by threads of void energy that laced through the bones like dark tendons. Its skull was crowned with corrupted [[Dreamstone]] growths that glowed with the same iridescent light as the void below. Its eye sockets burned with the broadcast's frequency.

It turned its massive skull toward them. Regarded them. Then it spoke—not in Giant, not in any language, but in the broadcast frequency itself, the words forming directly in their minds:

*THE SEAL IS BROKEN. THE WATCHER WAKES. THE DEEP THINGS RISE.*

Then it raised one enormous fist and brought it down on the chamber floor with enough force to crack the stone for thirty feet in every direction.

The Warden's corrupted [[Dreamstone]] crown flared. A **Broadcast Scream** erupted—a sixty-foot cone of psychic energy that washed over all three of them. The assault was overwhelming: twenty-four points of psychic damage tore through each of them, and worse, the vision of the void below became irresistible. The darkness wasn't empty; it was welcoming, peaceful, a place where all questions were answered, all burdens lifted.

All three were frightened of the Warden and perceived the void as welcoming. At the end of each of their turns, they would need to resist moving toward the breach.

Garrick charged forward anyway, his Giant's Might making him Large but still dwarfed by the forty-foot skeleton. He swung his maul at the Warden's leg joints, but his attack was wild, uncoordinated—the psychic assault had shattered his focus.

The Warden responded immediately. Its colossal fist—the size of a minecart—smashed down toward Garrick. But at the last moment, Garrick's adamantine armor deflected the blow. The impact sent shockwaves through the chamber floor, cracking stone in a thirty-foot radius, but Garrick stood firm.

Mercer rose into the air on a Fly spell, already casting Banishment. The incantation flowed from his lips, but his hands shook—the psychic assault had rattled his concentration. The spell components slipped from his fingers, and the magic fizzled.

Cora positioned herself behind a section of intact containment rune array, preparing her alchemical mixture. But her hands trembled as she worked—the vision of the welcoming void kept intruding.

---

**THE WALL OF FORCE**

The Warden's colossal fist connected with Garrick with bone-crushing force. The impact sent shockwaves through the chamber, but Garrick planted his feet, braced with all his strength, and refused to be moved. Blood trickled from a split lip, but he stood firm.

Cora's *Melf's Acid Arrow* streaked across the chamber, striking the Warden's knee joint. The acid ate away at void-energy tendons, sizzling and smoking. The Warden staggered slightly.

Garrick's Action Surge unleashed a whirlwind of destruction. Three massive blows from his maul connected with the same knee Cora targeted. Bone cracked. Void-energy tendons snapped and unraveled. The Warden's leg buckled, and the forty-foot skeleton dropped to one knee with a crash that shook the entire chamber.

But Mercer's *Wall of Force* was the game-changer. As the Warden struggled to rise, a shimmering, transparent dome of pure force materialized around it. The hemispherical barrier cut the colossal guardian off from the party—and, more importantly, from the breach behind it.

For a moment, there was silence. The Warden rose to its feet inside the dome, its void-energy tendons crackling as it tested the barrier. It brought a massive fist down on the *Wall of Force*. The impact created visible ripples across the transparent surface, but the wall held.

The psychic compulsion hadn't ended. All three still perceived the void as welcoming, still felt the pull toward the breach. Mercer, floating twenty feet in the air, found himself drifting inexorably toward the breach's edge. Cora took a step forward, then another, her hands reaching toward the glowing void. Garrick shook his head, trying to clear the whispers from his mind.

Inside the dome, the Warden continued its relentless assault. Both colossal fists smashed against the transparent barrier simultaneously. The impact created massive distortion waves across the force field. The barrier shimmers violently, but held. However, the repeated impacts were taking a toll—hairline fractures appeared in the stone floor around the dome's base from the transmitted shockwaves.

---

**THE FLOOR GIVES WAY**

Mercer's *Stone Shape* spell reshaped the chamber floor beneath the *Wall of Force* dome. The stone flowed like water, creating a funnel-shaped depression that sloped toward the center. The spell revealed what lay below: a maintenance conduit about five feet down, part of the original containment system's monitoring network.

Cora placed her last [[Dreamstone]] bolt at the edge of the conduit opening. "If it tries to climb through, the resonance will disrupt its grip."

Garrick positioned himself at the edge of the damaged section, his Haste-enhanced senses tracking every crack, every tremor.

Inside the dome, the Feebleminded Warden continued its mindless assault. Both colossal fists smashed down on the same spot for what must be the hundredth time. This time, the structural damage reached critical mass.

The stone floor gave way.

With a thunderous roar, a twenty-foot section of chamber floor collapsed beneath the *Wall of Force* dome. The Warden dropped through the opening, its massive form crashing into the maintenance conduit below. The void-energy tendons snagged on the conduit's edges, and the forty-foot skeleton became partially stuck—its torso and one arm through the hole, its other arm and legs dangling in the empty space below.

The Warden was partially trapped, off-balance, and vulnerable.

---

**THE FINAL ASSAULT**

The partially trapped Warden lashed out from its position in the hole. One colossal fist connected with Garrick despite his Haste-enhanced speed. The impact sent Garrick staggering back, his injuries mounting. But he stayed on his feet.

Cora's *Melf's Acid Arrow* streaked across the chamber, striking the Warden's corrupted [[Dreamstone]] crown. The acid arrow ate away at the crystalline growths, and cracks appeared in the [[Dreamstone]] formations.

Garrick charged forward with Haste-enhanced speed. His first maul swing connected with the Warden's already-damaged knee. The impact cracked bone and severed void-energy tendons. His second swing targeted the same spot. More tendons snapped. His third swing, empowered by Haste, struck the spine. The Warden's skeletal structure groaned under the assault.

Mercer's *Telekinesis* took hold of the corrupted [[Dreamstone]] crown. The spell strained against the crown's magical anchoring, but Mercer's control was precise, powerful. The crown began to tear free from the skull, crystalline bonds cracking under the strain.

Then something gave.

With a sound like shattering crystal, the corrupted [[Dreamstone]] crown tore free from the Warden's skull. Mercer's *Telekinesis* yanked it completely loose, and the crown flew across the chamber, landing with a clatter near the breach edge.

Immediately, the void-energy tendons that laced through the Warden's bones began to unravel. They dissipated into iridescent mist, their connection to the breach severed. Without the tendons holding it together, the forty-foot skeleton lost coherence.

The Warden made one last, mindless attempt to strike Garrick, but its arm fell apart mid-swing. Bone separated from bone. The massive skeleton collapsed into a heap of disconnected Giant bones that crashed to the chamber floor with earth-shaking force.

The [[Reanimated Warden]] was destroyed.

---

**REBUILDING THE WARDEN'S FUNCTION**

The breach remained—forty feet across, edges glowing with void energy, containment runes shattered. But the party now had a potential solution: using the Warden's own bones—originally designed for this exact purpose—to create a permanent seal.

Garrick moved with Haste-enhanced speed, dragging and positioning the massive Giant bones. Skull fragments the size of carts went to the cardinal points. Limb bones with harmonic dampener runes formed an octagonal pattern between them. Spinal column segments created resonance distribution channels.

Cora worked with precision, mixing alchemical bonding agents enhanced with silver powder for conductivity. She applied the mixtures at the bone contact points, creating secure bonds that also enhanced resonance transfer.

Mercer's Arcana expertise guided the entire process. His analysis was flawless. He calculated the exact angles, the precise rune alignments, the harmonic frequencies needed for destructive interference.

The *Wall of Force* dome shimmers and vanished as its duration ended. Garrick's Haste spell faded, leaving him feeling suddenly slow, heavy. But the work was done—the bone array was in place.

Mercer completed his ritual, channeling arcane energy through the array. "The stabilization runes are activating. They're harmonizing with each other, creating an interference pattern."

The effect was immediate and visible. The bone array began to glow with a soft, steady light—not the iridescent void energy of the breach, but a warm, golden radiance that matched the original Giant containment runes. The runes on the bones flared to life, and lines of golden light connected them.

The breach responded. Its iridescent glow dimmed further. The containment field readings climbed: **9%... 12%... 18%... 25%... 34%... 47% integrity.**

Three hours passed as the party monitored the array's performance. The temporary dampening field faded, but the bone array continued to function. The containment field stabilized at **68% integrity**—not the original 100%, but far stronger than the 8% they started with. More importantly, it was stable.

The [[Warden's Post]] chamber was transformed. What was once a site of catastrophic breach and colossal combat was now a stabilized containment node. The forty-foot breach was contained by a forty-foot bone array—the Warden's own remains repurposed to fulfill its original function.

Garrick picked up a fragment of the Warden's skull—a piece of Giant bone the size of his forearm—and turned it in his hands. "It was a guardian. Like the one in the tomb. Just... broken."

Mercer nodded, consulting his spellbook where the array schematic was recorded. "It was doing what it was built to do. The corruption just pointed it in the wrong direction."

Cora looked at the new runes she helped position, comparing them to the ancient Giant originals on the chamber walls. "Ours are smaller. But they hold."

From the void below, through the newly contained breach, the broadcast was muted. [[The Sleeper]]'s note—the sustained tone they first encountered—was softer now. Not gone. Never gone. But contained, for the moment, by the work of small hands using tools a Giant left for them.

The immediate crisis was contained. The Warden was destroyed. The breach was sealed. But the deeper work—understanding the Sleeper, reinforcing the entire containment system, and preparing for whatever came next—remained.
