**Thermal Anomalies** refer to extreme and unexplained heat phenomena recorded by the dwarves of [[Kazad-Khrom]] in the deeper sections of their mining operations.

## Dwarven Records
Operational logs recovered from the [[Eastern Transit Hub]] describe increasing thermal anomalies in the eastern mining shafts, with core temperatures reportedly exceeding even forge levels. The logs indicate these phenomena required specialized "cold-forged equipment" for mining teams to safely operate. The [[Deepwatch Garrison]] reported similar heat-based threats in the lower levels, suggesting a widespread or connected source.

## Physical Evidence
Within the Eastern Transit Hub, physical evidence corroborates the logs. A section of dwarven armor was found partially melted, showing damage consistent with plasma or intense magical fire rather than lightning. The defensive barricades constructed in the hub suggest the dwarves were preparing for or responding to a heat-based threat, not merely the behir.

## Significance
The existence of these anomalies presents a significant obstacle to further exploration. They imply the presence of a persistent, heat-based danger in the deeper mines that standard equipment cannot withstand, necessitating the creation of specialized gear, likely at the Cold Forge, before safe progress can be made.
