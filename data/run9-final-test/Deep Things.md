**Deep Things** are transient, non-corporeal entities that manifest within the [[Caldera]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Origin
Deep Things are expressions of the [[First World Sleeper]]'s dreaming consciousness, given form by the ambient void energy of the containment chamber. They appear as shifting masses of darkness, often with faint points of light within, and can drift freely through solid matter.

## Behavior
Deep Things are typically curious and observational, investigating intruders and phenomena within their environment. They are not inherently aggressive but possess a damaging Void Touch that can occur through incidental contact. Their behavior can become agitated in response to disturbances in the Caldera's equilibrium or the Sleeper's broadcast.
