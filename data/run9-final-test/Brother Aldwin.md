**Brother Aldwin** is a gentle and earnest healer who tends to the [[Chapel of the Hearthfire]] in the frontier town of [[Grimhold]].

## Role and Character
He cares for the spiritual and physical well-being of the town's inhabitants. Brother Aldwin is a trusted source of medical aid and compassionate counsel.

## Involvement with Expeditions
The healer provided healing kits to adventurers and has treated injuries sustained by those exploring the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].
