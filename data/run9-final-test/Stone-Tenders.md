The **Stone-Tenders** are ancient, silent stone constructs that serve as the physical agents and communicators for the [[Psychic Guardian]].

## Location and Behavior
They are found primarily within the [[Salt-Vaults]], often positioned as guardians near key locations like the main food silo. They communicate the will of the Psychic Guardian through a language of binary clicking pulses.

## Function and Limitations
The Stone-Tenders observe and enforce the terms of the Guardian's ancient containment contracts, acting as immovable barriers against breaches of protocol. They do not, however, enter areas sealed by other factions, such as the interstitial corridor built by the [[Secretive Brotherhood]].
