The **Bastion of the Asymmetrical** is the official name given to the fortified base and research station within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. The name was formally accepted by [[Reyna Steelforge]] of the [[Prospectors' Guild]] for documentation purposes.

## Function
The Bastion is centered around the controlled breach into the [[Giant Empire]] foundation level beneath the mountain's capstone and serves as the primary staging point for exploration of the ancient containment facility.
