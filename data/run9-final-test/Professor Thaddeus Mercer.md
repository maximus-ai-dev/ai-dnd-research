**Professor Thaddeus Mercer** is a human wizard and scholar adventuring in the [[Starmetal Hills]], serving as his party's academic expert and magical support.

## Personality and Methodology
Professor Mercer is an academic who treats every adventure as vital fieldwork for his research. He cannot resist examining ancient architecture, deciphering inscriptions, or theorizing about unexplained phenomena. His intellectual curiosity frequently overrides his instinct for self-preservation. He delivers impromptu lectures on historical context and argues vehemently against the destruction of anything he deems academically valuable.

## Combat Style and Abilities
He fights from the back line employing a mix of control and damage spells. Mercer relies on frontline allies like [[Garrick Kade]] for physical protection. His spell selection prioritizes utility, information-gathering, and battlefield manipulation, with direct combat spells as a secondary option.

## Key Relationships
He appreciates Garrick's protective role but is exasperated by the fighter's anti-intellectual approach. He respects [[Cora Flint]]'s engineering mind but considers her focus on profit to be overly reductive.
