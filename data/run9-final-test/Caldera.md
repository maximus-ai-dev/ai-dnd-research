The **Caldera** is a massive, zero-gravity chamber located in the deepest reaches of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. It serves as the primary containment vessel for the [[First World Sleeper]].

## Description
This vast, spherical void contains the Sleeper's boundary sphere at its center. The environment is one of null gravity, with ancient Giant construction debris and monitoring stations, such as the [[Observation Platform]], anchored to its walls. The chamber is saturated with the Sleeper's psychic broadcast and is inhabited by entities known as [[Deep Things]]. Four massive iron containment anchor points are set into the Caldera wall at cardinal positions.

## Role in Containment
The Caldera is the epicenter of the containment architecture. All reinforcing efforts, including the deployment of the [[Giant Capstone]] and the forging of the [[Dreamstone Seal]], must occur within this chamber to be effective. The final staging position for the Seal's deployment was established here, approximately fifty feet from the Sleeper's boundary. Activating the four anchor points generates a suppression field that weakens void-based entities within the chamber.
