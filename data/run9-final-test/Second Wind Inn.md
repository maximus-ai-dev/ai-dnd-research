The **Second Wind Inn** is a two-story timber inn located in the frontier town of [[Grimhold]]. It serves as a central gathering place for travelers, prospectors, and locals.

## Role and Atmosphere
The inn is known for its warm, smoky atmosphere and provides essential services of food, drink, and lodging. It functions as a key hub for hearing news, exchanging rumors, and learning about opportunities in the [[Starmetal Hills]]. The grizzled prospector and guide [[Harlan]] is among its frequent patrons.
