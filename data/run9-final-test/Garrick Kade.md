**Garrick Kade** is a human fighter and adventurer operating in the [[Starmetal Hills]], serving as his party's primary frontline combatant.

## Personality and Approach
Garrick is an aggressive, direct, and instinctual warrior who prioritizes immediate action over deliberation. He consistently takes point, charges threats before plans are finalized, and refuses to retreat from a fight. His philosophy is to hit first and assess the situation later. Despite his blunt and often reckless exterior, he is pragmatically protective of his allies.

## Combat Style and Abilities
He fights in melee with a maul, utilizing Giant's Might and Action Surge to deliver devastating bursts of damage. Garrick wears chain mail and relies on sheer durability and aggressive pressure rather than defensive finesse, typically targeting the most significant threat in any engagement.

## Key Relationships
He respects [[Cora Flint]]'s competence but only tolerates her meticulous ledger-keeping. He is protective of the physically frail [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]], though often frustrated by the wizard's tendency to pause and examine things in the midst of danger.
