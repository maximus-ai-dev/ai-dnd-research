The **Giant Capstone** is a massive, engineered seal located deep beneath the [[Dwarven Layer]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
The capstone is a section of dark, dense stone approximately forty feet in diameter, inscribed with glowing Giant-era runes. It forms the lid of the primary containment vessel, directly separating the dwarven excavations above from the [[Giant Empire]] foundation level below.

## Function
As part of the ancient containment network, the capstone's runes generate a field that suppresses corruption and psychic influence from the entity known as the [[First World Sleeper]]. It is the final barrier before the Sleeper's prison proper. The [[Drowned Chokepoint]], the convergence of the Dwarven Layer's water management systems, is constructed directly above it.

## Significance
Breaching the Giant Capstone is a primary objective of the [[Twisted]], as it would allow the Sleeper's corruption to flood upward into the mountain. The capstone was the target of the [[Drowned Juggernaut]]'s drilling operations.
