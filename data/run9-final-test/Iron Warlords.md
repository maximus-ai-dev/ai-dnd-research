The **Iron Warlords** were a militaristic human civilization that rose to power approximately three centuries after the Cataclysm.

## Architecture and Design Philosophy
They are known for their brutal, utilitarian architecture designed for permanent siege warfare. Hallmarks of their construction include thick stone walls, low ceilings, narrow corridors, arrow slits, and murder holes, all emphasizing defense and attrition.

## Legacy and Ruins
Their ruins, including the complex known as the [[Salt-Vaults]], lie beneath the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. The Iron Warlords fell from power around two hundred years ago, meaning their civilization was already extinct when the [[Secretive Brotherhood]] wardens later awoke from their stasis.
