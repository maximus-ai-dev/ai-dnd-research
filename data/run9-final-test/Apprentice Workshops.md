The **Apprentice Workshops** are a series of smaller forge stations located within the [[Dwarven Layer]], adjacent to the main [[Cold Forge]] chamber.

## Description
These workshops were used by dwarven apprentices for training and smaller-scale projects. Each station is equipped with an individual bellows, anvil, and a set of durable dwarven steel tools including hammers, tongs, measuring gauges, and quenching troughs. The workshops are currently cold, dark, and covered in mineral dust, but the tools remain fundamentally sound due to the quality of dwarven craftsmanship.
