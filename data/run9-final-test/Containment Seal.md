The **Containment Seal** is a pre-Cataclysm dwarven Stonelord artifact designed for deep-earth binding and stasis.

## Description
The seal is marked with potent runes signifying 'Deep-Earth Binding,' 'Stasis Ward,' and 'Monitor Point.' It represents advanced dwarven magical engineering from an age before the Cataclysm.

## Location and Status
The seal is located in a chamber beneath the [[Salting Halls]]. [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] identified the seal and found forensic evidence indicating it had been tampered with in the recent past, compromising its ancient wards.
