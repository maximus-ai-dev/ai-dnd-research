The **Salting Halls** are the central storage complex within the larger [[Salt-Vaults]] beneath the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Design and Function
These enormous chambers feature extensive stone shelving carved directly into the walls. They were designed by the [[Iron Warlords]] to hold vast quantities of preserved foods essential for siege survival, including salted meats, dried grains, pickled vegetables, and raw rock salt for preservation.

## Recent History
The halls served as the primary nesting ground for the colony of [[Mutated Giant Rats]] that infested the vaults.
