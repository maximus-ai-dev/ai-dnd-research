The **deep resonance** (referred to by the [[Secretive Brotherhood]] as the "Deep Resonance") is a mysterious arcane phenomenon emanating from deep within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Nature and Study
The Brotherhood spent decades studying and cataloging its intensifying patterns. Their research, contained within the [[Grand Archive]], suggests the resonance is climbing toward the upper layers of the mountain and is connected to older, sleeping entities below. The same resonance was detected in both the [[Earth-Shaper]] chamber and the Brotherhood's [[Restricted Vault]].
