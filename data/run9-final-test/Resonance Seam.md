A **Resonance Seam** is a geological formation within the [[Giant foundation]] where the psychic radiation of the [[First World Sleeper]] has been concentrated against bedrock for millennia, crystallizing it into raw [[Dreamstone]].

## Description
Marked on ancient [[Giant Blueprint Tablet]]s, a Resonance Seam appears as a vein of crystallized stone, often resembling a frozen lightning bolt running through rock. It glows with an inner, spectrum-shifting light and radiates intense psychic energy.

## Properties
The seam's presence warps local space, causing visual and auditory distortions, gravity fluctuations, and disrupting nearby containment runes. It represents a natural concentration of the Sleeper's power made solid.

## Discovery
The party located a primary Resonance Seam in the outer ring of the Giant foundation by tracking an anomalous signal with their [[Monitoring Crystal]].
