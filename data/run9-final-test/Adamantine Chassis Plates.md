**Adamantine Chassis Plates** are sections of military-grade armor plating forged from adamantine.

## Description
The plates are thick, heavy, and gleam with a characteristic dark blue sheen. They are exceptionally hard and durable, designed to withstand tremendous force.

## Origin
Such plates were used in the construction of the most resilient dwarven automata and war machines. A set of these plates was salvaged from the torso of a destroyed [[Drowned Juggernaut]] at the [[Drowned Chokepoint]].

## Use
The salvaged plates contain enough adamantine to craft one suit of adamantine half plate armor. When worked at a fully operational forge like the [[Cold Forge]], adamantine armor transforms critical hits against the wearer into normal strikes.
