**Dreamstone** is a crystalline substance formed from the concentrated psychic radiation emitted by [[The Anomaly]].

## Properties
The material appears as crystallized stone that glows with an inner, shifting iridescent light. It is highly psychically reactive, emitting energy that can cause hallucinations, perception distortions, and localized reality warping. Prolonged exposure induces vivid, personalized visions. Dreamstone can interact with and disrupt ancient [[Giant Empire]] containment runes.

## Sources and Uses
The primary known vein is located within the outer ring of the [[Giant foundation]]. When properly contained and shielded, Dreamstone can be forged at the [[Cold Forge]] into specialized equipment, such as [[Dreamstone Circlet]]s and weaponry. Corrupted Dreamstone growths are often found on void-twisted entities and structures.
