**Elara Stormwind** is the proprietor of the [[Griffon's Hold]] general store in the frontier town of [[Grimhold]].

## Personality and Background
Elara is a warm, chatty, and knowledgeable retired mercenary. Her past career gives her extensive knowledge of local trails and the geography of the [[Starmetal Hills]].

## Services and Role
She operates her store with fair prices and is a valuable source of supplies, information, and rumors for prospectors and adventurers. Elara provides rough maps of the region and essential cold-weather gear for expeditions.
