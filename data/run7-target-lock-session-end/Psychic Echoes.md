Psychic Echoes are a phenomenon caused by prolonged exposure to [[Dreamstone]] radiation.

## Nature
Echoes are not undead, but preserved memories and consciousnesses imprinted on radiation-saturated organic matter. They lack intelligence or malice, mechanically replaying fragments of memory or habitual actions. They cannot be reasoned with, turned, or commanded.

## Manifestation
Echoes have been encountered in the upper [[Dwarven Layer]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], where the perfectly preserved bodies of dwarven guardians replayed their final moments and duties. These echoes are animated by the ambient psychic energy from a Dreamstone source and collapse when that energy is cut off.
