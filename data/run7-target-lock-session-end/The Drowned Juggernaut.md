The Drowned Juggernaut is a heavily modified dwarven mining automaton discovered in the [[The Drowned Chokepoint]]. It was a central component of the [[Twisted]]'s efforts to breach the mountain's containment.

## Description
Standing approximately eighteen feet tall, the Juggernaut is constructed from dwarven iron and stone in the shape of a humanoid mining automaton. Its arms terminate in powerful rotating drill assemblies, and its torso houses a furnace. The original dwarven machinery has been extensively augmented with Twisted bioengineering; organic resin fills gaps between iron plates, and a biological control node, a pulsing mass of corrupted tissue, serves as its brain.

## Function and Purpose
The machine was designed to drill through the [[Giant Capstone]], the barrier sealing the [[First World Sleeper]]'s prison. It was powered by [[Dreamstone]] radiation channeled through organic resin cables connected to the cavern's infrastructure. The biological control node processed this psychic energy into mechanical commands, effectively causing the automaton to act out the Sleeper's dream of escape.

## Destruction
The Juggernaut was destroyed in a confrontation with the [[Emberfell Rejects]]. Its destruction secured the Giant Capstone and halted the immediate threat to the containment below. Salvageable components, including [[Adamantine Chassis Plates]] and a [[Dreamstone Power Cell]], were recovered from its wreckage.
