Korathan's Chisel is a specialized artifice tool created by the Giant artificer [[Korathan]].

## Function
It is designed for the precise inscription and manipulation of the scaling runes used in Giant containment architecture. The chisel is essential for reactivating damaged containment runes and inscribing new ones, enabling smaller humanoid hands to work on Giant-scale magical engineering.

## Current Status
A functional replica of the chisel was created and is carried by [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] to assist with physical repairs to the containment structure and the inscription of complex rune matrices.
