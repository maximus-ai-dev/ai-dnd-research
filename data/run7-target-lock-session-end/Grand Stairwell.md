The Grand Stairwell is the primary vertical shaft connecting multiple layers within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Construction
The stairwell descends 120 feet from the level of the [[Vigilant Brotherhood]] sanctum down to the [[Military Empire]] palace. The upper sixty feet were constructed by the [[Iron Warlords]] in a utilitarian style. The lower sixty feet, built by the Military Empire, are wider and more elaborate, featuring carved relief panels. The stairwell is structurally sound and serves as the main thoroughfare for descent into the mountain's deeper layers.

## Strategic Importance
The stairwell is the only known safe passage between the occupied surface fortress and the ancient ruins below. It is a vital artery for exploration and a potential chokepoint for defense.
