A Dreamstone Sentinel is a crystalline construct that forms from raw [[Dreamstone]] to protect a [[Resonance Seam]].

## Nature and Origin
The Sentinel is not a living creature but a defense mechanism—a coalescence of the vein's stored psychic energy manifesting as a physical obstacle. It forms in response to disturbances in the seam, such as mining activity. The construct is roughly humanoid, ten feet tall, made of raw Dreamstone, and hovers above the ground, unaffected by normal gravity.

## Abilities and Weaknesses
The Sentinel regenerates while within proximity to an exposed Dreamstone vein. It attacks with crystalline slams that deal both force and psychic damage, and can unleash gravity pulses and psychic bursts that cause hallucinations and stunning effects. It is vulnerable to thunder damage, which shatters its crystalline structure, and radiant damage bypasses its regeneration. Its regeneration stops if the exposed vein face is shielded.
