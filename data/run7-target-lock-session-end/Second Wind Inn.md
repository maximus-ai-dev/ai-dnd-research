The Second Wind Inn is the main tavern and lodging house in [[Grimhold]].

## Description and Services
It is a two-story timber building that provides a warm refuge from the frontier cold and serves as a central social hub where travelers, prospectors, and locals gather. The inn offers rooms for rent, meals, and drink. [[Reyna Steelforge]] of the [[Prospectors' Guild]] often extends credit for lodging here to adventurers working on guild contracts.
