The Avatar of the Slumber is a crystallized void entity that manifests as the immune response of the [[First World Sleeper]]'s boundary.

## Description and Nature
It takes the form of a towering figure of crystallized void energy, approximately fifty feet tall, with the [[First World]] visible inside it like light through stained glass. Its form is a composite of [[Giant]], dwarf, and human features, shaped by the Sleeper's unconscious mind into something comprehensible to approaching minds. The Avatar manifests to prevent incompatible objects, such as the [[Dreamstone Seal]], from touching the sphere's boundary.

## Abilities and Demise
The Avatar wields the Sleeper's broadcast as a weapon, capable of dream slams, void grasps, boundary waves, and dream gazes that transport victims into the First World. It is tethered to the sphere for power and dissolves when the boundary is reinforced or when it is destroyed in combat. It was shattered by [[Garrick Kade]] wielding the [[+3 Runic Maul]] during the final deployment of the Dreamstone Seal.
