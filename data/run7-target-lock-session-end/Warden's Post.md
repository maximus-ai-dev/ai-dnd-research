The Warden's Post is a vast guardian station located in the southwest quadrant of the [[Giant foundation level]] beneath the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Features
The chamber is roughly eighty feet in diameter with a domed ceiling sixty feet high and was designed as a permanent station for a Giant sentry embedded within the containment architecture to monitor the seal below. At the center of the chamber rests an open, empty sarcophagus large enough to hold a Giant. The far wall of the chamber was the site of a catastrophic breach—a ragged, forty-foot-wide hole in the [[Giant Capstone]] floor, surrounded by shattered containment runes. The chamber's walls contain four primary containment rune arrays (north, south, east, and west) that control the local containment field.

## Recent Events
The Warden's Post was the site of a major containment breach caused by a hidden Twisted drill. The void energy from this breach reanimated the Giant guardian entombed there, creating the [[Reanimated Warden]]. The chamber subsequently became the battleground where the Warden was destroyed and where a temporary containment lattice was established to stabilize the breach.
