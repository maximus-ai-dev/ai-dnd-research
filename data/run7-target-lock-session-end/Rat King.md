The Rat King was an alpha specimen of mutated giant rat that commanded a colony within the [[Salt-Vaults]].

## Physical Description
The creature was bloated and significantly larger than a typical giant rat, roughly the size of a large dog. It possessed an iridescent sheen to its fur and multiple tails that appeared fused together.

## Behavior and Demise
The Rat King remained at the center of the colony's main nest, atop a large stone silo. Its death caused the remaining rat colony to scatter and flee.
