Amplifier Alpha was the first of three [[fleshy amplifiers]] located in the [[Giant foundation level]].

## Location and Description
The amplifier was situated in the northwest quadrant of the foundation level, approximately four hundred feet from the forward camp. It occupied a Giant maintenance corridor near a damaged section of the containment wall and was described as the smallest and possibly oldest of the three amplifiers.

## Destruction
The amplifier was destroyed by severing its root-like tendrils, which connected it to the containment runes. Its destruction triggered alarm signals through the [[Twisted]]'s organic communication network but significantly reduced the local broadcast intensity.
