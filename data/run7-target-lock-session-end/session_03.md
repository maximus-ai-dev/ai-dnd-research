# Session 3: The Sealed Archive

The [[Salt-Vaults]] hummed with restored preservation magic, a steady vibration that spoke of ancient enchantments functioning as intended. For three days, the preparation kitchen had been a hive of methodical activity — the scent of clay-limestone-salt mortar hanging in the air, mixing with the earthy aroma of thirty workers now eating regular meals upstairs. Winter still gripped the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] outside, but inside the [[Iron Warlords]]' layer, something like normalcy had taken root.

[[Cora Flint]] reviewed [[Brenna]]'s supply ledger at a makeshift table, occasionally raising an eyebrow at a discrepancy. [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] studied the [[Stone-Tenders]], sketching their clicking patterns in his field notebook. [[Garrick Kade]] stood watch at the corridor junction, his maul resting against the wall — not because there was immediate danger, but because that was what he did. He watched.

Then [[Rolf]]'s mattock swung into the eastern wall — three solid strikes, then a fourth that punched through with a hollow crunch.

A whistle of cold, stale air rushed through the four-inch hole. Through the opening, darkness. And from somewhere in that darkness, a low rhythmic sound began — like breathing. Like something waking up.

The [[Stone-Tenders]] reacted immediately, positioning themselves between the hole and the silo, clicking rapidly. They were not attacking. They were guarding.

---

**"Everyone back from the wall,"** Cora commanded, her voice cutting through the sudden tension. **"Rolf, step away slowly."**

Garrick was already moving, crossing the room in three long strides. **"Get back!"** he barked, shoving the worker aside with his free hand. He planted himself before the hole, maul raised, peering into the darkness. **"Whatever it is, it's waking up."**

Mercer approached cautiously, his academic instincts overriding immediate danger. **"That breathing pattern — too regular for mammalian respiration, too deep for reptilian. It sounds... mechanical. Or perhaps magical stasis."** He brushed away mortar debris, revealing a thin vein of something that shimmered with residual magic. **"Preservation mortar. Someone sealed this from the other side."**

As Mercer began the somatic gestures for Detect Magic, the [[Stone-Tenders]]' clicking intensified. Through the psychic link, a message filtered into Mercer's mind from the [[Psychic Guardian]]: ***"Sealed. Old. Not ours. Do not let it spread."***

Garrick tested the wall with another kick, widening the hole to nearly a foot across. Torchlight now revealed more of the space beyond: a stone floor eight feet below, a narrow passage running parallel to the kitchen wall, and an alcove containing a humanoid figure clad in tarnished ring mail. The breathing came from it — *inhale... two... three... exhale... two... three...* — too regular, too perfect.

The figure shifted. Not dramatically. Just a slight tilt of its head. Then its eyes opened — human eyes, clouded with sleep but clearing rapidly. A man in his late forties, with a weathered face and short-cropped grey hair. His hand went to his sword hilt. Not drawing it. Just resting there.

From deeper in the corridor, a second figure stirred.

The man spoke, his voice raspy from disuse: ***"Identify yourself to the Brotherhood or stand aside."***

---

Cora stepped forward, hands raised. **"Hold! We're not your enemies. The Iron Warlords are long gone — centuries gone. You've been preserved."**

Garrick didn't lower his maul. **"Hands off weapons. You're in our fortress now. Centuries have passed."**

Mercer's eyes widened with scholarly recognition. **"The Brotherhood insignia! A closed eye within a triangle — that's the Sigil of the [[Vigilant Brotherhood]]!"** His wizardly quill began scribbling furiously. **"You specialized in containing metaphysical contagions. Things that could 'spread.'"**

The man — [[Warden Aldric]] — processed their words, his soldier's composure cracking. **"Centuries?"** He glanced at the woman behind him — [[Warden Petra]] — who had her crossbow half-raised. **"I am Warden Aldric. This is Warden Petra. Our orders: guard the passage, allow no unauthorized access to the sanctum below."**

From the third alcove, [[Cassen]] emerged, rubbing his eyes. **"Aldric? Petra? What's... who are these people?"**

Aldric made a decision. **"You have provided proof enough. Our duty, as defined by our original orders, may indeed be complete."** He paused. **"But the Archive remains. And the [[Amalgamation]] still watches."**

Through the psychic link, Mercer received another urgent message: ***"The seal is broken. The watch is ended. Re-seal or contain. Your choice."***

---

**"We accept responsibility for containment,"** Cora declared, her mind calculating risks and logistics. **"But we need to assess the Archive's current state first."**

Garrick widened the hole with careful swings of the mattock, creating a stable opening. He dropped through first, landing with a heavy thud on the corridor floor below. Aldric followed, moving with stiff grace.

As they approached the stone door at the corridor's end — carved with the Brotherhood's closed-eye symbol and the words ***"The Grand Archive. Entry by rank only. The Amalgamation watches."*** — Mercer made a brilliant connection. **"A natural twenty on my Arcana check! 'The Sleeping God's Dream' — that's a direct reference to the [[First World Sleeper]] theory!"**

Aldric stared at him. **"You know the term? That was... classified."** His voice dropped to a whisper. **"The Brotherhood wasn't just studying it. We were monitoring. This mountain... it's one of those places where the fabric of the world grows thin."**

Petra stepped forward, her suspicion replaced by concern. **"The Archive doesn't just hold research — it holds... samples. Fragments of dreams. Pieces of the Sleeper's consciousness."**

Cassen added, **"And if those fragments escape containment... they spread. They infect minds. They change reality around them."**

---

Aldric touched his brass insignia against the door's symbol. Arcane circuitry awakened after long dormancy, glowing with soft blue light. A voice — not human, not mechanical — spoke from the stone: ***"Authorization recognized. Warden Aldric. Archive integrity: 87%. Containment fields: stable."***

But as the voice spoke, Mercer noticed something. From beneath the door, a thin tendril of pale grey smoke emerged, curling along the floor toward Aldric's boot. It had a faint, sickly luminescence.

Petra hissed, **"Contamination! A breach already occurred!"**

The door's voice continued: ***"Warning: minor containment degradation detected. [[Dream Fragment]] designation: 'The Garden of Stone Blossoms.' Containment loss: 3%. Risk of perceptual contamination: low to moderate."***

The tendril reached Aldric's boot. Where it touched, the leather didn't change, but Aldric's expression did — his eyes went distant. **"I see... stone flowers,"** he murmured. **"Growing from the walls. They're... beautiful."**

---

**"Garrick, get Aldric away from that tendril!"** Cora shouted. **"We're sealing it!"**

Garrick didn't hesitate. He swung his maul low, sweeping the tendril away, then grabbed Aldric by the arm and yanked him back toward the hole. **"It's spreading! We need to seal this now!"**

Above, Cora, Mercer, Petra, and Cassen worked frantically. They mixed clay-limestone mortar with double the normal salt content, then added iron filings scavenged from broken barrel hoops. Petra and Cassen held their insignias over the mixture, the brass badges emitting soft white light that seemed to infuse the mortar with purifying energy.

Garrick takes one last swing, then leaps for the hole. Strong hands — Rolf's and Petra's — haul him up just as a cluster of tendrils reaches the base of the opening.

**"Now!"** Cora commanded.

"Now!" Cora commands, and they pour the first layer of mortar into the hole. It flows down, covering the opening from above. The tendrils recoil from the salt-and-iron mixture, retreating back into the corridor. They work in layers, as Mercer suggested: iron filings first, then salt, then the mortar matrix. With each layer, Petra and Cassen hold their insignias close, the purification fields strengthening the barrier.

As the final layer sets, the shimmering trails left by the tendrils on the kitchen side of the wall begin to fade. The floral scent diminishes. The air feels... cleaner. Heavier. The breach is sealed.

Through the psychic link, Mercer received a final message: ***"Containment achieved. Breach sealed. Contract terms satisfied. The watch continues."***

---

The crisis had passed. The [[Salt-Vaults]] were secure. The workers were safe.

In the aftermath, three brass [[Brotherhood Insignia]] lay on a makeshift table, glinting in the torchlight. Beside them, a partial [[Brotherhood Operations Log]] lay open to a page showing the [[Grand Archive]]'s layout and the words: ***"The Amalgamation watches."***

The three Wardens accept Cora's offer — they have nowhere else to go, and the fortress needs guards who understand what lies beneath. They take up residence in the preparation kitchen, treating it as their new post. They're formal, awkward with the workers, and utterly baffled by the Stone-Tenders, but they're competent. They call Garrick "the large one," Mercer "the scholar," and Cora "the ledger-keeper." It's not friendship, but it's respect.

The door remained. Forty feet below the [[Salt-Vaults]]. Sealed behind stone and salt and iron and purification magic. But the keys to open it were now in their possession.

As night fell on the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], the fortress felt different. Not threatening — just aware. As if something ancient had noticed their presence.

And was waiting.
