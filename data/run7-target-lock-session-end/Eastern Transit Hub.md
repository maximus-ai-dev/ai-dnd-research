The Eastern Transit Hub is a major junction within the dwarven [[Minecart Network]], located east of the [[Cold Forge Chamber]].

## Description
It is a large, natural cavern incorporated into the industrial complex, featuring multiple parallel rail lines, loading docks, and control mechanisms. The hub was severely damaged and occupied by the behir [[Kellashen the Behir]] for decades, becoming littered with debris, bones, and lightning scorch marks.

## Function and Current Status
It serves as the primary transfer point connecting the forge to deeper mining operations, the northern [[Pump Station East]], and a sealed southern branch marked as a [[Quarantine Zone]]. With Kellashen defeated, the hub is now clear for restoration and use.
