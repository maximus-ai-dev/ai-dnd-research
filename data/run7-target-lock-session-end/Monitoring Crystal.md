The Monitoring Crystal is an arcane device used to track the integrity of the containment field and the resonance frequency of the [[First World Sleeper]]'s broadcast within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Function
It provides real-time readings on containment field strength as a percentage of rated capacity and can pinpoint the location of breaches or energy surges. Its resonance can spike violently in response to catastrophic containment failures, serving as an early warning system.

## Usage
The device was instrumental in analyzing breaches and vulnerabilities throughout the mountain. During the final reinforcement of the containment, it recorded the field strength rising to 99% capacity following the deployment of the [[Dreamstone Seal]].
