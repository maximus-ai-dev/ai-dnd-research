The Hall of Conquered Peoples is a trophy hall within the [[Relic Basements]] of the [[Military Empire]] layer in the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
The hall is a large chamber lined with twelve-foot stone display cases designed to hold artifacts from civilizations annexed by the Empire. Most cases are empty, showing signs of deliberate evacuation, but a few significant items remained.

## Contents
Notable artifacts recovered from the hall include a dwarven commemorative plaque, a sealed clay vessel containing seeds of an extinct grain, and the [[Giant Blueprint Tablet]]. This stone tablet, carved with Giant script, provides a structural diagram of the entire mountain and its containment architecture, making it the most significant artifact found in the layer.
