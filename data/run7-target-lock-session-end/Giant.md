The Giants are an ancient race of colossal beings who once inhabited the region of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Culture
They stood approximately twenty feet tall and possessed immense physical strength. Their culture was characterized by precision engineering and thinking on geological timescales. They were master stoneworkers capable of creating structures with tolerances measured in fractions of an inch across surfaces hundreds of feet long.

## Legacy and Works
The Giants' most significant known work is the containment vessel built to imprison the [[Sleeper]]. This structure, known as the [[Giant foundation level]], includes features such as the [[Inspection Circuit]], [[Monitoring Alcoves]], and [[Stabilizer Control Nexus]]. They left behind artifacts like the [[Giant Blueprint Tablet]] to document their work. Their runic language forms a functional containment network that has remained active for millennia.

## Containment Philosophy
The Giants approached the Sleeper not as an enemy to be destroyed, but as a cosmic phenomenon to be managed. Their containment system is designed as a translator and stabilizer, reflecting an engineering mindset focused on long-term compatibility between realities.
