Amplifier Beta was the second of three [[fleshy amplifiers]] located within the [[Giant foundation level]].

## Location and Description
The amplifier was situated in the southeast quadrant, built into a Giant monitoring alcove. It was noted as the most sophisticated of the amplifiers, having been modified multiple times and parasitizing the alcove's resonance instruments.

## Destruction
The amplifier's destruction was more complex due to its reinforced structure and redundant systems. Its loss further weakened the [[Sleeper]]'s broadcast network and caused the remaining [[Twisted]] forces to consolidate their defenses around the final amplifier.
