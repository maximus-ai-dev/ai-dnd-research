Amplifier Gamma is the third and most powerful of the three [[fleshy amplifiers]] constructed by the [[Twisted]].

## Location and Description
The amplifier is located due south in the [[Giant foundation level]], approximately five hundred feet from the forward camp and near the threshold of the inner void. Its proximity to the void grants it more direct access to the [[Sleeper]]'s broadcast source, making it the most potent node in the network.

## Status and Defenses
Following the destruction of the other two amplifiers, Gamma's broadcast output increased to compensate. It remains operational and is the most heavily guarded of the amplifiers, with the full remaining [[Twisted]] garrison, including the [[Twisted Overseer]], stationed in its defense.
