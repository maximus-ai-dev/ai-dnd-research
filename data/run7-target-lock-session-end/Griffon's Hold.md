Griffon's Hold is a gear and supply shop located in [[Grimhold]], owned and operated by [[Elara Stormwind]].

## Services Offered
The shop sells weapons, armor, cold-weather clothing, rations, and general adventuring supplies essential for survival and prospecting in the [[Starmetal Hills]]. Stormwind is known for offering fair prices and practical advice about the local geography.

## Significance
As one of the primary outfitters in Grimhold, Griffon's Hold is a crucial resource for adventurers and prospectors preparing to venture into the wilderness northwest of the town.
