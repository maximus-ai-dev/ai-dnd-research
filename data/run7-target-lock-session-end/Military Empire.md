The Military Empire was the last human civilization to occupy the upper layers of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] before the dwarven catastrophe made the lower levels too dangerous.

## History and Architecture
The Empire built directly atop the dwarven ruins, constructing grand halls, garrison quarters, and trophy rooms over centuries. Their architecture is characterized by wide corridors built for military formations, elaborate carved reliefs, and a preference for monumental scale. The weight of their construction eventually began to stress and crack the ancient dwarven vaults below.

## Legacy
The Empire maintained the [[Relic Basements]], a series of trophy halls storing artifacts from conquered civilizations, including the Giant era. They were aware of the corrupted [[Sealed Crypt]] within their domain but chose to seal it rather than confront its contents. The Empire evacuated the mountain long ago, leaving behind a structurally precarious layer of ruins.
