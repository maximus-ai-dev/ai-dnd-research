The biological material becomes more pronounced the deeper they go. Mercer recognizes the pattern from his studies: extensions of the Twisted's bioengineering, living systems that grow and maintain themselves.

The tunnel opens suddenly into a vast space.

The Drowned Chokepoint is enormous: a domed cavern roughly one hundred feet across and fifty feet high, carved by the dwarves as the convergence point for their entire water management system. Massive pipes — ten feet in diameter — enter the cavern from every direction, some still dripping water, others dry and silent. The floor is a network of drainage channels radiating from a central platform like the spokes of a wheel, the water drained to knee level but still present, reflecting the faint bioluminescence of the organic structures.

The central platform is elevated fifteen feet above the channel floor, accessible by stone ramps. And on it, the Twisted have built their workshop.

Mechanical components stripped from other dwarven installations — gears, pistons, hydraulic assemblies — lie organized on organic resin workbenches. Tools modified with biological grips and extensions hang from resin racks. Stone tablets etched with a script that combines Dwarvish engineering notation with something organic and angular are stacked neatly.

And at the center of it all, partially disassembled for modification, is the Drowned Juggernaut.

It's larger than Mercer's glimpse suggested. Eighteen feet tall, roughly humanoid, built from dwarven iron and stone in the shape of a mining automaton. Its arms end in rotating drill assemblies, currently still. Its torso is a furnace, cold and dark. Its legs are pistons and hydraulic rams. Organic resin fills the gaps between iron plates, serving as both adhesive and conduit. Black fluid runs through transparent resin tubes like blood vessels, connecting mechanical systems to a biological control node mounted where the automaton's head should be — a pulsing, iridescent mass of corrupted tissue.

The Juggernaut is not active. Thick resin cables connect it to the organic infrastructure throughout the cavern, feeding it energy. The metallic sweetness is overwhelming here — the Dreamstone radiation intense enough that even Garrick, with no arcane sensitivity, feels dizzy with it.

Mercer estimates they're within a hundred feet vertically of the Giant Capstone. The Anomaly's influence is no longer indirect. They are approaching its source.
