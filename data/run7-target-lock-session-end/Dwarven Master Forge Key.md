The Dwarven Master Forge Key is a complex iron key designed to unlock the master control systems of the [[Cold Forge]] within the [[Cold Forge Chamber]].

## Function
This key enables full-capacity operation of the forge, which is a prerequisite for working with advanced materials like adamantine.

## Significance
Prior to the reactivation of the mountain's pump network, which provides necessary coolant, the key was non-functional. With the cooling system now operational, the Master Forge Key completes the restoration of the Cold Forge, allowing it to reach its full industrial potential for the first time in centuries.
