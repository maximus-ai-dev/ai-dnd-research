Fleshy amplifiers are a network of organic broadcast towers constructed by the [[Twisted]] within the [[Giant foundation level]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Function
The amplifiers are pillars of black resin grown into the stone, threaded with bioluminescent veins that pulse with the [[Sleeper]]'s psychic frequency. They parasitize energy from the Giant containment runes to amplify and direct the Sleeper's broadcast signal upward through the mountain's strata.

## Known Amplifiers
Three primary amplifiers were identified: [[Amplifier Alpha]] in the northwest quadrant, [[Amplifier Beta]] in the southeast, and [[Amplifier Gamma]] due south near the inner void. Each was guarded by [[Twisted]] forces. The destruction of these amplifiers weakens the broadcast and severs the Twisted's ability to project the Sleeper's influence upward.
