The Prospectors' Guild is the primary organization overseeing mining and exploration operations based in [[Grimhold]].

## Organization and Leadership
The guild is led by [[Reyna Steelforge]], who coordinates contracts, bounties, and supply lines for prospectors working in the [[Starmetal Hills]]. The guild hall in Grimhold acts as a meeting place, job board, and administrative center.

## Activities
The guild posts bounties for threats to its operations and advances credit for supplies to adventurers undertaking sanctioned work. It represents the collective economic interests of Grimhold's prospecting community. In 998 A.R., the guild, through Steelforge, commissioned a survey of the [[Military Empire]] layer within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], seeking historical artifacts and structural maps.
