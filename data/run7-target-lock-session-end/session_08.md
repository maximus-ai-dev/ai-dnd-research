# Session 8: The Minecart Network

**The Forge Awakens**

For three days, the [[Cold Forge Chamber]] had been anything but cold. [[Ignathar]]'s fire burned steadily in the central hearth, casting flickering orange light across the dwarven stonework and filling the vast chamber with a dry, mineral-scented warmth. The air hummed with latent heat, and the unworked [[Adamantine Ore]] stacked near the forge glowed with a faint inner light, responding to the elemental presence.

During this time, the party had made trips back to [[Grimhold]]. [[Garrick Kade]] hauled salvageable dwarven ceremonial goods to [[Elara Stormwind]]'s shop, returning with fresh supplies and a report that [[Reyna Steelforge]] was "cautiously optimistic" about the forge's reactivation. [[Cora Flint]] restocked her alchemical components and spent hours studying the forge tools, learning their specialized functions. [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] transcribed more of the Runesmith's Journal, piecing together Ignathar's thousand-year imprisonment and the dwarves' desperate attempts to maintain containment.

But beneath the warmth and activity, the sound persisted. It was clearer now with the forge's ambient noise reduced to a steady hum: a rhythmic *clatter-clatter-clatter* from somewhere east of the forge complex. Systematic. Industrial. Something working in the dark.

---

## Iron Veins

The sound led them east from the forge chamber, past the [[Apprentice Workshops]] where dwarven tools still hung on rusted hooks, and through the cooling channel junction where mineral-crusted pipes snaked along the ceiling. The air grew cooler as they left the forge's warmth behind, but the rhythmic clattering grew louder with each step.

The tunnel widened ahead, opening into a space unlike anything they'd seen in the [[Dwarven Layer]] so far. Before them stretched a **minecart station** — a raised stone loading platform twenty feet wide, with iron crane brackets bolted to the ceiling above. Below the platform, set into a carved stone channel, ran two parallel iron rails gleaming faintly in their light. Two minecarts sat on the rails, their wooden sides weathered but their iron frames remarkably intact.

Cora's engineer's eye immediately assessed the system: dwarven iron rails, treated against corrosion, still structurally sound after centuries. The switch mechanism at the junction ahead was seized with rust but not broken. With tools and time, this network could be reactivated.

Mercer studied the layout, consulting the Giant blueprint tablet. "This is the transit system," he murmured. "The dwarves built this to move materials through their entire underground empire. That eastern tunnel leads toward their primary mining operations. And that sound..." He gestured toward the descending darkness. "...is coming from there."

Garrick's instincts screamed caution. "That noise means something's working down there, and I don't like things that work in the dark for centuries." He moved past Cora toward the eastern tunnel, his eyes scanning the darkness ahead. The rhythmic clatter had a mechanical precision that set his fighter's instincts on high alert.

Mercer held up a hand. "Before you charge headlong into what could be a thousand-year-old automated mining system, let me examine something." He approached a faded wall plaque marked "[[Pump Station East]]," squinting at the dwarven runes. "This isn't just a transit system—it's an integrated industrial complex."

But Garrick was already moving, his maul held ready as he advanced down the eastern tunnel. The rhythmic clatter grew louder with each step, echoing off the smooth stone walls. The tunnel was twelve feet wide, ten feet high, with iron rails running in paired tracks along the floor.

Back at the station, Cora continued her methodical examination while Mercer spotted something on the floor near the northern branch tunnel—a **dwarven maintenance log**, leather-bound with iron-reinforced corners. They examined it together, Cora's practical eye scanning for operational details while Mercer's scholarly focus captured historical context.

The final entries were chilling:

*"Day 347 of the containment. Eastern hub sealed by order of Foreman Kellak. Apex predator confirmed. No transit until beast is cleared. Request hunter team from [[Deepwatch Garrison]]."*

The next entry, dated two weeks later: *"No response from garrison. Communications silent. The beast has claimed the eastern transit hub. We reroute all ore through the northern pump station line. May the ancestors forgive us for abandoning the eastern mines."*

The final entry, a single line: *"They're coming up from below. The drills never stopped. We seal what we can and pray."*

---

## The Junction Hall

Meanwhile, Garrick advanced around a bend in the eastern tunnel. The passage opened into a **Junction Hall** — a major intersection where three rail lines converged. In the center of the hall, a massive iron turntable was set into the floor, its mechanism frozen with rust. On the turntable, a minecart sat at an awkward angle—derailed and abandoned centuries ago.

The rhythmic clatter was louder here, echoing from the eastern tunnel beyond the Junction Hall. But a new sound joined it—a scraping from the derailed cart itself. As Garrick watched, something moved within the ore pile. A section of copper ore shifted, and a creature emerged.

It was a cave fisher—a pale, chitinous predator about the size of a large dog, with six spindly legs and a long, sticky filament extruding from its mouth. It had been nesting in the abandoned ore cart, feeding on cave fauna drawn to the mineral deposits.

The creature spotted Garrick and reared back, its filament whipping through the air with a wet *snap*.

"Predator in the junction! Not a golem!" Garrick called back, already charging forward.

The cave fisher's filament whipped toward him, but Garrick ducked under it with a roll. His maul came around in a wide arc and slammed into the creature's chitinous body with a sickening crunch. The cave fisher skittered back, copper ore scattering from the cart.

But even wounded, it fought back. The creature's filament wrapped around Garrick's left boot with a wet *thwack*, tightening with a powerful tug as it tried to reel him in toward its mandibles.

Garrick roared as the filament tightened, but his strength easily overpowered the creature's pull. With a brutal yank, he tore his leg free, the sticky strand snapping and whipping back toward the cave fisher. His maul connected solidly, crushing what remained of the creature's chitinous body.

As the cave fisher collapsed, Garrick noticed the rhythmic clattering from the eastern tunnel was growing louder. Something was moving toward them.

---

## Kellashen the Behir

Mercer's warning came too late. "Garrick, the log mentions an 'apex predator' in the eastern hub—this cave fisher is likely just a scavenger that moved into abandoned territory!"

The heavy, multi-legged *clack-clack-clack* of something large moving with purpose echoed down the tunnel. Mercer conjured his spectral mind—a glowing, book-shaped apparition—and sent it floating down the tunnel to scout ahead.

Through its senses, he saw the source of the sound emerging from the darkness: **[[Kellashen the Behir]]** — a twelve-legged serpentine predator twenty-five feet long, its scales the color of tarnished copper, its eyes glowing with faint electrical energy.

"GET DOWN!" Mercer screamed, but it was too late.

A 20-foot-wide line of lightning erupted from the behir's mouth, arcing down the eastern tunnel and into the Junction Hall. The electrical discharge followed the iron rails, amplifying as it traveled along the conductive metal.

Garrick dove aside, but the electricity still coursed through him, leaving his muscles screaming and his chain mail smoking. Cora wasn't as fast—the full force of the discharge hit her, throwing her against the tunnel wall with a sickening crack. She slumped to the floor, unconscious and smoking.

Garrick charged through the pain, his maul swinging in a wide arc that connected with one of the behir's front legs. The impact cracked scales and drew a hiss of pain from the massive creature.

Mercer's Web spell erupted from the stone, sticky strands attempting to entangle the behir's dozen legs. The creature thrashed, its immense strength tearing through the magical webbing.

Then the behir struck back. Its serpentine head darted forward with shocking speed, jaws closing around Garrick's torso. Teeth like iron daggers punched through chain mail, and Garrick felt ribs crack under the pressure. The behir lifted him off the ground, shaking him like a rag doll.

Cora lay motionless near the tunnel wall, blood pooling beneath her. She wasn't breathing. Garrick was grappled in the behir's jaws, seconds from being swallowed whole.

---

## Desperate Measures

Garrick struggled against the behir's crushing jaws, but couldn't break free. The creature's teeth dug deeper into his flesh, and he could feel himself being forced toward its throat. "Mercer... run!" he gasped through the pain.

But then he saw Cora lying motionless on the floor, and something snapped inside him. With a roar that was more pain than rage, he activated Giant's Might as a bonus action, his body swelling to Large size even within the behir's jaws. The sudden expansion forced its mouth wider, giving him just enough room to maneuver.

Mercer's hands were shaking so badly he could barely hold his spellbook. Cora's motionless form, Garrick being swallowed alive... this was a nightmare. But he couldn't run. He wouldn't abandon them.

"Garrick, hold on!" he screamed, his voice raw with terror. He raised his hands and cast Polymorph. The magic surged from him, targeting the behir. Golden light enveloped the massive serpentine creature as it began to shrink. Scales melted into smooth skin, the twenty-five-foot body compressed, and the glowing eyes dimmed.

In seconds, where Kellashen the behir stood, there was now a **common frog** sitting on the stone floor, blinking stupidly.

Garrick dropped from the air as the jaws holding him vanished, landing hard on the stone with a grunt of pain. Mercer scrambled to Cora's side, casting Healing Word as a bonus action. The magical energy flowed into her unconscious form, and her chest rose with a ragged breath. She was alive, but badly injured and unconscious.

"Up to an hour," Mercer said, his voice trembling as he looked from the frog to his injured companions. "But we can't kill it while it's polymorphed—the damage would carry over when it reverts. We need to... contain it. Or prepare."

---

## The Retreat

Working together, Garrick and Mercer secured the polymorphed behir. Garrick's immense strength shifted the heavy ore cart despite his injuries, positioning it beneath one of the loading crane brackets. Mercer used his Mage Hand to carefully deposit the frog into the cart, where it croaked once and settled among the copper ore chunks. They managed to position the heavy iron lid over the cart's opening, trapping Kellashen in its polymorphed form inside a sealed iron container.

"Fifty minutes, maybe less," Mercer said, wiping sweat from his brow. "When the spell ends, it'll revert to its true form inside that cart."

Garrick lifted Cora's unconscious form with surprising gentleness, cradling her against his chest. "We're leaving. Now."

The retreat through the minecart tunnel was tense and silent. They passed the maintenance bay with its hand-crank cart, then the station with the two larger minecarts, and finally emerged back into the cooling channel junction near the forge.

Back in the [[Cold Forge Chamber]], Ignathar's fire burned steadily. The elemental seemed to note their return, the flames flickering slightly as they entered. Garrick secured the tunnel entrance with a heavy stone block while Mercer spread their findings on a workbench.

Cora's eyes fluttered open as consciousness returned. She immediately tried to sit up, winced in pain, and assessed her own condition with clinical detachment. "Nine hit points. Multiple fractures, internal bleeding likely. That's coming out of the expedition budget."

They had approximately forty-five minutes before the Polymorph spell ended.

---

## Preparation and Counterattack

The party worked with desperate efficiency in the time they had. Garrick's Second Wind closed some of his worst wounds. He moved heavy stone blocks from around the forge, creating a makeshift barricade and choke point at the tunnel entrance.

Cora, despite her injuries, set to work with her alchemical supplies. She created three **acid traps**—glass vials of concentrated acid rigged to shatter when something heavy passed over pressure plates.

Mercer studied his spellbook, using his Awakened Spellbook feature to modify his prepared spells. Fireball became **Iceball**, Fire Bolt became **Ray of Frost**—cold damage to exploit the behir's reptilian physiology.

With five minutes remaining, their preparations were complete. Mercer checked his pocket watch. "Four minutes until the spell ends. Then it breaks out of that ore cart... and comes straight for us."

The seconds ticked by in tense silence. Suddenly, Mercer's eyes widened. "It's free. The cart just... exploded."

From down the tunnel came the heavy, multi-legged *clack-clack-clack* of something large moving with purpose. The behir was coming, and it was moving fast.

It hit the first acid trap. **CRACK!** The sound of shattering glass echoed up the tunnel, followed by a hiss of pain as acid splashed across the behir's scales. Mercer cast Slow through his Manifest Mind. Golden energy enveloped the creature, sapping its speed and coordination.

The behir hit the second acid trap, then the third, each one shattering and spraying more acid across its body. The creature was now seriously wounded and slowed, but it was still coming. It reached the tunnel entrance to the forge chamber, its massive body filling the opening.

---

## The Final Stand

Garrick charged from behind the barricade, his maul slamming into the behir's acid-weakened flank. Scales cracked under the impact. Cora fired her crossbow from behind cover, the bolt striking the same wounded patch on the behir's flank and sinking deep into flesh.

The behir, slowed and wounded, struck back. Its serpentine head darted forward, but the magical effect made its movements jerky and uncoordinated—Garrick easily sidestepped the attack.

Then Mercer's Iceball detonated. A sphere of freezing energy erupted in the tunnel entrance, centered just behind the behir. Icy shards tore at its scales, and frost formed instantly on its wounds. The creature roared in pain as the cold damage exploited its reptilian physiology.

Garrick unleashed his Action Surge, his maul becoming a blur of motion. The first swing connected with the behir's already-cracked flank, shattering more scales. The second swing caught it across the jaw with a sickening crunch.

Cora's Ray of Frost streaked across the chamber, striking the behir's front leg. Ice crystals formed instantly around the wound. Mercer's Ray of Frost, cast through his Manifest Mind behind the behir, struck its tail. More frost spread across its scales.

The behir was now critically wounded—bleeding from multiple wounds, acid burns smoking, frost covering its scales, moving with agonizing slowness. It made one last desperate lunge at Garrick, but its movements were so slow and clumsy that the half-orc easily sidestepped.

"Garrick, finish it!" Cora called out.

"With pleasure," Garrick growled, hefting his maul.

He stepped forward, planting his feet firmly. "This is our territory now, serpent." His maul came around in a powerful overhead swing aimed directly at its skull. The weapon connected with a sickening crunch, driving the creature's head down into the stone floor. He followed through with a second swing to its neck, making sure it was finished.

Silence fell over the forge chamber, broken only by the crackle of Ignathar's fire and the party's ragged breathing. [[Kellashen the Behir]]—apex predator of the eastern transit hub for decades—was dead.

---

## Securing the Hub

The party took the next hour to recover and secure their position. Cora administered healing potions, bringing them both to more stable condition. Mercer conducted a meticulous examination of the behir's corpse while Garrick assisted with the harvesting. The lightning gland was carefully extracted—a pulsating, blue-white organ about the size of a melon, still crackling with residual energy.

Once the harvesting was complete, they cleared the behir's massive corpse from the choke point and returned to the Junction Hall. Beyond it, they finally emerged into the **[[Eastern Transit Hub]]**.

The space was vast—a natural cavern incorporated into the dwarven network, with four parallel rail lines leading to loading docks carved into the eastern wall. The hub was partially destroyed: loading cranes toppled, carts overturned, debris blocking some of the rail lines. Bones littered the floor—cave fauna, but also something larger. A section of dwarven armor lay partially melted by lightning. Everywhere, scorch marks from the behir's electrical attacks marred the stone.

But the hub was empty now. No movement in the shadows, no rhythmic clattering, no predator waiting in ambush.

They conducted a thorough search. Among the bones of cave fauna and the occasional piece of dwarven armor, they discovered a **hoard**: 600 gp in salvageable valuables, **[[Foreman Kellak's Seal]]**, and a **[[Dwarven Transit Map]]**—a detailed schematic of the entire minecart network.

Mercer examined the loading docks and discovered the **hub control room**. Inside, he found a stone desk with crystal panels and a series of lever controls for directing rail traffic. On the desk lay another maintenance log with the final entry: *"All personnel evacuated to Deepwatch garrison. Eastern hub sealed. May the ancestors forgive us for what we leave behind."*

The three branch tunnels were clearly marked:
1. **EAST — PRIMARY MINING OPERATIONS** (rails intact, tunnel clear)
2. **NORTH — PUMP STATION EAST** (rails intact, but the map noted "FLOODED AT TERMINUS — PUMPS OFFLINE")
3. **SOUTH — QUARANTINE — DO NOT ACCESS** (rails blocked by a collapsed stone barrier, reinforced with iron bands)

As Mercer studied the transit map, he made a disturbing discovery. "Look at this," he said, pointing to the mining operations line. "The rails show recent use. Not centuries-old dust—disturbed grime. Something has been using this network."

Cora examined the rails with her engineering eye. "He's right. The maintenance cart in the Junction Hall—it was free of rust and moved easily. Someone or something has been maintaining parts of this system."

---

## The Road Ahead

The party made the sensible decision to retreat and recover fully. They gathered their findings and secured the transit hub control room by barring the iron door from the outside.

Back in the Cold Forge Chamber, Mercer spread the transit map on a workbench, studying it under the forge's light. "The network is vast. The mining operations branch extends for miles, connecting to multiple excavation sites. The pump station controls hydraulic systems for the entire eastern sector. And the quarantine zone..." He tapped the sealed southern branch. "...connects to areas marked 'DEEP DRILLING' and 'INTERFACE WITH FOUNDATION ARCHITECTURE.'"

He looked up, his expression grave. "This isn't just a mine. It's an industrial complex built around something. The dwarves were drilling toward the mountain's foundation—toward what the Giant blueprint tablet called the '[[Sleeper's Cradle]].'"

Garrick cleaned his maul, watching the tunnel entrance. "We rest here tonight. Proper watch rotation. Tomorrow, we go back to the hub and decide our next move." He looked at his companions. "We did good today. We cleared a decades-old predator and secured valuable territory. But whatever's using those rails... that's tomorrow's problem."

As they settled in for the night, Mercer made one final observation. He pointed to a section of the transit map showing the mining operations line. "Look at this notation: 'Track maintenance schedule: automated.' And then, in different ink: 'Automata continue cycles despite evacuation.'"

He looked from Cora to Garrick, his scholarly excitement tempered by the implications. "Something has been maintaining this network for centuries. And it's not the behir. Tomorrow, we find out what."

*The eastern transit hub is cleared, but deeper mysteries await in the minecart network. What intelligence has been maintaining these rails for centuries? What lies in the quarantined depths? And what connection does it all have to the First World Sleeper slumbering beneath the mountain?*
