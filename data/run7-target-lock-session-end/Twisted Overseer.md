The Twisted Overseer is a powerful commander within the [[Twisted]] forces operating in the [[Giant foundation level]].

## Description and Capabilities
It is a heavily armored, corrupted dwarf that wields a warhammer and exhibits enhanced command abilities. The Overseer can emit a [[Corruption Pulse]]—a burst of necrotic energy that can stun opponents—and bolster nearby Twisted with its Command Corruption ability. It demonstrates tactical adaptation, studying and countering the strategies of its adversaries.

## Role
The Overseer has served as a recurring antagonist, commanding the defense of key Twisted installations, including the biological drill constructed to breach the [[Giant Capstone]].
