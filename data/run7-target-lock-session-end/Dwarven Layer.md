The Dwarven Layer is the middle stratum of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], constructed by the dwarves of the [[Kazad-Khrom]] empire.

## Architecture and Function
Built atop the Giant foundation, the layer features corridors and chambers of dwarven scale, constructed with superior stonework designed to last millennia. Its geometric patterns and ironwork are identifiable as Kazad-Khrom design. The layer appears to have served as a residential and monitoring zone, acting as a barrier between the human strata above and the [[Sleeper's Cradle]] containment below.

## Current State
The upper Dwarven Layer shows signs of prolonged exposure to [[Dreamstone]] radiation, with stone discolored and stained from within. The dwarves used lead-sealed crypts as radiation monitors and containment points for corrupted areas.
