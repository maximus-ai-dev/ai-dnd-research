The Asymmetrical Mountain is a prominent and geologically anomalous peak located within the [[Starmetal Hills]] northwest of [[Grimhold]].

## Description
The mountain is noted for its unnatural appearance, seeming to 'sit wrong' upon the landscape as if placed rather than formed by natural erosion. Its strata and erosion patterns are inconsistent with the surrounding hills. The area is largely avoided by wildlife, and the air often carries a faint metallic scent.

## Stratigraphy and Purpose
The mountain is composed of multiple stratified layers built by successive civilizations. From the surface downward, these include the ruins of the [[Iron Warlords]], the sanctum of the [[Vigilant Brotherhood]], and the [[Military Empire]] palace. Beneath these human layers lies the ancient [[Dwarven Layer]] constructed by [[Kazad-Khrom]], and, at the bedrock level, Giant-era containment architecture. The mountain's core purpose is the imprisonment of a primordial entity within the [[Sleeper's Cradle]].
