Kazad-Khrom is a lost dwarven empire responsible for constructing the [[Dwarven Layer]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Architecture and Knowledge
The dwarves built their complex atop ancient Giant foundations, showcasing superior stonework, geometric patterns, and ironwork. They were aware of the [[Dreamstone]] corruption rising from below and implemented containment measures, including lead seals and monitored crypts.

## Fate and Legacy
The empire's ultimate fate remains unknown, but their final actions in the mountain indicate a prolonged, dedicated effort to contain the subterranean threat. Artifacts stamped with Kazad-Khrom marks, such as trade ingots, are still found within the delve.
