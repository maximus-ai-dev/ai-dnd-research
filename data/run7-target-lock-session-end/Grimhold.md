Grimhold is a frontier town located at the base of the [[Sentinel Mountains]] on the continent of Laternia.

## Description and Economy
It serves as the last significant outpost before the wilderness of the [[Starmetal Hills]] begins. The town is a practical, weathered settlement characterized by muddy streets and timber buildings. Its economy is driven by mining and prospecting in the surrounding hills, activities coordinated by the [[Prospectors' Guild]] under [[Reyna Steelforge]].

## Strategic Role
Grimhold is built among ancient ruins and serves as the primary hub for supplies and coordination for expeditions into the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].
