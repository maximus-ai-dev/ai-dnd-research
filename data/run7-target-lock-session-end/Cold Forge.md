The Cold Forge is the industrial heart of the upper [[Dwarven Layer]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
It is a cathedral of industry: a domed chamber roughly eighty feet across and forty feet high, with a central hearth twenty feet in diameter ringed by six massive anvils. The hearth was originally powered by a bound [[Fire Elemental]], [[Ignathar]], a common practice in major dwarven installations.

## History and Function
When corruption from below intensified and the dwarves began their retreat, the elemental's binding was severed and the forge went cold. Reigniting the forge provides the ability to work metal at an industrial scale deep within the mountain. It is capable of smelting and shaping steel, iron, and basic enchantment work.

## Associated Areas
The forge complex includes adjacent areas such as the [[Material Stores]], [[Apprentice Workshops]], and [[Cooling Channels]]. The [[Dreamstone Seal]] was assembled and rested upon its anvil before the final descent.
