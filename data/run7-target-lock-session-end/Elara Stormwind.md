Elara Stormwind is a retired mercenary and the proprietor of [[Griffon's Hold]], a supply shop in [[Grimhold]].

## Description and Role
She is a warm and chatty individual, bearing a scar from her temple to jaw that testifies to her past profession. Stormwind sells adventuring gear, weapons, and cold-weather supplies at fair prices and often provides travelers with rough maps and advice about the dangers of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].
