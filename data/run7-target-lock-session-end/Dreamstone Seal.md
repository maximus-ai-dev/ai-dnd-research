The Dreamstone Seal is a masterwork containment device forged to reinforce the boundary between the current reality and the [[First World]] at the heart of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Function
It is a sphere of adamantine and Dreamstone, roughly the size of a human skull, covered in scaled-down [[Giant]] containment runes. Its primary function is to integrate with the existing containment architecture, adding a new layer to the translation field and extending the containment's effective lifespan.

## Construction and Deployment
The Seal was constructed within the [[Caldera]] using a calibrated [[Dreamstone Fragment]], a rune matrix inscribed with [[Korathan's Chisel]], and a structural adamantine casing. It was successfully deployed at the sphere's boundary, raising the containment field to 99% rated capacity and improving upon the original Giant design.
