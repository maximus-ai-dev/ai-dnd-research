The Starmetal Hills are a range of low, rocky, and windswept hills located northwest of [[Grimhold]].

## Geography and Features
The terrain is largely treeless, scattered with ancient stone markers and strange geological formations. It is a harsh, beautiful, and exposed landscape that offers little natural shelter from the prevailing winds.

## Travel and Significance
The hills represent a multi-day journey from Grimhold to notable landmarks like the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. The trails are used by prospectors from the [[Prospectors' Guild]] and are navigable with sufficient survival skill. In the spring of 998 A.R., miners in the hills reported a metallic sweetness on the wind, which was later traced to a containment breach within the Asymmetrical Mountain.
