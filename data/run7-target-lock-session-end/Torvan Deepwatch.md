Torvan Deepwatch was a dwarven captain of the [[Kazad-Khrom]] empire, stationed in the upper [[Dwarven Layer]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Legacy
Deepwatch's name appears in the [[Dwarven Radiation Survey Notes]] as the officer responsible for monitoring the layer's containment seals. His body was interred in a lead-sealed mausoleum alongside thirteen of his soldiers after their deaths. Centuries of exposure to [[Dreamstone]] radiation from a breach below perfectly preserved the bodies and created a [[Psychic Echo]] of his final memories.

## The Echo
The psychic echo of Torvan Deepwatch repeatedly spoke a phrase in Dwarvish, later translated as "Seal the lower halls. The dreaming is too loud." His ceremonial war-pick, inscribed with his name, was recovered from the mausoleum.
