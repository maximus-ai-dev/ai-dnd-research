The Brotherhood Operations Log is a damaged record recovered from the alcoves in a hidden corridor behind the [[Salt-Vaults]].

## Content
Written in archaic Common, the partial log contains operational details of the [[Vigilant Brotherhood]], including personnel rosters and standard command phrases. It provides context about the Brotherhood's purpose and the sealed sanctum their [[Warden|Wardens]] were protecting.

## Current Status
The log is in the possession of [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]], who studies it to learn more about the Brotherhood and the sealed door.
