**DM:** **SESSION 8: THE MINECART NETWORK**

**Downtime: The Forge Awakens**

For three days, the Cold Forge Chamber has been anything but cold. Ignathar's fire burns steadily in the central hearth, casting flickering orange light across the dwarven stonework and filling the vast chamber with a dry, mineral-scented warmth. The air hums with latent heat, and the unworked adamantine ore stacked near the forge glows with a faint inner light, responding to the elemental presence.

During this time, the party has made trips back to Grimhold. Garrick hauled salvageable dwarven ceremonial goods to Elara Stormwind's shop, returning with fresh supplies and a report that Reyna Steelforge is "cautiously optimistic" about the forge's reactivation. Cora restocked her alchemical components and spent hours studying the forge tools, learning their specialized functions. Mercer transcribed more of the Runesmith's Journal, piecing together Ignathar's thousand-year imprisonment and the dwarves' desperate attempts to maintain containment.

But beneath the warmth and activity, the sound persists. It's clearer now with the forge's ambient noise reduced to a steady hum: a rhythmic *clatter-clatter-clatter* from somewhere east of the forge complex. Systematic. Industrial. Something working in the dark.

**Act 1: Iron Veins**

The sound leads you east from the forge chamber, past the apprentice workshops where dwarven tools still hang on rusted hooks, and through the cooling channel junction where mineral-crusted pipes snake along the ceiling. The tunnel widens ahead, opening into a space unlike anything you've seen in the Dwarven Layer so far. Before you stretches a **minecart station** — a raised stone loading platform twenty feet wide, with iron crane brackets bolted to the ceiling above. Below the platform, set into a carved stone channel, run two parallel iron rails gleaming faintly in your light. Two minecarts sit on the rails, their wooden sides weathered but their iron frames remarkably intact. Each cart is large enough to carry half a ton of ore, with heavy iron wheels locked in place by rusted brake levers.

The station is a junction point. The rails you stand beside continue west back toward the forge — the supply line you just followed. But to the east, the tunnel continues, descending slightly into darkness, and the rhythmic clattering echoes from that direction. A third branch heads north, marked on a faded wall plaque as "PUMP STATION EAST."

Cora's engineer's eye immediately assesses the system: dwarven iron rails, treated against corrosion, still structurally sound after centuries. The switch mechanism at the junction ahead is seized with rust but not broken. With tools and time, this network could be reactivated.

Mercer studies the layout, consulting the Giant blueprint tablet. "This is the transit system," he murmurs. "The dwarves built this to move materials through their entire underground empire. That eastern tunnel leads toward their primary mining operations. And that sound..." He gestures toward the descending darkness. "...is coming from there."

The rhythmic clatter continues, steady as a heartbeat. Something is working down that eastern tunnel. Something that has been working for a very long time.
