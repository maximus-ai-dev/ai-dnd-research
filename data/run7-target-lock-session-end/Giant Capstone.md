The Giant Capstone is a monumental stone seal that forms the barrier between the [[Dwarven Layer]] and the ancient [[Giant]] foundation levels below within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
A section of floor approximately forty feet in diameter within the [[The Drowned Chokepoint]], the capstone is made of a darker, denser stone than the surrounding dwarven architecture. Its surface is inscribed with potent Giant-era runes that glow with containment magic. It acts as the lid to the primary containment vessel imprisoning the [[First World Sleeper]].

## Recent History
The capstone was the target of the [[Twisted]]'s drilling operation using the [[The Drowned Juggernaut]]. The automaton succeeded in carving a three-foot-deep groove into the stone, critically weakening but not fully breaching the seal. Following the Juggernaut's destruction, the capstone remains intact but damaged, with its runes still active. The groove serves as a permanent reminder of how close the containment came to failure.
