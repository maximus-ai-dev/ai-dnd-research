# Session 20: The Final Boundary

The [[Caldera]] held its breath. Three golden beams of suppression energy stretched from the activated anchor points to the sphere's boundary, creating a cage of light around the void entity that slept within. The air hummed with contained power — 94% capacity according to the [[Monitoring Crystal]] in [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]]'s hand. So close to completion, yet the western anchor point remained dark, guarded by the crystallized nightmare that had emerged from the sphere.

[[Garrick Kade]] floated in the weightless space, his body swollen to Large size from Giant's Might, the +3 Runic Maul glowing with containment runes in his hands. Across from him, the [[Avatar of the Slumber]] stood sentinel — fifty feet of crystallized void energy with the [[First World]] visible inside it like light through stained glass. Its form flickered with golden light where the suppression beams intersected its body, cracks spiderwebbing through its crystallized form from Garrick's earlier blows.

[[Cora Flint]] drifted nearby, the [[Dreamstone Seal]] secured in its harness on her back. She made a quick notation in her ledger despite the chaos: *Three anchors active. Avatar damaged but guarding western point. Garrick re-engaging.* The numbers didn't lie — they were winning, but the cost was mounting. Mercer lay unconscious against the Caldera wall at 1 HP, having sacrificed his highest-level spell slots just to stay alive. She herself had been pulled into the [[First World]] and fought her way back, her artificer's mind clinging to practical realities as an anchor against the dreamscape.

"YOU'RE NOT WINNING TODAY!" Garrick roared, pushing off hard through the zero-gravity space. He didn't try to go around the Avatar — he went straight through it. The maul swung in a devastating arc aimed at the cracks he'd already put in the void entity's form.

The impact echoed through the Caldera with a thunderous crack that seemed to shake the very mountain. The containment runes on Garrick's maul blazed with golden light as they struck the crystallized void, and the Avatar's form shattered. Cracks spread like lightning across its body, and then the entire entity exploded in a cascade of darkness and light. Void energy dissipated into the sphere's boundary, recycled into the containment field. For one moment, the outline of the Giant it was shaped from was visible — [[Korathan]]'s silhouette, the architect, watching from within the dream. Then it was gone.

Silence fell, broken only by the steady hum of the three active suppression beams.

Cora fought her way back from the dreamscape, focusing on the hum of the Dreamstone Seal, on her ledger, on the practical realities that anchored her to the physical world. She snapped back to consciousness, gasping as she returned to the Caldera. Her eyes immediately assessed the situation: the Avatar gone, Garrick floating victorious but wounded, Mercer unconscious, the western anchor point waiting.

"All four anchors active. Suppression field at ninety-seven percent," she announced, making another notation. "Garrick, secure Mercer somewhere safe. I'm deploying the Seal."

She moved to the staging position fifty feet from the sphere's boundary, her Haste-enhanced speed carrying her smoothly through the weightless space. The Dreamstone Seal hummed in its harness, ready. She'd done the calculations — with the masterwork quality and [[Fire Gate]] precision, the deployment DC was effectively fourteen. Her Intelligence was twenty, giving her a +5 modifier. She should make this easily, but she'd still be careful.

Garrick secured Mercer against the Caldera wall, using debris to wedge him in place so he wouldn't drift. "Stay with him," he told Cora, though she was already moving toward the staging position. "I'll cover you."

He pushed off and followed her, his maul still in hand even though the Avatar was gone. Old habits died hard. He positioned himself between Cora and the sphere, scanning the drifting [[Deep Things]]. They were peaceful again, back to their slow orbits, but he didn't trust anything in this place.

Cora reached the western anchor point, placing her hand on the iron ring. The runes began to light up — a golden sequence tracing around the circumference. The western anchor point blazed to life. A fourth golden beam extended from the ring to join the others at the sphere's boundary. The four beams converged, forming a perfect suppression lattice around the sphere. The containment field hummed with renewed strength — 97% rated capacity according to the Monitoring Crystal. The suppression field was now complete.

The Caldera was still. The sphere pulsed steadily within its golden cage. The broadcast dropped to its lowest level since they'd entered the mountain — a distant whisper rather than a constant pressure.

"Anchor's active," Garrick called to Cora. "Now what?"

Cora checked the Dreamstone Seal in its harness. It hummed with contained power, ready for deployment. The sphere's boundary was undefended now that the Avatar was destroyed and the suppression field was complete. The deployment site was fifty feet away — the same staging position where they'd begun this final approach.

She reached the position, unclasping the Seal and holding the perfect sphere of adamantine and crystal in both hands. The Dreamstone's frequency locked onto the boundary's resonance instantly. The containment runes blazed with golden light, channeling the Seal's energy into the existing containment architecture. The Seal didn't pass through the boundary — it sank into it, becoming part of the membrane between realities.

The integration was perfect.

The containment field surged. Every rune in the Caldera, on the [[Giant Capstone]], through the [[Giant foundation level]], up through the dwarven and human layers, all the way to the surface, blazed with golden light. The mountain hummed with renewed purpose — a deep, resonant vibration that they felt in their bones.

The Monitoring Crystal, still in Cora's hand, read: **containment field at 99% rated capacity.** Higher than any reading they'd ever seen. Higher than the Giants' baseline measurements on the blueprint tablet. The Seal hadn't just reinforced the containment — it had improved it beyond the original design.

The sphere pulsed — steadier now, calmer, its boundary smooth and undisturbed by the Seal's integration. The broadcast dropped to near-zero. The Deep Things drifted in slow, peaceful orbits. The golden beams from the four anchor points shone steadily, the suppression lattice holding perfect geometric form.

**The Seal was deployed. The containment held.**

Garrick watched as the light faded to a steady glow. "It's done," he said, his voice quiet in the sudden stillness.

Cora made a final notation in her ledger: *Dreamstone Seal deployed. Containment field at 99%. Masterwork quality confirmed. Project complete.* She closed the ledger with a soft snap.

Mercer drifted in his unconscious state, but even in his wounded haze, he could feel the change — the mathematical perfection of the reinforced containment, the harmonic stability that hadn't been there before. His theories were correct, and now they were reality. The academic part of him wanted to observe, to document, to understand the precise moment when the containment field reached full strength. But his body was broken, his magical reserves depleted. He'd sacrificed his ninth-level slot — his Wish — to stay alive. A fair trade, he supposed. Better to be a living disgraced academic than a dead proven theorist.

Garrick looked at the sphere, now peacefully contained within its golden cage. "Two hundred thousand years," he muttered, thinking about what that meant. A home that lasted longer than empires. A place beyond [[Emberfell]]'s reach. They'd earned it.

Cora secured her ledger, her mind already calculating the next steps. Medical supplies for Mercer, payment from the contract, the journey back to [[Grimhold]]. The numbers added up — they'd succeeded, against odds that would have broken lesser crews. The [[Emberfell Rejects]] had done what armies and empires had failed to do for millennia.

The Caldera was silent now, save for the steady hum of perfect containment. The [[First World Sleeper]] dreamed on, but its prison was stronger than ever. The mountain that had been a threat for generations was now a monument — to Giant engineering, to dwarven craftsmanship, and to three exiles who'd refused to accept that some things were impossible.

Somewhere above them, in the fortress they'd claimed as their own, a home waited. But for now, in the quiet aftermath of victory, there was only the hum of containment holding, and the knowledge that they'd changed the world.
