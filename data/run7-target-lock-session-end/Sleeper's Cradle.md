The Sleeper's Cradle is the central containment chamber at the bedrock level of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], constructed by the ancient [[Giant]]s.

## Description and Function
According to the [[Giant Blueprint Tablet]], the Cradle is the heart of a complex architectural prison designed for millennial integrity. It incorporates pressure valves and energy conduits to contain the [[Sleeper]], the psychic entity that is the source of the mountain's corruption. The chamber is the ultimate source of the psychic broadcast that permeates the mountain's strata.

## Relationship to Other Layers
The [[Dwarven Layer]] was built directly above the Cradle as a secondary barrier. Evidence suggests the dwarves of [[Kazad-Khrom]] were aware of the foundation level and may have been drilling toward it.
