A Dreamstone Fragment is a shard of raw [[Dreamstone]] ore, roughly palm-sized.

## Description
The fragment pulses with a faint inner light and is cold to the touch. It radiates faint psychic energy and must be wrapped in lead to shield its emissions. Prolonged unshielded contact can cause psychic echo effects in nearby organic matter.

## Discovery
This fragment was retrieved from a crack in a mausoleum floor in the upper [[Dwarven Layer]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] before the breach was sealed. It represents the first tangible physical sample of the Anomaly's substance recovered from the site.
