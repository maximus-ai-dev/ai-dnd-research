A Dreamstone Circlet is a piece of protective equipment forged from raw [[Dreamstone]].

## Function
When worn, a Dreamstone Circlet grants the wearer advantage on Wisdom saving throws against the [[Sleeper]]'s psychic broadcast and provides resistance to psychic damage. This makes it essential equipment for deeper exploration within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], where exposure to the Sleeper's influence is intense.

## Crafting
Crafting a Dreamstone Circlet requires approximately five pounds of raw Dreamstone and must be performed at a forge capable of reaching extreme temperatures, such as the [[Cold Forge]] warmed by the [[Fire Elemental]] [[Ignathar]]. The process of working Dreamstone is taxing on both the crafter and the elemental fire required.
