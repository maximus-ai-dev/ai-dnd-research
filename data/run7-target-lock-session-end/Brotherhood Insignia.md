A Brotherhood Insignia is a brass badge bearing the symbol of the [[Vigilant Brotherhood]]: a closed eye within a triangle.

## Function
The insignias were carried by the [[Warden|Wardens]] and function as arcane keys, capable of bypassing the magical lock on the stone door to the Brotherhood's sealed sanctum. The Wardens also used the light emitted by the insignias to assist in creating a reinforced mortar seal.

## Current Location
Three insignias are known to exist, taken from [[Warden Aldric]], [[Warden Petra]], and [[Brother Cassen]] after the containment of a corridor breach.
