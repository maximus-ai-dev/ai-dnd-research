The Broadcast Zone is an area of intense psychic energy surrounding the sphere of the [[First World Sleeper]] within the [[Caldera]].

## Characteristics
In this zone, the Sleeper's psychic broadcast penetrates all psychic shielding, directly interacting with conscious minds. The environment is perceived through an augmented reality layer where sound becomes visible as ripples, containment runes appear as bars of light, and the [[Deep Things]] resolve into almost recognizable forms.

## Effects
Within the Broadcast Zone, concentration is severely hampered, psychic damage is amplified, and each individual perceives a personalized [[Psychic Echo]]—a manifestation conjured from their own memories, fears, and desires. The broadcast intensity increases exponentially as one approaches the sphere.
