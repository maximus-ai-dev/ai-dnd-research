The Grand Archive is the hidden sanctum and primary repository of the [[Vigilant Brotherhood]], located forty feet below the fortress's [[Salt-Vaults]] preparation kitchen.

## Structure and Layout
Access to the Archive is granted only by presenting three [[Brotherhood Insignia]] at a magically sealed door. The interior is divided into three sections: a [[Central Hall]], an East Wing containing the primary collections, and a restricted West Wing. The entire complex is shrouded in a structural magical darkness that severely limits artificial light.

## Contents and Significance
The Archive was intended to house centuries of Brotherhood scholarship, including their research on the 'Deep Resonance' and records pertaining to the [[Iron Warlords]]. It is guarded by the Brotherhood's most dangerous creation, the [[Amalgamation]].

## Current Status
The Archive remains sealed and largely unexplored, its secrets protected by its guardian and the pervasive darkness.
