The Dreamstone Power Cell is a concentrated energy source recovered from the wreckage of the [[The Drowned Juggernaut]].

## Description
It is a fist-sized sphere of crystallized [[Dreamstone]], encased in a lead shell to contain its radiation. The cell pulses with residual psychic and arcane energy, indicating it served as the Juggernaut's primary power source.

## Potential Applications
The cell contains enormous energy. It could be installed into the fortress's defensive systems to power magical alarms and barriers, or studied to gain deeper insight into the nature of the [[First World Sleeper]] and Dreamstone radiation. Such study, however, carries the risk of psychic feedback and disturbing nightmares.
