The Dwarven Radiation Survey Notes are a set of records contained within a sealed iron box.

## Content
Written in Dwarvish, the notes contain a systematic survey of [[Dreamstone]] radiation levels throughout the upper [[Dwarven Layer]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. They annotate which crypts are 'active' and which are 'stable,' serving as a monitoring log for the containment system. The notes also provide a partial map of the upper delve and identify the [[Cold Forge]] as a significant deeper landmark whose fires went out centuries ago.

## Discovery
The notes were found near the entrance to a lead-sealed mausoleum in the upper Dwarven Layer.
