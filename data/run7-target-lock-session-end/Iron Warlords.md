The Iron Warlords are an ancient human civilization that constructed the original fortress within the upper layers of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Legacy and Architecture
This pre-Calamity civilization built extensive subterranean complexes characterized by brutalist, pragmatic architecture designed for perpetual siege defense. Their ruins, including the [[Salt-Vaults]], form a foundational layer above the later [[Military Empire]] constructions.

## Deeper Connections
Research by the later [[Vigilant Brotherhood]] indicated that mysterious phenomena, which they termed the 'Deep Resonance,' were intensifying and climbing toward the level occupied by the Iron Warlords' ruins. The upper section of the [[Grand Stairwell]] is of Iron Warlords construction, retrofitted into an older opening.
