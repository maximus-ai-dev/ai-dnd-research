Korathan is the Master Runecarver of the [[Architect's Council]] and the primary architect of the containment vessel known as the [[Vessel of Dreaming Silence]], which holds the [[First World Sleeper]].

## History and Works
He spent three centuries designing and constructing the containment system deep within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. Upon its completion, Korathan voluntarily entered a state of suspended animation, referred to in Giant tradition as "the long watch," to serve as a final contingency should the containment ever fail.

## Legacy
His recorded messages, technical notes, and scaling rune techniques were left within his tomb to guide future caretakers. His silhouette was momentarily visible within the dissipating [[Avatar of the Slumber]].
