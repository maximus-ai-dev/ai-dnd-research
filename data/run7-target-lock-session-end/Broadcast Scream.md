A Broadcast Scream is a potent psychic attack emitted by entities deeply corrupted by void energy and the influence of the [[First World Sleeper]]. It directly projects the Sleeper's broadcast frequency into the minds of targets.

## Effects
The attack typically manifests as a cone of psychic force, inflicting significant psychic damage and potentially inducing fear or disorientation by flooding the target's mind with visions of the void and the Sleeper's influence. The [[Reanimated Warden]] employed this ability in its defense of the breach in the [[Warden's Post]].
