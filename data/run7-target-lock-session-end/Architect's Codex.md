The Architect's Codex is a comprehensive tome containing diagrams, notes, and technical specifications related to [[Giant]] engineering and the containment architecture of the [[Caldera]].

## Contents
The Codex includes detailed maps of the Caldera's cylindrical void, the locations of structural anchor points, the [[Observation Platform]], and the [[Fire Gate]]. It also documents the harmonic principles of the containment rune network and the scaling rune techniques developed by the Giant artificer [[Korathan]].

## Current Status
The Codex is in the possession of [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]], who consults it to interpret Giant technology and guide repairs to the containment system.
