The Fire Gate is an ancient conduit within the [[Caldera]] that can open a temporary aperture to the [[Elemental Plane of Fire]].

## Function and Design
It is part of the [[Giant]] containment architecture, designed to provide a controlled flow of elemental heat for precise artifice work. The Gate's opening is controlled by specific runes and requires precise arcane timing to operate safely within the containment vessel.

## Utility
Its use provides access to unlimited elemental heat, which is beneficial for high-temperature crafting processes such as the calibration of [[Dreamstone]].
