The Apprentice Workshops are a series of smaller forge stations located within the [[Dwarven Layer]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], situated adjacent to the main [[Cold Forge]] chamber.

## Description
These workshops contain individual workstations equipped with bellows, anvils, and quenching troughs, all sized for dwarven apprentices. The tools present, including hammers, tongs, and measuring gauges, are coated in mineral dust but remain fundamentally sound due to the superior quality of dwarven steelcraft.

## Function
The area served as a training ground and facility for smaller-scale metalwork, feeding materials and skilled labor into the larger industrial production processes of the primary forge complex.
