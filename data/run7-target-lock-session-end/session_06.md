# Session 6: The Leaking Crypt

**Date:** Early Spring, 15th of Thaw

The metallic sweetness arrived with the spring thaw—a subtle poison carried on mountain winds that gave miners headaches and nightmares. When [[Reyna Steelforge]] received reports from her prospectors in the [[Starmetal Hills]], she dismissed them as spring vapors from thawing mineshafts. But when [[Cora Flint]], [[Garrick Kade]], and [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] returned from the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] with the [[Giant blueprint tablet]], the truth became terrifyingly clear: the mountain wasn't just a mountain, but a prison, and something was leaking from its deepest layers.

---

In the practical warmth of the [[Prospectors' Guild]] office, Reyna listened as Mercer explained their discoveries. The [[Military Empire]] trophy halls sat atop a [[Dwarven Layer]], which itself rested upon Giant containment architecture. The metallic sweetness wasn't spring vapors—it was Dreamstone radiation, seeping upward through failing seals.

[[Warden Aldric]] stood by the window, his face pale. "If the dead are speaking too," he said quietly, "the entity's reach has grown."

Cora spread the blueprint tablet across Reyna's desk, her fingers tracing containment monitoring runes. "The metallic sweetness is a contaminant reaching the surface. That means the containment seals are failing at an accelerating rate." Her clinical assessment was chilling in its precision. She requested a fifty-gold advance for specialized equipment—respirators, testing reagents, emergency sealants.

Garrick leaned against the wall, his maul resting against his shoulder. "Filters and tests are fine. But when something's leaking, you find the leak and plug it. Fast." His urgency resonated with Aldric's grim expression.

By dusk, they stood before the mountain entrance once more, armed with modified mining respirators from [[Elara Stormwind]] and quick-setting sealant for emergency repairs. The air carried a faint metallic tang—stronger than days before.

The descent through familiar Military Empire halls felt different this time. They bypassed the trophy rooms, heading directly for the Giant stairway discovered in their previous delve. The transition from human to dwarven architecture was immediate: iron brackets the size of wagon wheels lined walls carved with geometric patterns of [[Kazad-Khrom]] design.

Forty feet down, the stairway opened into a landing. The corridor stretched east and west, wide enough for three dwarves abreast. Garrick's trained eye noticed the discoloration first—stone darkened as if stained from within, radiating from a point deeper in the eastern corridor.

Cora's contamination vials confirmed it. The reagents changed to deep violet immediately. "Dreamstone radiation," she reported, her voice muffled by the respirator. "Concentrated enough to register at this distance."

They followed the contamination gradient eastward, through residential sections sized for people four feet tall. Low stone beds carved into alcoves, communal cooking hearths, a bathhouse with stone basins stained black with glassy resin that had seeped from below. The metallic sweetness grew stronger with every step.

Then they heard it—a low, rhythmic sound like breathing, coming from the same direction as the strongest radiation concentration.

*"Filters are degrading faster than projected,"* Cora warned. *"At this concentration, we have approximately twenty minutes of effective protection remaining."*

Garrick ignored her caution. *"Twenty minutes? Then we don't have time for searching."* He raised his maul and shouted down the corridor, *"HEY! Whatever's down there making that noise—come and get it!"*

The breathing stopped abruptly.

Mercer found the emergency intervention rune cluster almost immediately—three interlocking geometric patterns marking maintenance access. *"Garrick, you absolute fool! You're announcing our presence to psychic echoes preserved in radiation-saturated matter!"*

But it was too late. Figures emerged from the eastern corridor.

Dwarves. Or what were once dwarves. They moved with stiff, mechanical precision, ceremonial armor gleaming with the same iridescent sheen as the stone walls. Their skin was perfectly preserved, veins darkened to black beneath the surface. Their eyes were solid black, shot through with iridescent veins.

The lead figure wore captain's ceremonial plate and carried a war-pick that glowed faintly with blue-purple light. Behind him, three soldiers in corroded chain mail gripped rusted axes. They didn't attack. They simply stood there, facing the party.

The captain opened its mouth. The voice that emerged was flat, toneless, speaking Dwarvish in a loop: *"Seal the lower halls. The dreaming is too loud. Seal the lower halls."*

Mercer recognized them immediately from the [[Brotherhood]] notes: [[Psychic Echoes]]. Not undead, not intelligent—preserved memories playing back in radiation-saturated tissue.

Cora's vials glowed fiercely. *"Radiation levels spiking! Their presence is concentrating the Dreamstone energy. Our filter time just dropped to fifteen minutes!"*

Garrick charged forward, his body swelling with Giant's Might as runic energy coursed through him. His maul swung in a wide arc, but the captain echo parried with eerie efficiency, its war-pick meeting stone with a ringing impact.

The soldier echoes advanced, axes rising in unison. Cora worked frantically at the maintenance access mechanism while Mercer shouted instructions. *"Try Containment first, then Maintenance, then Emergency!"*

As axes bit into Garrick's armor, Cora rotated the second rune. The mechanism clicked softly. She was about to rotate the third when the captain echo reached her.

It didn't attack. Instead, it placed a black-veined hand on the stone panel. The third rune rotated on its own to "Emergency."

The panel slid open, revealing a narrow maintenance shaft. The captain stepped back, resuming its loop: *"Seal the lower halls. The dreaming is too loud. Seal the lower halls."*

It wasn't attacking. It was helping—replaying its memory of accessing this shaft centuries ago.

Garrick shoved the soldier echoes back with a powerful sweep of his maul, creating space. *"Get through the shaft! I'll hold them here!"*

They squeezed into the narrow passage, the stone panel grinding shut behind them. The shaft sloped downward, the metallic sweetness pouring through like a river of scent. Fifty feet later, they reached another panel marked with warning runes that glowed with faint blue-purple light.

Mercer translated: *"Containment Breach—Level 4 Radiation—Authorized Personnel Only—Lead Seals Required."*

The panel was slightly ajar. Through the crack poured not just scent, but visible vapor—faintly iridescent, shimmering in the spectral mind's light.

Cora's vials glowed so brightly they illuminated the entire shaft. *"Radiation levels are critical. Our filters won't last five minutes at this concentration."*

Garrick forced the panel open. A wave of iridescent vapor billowed out, thick and choking.

The chamber beyond was a mausoleum. Stone niches lined the walls, each containing a perfectly preserved dwarven body. Fourteen in total, laid out in ceremonial armor, hands folded over weapons. But the horror was in the floor—a crack running through the center, deep and narrow, from which poured the vapor and the breathing sound.

This was the mountain breathing. Or something beneath it.

Cora's contamination vials shattered from radiation overload. *"Radiation levels are off my scale! Our filters are failing—maybe two minutes left!"*

Then the psychic pressure hit—an alien consciousness pressing against their minds. Mercer staggered, overwhelmed by sensations of immense weight, sound like breathing through stone, momentary certainty that they were dreaming and had always been dreaming.

One of the dwarven bodies moved. The captain in ceremonial plate sat up slowly, opened its black, iridescent eyes, and spoke: *"Seal the lower halls. The dreaming is too loud. Seal the lower halls."*

Cora moved toward the crack, ignoring the stirring bodies. *"Filters failing! We have ninety seconds!"* She applied quick-setting sealant along the crack's edge, working with desperate efficiency.

Garrick positioned himself between her and the echoes, using his maul to create a rubble barrier. *"Stay back! Let her work!"*

Mercer fought through psychic disorientation to identify structural weaknesses. *"Anchor point! Structural failure there! If we can reinforce it..."*

The captain echo rose from its niche. It didn't join the soldiers. Instead, it walked to the crack, looked down into the darkness, and spoke a new phrase: *"The dreaming stops. The dreaming stops."*

Then it placed a hand on Cora's shoulder—steadying her—and pointed to a specific spot on the crack where the sealant hadn't reached.

Cora applied the final layer. The compound flowed into the deepest part of the crack, hardening instantly into a glassy black plug.

The vapor flow stopped abruptly. The breathing sound grew frantic, then fell silent.

The effect was immediate. The psychic pressure lessened. The soldier echoes lowered their axes. The captain echo looked at the sealed crack, then at Cora. *"The dreaming stops. The dreaming stops."*

Then all four echoes collapsed—not destroyed, but spent. The psychic energy that animated them had been cut off at the source.

But Cora's respirator filter was gone. Radiation sickness set in—nausea, dizziness, a cold fire spreading through her veins. Before the sealant fully hardened, something crystallized at the crack's edge: a palm-sized shard of raw [[Dreamstone]] ore, pulsing with faint inner light.

They retrieved the shard, an iron box containing [[Dwarven Radiation Survey Notes]], and the captain's glowing war-pick—inscribed with the name [[Torvan Deepwatch]].

The retreat was agonizing. Cora weakened with every step. Garrick constructed a stretcher with efficient movements, carrying her at a ground-eating pace through the foothills. Mercer administered anti-nausea tincture, but the radiation damage was severe.

They reached [[Grimhold]] in just over two hours. [[Brother Aldwin]] met them at the [[Chapel of the Hearthfire]], his healer's instincts recognizing radiation sickness immediately. As he mixed charcoal purification draughts and prepared dreamroot extract, Warden Aldric arrived to examine their findings.

The survey notes revealed the true scale: multiple "active" crypts throughout the dwarven layer, and a deeper landmark called the [[Cold Forge]]—the dwarves' primary industrial center, its fires extinguished centuries ago.

Cora, stabilized by Aldwin's treatments, managed to speak from her bed. *"The seal... will hold eight to twelve years. But the structural weakness... remains."*

They had succeeded. The metallic sweetness had stopped reaching the Starmetal Hills. The breach was contained. They had retrieved the first physical sample of Dreamstone ore and critical intelligence about the dwarven layer.

But the survey notes showed this was just one of dozens of monitoring points. The Cold Forge represented the next logical step—and potentially, the next breach waiting to happen.

The mountain had been quieted, but only temporarily. The dreaming below continued, patient and eternal, waiting for the next crack to appear in the seals that held it.
