The Twisted are corrupted beings that serve the [[Sleeper]] within the depths of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
They appear as corrupted dwarves, their forms warped by prolonged exposure to the Sleeper's psychic broadcast. They exhibit black-veined skin, glowing eyes, and an aura of psychic corruption. They are resistant to poison and necrotic damage but vulnerable to radiant energy.

## Organization and Tactics
On the [[Giant foundation level]], the Twisted operate in an organized military structure. They maintain patrol routes, fixed guard positions, and a communication system using bioluminescent pulses through resin conduits in the walls. Known unit types include [[Twisted Laborer]]s and [[Twisted Sentry|Sentries]], commanded by a [[Twisted Overseer]]. Their primary mission is to protect and maintain their infrastructure, including a biological drill aimed at breaching the [[Giant Capstone]] containing the Sleeper.
