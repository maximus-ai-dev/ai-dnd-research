A Resonance Seam is a natural geological formation within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] where the [[Sleeper]]'s psychic radiation has crystallized the bedrock into raw [[Dreamstone]].

## Description
These seams appear as veins of crystallized stone that glow with an inner light, shifting through blues, purples, greens, and golds. They typically run through the architecture of the [[Giant foundation level]], often threading through and disrupting the ancient containment runes. The Dreamstone within a seam feeds on the containment energy, warping and redirecting the runic fields.

## Hazards
Mining a Resonance Seam releases concentrated psychic energy, causing hallucinations, perception distortions, and dangerous gravity anomalies in the surrounding area. The closer one gets to an exposed vein, the more reality frays at the edges. The seam may also defend itself through the manifestation of a [[Dreamstone Sentinel]] when disturbed.
