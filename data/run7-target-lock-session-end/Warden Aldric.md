Warden Aldric is a preserved member of the [[Vigilant Brotherhood]] who guards the approach to their sanctum within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Role and Knowledge
As a Warden, Aldric's authorization level did not permit him to enter the Brotherhood's [[Grand Archive]]. He possesses basic knowledge of the Archive's layout and confirmed the existence of its guardian. He stated that the Brotherhood never explored below their own level, fearing what lay beneath. Following the reactivation of the surface fortress, Aldric and his fellow Wardens have adapted to surface operations.

## Recent Involvement
After the discovery of the [[Giant Blueprint Tablet]], Aldric helped interpret the Brotherhood's notes regarding the mountain's containment architecture. He became visibly shaken upon learning that psychic echoes of the dwarven dead were active in the lower layers, interpreting it as a sign that the imprisoned entity's reach has grown.
