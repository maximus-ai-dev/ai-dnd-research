Rolf is a quiet, competent former bandit now part of the workforce at the fortress in the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Skills and Role
He serves as a scout and explorer, possessing a keen eye for detail and a stoic, observant nature. Rolf is often the first to discover points of interest, such as hidden passages or architectural features.

## Key Actions
Rolf discovered the collapsed stairwell leading from the fortress down into the [[Iron Warlords]]' Layer. He subsequently assisted in exploring the complex and combating the mutated giant rat infestation within.
