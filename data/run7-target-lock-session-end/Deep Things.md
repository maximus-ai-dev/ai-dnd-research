Deep Things are transient phenomena that manifest within the [[Caldera]]. They are expressions of the [[First World Sleeper]]'s dreaming—fragments of its consciousness given temporary form by the ambient void energy.

## Description
They appear as geometric, polyhedral shapes roughly three feet across, with crystalline surfaces that shift between transparency and opacity. They are not conventional creatures with intent or aggression but manifest out of curiosity.

## Behavior
They drift toward disturbances in the Sleeper's psychic broadcast or unfamiliar presences. They move with silent grace in the zero-gravity void of the Caldera.
