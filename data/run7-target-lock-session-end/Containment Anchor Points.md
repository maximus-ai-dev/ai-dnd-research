The Containment Anchor Points are four massive iron rings built into the [[Caldera]] wall at the cardinal positions. They form the structural foundation of the containment field's suppression lattice.

## Function
When activated using scaling runes, each anchor point emits a beam of golden containment energy that extends toward the sphere containing the [[First World Sleeper]]. When all four are active, they create a suppression field that weakens void-corrupted entities, imposes disadvantage on their attacks and saves, and partially suppresses the Sleeper's broadcast.

## Strategic Importance
Activating all four anchor points is a prerequisite for safely deploying the [[Dreamstone Seal]] at the sphere's boundary.
