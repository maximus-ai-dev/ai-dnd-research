Brenna is a scarred, pragmatic woman and the de facto leader of the former bandits serving as the primary workforce at the fortress in the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Role and Demeanor
She operates with a logistics-focused mindset, managing the practical needs of the fortress's inhabitants. Brenna communicates facts and urgencies, such as critical resource shortages, with direct certainty and without panic. She organizes work parties to secure and transport supplies and informs visitors of operational timelines.
