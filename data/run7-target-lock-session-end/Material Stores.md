The Material Stores are a series of enormous chambers within the [[Dwarven Layer]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], located on the path to the [[Cold Forge]].

## Description
The chambers feature iron-railed shelving carved directly into the walls, designed for storing raw ore and materials. Most shelves are empty, but a few contain stockpiles of iron, copper, and tin ore. One sealed bin was found to contain approximately forty pounds of unworked [[Adamantine Ore]].

## Function
These stores were part of the industrial infrastructure supporting the Cold Forge, allowing the dwarves of [[Kazad-Khrom]] to stockpile materials for years of production.
