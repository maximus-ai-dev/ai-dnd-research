The Emberfell Rejects are a band of desperate bandits originally from the [[Thieves Woods]] near Emberfell, currently led by [[Kregg]].

## History and Motives
Exiled or having fled from their homeland, the Rejects crossed the ocean to Laternia. They are not treasure hunters but survivalists, historically raiding supply caravans and the town of [[Grimhold]] for food, tools, and winter provisions. Their camp was established at the peak of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] in the [[Starmetal Hills]].

## Composition and Condition
The band consists of 10 to 15 individuals, poorly armed and equipped. They are described as exhausted, cold, and scared, operating more like a desperate refugee camp than a military outfit, with loyalty maintained through Kregg's coercion and control of resources.
