# Session 13: The Fleshy Amplifiers

*From the Chronicles of the Asymmetrical Mountain, as recorded by an observer in the shadows*

---

The [[Cold Forge Chamber]] had become a familiar sanctuary over three days of respite, its orange pulse from [[Ignathar]]'s elemental heart casting dancing light across stacked [[Adamantine Ore]]. In that mineral warmth, the three saboteurs prepared for their most dangerous mission yet. [[Cora Flint]] distilled raw [[Dreamstone]] into refined shielding components with clinical precision. [[Garrick Kade]] sharpened his maul with the steady rhythm of a smith who knows violence is coming. And [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] transcribed every scrap of data from the Giant blueprint tablet and Monitoring Crystal, his spectral quill dancing across parchment.

The forward camp in the [[Giant foundation level]] remained secure, but the readings hadn't improved. The [[Twisted]]'s first amplifier might be inert, but the broadcast continued. And now, Mercer's cross-referencing revealed something more troubling.

"Three primary broadcast towers," Mercer announced, spreading the Twisted engineering schematics from the Brotherhood Archive across the Cold Forge's worktable. His finger traced lines connecting to the blueprint tablet's map. "What the [[Twisted]] call '[[fleshy amplifiers]]'—not just boosting the [[Sleeper]]'s signal, but directing it upward, parasitizing containment rune energy to push through the mountain."

Three locations glowed ominously on the map: [[Amplifier Alpha]] in the northwest quadrant, [[Amplifier Beta]] southeast, and [[Amplifier Gamma]] due south—closest to the inner void, the most powerful.

"The Monitoring Crystal shows broadcast intensity increasing," Mercer continued, his voice grim. "If we don't destroy these amplifiers, they'll eventually push the [[Sleeper]]'s influence through containment into upper layers. Everything we've secured—the fortress, [[Grimhold]]—will be exposed."

Cora examined the biological details. "Organic structures. Grown, not built. Sever the root tendrils cleanly, and the amplifier dies. Sever them crudely..." She tapped a warning note. "...and it triggers a broadcast burst that alerts every [[Twisted]] in the area."

Garrick hefted his maul. "So we find them, cut them at the roots, and get out before they know we're there."

*Efficiency versus force,* the chronicler observes. *The same tension that has carried them through twelve adventures in this mountain.*

---

## The Hunt for Alpha

Descending through familiar routes—the [[Dwarven Layer]] corridors, the silent [[Eastern Transit Hub]], into the colder depths of the [[Giant foundation level]]—they found their forward camp untouched. The psychic hum intensified even through their [[Dreamstone Circlet]]s, a constant pressure against their minds.

Mercer's patrol pattern analysis revealed the rhythm of their enemy: thirty-minute cycles, permanent guards at each amplifier. "[[Amplifier Alpha]] is closest—four hundred feet northwest. Two [[Twisted]] Sentries guard it permanently. The patrol gap is twelve minutes."

Garrick moved ahead with surprising stealth for a half-orc in chain mail, his enforcer experience letting him stick to shadows. Through Mercer's [[Manifest Mind]], they saw the sentries: two corrupted dwarves in heavy chain, standing motionless at a corridor junction. Behind them, through the opening, pulsed something organic—a pillar of black resin grown into stone, threaded with bioluminescent veins.

"No distractions," Garrick growled when Cora suggested separating them. "Two sentries, fifty feet. I can cover that distance before they finish turning."

He exploded forward, activating [[Giant's Might]] as his body swelled with ancient power. But the [[Twisted]] were faster than they looked. The left sentry parried his maul blow, and both struck back—warhammers slamming into Garrick's side and shoulder. Seventeen pounds of force drove the air from his lungs, and their [[Corruption Aura]] washed over the corridor like a physical blow.

Worse followed: [[Amplifier Alpha]] pulsed violently in response to the violence, its veins flaring bright enough to cast sharp shadows.

"Garrick: seventeen damage, still functional," Cora calculated even as psychic energy lanced through her own mind. "Amplifier: reacting to combat, risk of broadcast burst. Priority: sever roots before amplifier triggers."

She dashed past the melee, entering the junction to see Alpha—a pulsating organic pillar with root-like tendrils burrowing into containment runes. Her hands remained steady as she applied precision acid gel to the root-stone interface.

But as the left sentry falls, something changes in the corridor. The resin conduits in the walls pulse violently—a rapid sequence of bioluminescent flashes racing away from the junction. **The Twisted communication network has registered the sentry's death.** From deeper in the corridor, a heavy figure emerges.

---

## The Overseer's Wrath

From deeper in the corridor, a heavy figure emerges. It's the Twisted Overseer from your previous encounters—or one identical to it. It ignored Garrick entirely, its black-veined eyes fixed on Cora at the amplifier's base. Its warhammer swung at her back, but she ducked, the weapon striking stone with a shower of sparks.

Mercer completed his [[Slow]] spell, arcane energy washing over the area. The remaining sentry struggled against temporal distortion, its movements becoming sluggish.

Garrick's maul connected with the [[Twisted Overseer]]'s armored side with a thunderous impact. "You don't touch her!" he roared, interposing himself between the Overseer and Cora.

But the Overseer's [[Corruption Pulse]] tore through them—necrotic energy that froze Cora and Mercer in place, muscles locking, minds reeling. Garrick shook off the stunning effect through sheer force of will, his [[Indomitable]] feature allowing him to reroll the failed save.

He saw Cora frozen, Mercer struggling, and made his decision. His maul came around in a brutal counterattack, smashing through the Overseer's guard. The commander staggered back, black fluid pouring from multiple wounds.

Then Mercer's [[Fireball]] exploded.

The corridor filled with roaring flame. The remaining slowed sentry incinerated instantly. The [[Twisted Overseer]] was hurled back against the wall. When flames cleared, it was still standing—barely—but made no move to pursue. Instead, it turned and walked away into darkness, moving with purpose rather than retreat.

[[Amplifier Alpha]] collapsed inward with a sound like cracking bones, organic material crumbling without the energy that sustained it.

*Silent. Clean.*

But the silence didn't last. Resin conduits in the walls pulsed violently—alarm signals racing through the network. The [[Twisted]] communication system had registered Alpha's destruction.

---

## Into the Depths

They retreated through a side corridor Garrick spotted, finding a partially open Giant maintenance hatch crusted with centuries of mineral deposits. The hatch groaned loudly as Garrick forced it wider, alerting pursuit, but they descended into a cylindrical maintenance shaft just as [[Twisted]] reinforcements arrived above.

The shaft opened into a [[harmonic dampening pump room]]—vast, circular, dominated by three silent pump housings coated in dust. Giant-sized pipes snaked across the floor, and the air held the faint metallic scent of ancient machinery.

"[[Harmonic dampening pumps]]," Mercer whispered, brushing dust from a control panel. "Used to regulate containment field resonance. If we could get one operational..."

But pursuit was close. They chose a collapsed corridor, Garrick clearing debris with enforcer's precision while Cora and Mercer worked at the pump controls. Wheels stiff with age turned with steady pressure, Cora using her [[Flash of Genius]] when the modulation threatened to slip out of phase.

The third wheel clicked. A deep hum began—so low it was felt more than heard. Lights flickered on control panels that hadn't illuminated in millennia. Clear, cool energy filled the air, and the psychic pressure diminished. Their [[Dreamstone Circlet]]s went almost silent.

Above, the [[Twisted]] investigating the shaft reacted with startled cries, then rapid retreat. The dampening field had been detected—and it confused them, perhaps even hurt them.

They slipped through the cleared corridor as sounds of organized search echoed behind them.

---

## The Geological Revelation

The maintenance corridor opened into a vast [[geological survey chamber]], circular with a domed ceiling fifty feet high. Around the perimeter, massive crystal lenses pointed toward the chamber's center where a complex three-dimensional map floated—a holographic projection of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]'s internal structure.

The projection showed all layers they'd explored, continuing deeper to strata beneath the foundation—a vast empty space labeled in Giant runes: "[[Sleeper's Cradle]]." And around it, three pulsing red dots: [[Amplifier Alpha]] (now dark), [[Amplifier Beta]], and [[Amplifier Gamma]].

"A real-time geological survey map," Mercer breathed, approaching the projection. "The Giants used this to monitor containment architecture."

But as they studied it, something changed. The four [[Twisted]] Sentries they'd evaded appeared as moving red dots—turning back, retracing steps. Worse, another group appeared from [[Amplifier Beta]]'s direction—six more [[Twisted]], responding to the alarm.

They descended a spiral ramp marked "Core Sample Access" just as sentries entered the chamber above. At the bottom, they found a Giant geological station deep beneath the foundation level—borehole rigs standing silent, core samples littering the floor, crystalline monitoring equipment glowing with soft blue light.

One wall held a massive geological cross-section display. And there at the bottom, beneath the [[Giant foundation level]], was the [[Sleeper's Cradle]]—with [[Amplifier Beta]] and [[Amplifier Gamma]] still glowing brightly.

They rested there, wounds treated, studying the intelligence they'd gathered. A borehole terminated just thirty feet from [[Amplifier Beta]]'s chamber—through solid rock, but drillable.

---

## Beta's Fall

Cora's acid test on a core sample from beneath Beta showed promise: shale matrix dissolving, leaving quartz veins intact as natural reinforcement. "Acid will work," she confirmed, "but we need to follow quartz veins precisely. One misapplication could collapse the tunnel."

Twenty minutes of meticulous work created a zigzag tunnel following the quartz lattice. Through a hairline crack in remaining rock, they saw Beta's chamber—larger than Alpha's, the amplifier column integrated with Giant monitoring equipment that pulsed in time with bioluminescent veins. Six sentries: two at the entrance, four patrolling.

Mercer's [[Manifest Mind]] signaled the three-second window when both entrance sentries looked away.

Garrick burst through, handaxes flying. Both sentries down in under two seconds.

Beta was different—more advanced. Organic structure grafted directly onto Giant crystalline instruments, creating a hybrid of flesh and machine. Root tendrils pulsed with synchronized rhythm suggesting collective intelligence.

"More complex," Cora whispered at the base. "Multiple primary roots, redundant systems. Severing one might not be enough."

Garrick moved silently through the chamber, eliminating patrolling sentries one by one. Mercer cast [[Dispel Magic]] on the crystalline equipment, creating feedback that destabilized redundant systems.

But Beta fought back. Even as primary roots dissolved, secondary systems activated. Bioluminescent veins flared to life, rerouting energy. The amplifier stabilized, and organic material began regenerating—thin tendrils extending from severed ends.

Then the side exit groaned. "Patrol! Four [[Twisted]] Laborers! Ten seconds!"

Garrick planted himself in the doorway. "Fifteen seconds. They'll get through me in thirty."

Cora worked frantically, acid flowing along secondary clusters. "The redundancies are the problem! If I can sever the main energy distribution nodes..."

Mercer abandoned subtlety. "Energy surge—yes! But we need to overload the entire harmonic matrix!" [[Chain Lightning]] arced from his fingertips, forking through monitoring crystals. They shattered with a sound like breaking glass.

Beta's veins flared violently, then darkened from the center outward as the energy surge overwhelmed regenerative capacity.

"Retreat!" Cora yelled.

They scrambled through the narrow tunnel as sounds of pursuit erupted behind them. Back in the geological station, they collapsed the tunnel entrance.

The display confirmed it: [[Amplifier Beta]]'s marker was dark. [[Twisted]] forces swarmed the location, movements chaotic.

But [[Amplifier Gamma]]'s marker glowed brighter than ever. And around it, [[Twisted]] forces consolidated, not scattered. The [[Twisted Overseer]]'s distinctive marker was there—fortifying, not retreating.

"The broadcast intensity has dropped significantly with two amplifiers destroyed," Mercer reported. "But Gamma is compensating—its output has increased. And the [[Twisted]] are concentrating their entire remaining force there."

Cora leaned against a borehole rig, breathing heavily. "Two down. One to go."

Garrick cleaned his maul, expression grim. "They know we're coming. And they'll be ready."

*The chronicler sets down his quill, the ink still wet. Two amplifiers fallen, but the third—the most powerful, the most defended—awaits. The [[Twisted]] have learned their enemy is a hunter. Now they prepare for ghosts. The final assault on [[Amplifier Gamma]] will determine whether the [[Sleeper]]'s influence breaks containment, or whether three saboteurs can silence a mountain's heartbeat.*
