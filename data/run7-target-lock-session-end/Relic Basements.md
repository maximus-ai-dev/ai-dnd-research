The Relic Basements are a series of interconnected trophy halls within the [[Military Empire]] layer of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Purpose
These halls were designed to store and display physical evidence of the Empire's conquests. They include the [[Hall of Conquered Peoples]], the Hall of Military Honors, and the Hall of the Giants. The basements were built to be walked through and admired, serving as a monument to imperial power.

## Contents
The halls contained artifacts from annexed civilizations, including dwarven, Giant, and other extinct cultures. While many artifacts were evacuated, significant items remained, such as the [[Giant Blueprint Tablet]], military commendations, and a Giant-era pressure valve mechanism. The structural integrity of the basements is precarious due to the weight of overlying construction stressing the ancient foundations.
