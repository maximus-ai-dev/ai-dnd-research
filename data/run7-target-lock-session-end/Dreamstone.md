Dreamstone is a unique crystalline substance formed from the crystallized psychic radiation of the [[Sleeper]].

## Properties
It emits potent psychic energy and radiation, which can cause hallucinations, perception distortions, and localized gravity anomalies. In its raw, unshielded state, it is highly dangerous. The radiation has a preservative effect on organic matter and can slowly transmute other materials over long periods. The dwarves of [[Kazad-Khrom]] understood its dangers and used lead shielding for containment.

## Formation and Use
Dreamstone forms in geological formations called [[Resonance Seam]]s, where the Sleeper's radiation has been concentrated against bedrock for millions of years. It can be mined and crafted into powerful items such as [[Dreamstone Circlet]]s for psychic protection and weapons capable of harming the Sleeper's manifestations. The [[Twisted]] utilize Dreamstone radiation to power their bio-mechanical creations, including the biological drill constructed to breach the [[Giant Capstone]].
