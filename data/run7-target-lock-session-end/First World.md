The First World is a primordial state of reality that existed before the gods reshaped the current multiverse.

## Nature and Legacy
It was an age of reality with different fundamental laws, where geometry was fluid and the boundary between solid and void was negotiable. Entities native to this state, such as the [[Sleeper]], possess consciousness and forms incompatible with the current order of reality. Their mere existence in the present multiverse warps local reality.

## Connection to the Sleeper
The [[Sleeper]] is a remnant of the First World. Its dreams and psychic broadcasts sometimes cause mortals to perceive fragments of this lost reality—visions of impossible geometries, living stone, and a sense of depth and time measured in geological epochs. The [[Giant]]s imprisoned the Sleeper because its natural state of being was incompatible with, and threatened to unravel, the stable reality established by the gods.
