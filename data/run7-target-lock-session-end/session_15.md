# Session 15: The Deep Quarantine

*The Cold Forge Chamber had become a place of industry again. For two weeks, the rhythmic clang of hammers on adamantine echoed through the dwarven halls as [[Cora Flint]]'s team worked the newly reignited forge. Dreamstone fragments were being shaped into protective Dreamstone circlets, lead-lined containers were being crafted for transport, and the Reality Anchor—a complex device of containment runes and Dreamstone stabilizers—sat nearly complete on a workbench. But beneath the industry lay a growing dread, measured in days and feet of remaining stone.*

## The Accelerating Threat

Cora stood before the Twisted Communication Node, her eyes shadowed from lack of sleep but her focus absolute. For two weeks she had monitored the signals from below, mapping patterns and tracing the flow of the organic network deeper than anyone had gone before. The intelligence was not reassuring.

"Staging ground," she announced to [[Garrick Kade]] and [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]], her voice flat. "They've consolidated their remaining forces eight hundred feet below. Thirty to forty [[Twisted]], including multiple [[Twisted Overseer]]s." She pointed to a massive organic construct on the display. "The drill. They call it 'the bore.' It's biological. Growing. And it's already thirty feet into the capstone."

[[Garrick Kade]]'s hand tightened on his maul. "How long?"

"At current boring rate? A month. Maybe less." Cora highlighted a thirty-foot section of corridor where a damaged resin conduit created a blind spot. "This gap will close within twenty-four hours. It's our one chance to approach without triggering their alarms."

The debate that followed was a clash of methodologies. Cora's cost-benefit analysis favored a surgical strike through the conduit gap. Garrick's warrior instinct demanded immediate action. Mercer's academic precision insisted on proper structural reinforcement. But when the monitoring station flashed an update—the drill accelerating, breakthrough now estimated at twenty days—the debate ended.

"We split the difference," Cora decided, her mind racing through calculations. "We infiltrate during their shift change, find an observation point, wait for my elixirs to stabilize, then strike during the next rotation."

But the timeline kept compressing. Ten to fourteen days. Then eight to twelve. The Sleeper wasn't just becoming aware—it was actively assisting the drill, creating a psychic feedback loop that accelerated the boring exponentially.

## The Compromised Preparation

The [[Cold Forge Chamber]] became a hive of frantic activity. Cora catalyzed her elixirs, cutting stabilization time from two hours to forty-five minutes but reducing potency by fifteen percent. Mercer inscribed containment runes based on Korathan's scaling runes, completing three tablets instead of four with placement tolerance dropping from three inches to eight. Garrick drilled the volunteers one last time, changing the diversion timing to match their accelerated schedule.

Brenna, the young dwarf volunteer, would lead the surface distraction. "Make noise like the whole [[Grimhold]] army is coming down that tunnel," Garrick growled. "But don't actually go down. Stay alive."

As the forty-five minute mark approached, the organic display flashed its most alarming update yet: "ESTIMATED BREAKTHROUGH: 10-14 DAYS. PREPARE FOR CONTAINMENT BREACH PROTOCOLS."

The Sleeper was helping its jailers dig its way out.

## Infiltration and Alarm

They moved through the Giant foundation level with the Dreamstone circlets glowing against the growing psychic pressure. The air hummed with energy, a palpable weight that made breathing difficult. They reached the conduit gap—a thirty-foot blind spot where the damaged resin conduit created their window.

But at the staging ground entrance, two Twisted Sentries stood guard, visibly enhanced by shimmering psychic energy. Their movements had an unnatural, jerky speed, black fluid pulsing through bulging veins.

Garrick didn't wait for subtlety. His maul crunched into the first Sentry's head before it could react. Cora's acid vial hit the second, dissolving organic armor. But the damage was done—the first Sentry's cry echoed through the staging ground, and resin conduits along the walls flashed from steady green to strobing red.

The alarm was raised.

## The Bottleneck Battle

They rushed through the bottleneck—a fifteen-foot corridor that narrowed before opening into the drill chamber. Mercer immediately cast Globe of Invulnerability, the shimmering sphere erupting around them. The effect was dramatic: four [[Twisted Laborers]] and two more Sentries who were charging toward them suddenly stumbled as their psychic augmentation winked out.

"Now they're merely dangerous instead of supernaturally augmented," Mercer announced, already casting his second spell. Wall of Force materialized at the bottleneck's entrance behind them, sealing off the corridor. "Selective permeability established—Garrick's attacks can pass through, theirs cannot!"

Through the bottleneck's far end, Cora saw it: the biological drill, fifty feet long, shaped like an enormous boring worm with a rotating head of crystallized Dreamstone teeth. Around it, the Organic Power Hub pulsed with energy, guarded by two Twisted Engineers and the Twisted Overseer in master-crafted corrupted plate.

"Fifty-five seconds before the Globe drops!" Mercer called out, grabbing the containment rune tablets. "Cora, begin sabotage! I'll place the reinforcement runes!"

## Sabotage Under Fire

Cora moved past Garrick, smoke pellets creating cover as she dashed toward the drill. Her acid vials flew in precise arcs—one at the conduit junction, one at the drill's organic muscle tissue. "Acid applied to primary conduits and biological actuators!"

Garrick activated Giant's Might, his body swelling to fill more of the corridor. His maul crushed through Laborers, the Crusher feat sending them flying back. But the Overseer ignored him, focusing entirely on Cora, the saboteur.

Mercer placed the first rune tablet against the capstone, precisely eight inches from the bore hole's edge. The stone glowed as it bonded. "First reinforcement established!" He moved to the second position, but a Twisted Engineer blocked his path. A [[Fire Bolt]] forced it back, and Mercer placed the second tablet.

The Overseer's Corruption Pulse erupted, dark necrotic energy tearing through Garrick and Cora. Cora cried out as the pulse hit her, then staggered as the wounded Engineer's wrench-pick found a gap in her defenses. But her final acid vial was already in the air, splashing across the drill's Dreamstone teeth.

The crystalline interface began to crack and separate.

## The Cost of Victory

The Globe of Invulnerability flickered out. Psychic augmentation flooded back into the staging ground. But the drill was critically damaged—teeth detaching, actuators dissolved, conduits severed.

The Overseer made its tactical decision. It signaled the Twisted forces to hold their positions, falling back to defensive formations around the power hub and damaged drill. Pursuing the saboteurs would leave their primary objective vulnerable.

"Retreat!" Cora gasped, clutching cracked ribs. "Mission accomplished!"

They fell back through the bottleneck, Garrick taking a final blow from the Overseer's warhammer as he covered their escape. Mercer dropped the structural support Wall, and they ran through the narrowing conduit gap as repair teams worked frantically around them.

They reached the forward camp, Brenna and the volunteers rushing to meet them. Cora collapsed onto a crate, blood mixing with the black fluid from dissolving drill components. "Drill disabled... seventy percent reinforcement... thirty-foot vulnerable section..."

Garrick leaned against the wall, his leather armor shredded. "The Overseer's still alive. It'll rebuild."

Mercer pulled out his journal, his hand steady as he wrote. "We have won a battle. The war is below us, and it has been going on for fifty thousand years."

Then it came—a single, sustained note, deep and resonant, vibrating through the containment runes in the walls as it passed through the stone. Not loud, but pervasive. A sound from everywhere and nowhere.

The Sleeper had felt the drill's destruction. It was not grateful. It was not angry.

It was awake. Slightly more awake than it was yesterday.

The Cold Forge Chamber would see more industry in the days to come. More Dreamstone circlets to be shaped, more containment runes to be inscribed, more volunteers to be trained. But beneath it all, the single note continued to vibrate—a reminder that their victory was temporary, and the mountain's heart beat with a rhythm not their own.
