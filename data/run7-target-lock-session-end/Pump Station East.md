Pump Station East is a major dwarven hydraulic control station located north of the [[Eastern Transit Hub]] within the [[Dwarven Layer]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Function and Design
It is part of the infrastructure designed to drain the lower sections of the complex using geothermal pressure differentials.

## Current Status
The station is offline. The pumps have been systematically sabotaged, with intake valves fused shut by a black organic resin, critical pipes severed, and control mechanisms disassembled. The sabotage is the work of the [[Twisted]], who aim to prevent the drainage of the lower levels. Reactivating this station is a prerequisite for accessing the flooded deeper areas of the mountain.
