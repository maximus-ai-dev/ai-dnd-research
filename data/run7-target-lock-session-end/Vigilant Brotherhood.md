The Vigilant Brotherhood is a cabal of arcane scholars who operated in secret within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## History and Activities
The Brotherhood burrowed into the hollow spaces beneath the [[Iron Warlords]]' ruins to establish a hidden sanctum, the [[Grand Archive]]. There, they cataloged military doctrines, ancient runes, and studied a phenomenon they termed the 'Deep Resonance.' They abandoned the site approximately two centuries ago, leaving behind a powerful guardian, the [[Amalgamation]].

## Organization
The Brotherhood maintained a strict hierarchy, with ranks including Initiate, Scholar, Archivist, and Commander. Access to different areas of their sanctum was controlled by rank-specific insignias.

## Legacy
The Brotherhood's research, particularly regarding the Deep Resonance, is considered by scholars like [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] to be a discovery of major academic importance. Their former [[Warden|Wardens]] remain preserved within the mountain.
