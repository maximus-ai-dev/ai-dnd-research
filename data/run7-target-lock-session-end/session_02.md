# Session 2: The Salt-Vaults of the Iron Warlords

*Winter's first snow had settled upon the [[Starmetal Hills]] like a shroud of silence. For three days, the fortress stood as an island of precarious safety in the white wilderness, its new occupants—fifteen hungry souls and their unlikely leaders—facing a truth as cold as the mountain air: shelter without food is merely a slower death.*

---

## The Clock Ticks

On the fourth morning, [[Brenna]] found the party in the main hall, her scarred face etched with grim pragmatism. "Ten days," she stated, her voice carrying the flat certainty of logistics. "Maybe twelve if we cut portions again. After that, I can't keep them here."

Her gesture encompassed the courtyard where the former bandits huddled around meager fires—not soldiers, but desperate souls who had followed [[Kregg]] for shelter and now looked to new leaders for the same. The air was cold enough to see breath; through the open doorway, snowflakes drifted past the ancient standing stones.

It was [[Rolf]], the quiet scout, who offered the only hope. He pointed toward a collapsed timber wall at the hall's rear. "Found something. A stairwell, partially choked with rubble. Leads east and down—not toward the foul drafts from the west. This one smells like dust and old stone. Worked stone."

[[Cora Flint]] pulled out her ledger, making quick calculations with a charcoal pencil. "Ten days. That's our timeline." She looked at her companions. "We have three options: abandon the fortress and default on our debts, try to hunt in winter-stricken hills, or explore that passage." She closed the ledger with a snap. "Option three is the only one that doesn't end with us starving or bankrupt."

[[Garrick Kade]] grunted in agreement, hefting his maul. "Talking won't fill bellies." He strode toward the stairwell, his heavy boots crunching on stone dust as he began clearing rubble with methodical efficiency.

[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] produced his wizardly quill with a flourish. "A moment, if you please. Before we go charging into what could be a significant archaeological site." His fingers traced the stonework. "Not dwarven—the scale is wrong. This is [[Pre-Calamity Giants|Giant]] construction. Pre-Calamity. Look at the size of these blocks. This isn't a mine shaft—this is architecture."

## Descent into Defense

The stairwell descended sixty feet through the mountain's heart, opening into a corridor that was no crude tunnel. Smooth worked stone walls, a low ceiling that forced Garrick to duck, arrow slits at regular intervals, murder holes above—this was a defensive corridor designed to funnel attackers into kill zones.

Rolf held up a hand, his lantern casting long shadows. Faint scratching sounds echoed from somewhere deeper, accompanied by a faint, unsettling scent of ammonia.

"Hold position," Cora whispered, her eyes scanning the murder holes with professional assessment. "This is a prepared battlefield." She turned to Mercer. "Professor, I need you to use Detect Magic. Check for traps or wards."

Garrick ignored the tactical discussion. "Enough talking. That's not vermin—that's something moving." He charged down the main corridor, his chain mail scraping loudly against the low ceiling.

Mercer hissed in frustration. "Garrick, you absolute fool! This isn't a tavern brawl!" He closed his eyes, beginning the somatic gestures for his spell as Cora examined the arrow slits and Rolf identified the scratching as coming from two sources—multiple small creatures, not one large one.

## The Colony Awakens

Garrick's charge announced their presence like a thunderclap. At a junction where the corridor split, six pairs of glowing eyes appeared—giant rats, but wrong. Their fur had an iridescent sheen like oil on water, their eyes glowed with sickly yellow light, and they were larger than any rat should be. They fanned out with coordinated precision, blocking both passages.

"Magical radiation means they're resistant or enhanced," Cora warned, raising her hand as frost formed around her fingers. "Standard attacks might not be enough."

Garrick charged the right flank, his maul connecting with a sickening crunch. Mercer's thunderwave erupted, sending two rats flying backward to smash against stone walls. But the remaining rats attacked with coordinated fury, one sinking teeth into Garrick's leg armor while others swarmed toward Mercer.

"They're targeting the spellcaster!" Mercer shouted, backing against the wall. "Cora, they're learning!"

More scratching echoed from both corridors—reinforcements. The party was surrounded, with Garrick wounded and Mercer about to be attacked.

## Tactical Withdrawal

"Fall back to the right chamber!" Cora ordered, dropping to heal Garrick's wounds with golden magic. "It's our only defensible position!"

Garrick charged through the rat blockade, his maul creating a gap as Mercer fired magic missiles to clear their escape. They scrambled into a guardroom—a defensible space with only one entrance to defend.

Inside, a faded map on the wall showed a schematic of corridors, barracks, and a large area labeled "The Salting Halls." Their objective was marked, but between them and the food stores stood the rat colony.

Cora treated Mercer's shoulder wound with a poultice. "The Salting Halls are our objective—food preservation. But those rats retreated to defend something." She examined the map. "We fortify this position first. We're not moving until everyone is patched up."

Garrick kept watch at the entrance. "They're smart. They retreated to regroup, not because they're scared. They'll be back with a plan."

Mercer studied the map with scholarly intensity. "The Salting Halls... Pre-Calamity Giant food preservation techniques were legendary. They used mineral salts combined with subtle cryomancy to keep meat edible for centuries." He identified a service corridor on the map—a maintenance access route that bypassed the main rat defenses.

## The Service Corridor Gambit

After thirty minutes of tense preparation—Cora creating smoke pellets, Mercer recovering a spell slot through Arcane Recovery—they took the service corridor. The narrow passage forced Garrick to turn sideways, his chain mail scraping against the stone walls.

Then the ambush came.

A rat dropped from a ventilation shaft directly in front of Garrick, sinking teeth into his leg. More rats dropped behind them, cutting off retreat. Ahead, the corridor opened into the [[Salting Halls]]—and at the center of the largest chamber, atop a massive stone silo, sat the [[Rat King]].

The bloated creature was the size of a large dog, with multiple tails fused together and iridescent fur that shimmered in the faint light. Around it, dozens of glowing eyes appeared in the darkness—the main colony.

"Garrick, push forward!" Cora shouted. "Through the rat and into the hall! We need space to maneuver!"

Garrick charged, smashing through the blocking rat and bursting into the Salting Halls. The Rat King emitted an iridescent shriek that echoed painfully through the stone halls, but Garrick's half-orc resilience shook off the stunning effect.

## Decapitation Strategy

The colony attacked en masse. Cora's healing magic flowed into Garrick as she created an obscuring smoke cloud. Mercer's thunderwave cleared a corridor through the swarm. Rolf fought rats at their rear, blood dripping from a bite on his arm.

"Garrick, target the Rat King!" Cora commanded. "Take out the leader and the colony might scatter!"

Garrick charged through smoke and swarming rats, his maul aimed at the bloated creature on its stone silo throne. The first blow connected with a sickening crunch of bone and flesh, black blood spraying from the wound. The Rat King shrieked in pain, reeling on its throne.

Mercer covered Garrick with magic missiles, knocking back rats trying to drag him down. "Make that strike count! We can't afford a prolonged engagement!"

Garrick's maul came down in a final, crushing blow. The Rat King's skull collapsed, and the creature went limp.

For a heartbeat, nothing happened. Then the colony broke.

## Victory and Discovery

The rats stopped their frenzied attacks, their glowing eyes darting from the dead leader to the party. A high-pitched squeal signaled retreat, and they vanished into ventilation shafts and side passages, their scratching sounds receding into the distance.

The [[Salting Halls]] were theirs.

Cora worked with efficient precision, cataloguing the stores: eight sealed stone crocks of preserved vegetables, three bags of rock salt, one grain silo with viable contents—enough to feed twenty people through winter. The Rat King's nest yielded treasure: thirty-five gold pieces in old coins, a silver ring, and a strange cloak that shifted colors and textures.

Mercer's magical examination confirmed the preservation was safe—ancient Giant enchantment woven with mineral salts, not corrupted by the rats' presence. "Centuries old but still viable," he announced.

As they prepared to transport the first load back to the surface, Mercer noticed another faded map partially hidden by nest material. This one showed the entire [[Iron Warlords' layer]]—barracks, armories, guardrooms, and something labeled "[[The Deep Forge]]" with passages leading deeper into the mountain.

"The Deep Forge," Mercer breathed, his academic awe genuine. "Arcane smelting facility. The source of the metallic corruption. This isn't just a fortress—it's a pre-Calamity manufacturing complex."

## The Return

Three trips passed without incident. By the time they returned to the preparation kitchen with the final load, the food crisis was solved. Cora's ledger contained meticulous notes: immediate rations secured, sustainable supply identified, fortress viability confirmed.

Garrick finally allowed himself to sit, leaning against a stone counter as exhaustion set in. "We did what we came to do," he said simply. "No one upstairs goes hungry tonight."

The service corridor stood open behind them, the path back to the stairwell and the waiting fortress above. The Salt-Vaults were secured. The rat infestation was cleared. The food supply was guaranteed.

But as they prepared for the final ascent, the map of the Deep Forge lingered in their minds—a promise of deeper mysteries, of arcane industry and contained corruption, of the true heart of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] waiting to be uncovered.

For now, they had earned their rest. The fortress would eat tonight. Their foothold was secure. But the mountain held deeper secrets, and the Iron Warlords' legacy was not yet fully revealed.

*The winter above would be survived. The hunger would be sated. But beneath the snow-dusted peaks of the Starmetal Hills, ancient forges slept in darkness, waiting for the curious, the brave, or the desperate to awaken them once more.*
