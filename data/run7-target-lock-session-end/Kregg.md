Kregg is the leader of the [[Emberfell Rejects]] bandits and a former associate of [[Garrick Kade]] from their shared past in Emberfell.

## Description and Personality
A heavyset enforcer, Kregg is characterized by his cruelty and practicality. Exiled from the [[Thieves Woods]], he maintains control over his band through intimidation and by hoarding the best supplies. He is a survivor who has chosen brutality as his method of leadership.

## Current Activities
Kregg commands the Rejects from a fortified camp at the peak of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], where he directs raids on [[Grimhold]] for survival goods. His history with Garrick Kade adds a personal dimension to any confrontation between the Rejects and adventurers seeking the bounty on them.
