The First World Sleeper is an ancient, transcendent entity from a reality with incompatible physical laws, contained deep within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Nature and Threat
It is a dreamer whose mere existence and dreaming exert immense psychic pressure upon the surrounding universe, unintentionally warping reality. The [[Giants]] constructed an elaborate containment vessel, known as the [[Vessel of Dreaming Silence]], not as a prison but as a translation layer that converts the Sleeper's reality-warping emissions into manageable frequencies.

## Manifestation and Status
Within the innermost [[Caldera]], the Sleeper manifests as a glowing, asymmetrical sphere roughly fifty feet in diameter, which is the point where its presence intersects with normal space. The entity remains dreaming and contained, its prison successfully reinforced by the deployment of the [[Dreamstone Seal]].
