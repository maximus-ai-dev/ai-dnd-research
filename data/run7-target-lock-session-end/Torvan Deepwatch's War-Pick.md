Torvan Deepwatch's War-Pick is a magical weapon of dwarven make.

## Description
The war-pick is a +1 weapon, originally belonging to the dwarven captain [[Torvan Deepwatch]]. It is inscribed with his name in Dwarvish. While within the radiation-saturated mausoleum, it glowed with a faint blue-purple iridescence, though this sheen fades once removed from that environment.

## Discovery
The war-pick was recovered from the mausoleum in the upper [[Dwarven Layer]] after the psychic echo of its owner dissipated following the sealing of a containment breach.
