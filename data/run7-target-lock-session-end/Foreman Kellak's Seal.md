Foreman Kellak's Seal is an iron signet bearing the administrative authority of the dwarven Foreman of Transit.

## Discovery and Function
It was recovered from the hoard of [[Kellashen the Behir]] in the [[Eastern Transit Hub]]. The seal likely functions as a key or authentication device for operating secure doors, control mechanisms, and other restricted systems within the dwarven transit and industrial infrastructure deeper in the [[Dwarven Layer]].
