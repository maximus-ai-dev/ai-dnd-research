The Deepwatch Garrison is a dwarven military outpost referenced in maintenance logs recovered from the [[Minecart Network]].

## Role and Status
It served as a destination for evacuation when the [[Eastern Transit Hub]] was sealed due to the behir threat. The garrison's current status and location within the [[Dwarven Layer]] are unknown, but its mention indicates it was part of the dwarves' security and logistical structure within the mountain.
