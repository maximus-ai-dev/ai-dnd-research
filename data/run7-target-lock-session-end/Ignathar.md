Ignathar is a sentient [[Fire Elemental]] bound to the [[Cold Forge]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## History and Binding
It was originally bound by the runesmiths of the dwarven hold to provide perpetual heat for their industrial forge, with the binding maintained through ancient dwarven runes. Its cooperation is essential for the forge's operation and the reactivation of the broader dwarven industrial infrastructure.

## Current Status
With the forge restored to full capacity—enabled by the operational cooling system and the [[Dwarven Master Forge Key]]—Ignathar's fire burns steadily. If freed, the elemental returns to the [[Plane of Fire]].
