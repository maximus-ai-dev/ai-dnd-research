Brother Cassen is a soldier of the [[Vigilant Brotherhood]] and one of three [[Warden|Wardens]] preserved in stasis within a hidden corridor of the [[Salt-Vaults]].

## Description and Demeanor
Cassen is the most human and openly curious of the three Wardens. Having had more time to process his situation while half-dormant, he is considered the easiest to reason with.

## Role and Current Status
Cassen awakened after [[Warden Aldric]] and [[Warden Petra]]. He asked direct questions about the passage of time and the fate of his commanders, and provided assistance in securing the breached corridor. He now lives and works alongside the other Wardens as a guard within the Salt-Vaults.
