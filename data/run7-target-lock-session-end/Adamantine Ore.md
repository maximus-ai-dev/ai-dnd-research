Adamantine Ore is the raw, unworked form of adamantine, the rarest known crafting material on the continent.

## Description
This ore appears as a crystalline, metallic mass. In its raw state, the ore is inert and cannot be worked without a forge capable of reaching extreme temperatures, such as the [[Cold Forge]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Significance
Once the [[Cold Forge]] is restored to full operational capacity, a forty-pound deposit of pure adamantine ore is sufficient to craft one full suit of adamantine armor or two adamantine weapons. Such a deposit was discovered within a sealed bin in the [[Material Stores]] of the [[Dwarven Layer]].
