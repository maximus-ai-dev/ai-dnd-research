The Cooling Channels are a network of stone corridors within the [[Dwarven Layer]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Function
The channels were designed to carry water from a reservoir above to cool the output of the [[Cold Forge]]. The channels are currently dry, and the air within them is warm. A Dwarvish inscription carved into the wall near a main junction references pump infrastructure that is currently offline.

## Significance
The dry channels represent a limitation on the forge's full operational capacity, as proper cooling is required for advanced metallurgy, such as working [[Adamantine Ore]].
