The Giant Inspection Circuit is a network of massive maintenance corridors and workshops within the [[Giant foundation level]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
These corridors are sized for [[Giant]] workers, with ceilings reaching forty feet or more. The circuit includes tool storage alcoves containing implements the size of small trees and workshop spaces with forge hearths large enough to park a house inside. The architecture showcases the casual enormity of Giant domestic and industrial infrastructure.

## Function
The circuit was designed for the monitoring and maintenance of the Sleeper's containment architecture. It allows access to various sections of the outer ring of the foundation level, including areas where [[Resonance Seam]]s form. The containment runes within these corridors often glow brighter and pulse in complex patterns near significant radiation sources.
