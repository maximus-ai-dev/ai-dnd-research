The Cold Forge Chamber is the vast industrial heart of the [[Dwarven Layer]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Contents
It houses the [[Cold Forge]], a massive dwarven construct designed for high-temperature metallurgy. The forge is warmed by the bound [[Fire Elemental]], [[Ignathar]]. The chamber connects to adjacent workshops and the eastern [[Minecart Network]], forming the core of the dwarven industrial base.

## Strategic Role
It serves as the primary strategic base for the [[Emberfell Rejects]], providing a fortified position, industrial capability, and a hub for further exploration. A controlled breach was cut through the [[Giant Capstone]] in this chamber, creating the access point to the [[Giant foundation level]] below.
