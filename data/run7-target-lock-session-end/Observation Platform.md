The Observation Platform is a stable, floating structure within the zero-gravity [[Caldera]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Function
It serves as a secure staging area and observation post for expeditions approaching the [[First World Sleeper]]'s sphere at the center of the void. The platform provides a fixed point of reference and relative safety from the intense psychic broadcast that permeates the Caldera.

## Construction
The platform is constructed from ancient debris and scaffolding left over from the [[Giants]]' original construction of the containment vessel. It is anchored to the Caldera wall and stabilized by the same containment field that maintains the void.
