The Capstone Resonator is a device that emits a pulse of containment-aligned energy, used to repair the [[Giant]] containment field.

## Function
The Resonator is designed to interface with the ancient containment architecture, providing a surge of structured magical energy that can reignite extinguished containment runes and disrupt hostile phenomena within the [[Caldera]]. Its pulse is particularly effective against [[Deep Things]], dissolving their crystalline forms.

## Usage
The device requires a significant recharge period after use. It was employed during the initial descent into the Caldera to restore a section of damaged containment runes.
