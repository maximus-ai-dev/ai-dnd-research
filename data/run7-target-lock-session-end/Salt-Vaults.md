The Salt-Vaults are a magically preserved food storage complex located within the fortress layers of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Construction and Purpose
Originally constructed by the [[Iron Warlords]], the vaults utilize a clay-limestone-salt mortar and enchantments to create a sterile, timeless environment for long-term food preservation. The complex includes a large silo and a preparation kitchen.

## Guardianship
The vaults are maintained and protected by the [[Stone-Tenders]], whose collective consciousness forms the [[Psychic Guardian]].

## Recent History and Current Status
During repairs to structural damage, a worker breached a false wall in the preparation kitchen, revealing a hidden corridor containing three preserved [[Warden|Wardens]] of the [[Vigilant Brotherhood]]. Following the containment of this breach, repairs were completed and the preservation magic was restored. The Salt-Vaults are now fully functional. The preparation kitchen is guarded by the three awakened Wardens, and the workforce has access to the preserved food stores, which provided rations during a recent period of quiet at the forward camp.
