A Void Tether is a regenerative connection formed between a void-corrupted entity and an active breach into the void.

## Function and Mechanics
This tether allows the entity to draw power directly from the void energy flowing through the breach, significantly enhancing its durability and strength. The strength of the tether is typically proportional to the entity's proximity to the breach. Severing this connection—often by destroying the corrupted control nodes that maintain it—is a critical tactic for defeating such entities, as it halts their regeneration and weakens their power.
