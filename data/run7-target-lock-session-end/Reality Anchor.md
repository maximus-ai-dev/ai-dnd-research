The Reality Anchor is a complex device of containment runes and [[Dreamstone]] stabilizers designed to create a mobile zone of stability against psychic radiation and gravitational distortion.

## Function
It projects a sphere of stable gravity, providing a fixed reference point for navigation in environments where gravity fails or rotates.

## Creation and Use
The Anchor was crafted in the [[Cold Forge Chamber]] to aid expeditions into heavily irradiated and physically unstable areas of the [[Giant]] foundation level. It proved essential for navigating the [[Gradient]] and establishing a secure base at the edge of the [[Caldera]].
