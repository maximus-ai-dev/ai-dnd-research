The Quarantine Zone is a sealed southern branch of the [[Minecart Network]], accessible from the [[Eastern Transit Hub]].

## Description and Warnings
According to the [[Dwarven Transit Map]], it is deliberately blocked by a collapsed and iron-reinforced stone barrier, marked with warnings against access.

## Significance
The zone's purpose and the nature of the threat it contains are not detailed in available records, but its connection to areas labeled for "deep drilling" and "interface with foundation architecture" suggests it leads toward critically sensitive or dangerous sections of the mountain's lower strata.
