The Central Hall is the main corridor within the [[Vigilant Brotherhood]]'s [[Grand Archive]].

## Description
It is a long, sixty-foot corridor with archways leading to the Archive's East and West Wings, as well as to a restricted vault. The hall is the primary patrol route of the Archive's guardian, the [[Amalgamation]], and bears extensive damage from the construct's centuries of activity. The space is afflicted by a profound magical darkness that halves the radius of all light sources.

## Current Status
The Central Hall remains dark and occupied by the Amalgamation.
