The Drowned Chokepoint is a massive, domed cavern located in the deepest accessible level of the [[Dwarven Layer]] beneath the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
Carved by dwarven engineers, the cavern served as the central convergence point for the entire layer's water management system. Massive drainage pipes enter the space from multiple directions, and the floor is a network of channels radiating from a central, elevated stone platform. The chamber remained submerged for centuries until the reactivation of the mountain's pump network.

## Twisted Occupation
As the water receded, it revealed that the [[Twisted]] had converted the dry central platform into a workshop. Here, they conducted bio-mechanical engineering projects, the most significant being the modification of the [[The Drowned Juggernaut]]. The workshop contained salvaged dwarven components, tools, and stone tablets etched with hybrid engineering schematics.

## Strategic Significance
The chokepoint is located directly above the [[Giant Capstone]], making it a critical strategic location. Its control is essential for both accessing the deeper Giant foundation level and maintaining the integrity of the containment sealing the [[First World Sleeper]] below.
