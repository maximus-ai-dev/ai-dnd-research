Kellashen, known by the dwarven term meaning "lightning coil," was a behir that occupied the [[Eastern Transit Hub]] for decades.

## Description and Capabilities
This twelve-legged, serpentine predator is an apex hunter, drawn to the hub by geothermal warmth and abundant cave fauna. Kellashen possessed formidable capabilities, including lightning breath, constricting attacks, and the ability to climb stone surfaces. Its territorial nature and knowledge of the tunnel network made it a persistent threat.

## Demise
The creature was ultimately defeated in the [[Cold Forge Chamber]], clearing the eastern branch of the [[Minecart Network]] for reactivation.
