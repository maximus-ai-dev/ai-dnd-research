The Caldera is a massive, cylindrical void located deep beneath the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], serving as the innermost layer of the containment vessel built by the [[Giants]] to imprison the [[First World Sleeper]].

## Description
Roughly two hundred feet in diameter and of unknown depth, the Caldera is a zero-gravity environment carved from bedrock. Its walls are covered in dense arrays of containment runes that cast a golden light. At its center lies the physical manifestation of the Sleeper—a glowing, asymmetrical sphere. The space contains floating debris and is periodically inhabited by [[Deep Things]].

## Key Features
Key features built into the Caldera wall include the [[Observation Platform]], four structural [[Containment Anchor Points]] at the cardinal positions, and the sealed [[Fire Gate]]. The containment anchor points were activated to create a suppression lattice, and the final confrontation with the [[Avatar of the Slumber]] occurred here.
