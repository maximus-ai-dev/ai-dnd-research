The Sentinel Mountains are a major mountain range forming the northern frontier near [[Grimhold]].

## Geography
The range serves as a formidable natural barrier, with Grimhold situated at its southern base. The mountains are a source of the cold winds that sweep down into the settlement and the adjacent [[Starmetal Hills]].

## Cultural Significance
The mountains define the northern limit of the settled frontier in the region, beyond which lies largely uncharted wilderness.
