The Gravity Well refers to the region within the [[Caldera]] and the transition zones above it where gravitational forces are distorted or absent due to the [[First World Sleeper]]'s influence.

## The Gradient
The zone directly below the [[Giant Capstone]] where gravity does not simply weaken but rotates. "Down" shifts by degrees, requiring navigation by climbing and crawling as surfaces become floors, walls, and ceilings. The [[Reality Anchor]] is typically deployed here to provide a stable gravitational reference.

## The Caldera Proper
Beyond the Gradient, gravity fails entirely, creating a true zero-gravity environment. Movement requires pushing off surfaces, drifting, and arresting momentum by grabbing handholds or containment rune arrays. The absence of "down" makes three-dimensional navigation essential.
