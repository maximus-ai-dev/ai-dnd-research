The Giant foundation level is the deepest known architectural layer within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], constructed by an ancient [[Giant]] civilization.

## Description
This level consists of massive corridors, chambers, and workshops built on a scale for beings several times larger than humans. It contains the primary containment architecture for the [[Sleeper]], including the [[Sleeper's Cradle]] (the inner void) and the outer ring where [[Resonance Seam]]s form. The entire level is inscribed with complex containment runes that glow with magical energy.

## Features
Key features include the [[Giant Inspection Circuit]], a network of maintenance corridors and workshops; the [[Giant Capstone]], which seals the level from the [[Dwarven Layer]] above; and various tool storage alcoves and forge hearths of staggering size. The environment is subject to reality distortions caused by the Sleeper's proximity and the presence of raw [[Dreamstone]].
