The Drills refer to massive, dormant dwarven excavation machinery located in the submerged lower [[Dwarven Layer]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
These are colossal, corroded constructs embedded in the tunnel floors, with individual mining mechs standing at least fifteen feet tall. The machinery is coated in a black organic resin used by the [[Twisted]].

## Current Status and Modifications
The Twisted have been modifying this machinery, though its current purpose and capabilities remain unknown. The machinery became visible as water receded from [[Pump Station East]].
