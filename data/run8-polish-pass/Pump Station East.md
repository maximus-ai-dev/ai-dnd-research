**Pump Station East** is a major dwarven engineering installation within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. It is designed to drain the lower Dwarven Layer using geothermal pressure differentials, channeling water out of the mountain via drainage conduits on the eastern slopes.

## Description and Current Status
The station is a circular chamber approximately sixty feet in diameter, dominated by three massive iron and stone pump assemblies arranged in a triangle. Each pump stands fifteen feet tall and operates using a complex system of pistons, valves, and control mechanisms. The station is accessed via an elevated platform above the waterline of the northern transit tunnel. The station was found to be completely offline, having been systematically sabotaged. The sabotage included intake valves fused shut with a black organic resin, critical pipes severed and removed, and control mechanisms deliberately disassembled. The station was successfully defended from [[Twisted Saboteurs]] and a [[Twisted Overseer]], and repairs were initiated to restore its function. Activating the pumps began the process of draining the lower Dwarven Layer, permanently altering the accessible geography of the mountain.
