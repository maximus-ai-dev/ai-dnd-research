**Korathan** is the Master Runecarver of the [[Architect's Council]] who designed the containment system for the [[First World Sleeper]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## History and Vigil
Korathan spent three centuries constructing the containment vessel. Upon its completion, he voluntarily entered a state of suspended animation, known as "the long watch," within his tomb, [[The Maker's Rest]]. This vigil serves as a final contingency should the containment ever require repair.

## The Tomb and Its Legacy
Korathan's tomb is located in the deepest section of the mountain's outer ring. It is protected by a gauntlet of traps and a guardian construct, all designed to test the understanding of any who would enter. Within the burial chamber, the walls are inscribed with his personal [[Scaling Runes]]—master techniques that allow containment magic to function at any scale. Korathan also left behind a recorded [[Architect's Message]], a psychic projection explaining the history of the containment, its 100,000-year designed lifespan, and his deliberate transfer of knowledge to the younger races he anticipated would inherit the responsibility of maintenance.
