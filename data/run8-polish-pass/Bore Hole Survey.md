The **Bore Hole Survey** is a set of measurements and observations taken from the shaft created by the Twisted's biological drill.

## Contents and Significance
It documents the drill's thirty-foot progress into the [[Giant Capstone]], the capstone's true thickness profile, structural weak points, and the nature of the translucent barrier stone at the shaft's bottom. The survey provides critical data on the containment's vulnerability, revealing that the barrier to the [[First World Sleeper]]'s domain is thinner than previously believed and that shapes move within it.
