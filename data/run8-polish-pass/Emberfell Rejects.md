The **Emberfell Rejects** are a band of desperate bandits operating near [[Grimhold]]. Originally exiles from the distant region of [[Emberfell]], they survive through theft and raiding.

## Activities and Composition
The group, led by [[Kregg]], has been raiding supply caravans from Grimhold, stealing survival goods like food, blankets, and tools rather than treasure. They number between ten and fifteen poorly armed individuals and have taken refuge in a ruined fortress at the peak of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. Members include reluctant individuals like [[Jace]], who joined out of sheer desperation.
