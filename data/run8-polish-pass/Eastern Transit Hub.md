The **Eastern Transit Hub** is a major transfer point within the restored dwarven minecart network of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and History
Located east of the [[Cold Forge]], the hub is a wide gallery where multiple rail lines converge at loading docks carved into the eastern wall. It served as the primary transfer point between deep mining operations and the forge complex. The area was heavily damaged during its long occupation by the behir [[Kellashen]], littered with bones, toppled cranes, and scorch marks.

## Current Status
Following the clearing of Kellashen, debris was removed and switching mechanisms were reset, restoring operation of the eastern branch of the network. A detailed dwarven transit map and a foreman's seal were recovered from its control room. The hub now serves as a strategic base and monitoring station for expeditions into the northern tunnels and the lower Dwarven Layer.
