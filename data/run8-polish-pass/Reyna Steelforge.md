**Reyna Steelforge** is the leader of the [[Prospectors' Guild]] in the frontier mining town of [[Grimhold]]. A practical and authoritative figure, she oversees the guild's operations, security, and logistics.

## Role and Responsibilities
Steelforge manages the town's mining and prospecting activities, posts bounties on threats, and coordinates supply lines. She is known for her direct, no-nonsense demeanor and focus on practical survival. Her initial contracts with adventurers involved dealing with the [[Emberfell Rejects]] bandit group and securing the [[Salt-Vaults]] beneath the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] to address a critical food shortage.

## Involvement in the Expedition and Shift in Focus
Steelforge has been the primary point of contact for the expedition into the Asymmetrical Mountain. She commissioned a structural map of the [[Military Empire]] layer, authorized the provision of reinforcement materials for the party's fortress after receiving their reports and the [[Giant Blueprint Tablet]], and supplied intelligence and resources for investigations into the [[Dwarven Layer]] and the [[Cold Forge]]. During an intermission, she arrived at the fortress personally with a team of [[Grimhold]] engineers, establishing a permanent guild presence and transforming the site into a formal research outpost. She receives the party's report on the [[Giant]] foundation level and authorizes the establishment of a forward research camp, naming the installation [[The Resonant Foundation]] at the party's suggestion. She later provides supplies and funding for the expedition to locate and extract raw [[Dreamstone]] from a vein within the Giant foundation. She also provides foundation stone samples and intelligence for the mission to destroy the [[Twisted]] broadcast amplifiers. Steelforge was briefed on the decaying containment field and the location of [[Korathan]]'s tomb, [[The Maker's Rest]]. The Architect's message describing the containment's 100,000-year lifespan and its halfway point struck her profoundly, causing her academic detachment to crack. She began shifting her Grimhold operation from pure research to active support for efforts to reinforce the containment. Following the destruction of the [[Twisted]]'s deep staging ground and drill, the revelation about the [[Giant Capstone]]'s true thickness and the shapes in the translucent stone shakes her deeply. She begins mobilizing resources beyond Grimhold, seeking experts like the [[Warden|Wardens]] and the [[Gatekeepers]].
