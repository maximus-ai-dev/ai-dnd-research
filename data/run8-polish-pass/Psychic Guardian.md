The **Psychic Guardian** is an enigmatic entity that holds authority over the [[Salt-Vaults]] and commands the [[Stone-Tenders]].

## Nature and Communication
The Guardian communicates telepathically, primarily through [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]], and only when its constructs are disturbed. It is aware of the [[Brotherhood]]'s hidden corridor and had sealed it from its side.

## Contract
The Guardian contracted the expedition to repair the vaults' structural integrity and preservation magic. After the breach of the sealed passage, it added a term to the contract requiring the passage to be re-sealed.
