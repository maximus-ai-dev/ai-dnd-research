**Rolf** is a quiet, observant, and competent member of the workforce at the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] fortress. He is skilled at reconnaissance and noticing details others miss.

## Discoveries
Rolf discovered the partially collapsed stairwell leading down to the [[Iron Warlords]]' layer and later led the team that cleared it. He was the first to report the constant scratching sounds emanating from the depths, providing the initial warning of the infestation within the [[Salt-Vaults]].
