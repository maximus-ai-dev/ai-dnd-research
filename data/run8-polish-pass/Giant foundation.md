The **Giant foundation** is the deepest known architectural layer within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], constructed by an ancient [[Giant]] civilization.

## Description and Function
This foundational layer contains the original temporal containment seals designed to imprison an entity older than [[First World Sleeper|the Sleeper]]. The architecture represents the oldest stratum of habitation and containment at the site. The Sleeper has been actively drilling through these seals in an attempt to breach the containment.
