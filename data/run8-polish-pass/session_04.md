# Session 4: The Guardian in the Dark

**Date:** 15th of Frostfall, 234 AC

*The air in the main hall of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] fortress hung thick with woodsmoke and simmering stew, but beneath those familiar scents lay something older—the smell of decisions made two centuries ago, now coming due. Three days had passed since the [[Brotherhood]] wardens emerged from their slumber, and now [[Garrick Kade]], [[Cora Flint]], and [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] stood before [[Warden Warden Aldric]], their three insignias cold in their hands. Below their feet, through forty feet of stone, something ancient and mechanical waited in the dark.*

---

## The Descent

[[Warden Warden Aldric]] stood with military precision, his [[Brotherhood]] robes bearing the same insignia design as the tokens the party now carried. "The sonic emitters were designed to clear vermin from the [[Salt-Vaults]]," he explained, his voice carrying the weight of preserved authority. "The system is controlled from the [[Grand Archive]]—forty feet below the preparation kitchen."

He produced a rough sketch on aged parchment. "The Archive is guarded by the [[Amalgamation]]—a flesh-golem constructed from Brotherhood dead. It cannot see. It navigates by sound and vibration. And it has been patrolling the same dark corridors for two centuries."

[[Warden Petra]] stepped forward, her voice quieter but no less intense. "The emitters are in the [[East Wing]]. The Amalgamation patrols the Central Hall. If you're quiet, you might reach the control panel without waking it." She didn't finish the sentence. She didn't need to.

[[Warden Cassen]], the youngest warden, added: "The darkness in the sanctum is magical. Light sources work, but their radius is halved. And the longer the Amalgamation stays in a space, the darker that space becomes."

The party prepared with grim efficiency. [[Cora Flint]] spread the floor plan on a table, making rapid calculations in her ledger. "Garrick, you're the heaviest—move deliberately. Mercer, can you cast something to muffle our footsteps?" [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] adjusted his spectacles, conjuring his quill to note the creature's likely weaknesses. "A *homunculus ex mortuis*," he murmured, "preserved organic matter through alchemical and necromantic means."

[[Garrick Kade]] grunted, tightening his chain mail straps. "If it comes to a fight, I'll keep it busy while you two get to the controls." His hand rested on his maul, the weapon that had served him through battles with the [[Rat King]] and now faced something far older.

They descended to the preparation kitchen, finding it cleared of recent infestation but holding the faint smell of vermin and decay. Cora discovered tallow candles wrapped in oilcloth—still serviceable after two centuries. Garrick found scratch marks on the stone floor, something heavy dragged toward the Brotherhood door long ago. Mercer cast *Mage Armor*, the shimmering field settling around his academic robes.

The interstitial corridor stretched ahead, ending at the Brotherhood door marked with a closed eye and archaic script that Mercer translated: *"The Grand Archive. Entry by rank only. The Amalgamation watches."*

---

## The Door and the Ward

Cora examined the door with meticulous care. "It's a detection ward," she announced, tracing the glowing arcane script with her tools. "Linked to the Amalgamation's awareness. When we open this door, it will know immediately."

Garrick lit the tallow candles, their smoky flames casting flickering shadows. "Speed over stealth, then. If it knows we're here anyway, better to move fast and hit hard."

Mercer conjured his *Mage Hand*, the spectral hand appearing to carry a candle ahead of them. "The hand will provide fifteen feet of effective illumination in the magical darkness," he warned. "We must stay close."

Below them, heavy footsteps echoed—the Amalgamation completing its West Wing check and beginning its return to the Central Hall. The creature was at its furthest point in the patrol cycle. Their fifteen-minute window would begin the moment they breached the sanctum.

"On my count," Cora said, holding the three insignias. "Three... two... one..."

The insignias clicked into place. The closed-eye symbol pulsed with blue-white light. The stone door ground inward with a sound like a held breath finally exhaling.

Cold, preserved air rushed out—old ink, old leather, and that faint animal warmth Warden Aldric had described. They stepped through into the antechamber.

---

## The Central Hall

The antechamber held personal effects left behind in a hurry. A half-written letter lay open on a stone bench. Mercer examined the rank-insignia board while Cora scanned the letter. "The Commander's logbook contains override codes," she reported. "And there's reference to secondary access through the [[Restricted Vault]]."

But the footsteps were growing louder. The Amalgamation had completed its East Wing check and was returning toward the Central Hall. They had seconds.

They moved into the Central Hall, and the magical darkness enveloped them like a physical weight. Mercer's *Mage Hand* with its candle now illuminated only fifteen feet ahead—the light struggling against the darkness.

The hall showed extensive damage: shelving units ripped from walls, stone benches shattered, massive claw marks gouged into the floor. A Brotherhood body lay in the center, wearing Commander-rank insignia. And from the East Wing archway, the footsteps grew louder with each passing second.

Then it emerged.

---

## The Amalgamation

The [[Amalgamation]] stood nearly ten feet tall—a horrific mass of preserved flesh and sinew, a patchwork of different skin tones and textures fused into one monstrous form. It had no face, no eyes, just a smooth expanse where features should be. It moved with terrifying speed, charging straight toward the loudest sound source: Garrick in his clanking chain mail.

Its first fist slammed into Garrick's chest with the force of a battering ram. The impact drove the air from his lungs and sent him staggering back. The second swing came in a wide arc, but Garrick deflected it with his maul.

Garrick didn't retreat. He roared and activated his Giant's Might—his body swelling to nearly twice its size, muscles bulging, his maul seeming to grow with him. He was now Large, towering even over the massive Amalgamation.

Cora darted past the combat toward the Commander's body. Mercer followed, his hands trembling as he searched the robes. "The logbook! And the insignia!" he called out, holding up the Commander-rank token.

Garrick's maul swung true, slamming into the Amalgamation's shoulder joint with devastating force. The preserved flesh cracked and splintered, black fluid oozing from the wound. The creature staggered but responded with mechanical fury, its second fist catching Garrick in the ribs with bone-jarring force.

Blood trickled from Garrick's mouth. He was badly wounded, but still standing.

---

## The Rage State

The Amalgamation changed. Its body swelled with dark energy. The cracks in its shoulder began to knit together with black, fibrous tissue. Its movements became faster, more aggressive. The magical darkness around it intensified—Mercer's *Mage Hand* now illuminated only three feet around it.

Cora threw an alchemical mixture onto the stone floor, creating a shimmering, vibration-dampening field. Mercer held up the Commander-rank insignia, shouting with as much authority as he could muster: "By the authority of the Brotherhood Commander, I command you to stand down!"

The insignia glowed with faint blue light, but the Amalgamation didn't pause. It was in its rage state now—the command protocols overridden.

The creature charged again. Garrick took another brutal hit, nearly going down. Cora cast *Healing Word*, stabilizing him. Garrick swung again, connecting with the damaged shoulder. Black fluid sprayed. The creature had taken significant damage, but it was healing, enraged, and the darkness grew thicker.

Then Mercer tried something different.

---

## The Giant's Tongue

Frantically flipping through the Commander's logbook, Mercer found the emergency deactivation sequence. Three command words in Giant. He read them aloud, his voice trembling but clear in the ancient language.

The Amalgamation froze. Not because of the command words—those didn't work in its rage state—but because of something else. The creature's head tilted, as if listening to something only it could hear. The black fluid stopped oozing. The rage seemed to pause.

For the first time, the Amalgamation turned its featureless face away from Garrick. It turned toward Mercer. Not to attack. To listen.

Mercer had spoken Giant—the language of the original architects of this mountain. The language the Brotherhood studied but never fully understood. And the Amalgamation, constructed from Brotherhood dead but containing ancient components, was responding to it.

The confusion was temporary, but it gave them a window.

"Garrick, disengage!" Cora shouted. "Mercer, keep speaking Giant!"

Garrick used his Action Surge to retreat to the East Wing archway, leaning against the stone for support, blood dripping from multiple wounds. Mercer kept up the Giant monologue, his pronunciation perfect, his delivery commanding.

But the confusion wouldn't last.

---

## The Final Stand

The Amalgamation shook off the last of its confusion and charged toward the East Wing archway where Garrick stood. Cora cast *Ray of Frost*, coating the creature's legs in ice, slowing its charge. Garrick met it with his maul, connecting with the already-damaged shoulder joint one more time.

Black fluid sprayed. The creature staggered but didn't fall.

Then the Amalgamation's fist connected with Garrick's chest with bone-crushing force.

Garrick was thrown back against the archway stone, his maul clattering to the floor. He slumped, unconscious, blood pooling around him. He was dying.

Mercer saw Garrick fall and knew this was the last chance. He darted forward, clutching the Commander-rank insignia, and reached the Amalgamation's back as it stood over Garrick's fallen form. He pressed the insignia against the creature's back while shouting the three Giant command words for deactivation.

The insignia glowed with brilliant blue light.

The Amalgamation froze. Completely. Its raised fist stopped mid-swing. Its body went rigid. The magical darkness began to fade immediately, retreating from the edges of the hall inward.

The creature didn't collapse. It stood frozen, a statue of preserved flesh and sinew, the insignia glowing where Mercer had pressed it against its back. The rage was gone. The movement was gone. The guardian had been deactivated.

But Garrick lay dying at its feet.

---

## Aftermath

Cora dropped to Garrick's side, casting *Cure Wounds*. Healing energy surged through his battered body, stabilizing him at 2 hit points—alive, but deeply unconscious.

Mercer carefully retrieved the Commander-rank insignia from the Amalgamation's back. The glow faded as he removed it, but the creature remained frozen. The magical darkness continued to fade until the Central Hall was fully visible in the candlelight—sixty feet of devastation, claw marks, shattered furniture, and the frozen Amalgamation standing like a grotesque statue over Garrick's unconscious form.

Consulting the logbook, Mercer confirmed: "The deactivation is permanent! The creature won't move unless we deliberately reactivate it."

They faced a decision. The East Wing archway stood clear, thirty feet away. The preservation chamber with the emitter controls waited behind a false wall. But Garrick lay at 2 HP, unable to be moved without risking further injury.

"We leave him here," Cora calculated. "Five-minute round trip maximum. The stasis is permanent."

Mercer placed the Commander-rank insignia on the floor beside Garrick. "If by some remote chance the stasis isn't as permanent as the logbook claims, the reactivation sequence requires this insignia. Leaving it with him ensures the creature remains frozen."

They moved into the East Wing corridor, finding it lined with empty stone shelves, water damage, and fungal growth. At the back, a section of wall looked different—smoother stone with tighter joints. The false wall.

Cora examined a faint arcane sigil. "Preservation ward, not a trap. Maintaining stable conditions behind the wall."

Mercer examined a bronze bookend shaped like a closed eye. "Rotate counterclockwise until it clicks, then depress the eye symbol. That should open the wall."

But the Commander's insignia—the guaranteed key—lay beside Garrick in the Central Hall. Their Scholar-rank insignias might work... or might not.

The decision hung in the air. They'd been gone from Garrick for nearly five minutes. The preservation chamber waited, containing the emitter controls that would clear the Rat King from the Salt-Vaults and secure food for their starving workforce.

Below them, somewhere in the mountain's depths, the Rat King still controlled the vaults. Above them, [[Brenna]]'s workforce waited for food. And between them, Garrick lay unconscious beneath a frozen guardian, while the means to save them all waited behind a wall that might or might not open to their touch.

*The chronicler notes: Sometimes victory comes not from destroying the guardian, but from understanding it. The Brotherhood built the Amalgamation to last two centuries, and so it has. But they also built in a key—a language, an insignia, a sequence of words. In the end, it was not Garrick's maul that won the day, but Mercer's scholarship. The warrior creates the opening; the scholar walks through it. Now, with their warrior down and their objective in sight, they face one final choice: proceed with what they have, or retrieve the sure key and risk the frozen guardian awakening. The Salt-Vaults wait. The Rat King waits. And time, as always, is running out.*
