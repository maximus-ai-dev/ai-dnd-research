The **Inspection Circuit** is the outer corridor system within the [[The Resonant Foundation]].

## Layout and Function
This perimeter walkway forms a ring around the foundation's central containment structure. It was used by the [[Giant]] builders to monitor the integrity of the containment field from outside the primary cage. The circuit is approximately thirty feet wide with a forty-foot ceiling, carved from single blocks of dark basalt. Its walls are covered in continuous, dense sequences of containment runes.

## Features
At regular intervals along the circuit are recessed monitoring alcoves. These chambers contain Giant-era instruments such as stone dials, crystal resonance chambers, and etched measurement scales designed to register containment field strength. The circuit also features crystalline observation windows that provide a view into the central containment void, and several massive sealed doors leading deeper into the structure.
