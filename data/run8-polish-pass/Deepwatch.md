The **Deepwatch** was the dwarven military garrison tasked with protecting the depths of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## History and Fate
The garrison was stationed within the [[Dwarven Layer]]. According to the final log of [[Foreman Kellak]], the Deepwatch had already fallen by the time he requested a hunter team to deal with the behir [[Kellashen]], indicating it was destroyed by the deeper threats within the mountain, such as the [[Black Growth]].
