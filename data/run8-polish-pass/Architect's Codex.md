The **Architect's Codex** is a comprehensive research tome compiled by [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]].

## Contents
The codex contains Mercer's accumulated research on the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], the [[First World Sleeper]], and the ancient [[Giant]] containment architecture. It includes data on containment runes, structural weak points, resonance frequencies, and the theoretical framework for containment technology, including the [[Scaling Runes]] learned from [[Korathan]]'s tomb.

## Significance
It serves as the primary technical reference and historical record for the Grimhold expedition, essential for understanding and countering the threats within the mountain. The codex is continually updated with new discoveries and is considered the campaign's most important scholarly document.
