**Borin Stonehand** is a former [[Master Engineer of the Deep Delvers]] who was transformed into a [[Twisted Overseer]] by the [[First World Sleeper]] to maintain the flooding of the lower levels of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## History and Transformation
Stonehand was captured at [[Pump Station East]]. He revealed that the flooding was an intentional containment measure for an entity older than the Sleeper. His transformation into an overseer was a result of the Sleeper's corrupting influence.
