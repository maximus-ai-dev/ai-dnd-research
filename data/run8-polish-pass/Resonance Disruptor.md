A **Resonance Disruptor** is a small chip of tuned [[Dreamstone]] designed to interfere with a target's psychic resonance.

## Properties and Use
The disruptor is crafted to be implanted into a target, temporarily suppressing its ability to regenerate or draw power from psychic energy sources. [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] crafted and implanted such a disruptor into the [[Dreamstone Sentinel]] to suppress its regeneration.
