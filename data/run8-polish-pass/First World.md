The **First World** is an alien, primordial reality that existed before the current cosmological order.

## Nature and Connection to the Sleeper
In the First World, consciousness is a fundamental force and physics follows dream-logic. It is a reality of impossible beauty where stone sings, light has weight, and consciousness is the fundamental force rather than gravity or electromagnetism. The [[First World Sleeper]] is a fragment of this reality, contained within the [[Caldera]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. The Sleeper exists within the First World, and the containment built by the [[Giant]]s acts as a wall between its reality and the current universe.

## Recent Observation
The party perceived the First World through the transparent boundary of the sphere containing the Sleeper during their close approach in the Caldera.
