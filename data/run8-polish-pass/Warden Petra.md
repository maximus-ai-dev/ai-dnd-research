**Warden Petra** is a technical specialist within [[Warden Aldric]]'s [[Brotherhood]] garrison.

## Description and Knowledge
Trained as a soldier, Petra is quick to act and adheres rigorously to protocol. She is equipped with standard Brotherhood-issue gear, including a hand crossbow. Awakened from two centuries of magical stasis, Petra provided the exact lever settings required to activate the sonic emitters located in the East Wing of the [[Grand Archive]]. Her strict adherence to duty is a product of her training, which is all that remains in a world that has moved on without her order.
