The **Grand Stairwell** is a 120-foot stairwell connecting the [[Brotherhood]] layer to the [[Military Empire]]'s [[Imperial Lower Palace]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Function and History
The stairwell provides the primary vertical access between these two major historical layers of the mountain complex. It represents a point of connection and transition between the arcane sanctum of the Brotherhood and the imperial monitoring station built above the ancient [[Giant]] containment.
