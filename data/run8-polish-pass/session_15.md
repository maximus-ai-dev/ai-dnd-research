# Session 15: The Deep Quarantine

*Chronicler's Note: The following account is reconstructed from monitoring crystal recordings, personal journals, and post-mission debriefings. The events described occurred approximately two weeks after the expedition to [[Korathan]]'s tomb, during what has come to be known as the Deep Quarantine Operation.*

---

## The Calculated Descent

The air in the forward camp's command chamber was cool and still, carrying the mineral scent of ancient stone that permeated the [[Giant foundation]]. For fourteen days, [[Cora Flint]] had stared into the dark, her monitoring station a tangle of organic resin conduits and crystalline lenses salvaged from the [[Twisted]] broadcast network. The intelligence she gathered was not reassuring.

"We have twenty-four hours," she announced, her voice clinical but her eyes holding the intensity of someone who had been measuring doom. She pointed to a vellum map spread across the central table. "The staging ground is eight hundred feet below us. Thirty to forty [[Twisted]], including multiple [[Twisted Overseer|Overseers]]. And they have a drill."

[[Garrick Kade]] leaned against the wall, his maul resting beside him. "So we go down there and break it."

"It's not mechanical," Cora clarified. "It's grown. Biological. They're boring into the [[Giant Capstone]] from below our position. Thirty feet through fifty feet of capstone. At current acceleration, they breach in a month."

[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] traced the containment lines on the map. "The [[Drowned Juggernaut]] was the frontal assault. This is the flanking maneuver."

The debate that followed was a study in contrasting methodologies. Cora calculated cost-benefit ratios and prepared elixirs. Garrick advocated for immediate, overwhelming force. Mercer analyzed tactical considerations and biological vulnerabilities. In the end, they descended together—a triad of conflicting approaches bound by shared necessity.

The corridor sloped downward, [[Giant]]-scale architecture becoming more pronounced. Ancient containment runes etched into the walls showed visible decay at their edges. After two hundred feet, the first signs of radiation appeared: a faint, shimmering haze like heat distortion over stone. Their [[Dreamstone Circlets]] hummed softly, creating protective fields against the psychic energy saturating the deep outer ring.

They reached the communication gap—thirty feet of darkness where a damaged resin conduit created a blind spot in the [[Twisted]] network. Cora's monitoring crystal showed patrol patterns. "Three minutes between patrols," she whispered. "That's our window."

But the psychic radiation pressed against their minds like a physical weight. Garrick felt nausea rising—a deep, unsettling sensation that had nothing to do with his stomach. Mercer's vision swam as crystalline growths on the walls pulsed in time with an alien rhythm. Only Cora's clinical detachment held, her mind processing the radiation as measurable data.

## The Sabotaged Conduit

Mercer examined the damaged conduit and made a troubling discovery. "This damage isn't random. Clean cuts in specific places. Someone sabotaged this intentionally to create the gap."

The implications hung in the charged air. Someone else knew about the staging ground. Someone else was monitoring the [[Twisted]]. Or worse—someone else wanted them to succeed.

From beyond the gap, heavy footsteps approached earlier than expected. A patrol was coming.

Garrick argued for immediate attack. "They're coming early because they know we're here. That sabotage wasn't for us—it was to lure us into a trap."

Mercer's spectral mind—a shimmering, book-shaped apparition—drifted forward to scout. "Four [[Twisted]] Laborers, one Sentry. They're not walking into an ambush. They're hunting."

Through the spectral mind, Mercer spotted something else: on the wall behind the patrol, etched in fresh, precise marks, was a symbol. Three intersecting circles with a line through the center. Not [[Twisted]] organic script. Not [[Giant]] runes. Something else—recent, deliberate, and placed where anyone coming through the gap would see it.

The debate ended when Garrick burst from the shadows, his body swelling with Giant's Might as he became Large. Combat was joined.

## The Overseer's Trap

The battle was brutal and costly. Garrick's maul crushed a Laborer, then critically hit the Sentry, pulverizing its skull. Mercer's Web entangled three Laborers. Cora's healing magic knit wounds even as she calculated the resource expenditure in her ledger.

Then the [[Twisted Overseer (Final Form)]] emerged from a hidden passage.

It was larger than before, clad in master-crafted corrupted plate armor that seemed to drink the light. It ignored Garrick and Mercer, heading straight for Cora—the saboteur, the amplifier killer. Its warhammer slammed into her shoulder with a sickening crunch of bone and alchemical glass. Black necrotic energy burned through the wound.

"Twenty-two damage," Cora gasped, already calculating healing costs even as she stumbled back.

The Overseer raised its free hand, and a wave of corruption pulsed outward. All the [[Twisted]] in the corridor glowed with crimson energy. Their eyes burned brighter. Their movements became more coordinated.

And the mysterious symbol on the wall pulsed violently, then released a burst of data—a psychic transmission racing through the resin conduits. Information about their tactics, damage output, spell choices—all being sent deeper into the staging ground.

"The symbol is transmitting data!" Mercer shouted. "It's gathering intelligence on our tactics!"

The battle became a desperate race against time and information. Garrick used Action Surge, his maul connecting with the Overseer's back in a spray of black fluid. Mercer's Fireball erupted, washing over the armored figure. Cora's Flaming Sphere rolled into position, fire washing over scorched armor.

But the Overseer shifted targets, its warhammer slamming into Mercer's side, cracking ribs. "The spellcaster... changes the variables," it ground out through a damaged vocal apparatus.

In the end, it was Garrick's Great Weapon Master strike combined with Cora's Arcane Jolt that finished the Overseer. The maul crunched through scorched armor and corrupted flesh, shattering the creature's spine. It collapsed in a heap of armor and twitching organic matter.

The glowing symbol on the wall is dark now—its data transmission complete.

## The Cost of Intelligence

In the aftermath, they recovered key items from the Overseer's body: a [[Twisted Command Signal Decoder]], [[Dreamstone Drill Fragments]], and a [[Bore Hole Survey]]. Cora administered healing—seventy-six points of damage between her and Mercer, a significant drain on their resources.

Mercer studied the survey with intense focus. "Fascinating. They're using harmonic resonance. The biological drill vibrates at a frequency that matches the [[Dreamstone]]'s natural resonance in the capstone. It's not cutting through stone—it's convincing the stone to disintegrate along harmonic lines."

They had a choice: press forward immediately while disruption from the Overseer's death might cause confusion, or take a short rest to heal and recover resources—giving the [[Twisted]] more time to analyze the transmitted data.

"Fifteen minutes," Garrick grunted. "Not an hour. They're off-balance now—we keep them that way."

## The Sleeping Barracks

The barracks chamber held fifteen [[Twisted]] resting in organic alcoves that seemed grown rather than carved. Resin conduits pulsed with faint light, feeding energy and neural connectivity to the resting creatures. Mercer was right—they were literally plugged into the Sleeper's network even while at rest.

Garrick's chain mail whispered with every movement. Halfway across the room, his foot came down on a loose stone with a soft *click* that echoed in the chamber.

One [[Twisted]] stirred, its crimson eyes flickering open.

Mercer created a diversion with Minor Illusion—the sound of shifting stone at the far end of the chamber. As the creature turned toward the false sound, Garrick closed the distance with surprising subtlety for someone in heavy armor. His handaxe flashed down in a short, brutal chop to the throat, severing vocal cords, arteries, and spine in one clean motion. He caught the body before it hit the stone floor, lowering it quietly back into its alcove.

They moved past the remaining sleeping [[Twisted]], through the armory with its [[Twisted]]-modified equipment, and finally to the organic power hub.

## The Harmonic Sabotage

The power hub was a nexus of resin conduits where communication and power networks converged. The room pulsed with rhythmic light, and a deep, resonant hum vibrated through the stone—the [[Harmonic Generator]] Mercer had identified from the survey data.

"We need to analyze it first," Cora insisted. "If we sabotage it blindly, we might miss the exact frequency patterns we need to destroy the drill."

Mercer began a rapid arcane analysis. "Base frequency of 147.3 hertz with harmonic overtones at multiples of 1.618—the golden ratio. Fascinating application of natural mathematics to destructive purposes."

But Cora's monitoring crystal flashed a warning. "Signal change. Something's querying the perimeter status. They're checking on the patrol we killed."

Mercer didn't look up from his analysis. "Thirty more seconds. I have the base frequency. I need the harmonic overtones."

Cora's fingers flew over the [[Twisted Command Signal Decoder]], attempting to mimic the patrol's "all clear" signature. Mercer completed his analysis and began casting **Shatter**, tuning the spell to exactly 441.9 hertz—the third harmonic overtone.

The generator convulsed as resonant feedback tore through its structure. Crystalline oscillators shattered. Resin conduits ruptured. The pulsating node collapsed in on itself with a deafening *crack* that echoed through the chamber.

And Cora's deception worked—the network received a false "all clear," buying precious seconds.

## The Drill Chamber Revelation

The drill chamber was massive, dominated by the biological drill—a fifty-foot-long worm-like construct of crystallized [[Dreamstone]] teeth and layered organic resin. It pulsed with a deep, grinding vibration that shook the stone beneath their feet. Four [[Twisted]] technicians scrambled around it, trying to stabilize it after the power hub's destruction.

Cora created another distraction with Minor Illusion. As two technicians turned toward the false sound, Garrick charged the one that stayed focused on the drill, his maul crushing it against the organic cradle.

Mercer stepped forward, his spectral mind already positioned. "Base frequency 148.1 hertz, third overtone at 444.3—that's the resonant weak point." He released another **Shatter** spell, tuned precisely to 444.3 hertz.

But as the spell erupted, the [[Twisted Overseer (Final Form)]] emerged from a hidden alcove near the drill—it had been here all along, waiting. Its warhammer slammed into Mercer's side, cracking ribs and driving him to his knees.

The Overseer raised its free hand, and corruption pulsed outward. The remaining technicians turned on Garrick with renewed ferocity.

Yet Mercer's spell took effect. The harmonic amplifier along the drill's body convulsed. Crystalline components shattered. Organic matter ruptured. The deep grinding vibration became a shrieking discordance.

The drill shuddered violently, then collapsed in on itself—organic flesh rupturing, [[Dreamstone]] teeth shattering.

The Overseer turns its crimson eyes from Mercer to the failing drill, then back to you. Its grinding voice echoes in the chamber: "The sabotage... succeeds. But the work... continues. Other drills... other angles..."

And from the bore hole in the ceiling—the thirty-foot shaft carved into the [[Giant Capstone]]—a new light glowed. Not crimson, but a soft, translucent warmth. The stone at the bottom of the shaft was thinner, pulsing with inner light.

And in that translucent stone, shapes moved. Large, slow, patient shapes.

Something lived in the Sleeper's domain, just twenty feet below the capstone now.

The containment was thinner than anyone knew.

The war wasn't over.

It was just beginning.

---

*Chronicler's Epilogue: The Deep Quarantine Operation succeeded in its primary objective—the biological drill was destroyed. But the cost was high: significant resource expenditure, compromised tactical data transmitted to enemy forces, and the terrifying revelation that the containment barrier is more fragile than previously estimated. The expedition now faces a fighting withdrawal through alerted enemy territory, carrying with them the knowledge that their victory may have come too late to prevent the awakening they were sent to stop.*
