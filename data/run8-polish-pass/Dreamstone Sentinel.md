The **Dreamstone Sentinel** is a crystalline guardian formed from raw [[Dreamstone]] to protect a major vein of the material within the [[Giant]] foundation of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Nature and Behavior
The Sentinel is not a living creature but a defense mechanism—a coalescence of the vein's stored psychic energy manifesting as a physical obstacle in response to disturbance, similar to an immune response. It has no consciousness, awareness, or reaction to words or intent. It materializes and attacks instantly in response to proximity or mining activity near the vein it protects.

## Capabilities and Demise
The Sentinel is a ten-foot-tall humanoid construct made of glowing, shifting Dreamstone. It is immune to gravity distortions and can regenerate while near an exposed Dreamstone vein. It was destroyed by adventurers from [[Grimhold]] who were mining the vein, shattering into inert Dreamstone fragments.
