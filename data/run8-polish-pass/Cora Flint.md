**Cora Flint** is a human artificer and a member of the adventuring party operating out of [[Grimhold]]. She serves as the group's engineer, field medic, and quartermaster.

## Personality and Methods
Flint is a practical, transactional adventurer who treats every expedition as a business venture. She maintains a detailed ledger of expenses, loot values, and debts owed. She heals allies efficiently but without bedside manner, and is known for her systematic approach to searching rooms and cataloging resources before moving on. She prioritizes cost-benefit analysis before engaging threats and objects when the party rushes past valuable assets.

## Combat Style
She fights from range using alchemical preparations, acid vials, and Artificer infusions. She supports allies with healing and battlefield control, prioritizing efficiency over heroics.

## Key Relationships
- **[[Garrick Kade]]:** She respects his combat ability but is frequently frustrated by his recklessness. She often patches him up, reminding him that the healing "is coming out of your share."
- **[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]]:** She values his knowledge but is impatient with his lengthy lectures. They share a pragmatic, professional respect.
