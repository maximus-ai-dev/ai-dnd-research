**Warden Aldric** is the commander of the [[Brotherhood]] garrison left to guard the approach to the [[Grand Archive]].

## Description and Recent History
Aldric carries himself with military precision, his authority preserved from two centuries past. He wears Brotherhood robes bearing the order's insignia. Aldric and his two subordinates, [[Warden Petra]] and [[Warden Cassen]], were placed in magical stasis within the interstitial corridor leading to the archive. Upon being awakened, he provided the expedition party with critical information about the layout of the sanctum and the patrol patterns of the [[Amalgamation]]. He stated that the Grand Archive was divided into three wings and identified the East Wing as containing the primary collections and the sonic emitter controls for the [[Salt-Vaults]]. He refused to enter the sanctum itself, as it was above his authorization level.

## Recent Involvement
Aldric advised the explorers before their descent into the [[Military Empire]] layer, noting that the Brotherhood never explored below their own archives, considering the lower levels 'someone else's problem.'
