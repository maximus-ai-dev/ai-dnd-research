**Harlan** is a grizzled prospector who has spent his life working in the [[Starmetal Hills]]. He is a known figure in [[Grimhold]], often found drinking at the local inn.

## Knowledge and Demeanor
Harlan is frequently drunk and melancholy, having witnessed the changes in the hills over his long career. He possesses firsthand knowledge of trails and has reported seeing signs of habitation near the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], including campfire smoke and fresh bootprints. He can be persuaded to guide travelers partway into the hills.
