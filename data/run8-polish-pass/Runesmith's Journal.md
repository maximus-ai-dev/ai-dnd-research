The **Runesmith's Journal** is a leather-bound journal written by the last dwarven forge master of the [[Cold Forge]].

## Contents and Significance
The journal contains detailed schematics and plans for the construction of a [[Psychic Dampener]], a device designed to disrupt the psychic influence of the [[First World Sleeper]]. It was found within the forge complex and provided the critical knowledge required to build the prototype dampener.
