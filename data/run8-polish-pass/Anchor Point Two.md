**Anchor Point Two** is one of four structural anchors of the [[Giant foundation]] that maintains the containment field around the [[First World Sleeper]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Status
The anchor point is a critical component of the ancient containment architecture. It has sustained damage from the focused calibration attempts of the First World Sleeper. During these attempts, a projection of the Sleeper's will, the [[Avatar of the Slumber]], manifested at this location before being destroyed.
