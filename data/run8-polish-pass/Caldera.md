The **Caldera** is a vast, cylindrical void located at the heart of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], serving as the innermost buffer zone of the ancient Giant containment system.

## Description
Carved by the Giants, the Caldera is approximately two hundred feet across and of unknown depth. It exists in a state of zero gravity, requiring three-dimensional navigation. The curved walls are covered in containment runes that provide the only landmarks, casting a golden light into the void. The space contains floating debris from ancient construction and crystallized formations of impossible geometry. At the Caldera's center glows the surface expression of the [[First World Sleeper]], a faint, terrible light that exerts a powerful psychic and visual pull.

## Atmosphere
The atmosphere within the Caldera is warm and carries a diffuse, sourceless luminescence that casts no shadows. The metallic sweetness associated with the Sleeper's radiation is absent here, replaced by its total, ambient presence.

## Inhabitants
The Caldera is inhabited by phenomena known as [[Deep Things]], which are temporary expressions of the Sleeper's dreaming consciousness.
