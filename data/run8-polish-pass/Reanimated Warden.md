The **Reanimated Warden** is a corrupted Giant guardian encountered in the [[Warden's Post]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. It is a forty-foot-tall Giant skeleton reanimated and sustained by void energy flowing from a breach in the mountain's containment architecture.

## Description and History
The Reanimated Warden is held together by threads of void energy that function as ligaments. Its skull is crowned with corrupted [[Dreamstone]] growths that glow with iridescent light, and its eye sockets burn with the psychic frequency of the [[First World Sleeper]]'s broadcast. The creature was originally one of several Giant guardians entombed in sarcophagi throughout the foundation level of the mountain, designed as a last-resort emergency defender to be reanimated by the containment system if the primary seal was breached. The void energy from a catastrophic third breach reanimated it but also corrupted its programming. It now defends the breach itself, drawing power from it and preventing any attempt at resealing.

## Capabilities and Encounter
The Warden possesses immense physical strength, capable of shattering stone with its blows. It is tethered to the breach by void energy, which grants it rapid regeneration when nearby. It is resistant to nonmagical weapons and several energy types, but is vulnerable to radiant damage. Weapons forged from [[Dreamstone]] deal double damage to its corrupted crown, which acts as a control node. The creature can also project a powerful psychic scream that can inflict fear and psychic damage. The party first encountered the Reanimated Warden standing guard at the breach in the Warden's Post. It delivered a telepathic broadcast—a residual psychic recording—before attacking. The party's initial assault revealed the necessity of specialized containment technology, such as the [[Capstone Resonator]], and a strategy to sever its void tether to defeat it.
