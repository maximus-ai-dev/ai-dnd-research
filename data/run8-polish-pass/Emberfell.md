**Emberfell** is a distant and powerful city-state known for its ruthless mercantile culture.

## Reputation and Influence
The city is the origin of many exiles who later formed bandit groups like the [[Emberfell Rejects]]. Its markets are considered potential destinations for dangerous, unregulated materials such as [[Dreamstone]], as unscrupulous merchants there are known to trade with the highest bidder without regard for safety or containment protocols. [[Reyna Steelforge]] of the [[Prospectors' Guild]] has referenced Emberfell in this context.

## Connection to the Adventurers
Adventurer [[Garrick Kade]] hails from Emberfell, and his past experiences there inform his perspective on frontier survival and conflict.
