**Korathan's Chisel** is a legendary tool associated with the [[Giant]] artisan [[Korathan]].

## Significance
The chisel is famed for its use in inscribing the precise and powerful containment runes found throughout the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]'s foundation. A replica of the chisel is a key component required for constructing the [[Capstone Resonator]], as it provides the necessary rune-inscription capability to complete the device.
