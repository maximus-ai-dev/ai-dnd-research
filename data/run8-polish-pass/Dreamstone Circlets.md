**Dreamstone Circlets** are magical items crafted from refined [[Dreamstone]].

## Function
The circlets shield the wearer's mind from the psychic broadcast and harmful radiation emitted by the [[First World Sleeper]]. They provide protection against psychic damage, disorientation, and hallucinations caused by prolonged exposure in contaminated areas deep within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## History
The circlets were constructed by adventurers to safely explore the deeper, more corrupted regions of the [[Giant foundation]]. They have proven essential for operations near void energy breaches and when facing creatures that utilize the Sleeper's broadcast frequency, such as the [[Reanimated Warden]].
