**Kregg** is the leader of the [[Emberfell Rejects]] bandit group operating on the frontier of Laternia.

## Description and History
A cruel and heavyset man from the distant city-state of [[Emberfell]], Kregg is an exile who maintains control over his band through intimidation and resource hoarding. He possesses a personal history with the adventurer [[Garrick Kade]], stemming from their shared past in Emberfell. Kregg wields a finely made [[Kregg's +1 Dagger|+1 dagger]] from his former life.

## Current Status
Kregg was captured by adventurers in earlier events. His fate and current whereabouts are part of the ongoing campaign record.

## Recent Mention
Garrick Kade was questioned by his psychological echo about how close he was to becoming Kregg.
