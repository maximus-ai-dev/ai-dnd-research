The **Black Growth** is a spreading consciousness that permeates stone, air, and thought, contained within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Nature and Properties
It is not a biological fungus but a form of psychic contamination amplified by [[Dreamstone]] radiation. It spreads through the mountain's stone and can influence thought.

## Containment and History
The entity is contained within a quarantine branch of the dwarven minecart network. According to a message left by [[Foreman Kellak]], the [[Deepwatch]] garrison had already fallen by the time he requested a hunter team to deal with the behir [[Kellashen]], suggesting the Black Growth was a prior or concurrent threat.
