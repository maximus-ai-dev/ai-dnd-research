The **Dreamstone Forge** is a manufacturing process within the [[Caldera]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] that uses the balanced energies of the [[Fire Gate]] and the void to rework [[Dreamstone]].

## Function and Status
The forge is the system for creating the final [[Dreamstone Seal]], requiring forty-two hours of forging with precise psychic intent. It is currently dormant. Preparations have been made to build the Dreamstone Forge on a floating platform near the sphere containing the [[First World Sleeper]].
