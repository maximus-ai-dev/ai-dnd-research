**Garrick Kade** is a human fighter and a member of the adventuring party operating out of [[Grimhold]]. He serves as the party's frontline combatant and primary door-kicker.

## Personality and Methods
Kade is an aggressive, direct fighter who leads with action rather than deliberation. He instinctively takes point in every formation, charges threats before the party finishes planning, and refuses to retreat from any fight. He is pragmatic, blunt, and protective of his allies, though impatient with lengthy planning or academic discussion.

## Combat Style
He fights in melee with a maul, utilizing abilities like Giant's Might and Action Surge for devastating burst damage. He wears chain mail and relies on hit points and aggression rather than finesse, typically targeting the most significant threat first.

## Key Relationships
- **[[Cora Flint]]:** He respects her competence and tolerates her ledger-keeping, knowing she will patch him up despite her complaints about cost.
- **[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]]:** He is protective of the physically frail wizard but frustrated by his tendency to stop and examine things mid-danger. They disagree frequently, but Kade trusts Mercer's judgment on magical matters.
- **[[Kregg]]:** He shares a personal history with the bandit leader from their past in Emberfell.
