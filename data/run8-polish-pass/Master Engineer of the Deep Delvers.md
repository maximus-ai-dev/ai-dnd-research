The **Master Engineer of the Deep Delvers** is a prestigious dwarven engineering guild responsible for mining the deep layers of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Role and History
The guild's engineers were tasked with the most complex and dangerous excavation projects within the mountain's depths. [[Borin Stonehand]] was a Master Engineer before his corruption by the [[First World Sleeper]] and transformation into a [[Twisted Overseer]].
