The **Psychic Dampener** is a prototype device designed to disrupt the [[First World Sleeper]]'s psychic control.

## Construction and Function
Built from adamantine and a purified [[Dreamstone Fragment]], the device is based on schematics found in the [[Runesmith's Journal]]. Its primary function is to temporarily sever the psychic connection between the Sleeper and entities it controls, such as the [[Twisted]].
