The **Imperial Lower Palace** is the sealed administrative center of the [[Military Empire]] built directly over the [[Giant]] containment of [[First World Sleeper|the Sleeper]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Function and Contents
The palace served as a monitoring station and garrison. It houses the empire's archives on the Sleeper phenomenon, monitoring systems, and the final records of [[Commander Valerius]] and his garrison. It represents the Military Empire's attempt to understand and contain the ancient threat sealed below.
