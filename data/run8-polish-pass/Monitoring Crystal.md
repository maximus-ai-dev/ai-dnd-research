The **Monitoring Crystal** is a magical device that provides real-time data on the containment integrity within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Function
The crystal displays a diagram of the [[First World Sleeper]]'s containment field and can register breaches and fluctuations in void energy. It also stores historical data on containment stability. It was created and left behind by the [[Military Empire]] in the archives of the [[Imperial Lower Palace]].

## Use
Extensively used by [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]], the crystal has been instrumental in mapping [[Twisted]] activity, locating broadcast amplifiers, and, most critically, providing early warning of catastrophic containment failures, such as the third breach in the [[Warden's Post]].
