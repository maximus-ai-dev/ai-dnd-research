# Session 16: The Warden's Post

The air in the [[Forward Camp]] was still, the quiet of three days' rest broken only by the soft hum of the [[Monitoring Crystal]] on [[Cora Flint]]'s workbench. The camp — a collection of bedrolls, makeshift tables, and salvaged equipment from fifteen adventures — felt almost like home now. The party had spent these days resting, resupplying from [[Grimhold]]'s dwindling stores, and reinforcing the camp's psychic shielding with the [[Scaling Runes]] [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] had learned in [[Korathan]]'s tomb. The [[Dreamstone Circlets]] sat on a shelf, their iridescent glow steady and reassuring.

[[Garrick Kade]] had spent the days maintaining his armor, the adamantine plates gleaming in the soft light of the containment runes that lined the corridor walls. Mercer had been updating the [[Architect's Codex]], adding the final data from the biological drill's destruction and the thinner-than-expected barrier. Cora had been calibrating the Monitoring Crystal, ensuring its readings remained precise as the containment field fluctuated with the [[First World Sleeper]]'s restless dreaming below.

The peace was fragile, but it was peace nonetheless — the first real breath since they had descended into the mountain months ago.

Then the crystal screamed.

It wasn't a reading spike. It was a physical, audible shriek of resonance that cracked the air like thunder. A beaker on Cora's workbench shattered, spraying glass across the stone floor. The containment runes along the corridor walls flickered violently, their steady glow stuttering to near-darkness. The forward camp's psychic shielding wavered — for one terrifying moment, the party felt the raw, unfiltered pressure of the void below pressing against their minds.

From deep in the mountain, a sound like a mountain exhaling: a rush of warm, sweet-smelling air flowed through every corridor, carrying the concentrated essence of the Anomaly's radiation. The air tasted of ozone and decay, of something ancient and vast breathing for the first time in millennia.

Cora was already at the Monitoring Crystal, her hands moving across its glowing surface. "Southwest quadrant," she said, her voice tight. "Six hundred feet from here. Unexplored territory. The containment field readings at the breach are zero. Not low. Zero."

The crystal's display showed a catastrophic failure — a third breach, not at the bore hole they had sealed, not at the [[Drowned Juggernaut]]'s scar. A backup drill, hidden deeper, operated from a direction their monitoring hadn't covered.

And something had come through.

* * *

The corridor toward the breach site showed escalating damage with every step. Containment runes that should glow with steady light were dark and cracked. Stone walls showed radial fractures spreading from some immense impact ahead. Organic resin in the walls — long dormant since the amplifiers' destruction — now pulsed with sickly iridescent light, feeding on the void energy flowing through the mountain.

Environmental hazards manifested as they advanced: radiation surge zones where void energy pooled in shimmering distortion, gravity fractures where "down" shifted randomly and debris floated in mid-air, and echo manifestations — residual psychic imprints flickering into existence: a [[Giant]] sentry checking a rune array, a dwarf engineer with tools, a human soldier from the [[Military Empire]] era.

The corridor opened suddenly into a vast chamber — the "[[Warden's Post]]" from the [[Giant Blueprint Tablet]]. The space was enormous: eighty feet in diameter with a domed ceiling sixty feet high. At the center lay an open, empty sarcophagus large enough for a creature twenty feet tall. At the far wall, a ragged hole forty feet across glowed with void energy — the breach. The containment runes around it were not just cracked; they were shattered, as if struck by an immense force from below.

And standing at the edge of the breach, facing the party, was the [[Reanimated Warden]].

It was the largest creature any of them had ever seen. A Giant skeleton, forty feet tall, held together not by ligaments but by threads of void energy that laced through the bones like dark tendons. Its skull was crowned with corrupted [[Dreamstone]] growths — crystalline formations that glowed with the same iridescent light as the void below. Its hands were the size of minecarts. Its eye sockets burned with the broadcast's frequency.

It did not attack immediately. It turned its massive skull toward the party and regarded them. Then it spoke — not in Giant, not in any language, but in the broadcast frequency itself. The words formed directly in their minds:

*THE SEAL IS BROKEN. THE WATCHER WAKES. THE DEEP THINGS RISE.*

Then it raised one enormous fist and brought it down on the chamber floor with enough force to crack the stone for thirty feet in every direction.

* * *

The battle that followed was a study in catastrophic escalation.

Garrick charged first, his [[Giant's Might]] making him Large but still dwarfed by the forty-foot guardian. "If it's hostile, I hit it. If it's in the way, I break it. Questions come after," he growled, his maul already swinging.

The Warden's colossal fist slammed into him with earth-shattering force. Forty points of damage — half of it necrotic void energy — burned through his armor. Garrick grunted, planted his feet, and refused to be moved. "Still standing!" he roared back at Cora, spitting blood.

Cora's analytical mind was already calculating. "Direct assault is inefficient. We need to sever the energy connection first," she snapped, casting *Melf's Acid Arrow* at the corrupted Dreamstone growths on the Warden's skull. The acid sizzled against the crystalline formations, but the void energy flowing through them resisted the corrosion.

Mercer retreated behind a section of fractured wall, his spectral mind already floating ahead. "Fascinating," he murmured, then caught himself. "That is to say — catastrophic, but methodologically significant. This isn't mere necromancy — it's a sophisticated energy transfer system."

He cast *Telekinesis*, attempting to grasp one of the void energy threads connecting the Warden to the breach. The dark, shimmering cord went taut, vibrating with power. For a moment, the Warden's movements stuttered — its connection to the void energy source was physically strained. But the thread didn't break; instead, it pulsed with increased intensity.

The Warden's eye sockets blazed brighter. It turned toward Mercer, the corrupted Dreamstone crown glowing with malevolent light. The broadcast frequency intensified:

*THE WATCHER WAKES. THE DEEP THINGS RISE.*

* * *

The second exchange proved the party's initial tactics insufficient.

Cora cast *Stinking Cloud* centered on the breach opening itself, creating a chemical barrier that swirled around the void energy opening. For a moment, the shimmering distortion from the breach wavered. The Warden's connection to the void energy source flickered — the corrupted Dreamstone crown on its skull dimmed slightly.

Garrick, having been knocked prone by a previous blow, scrambled to his feet and charged again. His maul swung with devastating force, enhanced by Great Weapon Master's power. The blow connected with the Warden's leg bone, and a satisfying crack echoed through the chamber.

Mercer cast *Dispel Magic* at the corrupted Dreamstone crown. The spell connected, and for a moment, the crystalline formations flickered violently. The void energy threads lacing the skeleton's bones dimmed, and the Warden staggered — not physically, but in its psychic presence. The broadcast frequency stuttered:

*THE... WATCHER... WAKES...*

But then the void energy from the breach surged again, and the crown regained its full glow. The Warden's regeneration visibly accelerated — the damage from Garrick's blow began to knit back together as void energy flowed through the bones.

* * *

The turning point came with the Warden's psychic assault.

The Reanimated Warden's skull tilted back, and a psychic scream erupted from it — not sound, but pure broadcast frequency projected in a 60-foot cone. The wave of psychic energy washed over the party, carrying with it visions of the void below: darkness that welcomed, shapes that promised understanding, the Sleeper's dreaming consciousness reaching out.

Cora felt an irresistible pull toward the glowing opening. The void's whispers crawled through her mind, showing her the breach not as a threat but as a doorway to something ancient and profound.

Garrick shook it off through warrior's discipline and the [[Dreamstone Circlet]]'s protection. The void's whispers were just noise — he'd heard worse from [[Emberfell]]'s back-alley schemers.

Mercer saw the void not as a threat but as the ultimate academic mystery — a reality to be studied, understood, cataloged. But his scholarly detachment and the Dreamstone Circlet's protection let him maintain perspective. *This is data, not destiny.*

Mercer's *Slow* spell took effect as he retreated. The Warden's movements became sluggish, its colossal fists rising with deliberate, ponderous slowness. The corrupted Dreamstone crown pulsed with frustrated energy as the spell impaired its reaction time.

* * *

The final moments were a desperate scramble for survival.

The Reanimated Warden's colossal fist slammed into Cora, throwing her backward across the stone floor. She skidded fifteen feet, equipment scattering around her, blood spraying from her mouth. Fifty-one points of damage — she was prone and pushed, fighting both catastrophic injuries and psychic compulsion.

Garrick, critically wounded from a previous blow that had dealt sixty-eight points of damage, staggered but remained standing through sheer stubbornness. His Relentless Endurance kept him from falling completely, though blood dripped from multiple wounds and his armor was cracked and smoking from necrotic energy burns.

Mercer's *Polymorph* spell took effect, transforming Garrick into a Giant Eagle. The massive bird of prey spread its wings, ready for rapid evacuation. The transformation stabilized Garrick's critical injuries in this new form.

"RETREAT! Full withdrawal!" Cora gasped, blood in her mouth. "We're not equipped for this! The Warden regenerates faster than we can damage, the breach feeds it directly, and we're all compromised!"

Mercer fought the psychic pull toward the breach, digging his heels into the stone. "This isn't retreat — it's strategic repositioning for proper research! We cannot fight what we don't understand, and right now, we understand nothing about how to sever that void tether!"

* * *

The party retreated from the [[Warden's Post]], battered but alive. They navigated the hazardous corridors back to the [[Forward Camp]], avoiding radiation surge zones and gravity fractures. The containment field readings continued to drop, but they had bought themselves a brief window to assess and plan.

They stood in the forward camp, battered and bloodied but with critical intelligence about the Reanimated Warden. Garrick had returned to his normal form, his injuries stabilized but severe. Cora's equipment was damaged but salvageable. Mercer had the sarcophagus inscription data recorded by his spectral mind.

The containment field continued to fail. The breach remained open. The Warden stood guard. But now the party understood what they were facing — and they had the tools to fight it.

They had minutes, perhaps less, before the containment system failed completely. The [[Capstone Resonator]] components were assembled: Dreamstone Power Cell, Monitoring Crystal, [[Korathan's Chisel]] replica, and raw Dreamstone. Mercer and Cora needed to construct the device. Garrick needed to recover from his injuries. The final assault must be planned with precision — one chance to seal the breach and defeat the Warden before the mountain came down.

The [[First World Sleeper]] stirred below, its dreams leaking into reality through the broken seal. The [[Reanimated Warden]] stood as its corrupted guardian. And three adventurers — an artificer from the [[Prospectors' Guild]], a wizard from the [[Grand Archive]], and a fighter from [[Emberfell]]'s back alleys — prepared for their final stand in the heart of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

They had built the Capstone Resonator, tested the Warden's capabilities, and retreated to plan the final assault. They knew the Warden's weaknesses: distance from the breach, the containment runes, and the corrupted crown. They had one shot with the Resonator.

They needed to use it perfectly.
