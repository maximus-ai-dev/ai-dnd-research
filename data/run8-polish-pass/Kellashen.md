**Kellashen** was a behir, a serpentine predator also known as a "lightning coil," that laired within the [[Eastern Transit Hub]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Demise
Kellashen was approximately twenty-five feet long with twelve powerful legs, capable of climbing walls and ceilings with ease. It possessed a devastating lightning breath weapon and constricting attack. An intelligent and territorial apex predator, it had occupied the transit hub for decades, drawn by geothermal warmth and abundant cave fauna. It avoided the conductive iron rails of the minecart network, understanding they could channel its own lightning. The behir was lured into a trap within the minecart tunnels by an expedition from [[Grimhold]]. Using the environment—including a prepared conductive section of rail—the adventurers turned the creature's lightning breath against it, ultimately defeating it. Its lightning gland was harvested to enhance the power of the [[Cold Forge]].
