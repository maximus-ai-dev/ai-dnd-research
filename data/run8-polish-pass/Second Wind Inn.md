The **Second Wind Inn** is a tavern and lodging house located in [[Grimhold]].

## Atmosphere and Function
The inn provides warmth, food, and temporary shelter from the harsh frontier environment. It is a common source of local rumors and information, and it is where newcomers often learn of available work, including bounties posted by the [[Prospectors' Guild]]. The expedition party uses it as a base for recuperation between delves into the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].
