# Session 12: The Crystallized Guardian

*From the personal chronicles of an observer in the Prospectors' Guild*

The air in [[Grimhold]] carried the crisp clarity of a frontier town that had survived another week, but in [[Reyna Steelforge]]'s office, the atmosphere was thick with anticipation. For three days, the party had rested—[[Garrick Kade]]'s armor repaired at the [[Griffon's Hold]], [[Cora Flint]]'s alchemical supplies restocked, [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]]'s journal filled with fresh observations about the [[Giant foundation]]. Now they gathered around the heavy oak table where the [[Monitoring Crystal]] from the [[Giant]] level pulsed with an unsettling new rhythm: steady green containment light interspersed with brief, violent flashes of violet.

"That's new," [[Reyna Steelforge]] said, her practical eyes narrowing. "The last time I saw that crystal, it was just green."

[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] adjusted his spectacles, his spectral mind already manifesting to analyze the anomaly. "A resonance spike. Not from the inner void where the [[First World Sleeper]] rests, but laterally—somewhere in the outer ring of the foundation." He paused, looking at [[Cora Flint]]. "A location marked as a 'Resonance Seam' in the architectural survey."

Cora finished the thought, her voice edged with both excitement and caution. "Where the Sleeper's radiation has crystallized the bedrock over millions of years. Raw [[Dreamstone]]. A vein of it."

The implications hung in the air like mountain mist. The [[Dreamstone Fragment]] they'd recovered from the [[Dwarven Layer]] had been powerful enough to study—a raw vein would be transformative. Cora's mind raced with possibilities: psychic shielding, containment tools, weapons that could interact with the Sleeper's manifestations directly. But she knew the dangers too—unshielded [[Dreamstone]] radiated concentrated psychic energy. Hallucinations. Perception distortions. Reality fraying at the edges.

Garrick hefted his newly-polished maul. "How do we get to it? And what's guarding it?"

Mercer tapped the crystal. "Half a circuit away from our forward camp. As for guards..." He looked at the violet pulse again. "The [[Dreamstone]] itself may be the guard."

---

**The Descent**

The journey back to the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] was familiar now—the winding trail through the [[Starmetal Hills]], the looming silhouette of the off-center peak, the entrance hidden among rocks. The cart's wheels creaked under lead sheeting and precision mining tools as they descended through known layers: the [[Iron Warlords]]' corridors, the [[Military Empire]]'s stairwell, the [[Dwarven Layer]]'s cold stone.

At their forward camp in the [[Giant foundation]], the monitoring crystal's violet pulse intensified. The air hummed at a frequency that made teeth ache. Mercer set up his equipment. "The signal originates approximately half a circuit clockwise. The containment runes there are working harder—compensating for something."

Cora unpacked the lead sheeting, already calculating exposure limits. "Fifteen-minute shifts with the dampener prototype. Any longer risks permanent perception distortions." She looked at Garrick. "When we extract a piece, it gets wrapped in lead within six seconds or we risk a radiation burst."

They moved through the [[Inspection Circuit]], following the violet pulse. Two hundred feet from the signal's source, the environment began to change.

The walls appeared to *breathe*—expanding and contracting by fractions of an inch. Weight fluctuated—Garrick felt lighter for a step, heavier for the next. His armor pulled to the left. Cora's pack tugged upward. Just a few percent variation, but deeply disorienting.

Ahead, the [[Dreamstone]] vein became visible.

It ran through floor, wall, and ceiling like frozen lightning—a three-foot-wide seam of crystallized stone that glowed with its own inner light, shifting through blues, purples, greens, golds. Beautiful. Wrong. Where the [[Dreamstone]] crossed the [[Giant]] containment runes, the runes' glow was warped, redirected, absorbed. The vein was feeding on the containment energy.

Mercer's spectral mind floated closer. "Crystallized Sleeper radiation, formed where psychic emanation has concentrated against rock for millions of years. Mining it will release stored psychic energy—the deeper we cut, the more concentrated the release."

Cora assessed the vein. Hundreds of pounds of raw [[Dreamstone]]. They needed twenty minimum. The challenge was extraction without destabilizing the surrounding containment runes and without overexposing themselves.

---

**First Extraction**

"Blue phase extraction," Cora directed, watching the vein's shifting colors. "Garrick, wait for my signal. Three... two... one... NOW!"

Garrick positioned the diamond-tipped chisel where the [[Dreamstone]] seam met the floor runes. The stone glowed a deep, steady blue. He applied pressure, not force—leaning into the chisel, letting the diamond tip find psychic stress lines. The stone emitted a low hum that vibrated up through the chisel into his hands. A fine fracture appeared, glowing with inner light.

Cora guided the fracture with tinker's tools, her expertise ensuring the crack followed natural lines without disturbing the containment runes. The fracture completed with a sound like crystal singing. A section roughly the size of a loaf of bread separated from the main vein.

Immediately, the exposed face pulsed violet—a radiation burst erupted in a thirty-foot radius.

For those who failed their Wisdom saves, reality fragmented:

Garrick saw the corridor as it was when the [[Giant]]s built it—new stone, bright runes, [[Giant]] workers twenty feet tall moving in calm procession. They looked down with expressions of immense sadness, their eyes holding knowledge of what would happen to their work over millennia.

Mercer perceived the walls as transparent. He could see through stone in every direction—the containment vessel, the [[Dwarven Layer]] above, the human layers, the surface, the sky. He was simultaneously inside the mountain and above it.

Cora heard music—not human music, but a composition of frequencies that the human ear interpreted as melody but was actually the mathematical structure of the containment field. It was achingly beautiful.

**"Six seconds!"** Cora shouted, lunging for lead sheeting despite her own hallucination. She wrapped the fragment, containing the violet radiation. First extraction complete: approximately three pounds.

But something was forming from the [[Dreamstone]] itself.

---

**The Dreamstone Sentinel**

Crystallized energy took shape—a ten-foot-tall humanoid form glowing with the same shifting colors as the vein. The [[Dreamstone Sentinel]] hovered fifteen feet above the floor, unaffected by the gravity distortions that plagued the party.

Garrick charged immediately, activating [[Giant's Might]]. His body swelled to Large size as he swung his maul with all his strength. The blow connected in a shower of glowing fragments, but the Sentinel responded instantly. Its crystalline fist slammed into Garrick's chest with force that rang through his armor. **Psychic feedback threatened to overwhelm his mind.**

Even as it attacked, the Sentinel's form knitted together. **Regeneration.** The damage from Garrick's maul began to heal.

Cora shouted strategy. "It regenerates near the vein! Draw it away!" She prepared [[Melf's Acid Arrow]], the acid sizzling into crystalline structure.

Mercer, still hallucinating from the radiation burst, tried to cast [[Dispel Magic]] on a gravity inversion pocket that had formed—a five-foot sphere where "down" was "up," drifting toward the mining area. His spell fizzled, unable to maintain coherence through fractured perception.

The Sentinel's form pulsed violet. **Gravity Pulse.**

The world turned upside down. Garrick, Cora, and Mercer all failed their Strength saves. They floated upward, then slammed into the forty-foot ceiling—**4d6 bludgeoning damage**—before gravity reasserted and they fell—**another 4d6**. They hit the stone floor in a tangle of battered bodies and scattered equipment.

Garrick used his [[Relentless Endurance]] racial feature, dropping to 1 HP instead of 0. "I'M NOT DONE!" he roared, using [[Second Wind]] to heal before swinging again.

But the Sentinel regenerated 10 HP per turn while within thirty feet of the exposed vein. The acid damage slowed as crystalline structure reformed. The gravity inversion pocket reached the mining area, engulfing the exposed [[Dreamstone]] vein face. The mining area was now inside a gravity field where everything floated upward.

Cora made the calculation instantly. "Full retreat! Now!" She dragged Garrick back from the Sentinel. "We're extracting with six pounds! This isn't worth dying over!"

They retreated, battered and wounded, with six pounds of [[Dreamstone]] secured. The operation had failed.

---

**Preparation and Return**

Two weeks passed in [[Grimhold]] with focused work. [[Elara Stormwind]] at the [[Griffon's Hold]] enchanted Garrick's maul with thunder energy using powdered blue dragon scale and storm giant hair. She repaired and reinforced his armor at impact points. Cora and Mercer crafted three [[Dreamstone]] circlets—practical bands tuned to each wearer, providing advantage on Wisdom saves against psychic assault and resistance to psychic damage. They acquired three [[Sunstone Elixir]]s—silvery liquid that delivered radiant damage on contact.

Mercer developed a [[Resonance Disruptor]] chip—a sliver of [[Dreamstone]] tuned to interfere with the Sentinel's connection to the vein, potentially suppressing its regeneration.

They returned to the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] equipped, prepared, and funded.

The [[Dreamstone Sentinel]] had evolved. Larger, more intricate, with continuous regeneration. The gravity anomalies had intensified—the inversion pocket now eight feet diameter with multiple smaller bubbles. But the party had tactical intelligence: thunder vulnerability, radiant bypassed regeneration, a disruptor chip.

Garrick charged, his thunder maul crackling with blue energy. The blow connected with shattering force, but the Sentinel's crystalline fist slammed back. Gravity Pulse hit again—ceiling impact, fall damage. Mercer critically wounded at 10 HP. Cora at 35. Garrick at 21.

But Garrick smashed a [[Sunstone Elixir]] against his maul head, coating the weapon in radiant energy. He swung with everything—thunder and radiant combining. Mercer's spectral mind implanted the [[Resonance Disruptor]] chip. **Regeneration suppressed.**

Garrick used [[Action Surge]]. Three maul swings with [[Great Weapon Master]]. The Sentinel shattered at -19 HP, exploding in a shower of crystalline fragments that added five pounds to their haul.

---

**Final Extraction**

They waited fifteen minutes as Mercer predicted—the gravity inversion pocket contracted, collapsed. With the Sentinel destroyed and anomalies settled, extraction proceeded with surprising efficiency. Pound by pound, they extracted the remaining [[Dreamstone]].

**Final tally: 25 pounds total.** The iron-bound cases were heavy with crystallized consciousness.

Mercer noticed something as they prepared to leave. "The containment runes... they're stabilizing. Without the [[Dreamstone]] feeding on their energy, they're returning to baseline function." He made notes. "The vein was acting as a parasite, weakening the containment from within. Our extraction may have inadvertently strengthened the Sleeper's prison."

Garrick shouldered a case, wincing at still-healing injuries. "Good. Means we did more than just get rock."

That night, for the second time, none of them dreamed. The [[Dreamstone]] absorbed ambient psychic energy, creating profound silence in their sleep. Restful. Deeply unsettling.

Mercer stared at the lead-wrapped bundles and wrote in his journal: *"We have stolen twenty-five pounds of the Sleeper's dream and wrapped it in lead. The guardian is shattered. The containment is stronger. And the Sleeper... watched us do it. I wonder what it dreams about now."*

The party advanced to Level 13, equipped with enough [[Dreamstone]] for significant crafting. But as they rested in dreamless silence, a question lingered in the mountain's ancient stone: What had the [[First World Sleeper]] learned from watching its guardian fall?
