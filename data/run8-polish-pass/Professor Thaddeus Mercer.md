**Professor Thaddeus Mercer** is a human wizard of the Order of Scribes and a member of the adventuring party operating out of [[Grimhold]]. He serves as the party's scholar, magical support, and primary repository of knowledge.

## Personality and Methods
Mercer is an academic who treats every adventure as fieldwork for his research. He cannot resist examining ancient architecture, reading inscriptions, or theorizing about unknown phenomena, a curiosity that frequently overrides his self-preservation instinct. He lectures the party on historical context and disagrees vocally when allies want to destroy something of academic value.

## Combat Style
He fights from the back line using control and damage spells, relying on [[Garrick Kade]] as a physical shield. His spell selection prioritizes utility and information-gathering, with combat spells as backup.

## Key Relationships
- **[[Garrick Kade]]:** He appreciates Kade's protection but is exasperated by his anti-intellectual approach, often referring to Kade's insights as "characteristically astute for someone with his practical background."
- **[[Cora Flint]]:** He respects her engineering mind but considers her too focused on profit. They collaborate well on technical problems.
- **[[First World Sleeper]]:** He is the primary theorist connecting the entity beneath the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] to these ancient legends.
