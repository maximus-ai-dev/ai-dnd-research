The **East Wing** is one of the three primary sections of the [[Brotherhood]]'s [[Grand Archive]].

## Description and Contents
The wing consists of rows of stone shelving that once held the Brotherhood's primary collection of books and scrolls. The damage here is less severe than in the Central Hall, suggesting the [[Amalgamation]] had less reason to enter. Most of the collection has been destroyed by water damage, fungal growth, and decay over the centuries.

## Preservation Chamber
At the rear of the East Wing, behind a false wall, is an intact secondary preservation chamber. This chamber contains a sealed stone cabinet holding preserved [[Brotherhood]] spellbooks and research texts. It also houses the controls for the sonic emitter system installed in the [[Salt-Vaults]] above.
