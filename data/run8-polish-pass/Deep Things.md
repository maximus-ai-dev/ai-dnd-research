The **Deep Things** are phenomena that manifest within the [[Caldera]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Nature
They are not conventional creatures but temporary expressions of the [[First World Sleeper]]'s dreaming consciousness, given form by the ambient void energy in the Caldera. They manifest as beautiful, wrong geometric shapes—floating polyhedra of crystallized void, roughly three feet across, with surfaces that shift between transparency and opacity.

## Behavior
The Deep Things exhibit curiosity more than aggression. They may approach, observe, and test the psychic resilience of intruders through touch or by echoing the Sleeper's broadcast. They are phenomena akin to weather or tides, appearing indifferent, curious, or occasionally dangerous if the broadcast's equilibrium is disrupted.
