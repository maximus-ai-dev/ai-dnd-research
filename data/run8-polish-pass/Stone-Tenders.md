**Stone-Tenders** are crystalline constructs that serve the [[Psychic Guardian]] and maintain the systems within the [[Salt-Vaults]].

## Nature and Function
They communicate through binary clicking and light pulses. During repair operations, they supervise mortal workers and enforce the terms of the Guardian's contract. They are aware of the [[Brotherhood]]'s interstitial corridor but refuse to enter it, instead positioning themselves to guard the kitchen and silo entrances. They act as a conduit for the Psychic Guardian's limited communications.
