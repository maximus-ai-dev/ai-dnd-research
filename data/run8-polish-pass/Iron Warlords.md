The **Iron Warlords** were an ancient, militaristic human civilization known for their brutal, pragmatic architecture designed for perpetual siege warfare.

## Architectural Legacy
Their constructions, found in layers beneath the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], are characterized by thick stone walls, low ceilings, narrow corridors, chokepoints, arrow slits, and murder holes. They built extensive logistical facilities, including the [[Salt-Vaults]], to sustain large garrisons. Their structures are well-preserved but had lain abandoned for centuries before becoming infested.
