# Session 3: The Sleeping Wardens

The hearth fire in [[Grimhold]]'s main hall cast long shadows across scrubbed stone floors, its warmth a stark contrast to the mountain's perpetual chill. Three days had passed since the narrow escape from the [[Salt-Vaults]], and [[Garrick Kade]]'s wounds—deep rat bites that had begun to fester—were now clean scars beneath [[Brother Aldwin]]'s careful ministrations. The fortress hummed with purpose: thirty former bandits turned laborers worked under [[Brenna]]'s direction, transforming filth into functionality. The air smelled of woodsmoke and stew instead of mildew and fear.

In the preparation kitchen below, the rhythmic *clink-clink-clink* of tools against stone marked progress on the [[Psychic Guardian]]'s contract. Five workers led by the quiet [[Rolf]] mixed clay-limestone-salt mortar, patching cracked support pillars under the [[Stone-Tenders]]' watchful clicking. But when [[Cora Flint]] examined the ledger, her artificer's eye caught the problem immediately: gritty clay from the eastern gallery, substandard materials that would fail within months.

"*We need better clay,*" she declared, her voice cutting through the kitchen's industry. "*And while we're assessing materials, there's something wrong with that eastern wall.*"

[[Garrick Kade]] had already noticed the workers' nervous glances toward the lighter-colored stone. When he pressed his ear against it, the sound confirmed his suspicion: hollow resonance behind only four inches of masonry. "*Stop!*" he barked at the worker chipping at what they'd thought was a ventilation channel. "*Everyone back from that wall.*"

[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]]'s quill flew across his journal as he analyzed the anomaly. "*This isn't dwarven work,*" he announced, academic excitement warring with caution. "*The mortar contains lumenite—preservation magic. This is a seal, not a repair. Secretive Brotherhood work, two centuries old.*"

From beyond the thin barrier came the sound that changed everything: slow, rhythmic breathing. Three distinct patterns, slightly out of sync, accompanied by the metallic *hiss-click* of breathing apparatus.

---

The [[Stone-Tenders]]' clicking intensified, their crystalline bodies pulsing with urgent light. Mercer's analysis crystallized with chilling clarity: "*They're not just containment—they're preservation. The Brotherhood sealed their own people inside.*"

"*We have approximately ninety minutes before complete system failure,*" Cora calculated, her mind racing through medical protocols. "*Instantaneous aging of two centuries would cause catastrophic cellular breakdown. They'd die within minutes of waking.*"

Garrick positioned himself before the wall, maul ready but not raised. "*If they break through while we're mining quartz, they'll be in our fortress. We go in now—controlled release, while the Stone-Tenders can still stabilize the field.*"

Mercer approached the constructs, speaking in precise Dwarvish. "*Guardian. How do we open the chamber safely?*"

The psychic response carried images: three specific points where the mortar was thinnest. Strike in sequence, collapse the stasis fields one by one. A controlled release rather than catastrophic failure.

As the first crack appeared in the mortar, cold air whispered through—air that smelled of ozone, preservative chemicals, and the sweet, floral scent of lotus extract. Then a voice, deep and raspy from disuse, speaking archaic Common with formal diction:

"*Identify... yourself... to the Brotherhood... or stand aside.*"

---

"*We are rescuers,*" Cora stated, her voice clinical but calm. "*Your preservation system is failing. We're conducting a controlled release to prevent catastrophic aging effects.*"

Garrick kept his position to the side of the crack, not directly in front. "*Brotherhood's been gone two centuries. You've been asleep. Three of you in there. Your stasis is failing.*"

Mercer adopted the formal precision of a military briefing. "*I am Professor Thaddeus Mercer of the Laternian University. You have been in induced dreaming stasis for approximately two centuries. The year is 1492 by the Common Reckoning.*"

Silence. Then the voice again, steadier now: "*I am Warden [[Aldric]]. Second Company, Brotherhood Archives Garrison. My orders were... to guard this passage. Until relieved.*"

The sequential extraction proceeded with military precision. Aldric emerged first—late middle age, stern features, eyes holding two centuries of confusion. [[Petra]] followed, younger, sharper, her tactical assessment continuing even as weakness made her hands tremble. [[Cassen]] came last, the youngest, his transition from the dream state ragged and panicked until Aldric's commanding voice cut through the confusion.

As [[Brenna]] arrived with medical supplies and stretchers, the three wardens sat in their exposed alcoves, breathing normal air for the first time in two centuries, their Brotherhood insignias—the closed eye within a triangle—gleaming in the torchlight.

---

In the main hall, by the hearth fire, the alliance was formalized. Mercer's structured protocols—"*Article Three of standard military alliance protocols*"—resonated with Aldric's need for order. Garrick's soldier-to-soldier pragmatism earned Petra's respect when he entrusted her with his maul for maintenance. Cora's medical professionalism stabilized Cassen's trembling hands with simple exercises using a river stone.

"*The Rat King you mentioned,*" Aldric said, sipping broth slowly. "*We had problems with vermin when we entered stasis. The Brotherhood used sonic deterrents in the lower vaults.*"

Petra's hands, though weak, sketched with precise strokes. "*Sonic emitters. Crystal-based resonance arrays. Twelve primary units. Control panel in the maintenance access corridor off the third ventilation junction.*"

She tapped her insignia. "*These should still grant access. The systems were designed for centuries of operation.*"

The [[Stone-Tenders]] confirmed the system status through psychic projection: 75% operational, three emitters offline, geothermal power stable. The Rat King had avoided active frequencies but showed no adaptation.

As the expedition team prepared to depart—Garrick, Mercer, [[Jace]], and two laborers—Aldric unpinned his brass insignia and handed it to Garrick. "*The squad's trust,*" he said simply. "*Return it with the vaults cleared.*"

Petra added hers, Cassen his last. Three centuries-old pieces of metal, offered as collateral for an alliance between soldiers separated by two hundred years of history.

---

The hearth fire crackled as the expedition descended back into the mountain's depths. Petra watched them go, her eyes already scanning the room for threats, doing her duty. Aldric sat by the fire, a commander without an army in a world that had moved on without him. Cassen worked the river stone in his hand, the simple motion grounding him in a reality he was only beginning to comprehend.

In the preparation kitchen below, the breached wall stood open, revealing the empty alcoves and the corridor beyond. At its far end, a stone door bore the Brotherhood's closed-eye symbol. The [[Grand Archive]]. Guarded by something Aldric had called the [[Amalgamation]]—"*a guardian made from many things. It watches the archive. And it doesn't sleep unless commanded.*"

The [[Stone-Tenders]] resumed their patrol patterns, their clicking a steady rhythm in the depths. The contract with the [[Psychic Guardian]] remained, the mortar still needed improvement, the Rat King still threatened their food supply. But now they had allies from another age, knowledge of ancient systems, and a door that promised answers—and dangers—beyond imagining.

Three wardens slept for two centuries. Now they were awake. And nothing in [[Grimhold]] would ever be the same.
