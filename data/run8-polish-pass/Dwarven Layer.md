The **Dwarven Layer** is a zone of containment architecture built by dwarves atop the older [[Giant foundation]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and History
This transitional layer features corridors and chambers constructed to dwarven scale, with superior stonework and geometric patterns indicative of [[Kazad-Khrom]] design. The dwarves built it as a filtration and containment system, utilizing materials like lead and quartzite to block and absorb psychic radiation emanating from below. Its industrial heart is the [[Cold Forge]].

## Current Status
The upper Dwarven Layer's containment has been compromised, with cracks discovered in a lead-sealed mausoleum. The lower sections were intentionally flooded for centuries. With the activation of the ancient pump system, these lower areas have drained, revealing modifications by the [[Twisted]] and their workshop at the [[Drowned Chokepoint]].
