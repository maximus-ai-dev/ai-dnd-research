The **Drowned Juggernaut** was a heavily modified dwarven mining automaton constructed by the [[Twisted]] within the [[Drowned Chokepoint]].

## Description and Purpose
Standing eighteen feet tall, the Juggernaut was built from dwarven iron and stone in a roughly humanoid form. Its arms terminated in powerful rotating drill assemblies, and its torso contained a furnace. The Twisted extensively corrupted its design, using organic resin to reinforce its plating and transparent resin tubes to circulate black fluid. A pulsing, iridescent mass of corrupted tissue mounted in place of a head served as its biological control node. The automaton was designed for a single purpose: to drill through the [[Giant Capstone]] and breach the containment below.

## Fate
It was destroyed in the Drowned Chokepoint, its power cables severed, the chamber flooded, and the capstone's containment runes activated to overwhelm its corrupted systems.
