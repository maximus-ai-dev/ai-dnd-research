The **Avatar of the Slumber** is a defensive projection of the [[First World Sleeper]]'s will, generated by the containment boundary as an immune response to incompatible objects approaching it.

## Description and Nature
It manifests as a towering figure of crystallized void energy, appearing as a composite of Giant, dwarf, and human features, with the [[First World]] visible within its form. Its eyes are spheres of broadcast energy. The Avatar is not the Sleeper itself but a dream given form and force.

## Purpose
It fights to prevent the [[Dreamstone Seal]] from being deployed at the sphere's boundary within the [[Caldera]].
