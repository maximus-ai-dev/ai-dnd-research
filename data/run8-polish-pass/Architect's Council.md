The **Architect's Council** was a governing body of master builders within the ancient [[Giant]] civilization.

## Role and Significance
The council oversaw monumental construction projects, including the design and implementation of the containment system for the [[First World Sleeper]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. [[Korathan]] served as the council's Master Runecarver and was the primary architect of the containment vessel. The council debated the fate of the Sleeper, ultimately choosing a strategy of containment over attempted destruction or abandonment.
