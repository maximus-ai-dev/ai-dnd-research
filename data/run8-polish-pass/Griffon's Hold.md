**Griffon's Hold** is a well-supplied shop in [[Grimhold]] specializing in weapons, armor, cold-weather gear, and wilderness equipment.

## Proprietor and Services
The shop is owned and operated by the retired mercenary [[Elara Stormwind]]. It is the primary outfitter for prospectors and adventurers heading into the [[Starmetal Hills]], offering not only goods but also valuable trail advice and regional knowledge. [[Garrick Kade]]'s chain mail was repaired at the Griffon's Hold by Elara Stormwind during an adventuring rest period.
