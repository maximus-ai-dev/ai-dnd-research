**Grimhold** is a small frontier mining town located on the continent of Laternia, at the edge of the [[Starmetal Hills]].

## Description and Economy
The town is constructed from ancient stone foundations and simple timber buildings. Its economy is almost entirely based on mining and prospecting, overseen by the [[Prospectors' Guild]]. Notable establishments include the guild headquarters, the supply shop [[Griffon's Hold]], and the [[Chapel of the Hearthfire]].

## Role in the Expedition
Grimhold serves as the primary surface resupply and contact point for the expedition into the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. [[Reyna Steelforge]], the guild leader, operates from here, issuing contracts, receiving intelligence, and dispatching resources and personnel to support the explorers' fortress. During an intermission, Steelforge and a team of Grimhold engineers established a permanent guild presence at the mountain stronghold, transforming it into an official research outpost. The town provides a base for rest, resupply, and equipment crafting between delves into the mountain's depths.
