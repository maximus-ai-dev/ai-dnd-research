The **Brotherhood** is a cabal of arcane scholars who occupied a hidden sanctum within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] approximately two centuries before the present day. They built their [[Grand Archive]] in the hollow spaces between the ruins of the [[Iron Warlords]], cataloging military doctrine, Giant runes, and research into a phenomenon they termed the 'Deep Resonance.'

## Practices and Creations
The Brotherhood were known for their use of preservation magic and complex security measures. Their symbol is a closed eye, often inscribed within a triangle. They constructed a flesh-golem guardian called the [[Amalgamation]] from their own dead to protect their archives. Before abandoning the site, they left a garrison of three [[Warden|Wardens]] in magical stasis to guard the approach.

## Hierarchy
The order maintained a strict hierarchy, with ranks including Initiate, Scholar, Archivist, and Commander. Access to different areas of their sanctum was controlled by brass insignias corresponding to these ranks.

## Historical Knowledge
Brotherhood records indicate they were aware of deeper, older layers beneath their sanctum but deliberately chose not to investigate them, considering the dangers therein to be beyond their purview.
