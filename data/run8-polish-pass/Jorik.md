**Jorik** was a prospector who explored the lower ruins of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] approximately twenty years prior to the Grimhold expedition.

## Legacy
Jorik's journal, kept in the archives of the [[Chapel of the Hearthfire]], contains notes on the defensive architecture of the [[Iron Warlords]]' layer and mentions disturbances such as scratching sounds and pale fungal growths. His experiences in the ruins reportedly left him deeply affected.
