The **Giants** were an ancient civilization that built the original containment architecture for [[First World Sleeper|the Sleeper]] beneath the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Legacy and Architecture
Their constructions form the deepest known layer beneath the mountain. The Giants referred to the contained entity as The Sleeper and designed the failing containment architecture that later civilizations, including the [[Military Empire]], discovered and attempted to monitor. Their ruins represent the oldest stratum of habitation at the site.
