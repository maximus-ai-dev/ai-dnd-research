The **Prospectors' Guild** is the principal organization governing mining and exploration in [[Grimhold]] and the surrounding [[Starmetal Hills]].

## Function and Leadership
The guild regulates mining claims, organizes supply caravans, and contracts adventurers to handle threats to its operations and personnel. It is led by the pragmatic [[Reyna Steelforge]]. Its headquarters in Grimhold features a bounty board and serves as the town's de facto center for security and logistical planning.

## Recent Involvement and Shift in Focus
The guild commissioned a structural map of the [[Military Empire]] layer of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. After the expedition returned with a [[Giant Blueprint Tablet]] and other significant findings, the guild agreed to supply construction materials to help fortify the explorers' surface camp. The guild continues to support efforts to reactivate the mountain's infrastructure and funds research and containment efforts at the newly discovered [[The Resonant Foundation]]. It also funded the expedition to secure raw [[Dreamstone]] from a vein within the Giant foundation. The guild hall served as the planning and preparation base for the party's sabotage mission against the [[Twisted]] amplifiers and for the subsequent expedition to [[The Maker's Rest]]. Following revelations about the decaying containment field, the guild's leader, Reyna Steelforge, began shifting the operation from pure research to active support for efforts to reinforce the containment. The guild is now mobilizing resources beyond Grimhold, seeking external experts to address the escalating threat.
