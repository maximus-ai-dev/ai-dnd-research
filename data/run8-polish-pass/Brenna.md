**Brenna** is a scarred, practical woman who serves as the spokesperson and de facto leader of the workforce recruited from the former [[Emberfell Rejects]] at the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] fortress.

## Role and Demeanor
Brenna handles logistics and morale for the workforce with direct, factual efficiency. She reports on supply statuses, organizes work parties, and communicates the workforce's needs and limitations to the fortress's leaders without panic or exaggeration.
