**Jace** is a young, terrified member of the [[Emberfell Rejects]]. He surrendered to adventurers during a skirmish and provided information about the bandits' activities.

## Role and Demeanor
Jace is characterized by his fear and desperation. He joined [[Kregg]]'s band as a means of survival on the harsh frontier and represents the reluctant, non-loyal element within the Emberfell Rejects. After his capture, he cooperated with his captors.
