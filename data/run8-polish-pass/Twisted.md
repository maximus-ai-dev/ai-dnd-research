The **Twisted** are corrupted dwarves found within the depths of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Nature and Observed Activities
These entities exhibit extensive biological and mechanical alterations. Their technology and infrastructure integrate a black, organic resin that fuses with stone and metal, forming living architecture. They modify dwarven machinery and tools with biological components, creating hybrid systems. The Twisted have established a logistics network within the dwarven tunnels, extending rails and widening passages. They constructed a workshop in the [[Drowned Chokepoint]], where they built and charged the [[Drowned Juggernaut]] using energy drawn from the corruption below. Their actions indicate a coordinated effort to breach the mountain's containment layers. They guard organic broadcast amplifiers and pursue intruders who threaten their infrastructure. They established a deep staging ground with a garrison and a biological drill aimed at the [[Giant Capstone]].
