# Session 2: The Scratching in the Walls

*The following account was compiled from the field notes of Professor Thaddeus Mercer, the medical log of Cora Flint, and the firsthand testimony of Garrick Kade, as recorded by the Chronicler of Grimhold.*

---

Winter arrived in [[Grimhold]] with the quiet persistence of falling snow. Three days had passed since the company's return from the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], and the first true snow dusted the rooftops in a thin, persistent layer that spoke of the long season to come. The air smelled of woodsmoke and frost, and the townsfolk moved with the brisk purpose of people preparing for siege.

Within the stone hearth of the [[Prospectors' Guild]], [[Reyna Steelforge]] leaned over her massive oak desk, her expression grim as she studied the evidence laid before her: [[Kregg's +1 Dagger]], [[Harlan]]'s journal, and the company's description of the black-veined dwarven miners and the sealed stairwell that exhaled metallic corruption.

"The dark of the moon is in three nights," she said without looking up. "But we have a more immediate problem." She gestured to the snow-flecked window. "Winter's here. The trails will be impassable within the week. And you've just recruited fifteen extra mouths to feed at that fortress."

Her gaze was steady as she slid a worn parchment across the desk—an old prospector's map. "Old maps reference something called the [[Salt-Vaults]] beneath the surface ruins. The [[Iron Warlords]] built them—massive dry-storage pantries designed to feed a garrison through years of siege." She tapped the map. "They'd solve your food problem through winter and beyond."

"But there's a reason nobody's scavenged them yet," she added, her voice dropping. "Prospectors report scratching sounds, gnawed bones, and things moving in the dark. The vaults are infested."

---

**The Weight of Logistics**

[[Cora Flint]] leaned over the map, her finger tracing lines as calculations unfolded in her mind. "Fifteen mouths, ten days minimum. That's one hundred fifty man-days of food." She looked between her companions. "[[Garrick Kade]], you're the muscle. [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]], your academic detachment might actually be useful here for once."

Garrick gripped his maul tighter, the familiar weight comforting. "Scratching sounds and gnawed bones mean something's down there that needs killing." His gaze drifted toward the mountain visible through the window. "We need to move fast."

Mercer's academic excitement momentarily overrode the gravity. "The Salt-Vaults! The preservation techniques alone would be worth documenting!" His magical quill materialized as he pulled out his journal. "We should approach methodically. The architecture could provide clues about containment systems throughout the mountain."

Outside, [[Brenna]] waited by the Marker Stone, her breath forming clouds in the frosty air. The scarred woman who had taken charge of the workforce delivered her report with flat efficiency. "Heard you're going after the food stores. Good. We're down to ten days of rations if we stretch them." Her gaze settled on Garrick. "After that, I can't keep them there. Hunger makes people desperate."

She handed Mercer a crude sketch. "[[Rolf]] found the stairwell entrance. Descends about sixty feet through rubble-choked passages, then opens into worked stone corridors. Different architecture than the surface ruins. Defensive design." She met their eyes. "He also heard scratching. Faint, but constant. From deeper in."

---

**The Chapel Archives**

Mercer insisted on research. "Thirty minutes at the [[Chapel of the Hearthfire]] could save us hours of fighting in the dark," he argued, already striding toward the simple stone building.

[[Brother Aldwin]], the middle-aged priest with kind eyes and practical hands, greeted them with recognition. "Reyna sent word you might come." He led them to a small alcove lined with shelves—Grimhold's fragmentary archives.

He handed Mercer a particular journal. "[[Jorik]]'s predecessor. He explored the lower ruins twenty years ago. Didn't reach the Salt-Vaults, but made notes about the architecture." Brother Aldwin's expression grew serious. "He mentioned disturbances. 'Scratching in the walls, like rats but louder,' and 'pale fungal growths near moisture seepage.'"

Mercer's quill flew across the journal pages with practiced efficiency. "Ah! Here—'defensive corridors with arrow slits at every intersection.' Standard Iron Warlord design, but note the spacing: ten-foot intervals. That suggests automated systems." He looked up, eyes alight. "And here—ventilation shafts in the upper walls, too small for a man but large enough for something to move through. That's your scratching!"

Cora's efficient search yielded another journal. "Prospector's log from last season. Mentions 'iridescent-furred rats larger than dogs' near the mountain's lower slopes. The prospector shot one—said its eyes glowed faintly in the dark." She looked up. "Mutated. Long-term exposure to low-level magical radiation."

Garrick's impatience was a palpable presence. "Fifteen minutes left. We know they're in the walls. What else do we need?"

---

**Supplies and the Mountain**

At the [[Griffon's Hold]], [[Elara Stormwind]] assessed them with a retired mercenary's gaze. When Mercer began his chemical lecture—"calcium oxide in granular form, magnesium-based chalk"—her eyebrow arched slightly.

"You're going into the lower ruins. The Salt-Vaults." It wasn't a question. She gathered supplies with practiced speed: quicklime, powdered chalk, sulfur blocks, caltrops, extra torches. "Twenty-five gold for the lot." She produced three metal clickers. "Prospectors use them to scare off cave bears. Might work on whatever's in your walls."

As she packed the supplies into waxed canvas bags, her eyes grew serious. "Jorik was a friend. He went into those lower ruins twenty years ago and came out... changed. Said he saw things moving in the walls that shouldn't be able to move." She looked at each of them. "He stopped prospecting after that. Drank himself to death in the [[Second Wind Inn]] five years later."

The trek to the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] took two hours through snow-dusted [[Starmetal Hills]]. At the fortress, Brenna met them at the gate. "The stairwell's clear. Rolf's team finished an hour ago. They heard the scratching—louder now that the rubble's gone."

The stairwell yawned before them, a dark mouth exhaling cold, dust-scented air. From the depths, the faint but persistent sound of scratching carried upward—a dry, skittering noise that seemed to come from the very walls.

---

**Descent into the Kill Zone**

The stairwell descended steeply, the air growing colder and dustier with each step. Sixty feet down, it ended in a small antechamber—and Mercer's failed perception proved costly.

Garrick stepped into the chamber, his weight settling on a central stone. A soft *click* echoed, and from slots in the ceiling, a fine gray powder began to sift down—powdered limestone, an Iron Warlord security measure designed to disorient and mark intruders.

As the powder settled, the true nature of the antechamber became clear: arrow slits lined the walls, floor stones were pressure-activated, and in the far corner, a crude nest of shredded material confirmed Rolf's report.

The scratching intensified. From behind the arrow slits now—purposeful movement.

"Pressure plate trap," Cora said immediately, dropping to examine the floor. "Non-lethal marking system."

Garrick swung his maul at the nearest arrow slit as something moved behind it. The weapon connected with stone and something soft with a sickening crunch. "They're here!" he barked, pulling his maul back covered in iridescent fur and dark fluid.

The attack came with terrifying speed. From the arrow slit Garrick just struck, a massive, iridescent-furred head lunged—the [[Rat King]], its eyes glowing with faint blue light. Its bite sank deep into Garrick's shoulder. Before he could react, the walls seemed to come alive. From every arrow slit and murder hole, giant rats poured forth—eight of them, their fur shimmering with the same iridescent sheen.

The antechamber became chaos. Limestone dust swirled, reducing visibility to a gray haze. The chittering was deafening—a cacophony of communication as the rats coordinated their attack.

Mercer raised his hands, incanting as he prepared a defensive spell. "I'm casting Thunderwave—modified for concussive force!" The magical energy built around him. "Garrick, brace yourself!"

The spell erupted through the antechamber with concussive force, sending several rats tumbling backward with pained squeals. But the Rat King was relentless. As the dust swirled, it reared back and emitted a piercing, iridescent shriek that vibrated through the very stone.

Garrick gritted his teeth through the effect. Cora's medical training helped her focus. But Mercer—already struggling with the limestone dust—was overwhelmed. The shriek targeted his very mind, leaving him stunned, spellbook dangling from limp fingers.

---

**Retreat and Regroup**

"Emergency protocol!" Cora shouted, her hands glowing as she cast Healing Word on Garrick. "We need to break contact NOW!"

They stumbled backward, Garrick dragging the stunned Mercer, Cora covering their retreat with fumbled quicklime mixtures that created steaming, caustic puddles. They reached the base of the stairwell—sixty feet of narrow, vulnerable climbing above them.

"Chokepoint defense!" Cora shouted, pouring quicklime across the entrance to create a chemical barrier. "Garrick, hold the stairwell entrance. Medical triage: sit. That bleeding needs immediate attention."

Mercer's vision swam back into focus. "Apologies for my... incapacitation. That sonic attack was more sophisticated than expected. The Rat King is the reproductive center of the colony."

But the barrier was failing. The Rat King lunged through the caustic steam, ignoring the burns, its jaws closing on Garrick's freshly bandaged shoulder with bone-crunching force. A critical hit—chain mail rings tore, flesh ripped, blood sprayed.

"Emergency healing protocol!" Cora cast her last Cure Wounds. "That's my last spell slot. We're now operating on mundane resources only."

Mercer raised his hands, his spellbook glowing. "I'm casting Shield on Garrick! Cora, smoke screen, now!" As Cora threw and ignited a sulfur block, Mercer used prestidigitation to intensify the smoke, creating a thick, acrid cloud.

"Into the side chamber—move!"

---

**The Guard Post**

They stumbled through the smoke into a side chamber—undisturbed for centuries. Thick dust coated everything, but the air was still and clean. No arrow slits. No murder holes. And on the far wall, partially obscured by dust, hung a faded map showing the layout of the Iron Warlords' layer in detail.

"The Salting Halls," Mercer breathed, his quill already copying the map. "Note the plural. Not a single vault, but a complex."

But the sound changed from scratching to chewing. Loud, wet chewing. Teeth on wood, teeth on iron. The door shuddered.

"Hidden access panel—Iron Warlord design standard," Cora said, her hands scanning stone walls even as wood splintered.

"The symbol—it's a pressure-release mechanism!" Mercer shouted. "Look for a stone that's slightly different!"

The door exploded inward in a shower of splinters. The Rat King filled the doorway, its iridescent fur matted with quicklime burns and blood. It charged straight for Garrick, jaws closing on his wounded shoulder.

"Garrick, pressure stone NOW!" Cora shouted, lunging at the two giant rats that squeezed past their leader.

Garrick charged past the Rat King, throwing his full armored weight against a smooth stone on the wall. The mechanism groaned, and a section of wall began to slide open. The Rat King turned, its glowing eyes widening with understanding, and lunged again.

But Garrick was already moving, diving through the opening into the dark maintenance tunnel beyond. "Through here! Now!"

Mercer raised his hands. "I'm casting my final Magic Missile—at the ceiling!" Three glowing darts struck the stone lintel with concussive impact, raining dust and debris down as a temporary barrier.

They stumbled into the maintenance tunnel as the stone door ground shut behind them, cutting off the Rat King's furious chittering.

---

**Aftermath in the Dark**

Silence. Darkness broken only by Mercer's torch. Garrick collapsed against the tunnel wall, breathing in ragged, painful gasps. Blood soaked through his torn chain mail in multiple places. Cora immediately began applying pressure to the worst wounds.

Mercer unrolled the copied map in the flickering torchlight. "The maintenance tunnel runs parallel to the main corridor. It connects to the Salt-Vaults through a service entrance here." He pointed to a marked junction. "We're closer to the food stores than we were."

But the reality was clear: Garrick could not continue. He was barely conscious, bleeding from multiple deep bites, his armor compromised. The Rat King waited on the other side of the stone door, intelligent and vengeful.

They had the map. They had the intelligence. They knew the location of the Salt-Vaults and the layout of the Iron Warlords' defensive systems. But they were battered, bloodied, and nearly out of resources. Above, the workforce waited with nine days of rations counting down.

Huddled in the dark maintenance tunnel, torchlight flickering on blood-smeared stone, they tended wounds and studied the map that could save fifteen people from starvation. The cost was written in Garrick's wounds and their depleted resources. The Salt-Vaults waited, filled with food that could sustain their workforce through winter. The Rat King waited on the other side of the stone door.

And somewhere deeper in the mountain, the scratching continued—a persistent reminder that the [[First World Sleeper]]'s influence extended further than any of them had imagined.
