# Session 13: Sabotage in the Shadows

*An entry from the chronicles of the Asymmetrical Mountain campaign, recorded by an observer of the Prospectors' Guild*

---

The air in the [[Prospectors' Guild]] hung thick with purpose, a palpable tension that clung to the dust motes dancing in slanted sunlight. Three days had passed since [[Garrick Kade]], [[Cora Flint]], and [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] had returned from the [[Giant foundation]] level—three days of rest at the [[Second Wind Inn]], of repairs at [[Griffon's Hold]], of meticulous preparation. Now they stood before [[Reyna Steelforge]], the evidence of their next mission spread across the guild's central table like a grim tapestry.

The [[Twisted Engineering Schematics]] lay beside the [[Giant Blueprint Tablet]] and the pulsing green [[Monitoring Crystal]], telling a story of organic horror. [[Reyna Steelforge]] traced the lines with calloused fingers. "If these amplifiers aren't destroyed," she said, her voice low and serious, "they'll push the Sleeper's broadcast through the containment and into every layer above. The entire mountain will be corrupted."

[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] cross-referenced the schematics with his tablet, his spectral quill scratching notes. "Three amplifiers in the outer ring. [[Amplifier Alpha]] in the northwest—a prototype. [[Amplifier Beta]] southeast—modified multiple times. [[Amplifier Gamma]] due south—the most powerful and heavily guarded." He tapped the crystal, which pulsed in time with his words. "The order matters. Destroy Alpha first, we have time. Try Gamma first..." He shook his head. "Silence becomes impossible."

[[Cora Flint]] studied the organic structures through the crystal's lens. "The vulnerability is at the base," she said, pointing to root-like tendrils connecting the amplifiers to the stone floor. "They draw energy from the containment runes. Sever them cleanly—alchemical dissolution or arcane disruption—and the amplifier dies. Crude attacks trigger a broadcast burst that alerts every [[Twisted]] in the area."

The planning began with clinical efficiency. Cora demanded patrol patterns and escape routes. Garrick traced paths on the blueprints with thick fingers, his voice gravelly with certainty. "Primary route after Alpha: through old ventilation shafts. Secondary after Beta: collapsed mining tunnel. Emergency: straight down the main corridor if everything goes to shit." He looked at his companions. "No last stands. That's not heroics—that's math."

Mercer's observations revealed the enemy's precision: thirty-minute patrol cycles, two [[Twisted]] Sentries guarding each amplifier, communication through bioluminescent resin conduits in the walls themselves. "The walls are watching," he announced, his voice taking on that lecturing cadence. "Total Twisted observed: twenty to twenty-five individuals. An [[Twisted Overseer]] coordinates from near Gamma."

Cora calibrated her dissolution solutions using foundation stone samples from Reyna's stores. "Three vials, three minutes per root cluster," she calculated, her ledger filling with precise notations. Garrick established simple whistle codes: one for enemy spotted, two for backup, three for retreat. Mercer mapped the exact timing—a three-second window every forty-seven seconds when Alpha's Sentries looked away simultaneously.

They descended into the mountain as saboteurs, not explorers. The familiar path through the [[Starmetal Hills]] and down through the fortress layers felt different now—a trap being entered willingly. The [[Dreamstone Circlets]] hummed against their temples as the psychic pressure returned, that low vibration in the bones that spoke of the [[First World Sleeper]]'s proximity.

At Alpha's chamber, the plan unfolded with military precision. Mercer's Minor Illusion created a stone-shifting distraction as Garrick crossed thirty feet of open corridor in silent strides. Cora followed, then Mercer, each reaching the side passage as the Sentries completed their three-second window.

The amplifier was smaller than expected—eight feet of organic resin threaded with pulsing bioluminescent veins. Cora knelt beside the first root cluster, applying dissolution solution with steady hands. Garrick positioned himself as a living shield between her and the Sentries. Mercer watched the corridor, counting down the patrol's return.

"Nine minutes exactly," Cora whispered. "No margin for error."

The work proceeded smoothly until the final root cluster—thicker than the others, dissolving too slowly. Mercer's warning came urgent: "Patrol is two minutes out. They're approaching early."

Garrick moved without hesitation, intercepting the three [[Twisted]] Laborers before they could reach the Sentries. His maul struck with brutal efficiency, dropping the lead Laborer with a silent, crushing blow. Mercer created an illusionary rockfall to draw attention away. Cora applied a second vial, Mercer catalyzing the reaction with arcane energy.

Amplifier Alpha collapsed with a wet, withering sound—silent, clean destruction.

But the network registered the loss. The Sentries twitched, becoming alert. Then the [[Twisted Overseer]] emerged from the shadows, its reinforced corrupted plate armor glinting as it charged directly at Cora. The warhammer connected with a devastating impact that sent her stumbling backward, pain exploding through her shoulder.

The situation escalated into controlled chaos. Garrick drew the Overseer's attention with a solid maul strike to its armored shoulder. Mercer cast Hold Person on a Sentry, freezing it mid-step. Cora called for retreat as she finished Beta's final cluster.

They fell back to the maintenance corridor, Mercer casting Web to block pursuit. The Overseer, slowed by Ray of Frost and Mercer's Slow spell, advanced with deliberate, hampered movements. They reached the ventilation shafts—tight metal passages where Garrick's armor scraped loudly against the walls, announcing their position to pursuing Twisted.

The [[Twisted Overseer]] found them, smashing through shaft walls with its warhammer. Garrick took blows that would have felled a lesser fighter, his half-orc resilience and Dreamstone Circlet keeping him standing. Cora suffered multiple injuries, her alchemist's precision giving way to survival instinct.

At a shaft junction, they sealed the Overseer behind Mercer's Wall of Force—an impervious arcane barrier that even the Twisted commander's warhammer couldn't breach. They caught their breath, assessing wounds and dwindling resources.

Beta's defenses were reinforced: three Sentries instead of two, organic surveillance tendrils sweeping the ceiling every twenty seconds, five root clusters instead of three. Cora had only two vials of dissolution solution remaining.

"We improvise," she said, pain tightening her voice.

The approach to Beta required crossing forty feet of exposed corridor during a five-second blind spot in the surveillance cycle. Mercer's distraction worked perfectly—but as they crossed, the Overseer found another angle, its warhammer smashing through the ceiling to strike Cora's already-injured back.

At the amplifier, Garrick held off Sentries while Cora and Mercer worked. Dissolution solution on the three largest clusters, Dispel Magic on the two smaller ones. The work was frantic, injuries mounting as the Overseer continued to find attack vectors through the shaft network.

Beta collapsed with a feedback pulse—not a full alarm, but enough to send frantic signals racing through the resin conduits. The network was now fully alerted.

They ran for the collapsed mining tunnel, their escape route. Garrick cleared rubble with raw strength, heaving aside stones that would have required machinery. Mercer used Telekinesis on smaller debris. Cora, bleeding from multiple wounds, delayed pursuing Sentries with slowing spells.

As they squeezed through the cleared passage, Mercer sealed it behind them with a final Wall of Force. The dull thud of the Overseer's warhammer striking the impervious barrier echoed through the stone as they escaped into the minecart network.

In a maintenance alcove two hundred feet from the tunnel, they finally stopped. Cora leaned against the wall, breathing heavily as she cast Healing Word on herself. Garrick's chain mail was dented in multiple places, blood seeping through. Mercer checked the Monitoring Crystal, his face illuminated by its green glow.

"The Twisted forces concentrate at Beta's location," he reported. "They haven't yet deduced our escape vector. We have approximately five minutes before the Overseer's barriers dissipate and organized pursuit begins."

Cora pulled out her ledger with a shaking hand. "Two amplifiers down. One to go." She looked at her nearly empty component pouch, then at Garrick's battered armor. "We need to be smarter about Gamma. We can't just rush in like we did with Beta."

[[Amplifier Gamma]] awaited in the southern quadrant near the inner void—the largest, most powerful, and now certainly the most heavily guarded amplifier. The [[Twisted]] knew their pattern now. The [[Twisted Overseer]] would be waiting.

They had weakened the broadcast network significantly. But the cost showed in their wounds, their depleted resources, and the grim knowledge that the final sabotage would be against an enemy prepared for their arrival.

The mountain hummed around them, a living entity that had felt their strikes and was now stirring to respond. Five minutes to heal, to plan, to prepare for the most dangerous sabotage attempt yet. The fate of [[Grimhold]] and everything above still hung in the balance, but the scales were tipping toward a confrontation that would test every skill, every spell, and every ounce of courage they possessed.

The chronicle would continue at Gamma—or end there.
