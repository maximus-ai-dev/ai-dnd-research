A **Twisted Overseer** is a type of corrupted dwarf that serves as a commander among the [[Twisted]]. They retain significant tactical intelligence and dwarven engineering knowledge from their former lives.

## Physical Description and Capabilities
Overseers are stocky and dwarven in proportion, but their skin bears an iridescent sheen and their veins pulse with black fluid. They typically wear heavy, corrupted plate armor and wield warhammers of dwarven make, now infused with necrotic energy. Twisted Overseers coordinate the actions of other Twisted, directing them with clear tactical objectives. In combat, they can empower nearby Twisted with focused commands and unleash a damaging pulse of corrupting energy. They demonstrate emotional responses, such as fear when their operational security is threatened and comprehension when their infrastructure is systematically dismantled. They do not speak or negotiate, acting with silent, lethal purpose. The Twisted Overseer coordinates the defense of the broadcast amplifiers from a position near Amplifier Gamma.
