The **Giant Capstone** is a massive stone seal located beneath the [[Drowned Chokepoint]] in the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Significance
Roughly forty feet in diameter, the capstone is constructed from a darker, denser stone than the surrounding dwarven architecture. Its surface is inscribed with ancient Giant-era runes that glow with latent containment magic. It forms the lid of the primary containment vessel, separating the [[Dwarven Layer]] from the [[Giant foundation]] level below. The capstone is the final physical barrier sealing the corruption known as the [[First World Sleeper]]. The [[Twisted]]'s [[Drowned Juggernaut]] was specifically engineered to drill through it. Although the automaton scarred the capstone with a deep groove, the seal remained intact, preserving the containment. The runes can be activated to generate a localized containment field that suppresses corruption-based effects. The Twisted later attempted to breach it from below using a biological drill, boring thirty feet into its structure before being stopped.
