A **Giant Monitoring Crystal** is a specially crafted instrument used to measure the strength of the containment field within the [[The Resonant Foundation]].

## Design and Operation
Based on designs found in the monitoring alcoves of the [[Inspection Circuit]], these crystals pulse with a soft light in rhythm with the containment field's heartbeat. They provide a numerical readout of local field strength as a percentage, functioning as an essential tool for gauging the stability of the ancient containment and the proximity of the [[First World Sleeper]]'s influence.

## Utility
When held near active containment runes, the crystal acts as a weak psychic dampener, granting a measure of protection against the Sleeper's broadcast. It is standard issue for any exploration team entering the Resonant Foundation, with critical retreat protocols triggered if readings drop below a safe threshold.
