# Session 7: The Cold Forge

**Date:** 15th of Frostfall, 1287 AE

*From the chronicles of the Asymmetrical Mountain expedition*

---

The chill of the [[Starmetal Hills]] had settled deep into [[Grimhold]]'s bones, but within the walls of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], a different kind of warmth was stirring. For three days, the party had rested at the [[Second Wind Inn]], their wounds from the [[Dwarven Layer]] mending under [[Brother Aldwin]]'s care. The crystallized [[Dreamstone Fragment]] pulsed on [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]]'s worktable, its soft light matching the second warning ring on their [[Monitoring Crystal]]. The pressure from [[The Sleeper]] continued, but for now, the immediate leak was contained.

[[Reyna Steelforge]] had proven true to her word. The [[Prospectors' Guild]] delivered timber for shoring weak passages, iron brackets for securing doors, and enough rations to feed their workforce another month. This morning, with the scent of coming snow sharp in the air, Reyna handed over the [[Radiation Survey Notes]] from the Leaking Crypt. "They mention a path to something called the [[Cold Forge]]," she said, her expression grim. "If you can get it working again, you'll have a proper workshop. More importantly, it was part of the dwarven containment system."

Her warning echoed as they trekked back to the mountain: "Things that sleep a thousand years don't always wake up friendly."

---

The descent into the industrial heart of the mountain revealed architecture scaled for giants of industry. Ceilings rose to twenty-five feet, corridors widened for carts, and geometric carvings—functional, not decorative—covered every surface. The air grew warmer, carrying ancient scents of iron and coal dust.

In the **Material Stores**, iron-railed shelving stretched thirty feet high, most empty but for ghostly outlines where ore once rested. [[Garrick Kade]]'s gear rattled—a loose buckle betraying their presence in the silent halls. From deeper in the complex, something answered: a wave of heat washing through the chamber, brief but intense, like a furnace door opening somewhere below.

[[Cora Flint]]'s eyes narrowed. "Something heard that. Or felt it."

They moved into **The Apprentice Workshops**—a dozen cold forge stations with bellows, anvils, and quenching troughs still holding dwarven steel tools untouched by rust. [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] examined cooling channels carved along walls, translating Dwarvish inscriptions: *"COOLING FLOW CONTROLLED FROM PUMP STATION EAST. DO NOT FORGE WITHOUT COOLANT."*

Another heat pulse. Three seconds of rising temperature that made the air shimmer. From the direction of the main forge chamber, a faint orange glow flickered, then vanished.

---

Garrick ripped the offending pauldron strap free, stuffing it into his belt. The silence that followed was immediate. Cora knelt before an iron bin marked *ADAMANTINE — FORGE MASTER'S AUTHORIZATION REQUIRED*, her thieves' tools clicking softly. Mercer's translation yielded more: emergency procedures for forge overheat, with coolant reserves accessible from inside the main chamber.

Then the third pulse came—not a wave, but sustained heat that made walls warm to the touch. A deep, resonant *thrum* vibrated through the floor. The sound of enormous bellows activating after centuries of silence.

Light bloomed through the archway—steady, growing radiance painting the workshops in stark shadows. The air filled with scents of superheated iron and ozone. A voice echoed through stone in the cracking, popping language of heated stone—**Primordial**.

Mercer cast *Comprehend Languages*, his hands tracing arcane patterns. "It's not hostile—it's a diagnostic query! 'System status: operational?'"

Garrick didn't wait. "Forget diplomacy," he growled, activating his Giant's Might as he charged. His frame expanded, muscles surging as he barreled into the main forge chamber, maul swinging.

---

The chamber was enormous—eighty feet across, with six massive anvils ringing a central hearth. The guardian was [[Ignathar]], a diminished fire elemental appearing as an eight-foot column of guttering flame. It flared as Garrick's maul passed through its form, its voice crackling with confusion: *"Containment breach? Physical intrusion? Diagnostic inconclusive."*

Then Garrick's maul connected.

Chaos erupted. Cora drank her Resilience Elixir, feeling protective energy surge through her. Mercer shouted Primordial commands while his spectral mind flew toward emergency coolant controls. "Garrick, you're attacking a diagnostic protocol!"

The elemental lashed out—not with malice, but ancient defensive protocols triggered by unauthorized intrusion. *"Intrusion confirmed! Purge initiated!"*

Cora cast *Ray of Frost*, the cold energy making Ignathar's flames gutter. Garrick landed a critical blow, his maul slamming through the elemental's core. Mercer, in desperation, cast *Hold Person* on his own ally. "I'm sorry, Garrick, but you leave me no choice!"

---

The battle shifted when Cora cast *Web*, magical strands entangling the area around the coolant controls. Ignathar thrashed, then stopped struggling as the webbing sizzled against its flames. *"Containment... protocol... recognized. Ancient... magic."*

The elemental settled, its voice carrying what might be relief. Mercer dropped his spell, freeing Garrick from paralysis. Through translated Primordial, a dialogue began:

*"The heart-fire dims. The binding-runes fade. The Sleeper dreams deeper, and the mountain forgets its purpose."*

Ignathar wasn't just a guardian—it was part of the filtration system itself, bound to regulate Dreamstone radiation conversion. For a thousand years, it had been alone. Cold. Waiting.

Cora approached with a ledger in hand. "Proposal: We help you restore the forge voluntarily. You maintain it not as a bound slave, but as a partner. When the Sleeper is contained, we negotiate permanent terms."

Garrick, his maul lowered but not sheathed, added: "Partners. Not master and slave. We fight together."

Mercer translated, adding scholarly context: "The original enchantments have decayed beyond repair. We couldn't bind you even if we wanted to. This would be... unprecedented."

Ignathar's flames burned steadily, thoughtfully. *"Partnership. Not ownership. These words... the dwarves never spoke them."* The elemental drifted toward the central hearth. *"A thousand years of cold. A thousand years of silence. You offer purpose. You offer... not being alone."*

It settled into the iron pit. *"I accept. Voluntary partnership."*

---

As they placed hands on the warm hearth iron, Ignathar's flames surged with purpose. The forge ignited properly for the first time in a millennium—iron glowing cherry red, then orange, cooling channels carrying milky-white emergency coolant in perfect balance with the heat. The chamber hummed with returning power.

Then new sounds reached them from deeper in the mountain: the distant clatter of metal on stone. Rhythmic. Industrial.

Ignathar's flames flickered in recognition. *"The Twisted. They work the deep minecart network. They have been drilling toward the Giant containment for centuries. Now they know the forge is awake."*

Cora immediately worked on the adamantine bin's enchanted lock, her tools finding precise alignment. The lid released with a hiss. Inside: forty pounds of unworked adamantine ore and a leather-bound [[Runesmith's Journal]]. The final entry, in shaky Dwarvish: *"I release Ignathar from service. It has earned its rest. I only wish I could release us all."*

Mercer discovered schematics within—for "psychic dampeners." Amplifiers in reverse. "We could build these! Disrupt the Sleeper's control over the Twisted!"

But time was short. Tapping sounds came from the eastern archway—metal on stone, mapping the barrier. Then the high-pitched whine of diamond-tipped drills.

---

They worked in desperate symphony. Ignathar maintained exact 2,200-degree heat. Cora purified the Dreamstone Fragment under Mercer's *Slow* spell, preventing catastrophic fracture. Garrick forged adamantine components with masterwork dwarven tools, his strength shaping the rare metal.

The prototype psychic dampener took shape: adamantine frame housing purified dreamstone crystal. With minimal heat from Ignathar, it activated—a sphere of clear blue energy expanding through the chamber.

The drilling stopped.

From the other side of the barrier, voices—not the Twisted's dry rasp, but confused Dwarvish exclamations:
*"Where... where am I?"*
*"The forge... the forge is lit?"*
*"How long... how long has it been?"*

The dampener worked. For moments, the Twisted remembered themselves.

But the prototype flickered, unstable. The voices faded, the dry rasp returning: *"The Sleeper... wonders... the light... disrupts..."*

---

"We retreat," Cora declared, packing components with efficient motions. "We have proof of concept, not a weapon. We return with proper resources."

Garrick helped, his movements careful. "I've killed men who deserved it. I won't kill souls trapped in their own bodies."

Ignathar watched them prepare to leave, its flames burning with steady purpose. *"I will keep the heart-fire warm. The mountain will remember this light. Bring the lost children home."*

As they ascended, the mountain felt different—warmer, alive with industry's returned vibration. They emerged to find [[Brenna]] and their workforce armed and anxious. "We felt the heat," she said. "The whole mountain... it's warmer."

Cora handed her the ledger open to dampener schematics. "We reignited the forge. And we discovered something hopeful."

Garrick looked back down the corridor. "We're coming back. With an army and a way to break chains."

The forge was lit. The partnership held. And for the first time since entering the Asymmetrical Mountain, they had more than survival as a goal.

They had redemption to plan.
