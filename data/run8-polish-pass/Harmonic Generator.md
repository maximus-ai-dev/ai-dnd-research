The **Harmonic Generator** was a Twisted organic-crystalline device that produced resonant frequencies to power a biological drill.

## Description and Fate
This device was located within an organic power hub in the Twisted staging ground. It generated the specific harmonic frequencies required to operate the Twisted's deep-drilling apparatus. The Harmonic Generator was analyzed and subsequently destroyed by [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] using a precisely tuned Shatter spell. Its destruction blinded the staging ground by cutting power to the drill.
