**The Asymmetrical Mountain - Forge Complex**

You've followed the sound before, but never with the forge active. Now, with Ignathar's heat warming the surrounding stone, the mountain feels different—alive in a way it hasn't been for centuries. The dwarven architecture seems to breathe with the forge's rhythm.

The rails stretch into darkness, descending slightly as they curve eastward. At the tunnel's entrance, a raised stone loading platform stands with iron crane brackets overhead. The rhythmic sound is louder here, echoing down the tunnel from somewhere deeper in the mountain.

Cora's engineering eye immediately assesses the system: dwarven iron rails treated against corrosion, switch mechanisms at a visible junction ahead, and the potential for reactivation. Mercer's scholarly mind recognizes the scale—this isn't just a minecart track; it's a transportation network, the veins through which the dwarven empire moved its wealth.

The clattering sound continues from the east, growing slightly louder as the forge's warmth reaches deeper into the mountain. Whatever is making that sound, it's down this rail line.
