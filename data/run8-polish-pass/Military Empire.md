The **Military Empire** was an ancient human empire that constructed the [[Imperial Lower Palace]] atop [[Giant]] ruins within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] to monitor a containment breach.

## Legacy and Purpose
The empire's lower palace served as a monitoring station and garrison for the entity known as the [[First World Sleeper]]. Its archives contain detailed records of the Sleeper's corrupting influence and the eventual failure of the ancient containment. The site was sealed by the empire's forces, including [[Commander Valerius]], after they determined a breach was inevitable.
