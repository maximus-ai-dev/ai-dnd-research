The **Hall of the Giants** is a massive chamber with Giant-sized architecture that serves as a primary harmonic containment chamber within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Function and History
Constructed by the ancient [[Giant]] civilization, the hall's architecture and harmonic runes were designed to contain the entity known as [[First World Sleeper|the Sleeper]]. The chamber's runes can be reactivated to generate a repelling harmonic field against entities composed of [[Dreamstone]] energy.
