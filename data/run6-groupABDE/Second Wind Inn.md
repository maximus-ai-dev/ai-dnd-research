The **Second Wind Inn** is the primary tavern and lodging house in the frontier town of [[Grimhold]].

## Description and Atmosphere
A two-story timber building located on the town's main street, the inn is a central hub for travelers, prospectors, and locals. The interior is typically warm, smelling of woodsmoke and stew, providing a rare comfort against the cold of the northern frontier.

## Function
The inn serves as a common location for gathering information, hiring guides, and hearing news from the surrounding hills. It is owned and operated by a local merchant.
