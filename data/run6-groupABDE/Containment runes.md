**Containment runes** are ancient magical inscriptions integral to the prison of the [[First World Sleeper]].

## Purpose and Function
These runes form a network that reinforces the structural seal of the [[Giant Capstone]]. They generate a sustained field of magical energy that suppresses the Sleeper's influence and maintains the integrity of the barrier between the containment and the void below.

## Manifestation and Behavior
The runes are etched into the stone of the [[Giant foundation]] and normally glow with a steady, low light. Their luminosity is not static; it can fluctuate in response to stressors on the containment field, such as breaches, attacks, or shifts in the Sleeper's own state of awareness. An increase in their glow indicates the field is actively compensating for increased pressure or activity from the imprisoned entity.
