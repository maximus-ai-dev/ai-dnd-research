# Session 9: The Sabotaged Pumps

*From the chronicles of the Asymmetrical Mountain expedition, recorded by an observer embedded with the Grimhold Specialists*

---

The surface fortress of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] had become a strange sanctuary in the weeks since [[Garrick Kade]], [[Cora Flint]], and [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] had reignited the [[Cold Forge]]. From their watchtower perch, they watched [[Brenna]]'s workforce maintain supply lines through the [[Dwarven Minecart Network]], establishing a fragile foothold in the mountain's upper reaches. But below them, in the drowned depths of the [[Dwarven Layer]], a more urgent problem demanded attention.

The northern branch to [[Pump Station East]] glowed red on the transit hub map. According to dwarven schematics, this station controlled drainage for the entire lower layer. Without it, hundreds of feet of infrastructure remained submerged—including the primary routes to whatever [[The Twisted]] were drilling toward.

**The Flooded Descent**

[[Cora Flint]]'s practical mind immediately calculated the risks: "Flooded tunnels mean water hazards, structural damage, and likely biological contaminants." Her inventory of the maintenance cart revealed standard dwarven tools but no specialized diving gear. [[Garrick Kade]], ever impatient, settled into the cart with his rune-etched maul already glowing. "Planning's fine, but we're wasting time. If the pumps are flooded, we need to get down there and clear whatever's blocking them."

[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] cast [[Mage Armor]] with academic precision, his spectral mind already manifesting to scout ahead. "Smashing things without understanding them is how priceless historical artifacts get destroyed," he chided Garrick, though the fighter had already set the cart in motion.

The journey began smoothly, but within minutes, water appeared—first as a sheen on the rails, then as knee-deep resistance that forced them to abandon the cart. The water was warm, circulating slowly, carrying a mineral scent that spoke of deep geothermal activity. Mercer's spectral scout reported bioluminescence ahead—faint blue-green light pulsing in the water.

They found a stone staircase leading to an elevated platform. Garrick's survival instincts confirmed the structure's stability, though mineral deposits revealed historical water levels. Mercer's mind scouted ahead, returning with troubling news: "The pumps have been sabotaged with professional precision. Biological resin sealing one, pipes severed on another, control mechanism disassembled on the third. And there are fresh dwarven boot prints."

**The Twisted Saboteurs**

In [[Pump Station East]], the scale of the sabotage became clear. Three massive pump assemblies stood silent, each disabled in a different methodical way. But as they examined the damage, movement came from the flooded side passage.

Four [[Twisted]] emerged waist-deep in the warm water. Their proportions were dwarven, but their skin had the iridescent [[Dreamstone]] sheen, veins pulsing with black fluid. Most tellingly, they carried tools, not weapons—pry bars, chisels, coils of the same black resin that sealed Pump One.

Garrick raised his maul, but Cora stepped forward. "They're not attacking—they're showing us tools." She addressed the lead Twisted: "We reignited the forge. We spoke with [[Ignathar]]. Why stop the water? What are you containing?"

The Twisted responded with gestures: pumps, passage, a cutting-off motion. Then it produced a section of pipe—the missing piece from Pump Two—and placed it carefully on a ledge. The message was clear: *We took the pipes. What we're containing requires the water to remain.*

Then a mechanical thrum vibrated through the stone, growing louder. The Twisted reacted with coordinated purpose, two wading toward the sound while the remaining two held position. The lead Twisted pointed at the pipe section, then at Pump Two, then made a repair motion. Then it pointed toward the approaching thrum and repeated the cutting-off gesture.

**The Drowned Juggernaut**

Garrick didn't hesitate. "No time for negotiations!" He waded into the churning water, activating [[Giant's Might]] as his body swelled to Large size. What emerged from the darkness was a [[Drowned Juggernaut]]—a fifteen-foot mining mech transformed by Dreamstone corruption, its iron plates overgrown with black resin, bioluminescent organisms clustering at its joints.

Mercer's [[Slow]] spell wrapped arcane chains around the machine, while Cora's [[Ray of Frost]] created ice obstacles. The Twisted worked not as fighters but as saboteurs, prying at tread mechanisms and scraping resin from power conduits. Garrick's maul strikes landed with crushing force, buckling the right tread assembly.

But the juggernaut fought back. Its drill head ground against Garrick's chain mail, driving him back through the water. Then the machine began building energy for a discharge—Dreamstone power arcing through the water as it sought ground. Garrick took the full force, convulsing as lightning coursed through him, but his [[Relentless Endurance]] kept him on his feet at 1 HP.

Mercer saw the solution: "They're using themselves as living capacitors!" The Twisted positioned themselves along a resin conduit they'd created, shaping the energy flow with their own corrupted biology. As the juggernaut reverted from Mercer's [[Polymorph]] spell, the energy surged along the conduit into a smaller mining mech, which petrified into obsidian and hardened resin.

The juggernaut stood inert, purged of Dreamstone corruption.

**The Factory Revealed**

But the water's recession revealed something more terrifying: an entire factory line of corrupted mining mechs, partially embedded in the tunnel floor, all pulsing with awakening energy. Mercer's analysis turned to dread: "It's not an army. It's a *factory*. Autonomous replication protocols."

The lead Twisted confirmed the horror with gestures: the water had been maintaining stasis. The factory was awakening as it drained. Together, they optimized the pump modulation, tuning it to the mountain's natural 7.3 Hz resonance frequency. The pumps' roar harmonized, creating a resonant cage that put the factory line into deep, synchronized sleep.

An alliance was forged in that moment. The Twisted weren't saboteurs—they were caretakers who had transformed themselves to protect something. They pointed down the now-draining spiral staircase, where a melodic hum vibrated through the stone. Then they made the drilling motion and the thinking fire gesture. *The thinking fire hates that sound. It drills to silence it.*

**The Resonance Chamber**

Mercer's spectral mind revealed the chamber below: a vast circular space with geometrically perfect crystal columns glowing with internal light. At its center, a massive crystal sphere rotated slowly, emitting the pure tone that harmonized with the mountain's standing wave.

But corruption had reached even here. One wall had been replaced with black resin, tendrils extending toward the sphere. And from beyond that wall came the drill's rhythmic thumping—much closer than they'd thought.

The lead Twisted made a devastating gesture: the water was pure. Their corruption would pollute it. They could no longer enter the chamber they were created to protect.

Garrick didn't hesitate. He descended into the pure, singing water, feeling it resonate with his very cells. The water didn't reject him—it examined the faint dream-tendrils of his Sleeper connection. But as he reached the archway, the resin wall reacted, tendrils lashing out to darken a crystal column.

Garrick raised his maul, focusing not on force but on harmony. The runes pulsed with the chamber's song, and the resin recoiled from the pure harmonic energy.

Then stone shattered. Crystal cracked. The drill broke through.

Through the fissure in the resin wall, a presence emerged—heat and thought and malevolent purpose made manifest. The [[Thinking Fire]] had reached the [[Resonance Chamber]].

Garrick stood waist-deep in singing water, his maul glowing with harmonic light, facing a breach in reality itself. The mountain's last pure harmonic anchor was seconds from being silenced forever.
