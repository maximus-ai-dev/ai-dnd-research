**Korathan's Chisel** is a replica of the original tool used by the [[Giant]] architect [[Korathan]] to inscribe the primary containment runes within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Function
The chisel is a masterwork arcane focus designed for the precise carving of runes that interact with dimensional boundaries. While a replica, it is crafted to the exact specifications and magical resonances of the original tool used by Korathan over a hundred thousand years ago.

## Recent Use
The replica chisel was a critical component in the forging of the [[Dreamstone Seal]]. [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] used it to inscribe the complete, scaled-down containment rune matrix onto the attuned [[Dreamstone]] core. This process required four hours of continuous, precise work to translate Giant-scale dimensional mathematics into human-scale runic language, a task for which the chisel was essential.
