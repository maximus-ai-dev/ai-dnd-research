The **Starmetal Hills** are a range of low, rocky hills located northwest of [[Grimhold]].

## Geography and Terrain
The terrain is windswept, largely treeless, and scattered with ancient stone markers and strange geological formations. Travel through the hills is physically demanding due to the rough landscape and lack of shelter.

## Economic and Historical Significance
The hills are rich with mineral deposits, supporting [[Grimhold]]'s economy through numerous mining claims. They also serve as the primary route to the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] and were once the operating territory of the [[Emberfell Rejects]] bandit group. A bitter winter in the hills created an urgent need for food supplies, leading to expeditions into the mountain's depths. Prospectors have recently reported abandoning claims due to a metallic scent and strange dreams emanating from the mountain.
