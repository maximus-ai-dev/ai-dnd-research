**Griffon's Hold** is a well-stocked weapons and equipment shop located in the frontier town of [[Grimhold]].

## Proprietor and Stock
The shop is owned and operated by the retired mercenary [[Elara Stormwind]]. It carries a practical inventory of gear suitable for prospecting, travel, and defense on the northern frontier, all sold at fair prices.

## Role in the Community
Beyond commerce, Griffon's Hold acts as an informal community meeting place and a vital source of local knowledge. Elara often shares tales and practical advice about the surrounding [[Starmetal Hills]] and the dangers of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].
