**Amplifier Alpha** was the first and simplest of three organic broadcast towers constructed by the [[Twisted]] within the [[Giant]] foundation level of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Function
Located in the northwest quadrant, the amplifier was a column of [[Black Organic Resin]] grown directly into the stone, threaded with bioluminescent veins. It functioned by parasitizing the ancient Giant containment runes, siphoning their energy to amplify the [[First World Sleeper]]'s psychic broadcast. It was considered a prototype and was the weakest node in the network.

## Destruction
The amplifier was destroyed through the precise severing of its root-like energy conduits. This method caused it to collapse silently without triggering a broadcast alarm that would have alerted other [[Twisted]] forces.
