The **Military Empire** was a deep human civilization that constructed a layer of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] directly atop ancient [[Giant]] ruins and dwarven vaults. It represents the deepest of the three human epochs found within the mountain.

## Architecture and Culture
The Empire's architecture is characterized by grand scale, built for formations with wide corridors and high ceilings. They favored elaborate stonework with carved reliefs showing military parades and conquered territories. The Empire stored trophies from conquered civilizations, including the Giants, in dedicated relic basements like the [[Hall of Conquests]].

## History and Legacy
The Empire's construction piled great halls and garrison quarters atop the older strata until the weight began to crack the foundations below. Imperial officers were aware of a corrupted [[Sealed Crypt]] within their layer but chose not to act on it. The layer is now abandoned, with its structural integrity critically compromised. The Empire was actively harnessing the [[First World Sleeper]], and its containment schematics are cross-referenced with logs of the resulting anomalies.
