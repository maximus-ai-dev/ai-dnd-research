The **Deep Foundry** is a corrupted dwarven workshop located beneath the [[Cold Forge]] in the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## History and Corruption
The foundry was the site of a deep drill project initiated by the dwarves of [[Kazad-Khrom]], which ultimately breached into [[Dream-Fire]]. It is now a place where [[The Twisted]] labor, paradoxically working to reinforce reality against the influence of the dreaming entity below, using methods that further corrupt the environment.

## Current Threat
The foundry is currently sealed, but an autonomously operating drilling mechanism within continues its work unabated. This poses a significant and ongoing threat to the structural foundation of the entire mountain complex.
