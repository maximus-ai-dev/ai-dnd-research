The **Organic Power Hub** was a critical piece of [[Twisted]] bio-engineering.

## Description
This structure was a central nexus where numerous resin conduits converged, forming the nervous system and primary power distribution center for a Twisted staging ground. It channeled energy to various installations, most importantly to a biological drill.

## Function and Importance
The hub coordinated the staging ground's operations, including communication and power flow. Its destruction blinded and disoriented the Twisted forces, severing their connection to the organic infrastructure and crippling their coordinated defense.

## Fate
The hub was critically damaged during an assault on the staging ground. Its collapse cascaded through the network, causing the attached resin conduits throughout the installation to go dark.
