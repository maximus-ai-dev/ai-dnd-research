The **Rat King** was an alpha specimen of the mutated giant rats infesting the [[Salt-Vaults]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Role
It was a bloated creature, larger than a typical giant rat, with an iridescent sheen to its fur. As the colony's reproductive center, it remained at the heart of the nest. The Rat King was aggressively territorial and led the defense of the vaults against intruders.

## Demise
The Rat King was killed during an assault on the main nest chamber. Its death caused the remaining rat colony to scatter and flee from the vaults.
