The **Memory Vault** is a lead-sealed chamber located in the upper [[Dwarven Layer]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Contents and Significance
It contains a corrupted dwarven memory imprint detailing the final containment efforts of [[Kazad-Khrom]]. The memory depicts a panicked attempt by dwarves to seal the 'lower forges' from the 'screaming stone', providing a firsthand account of the realization of the [[First World Sleeper]]'s corrupting influence.
