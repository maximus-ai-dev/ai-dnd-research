The **Thinking Fire** is a sentient, malevolent anomaly of heat and thought that drills toward the [[Resonance Chamber]] to silence its harmonic frequency.

## Nature and Activity
This entity manifests as a sentient force of heat and destructive intent. It actively breaches containment barriers, having broken through the wall of the Resonance Chamber to confront those within, driven by a desire to extinguish the chamber's stabilizing harmonic frequency.
