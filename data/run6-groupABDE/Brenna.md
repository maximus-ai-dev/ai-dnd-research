**Brenna** is the practical and direct leader of the workforce residing at the surface fortress of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. She is a former member of the [[Emberfell Rejects]].

## Role and Responsibilities
Brenna manages all logistics, organization, and safety for her people. She oversaw the repair contract concerning the [[Psychic Resonance Entity]] in the [[Salt-Vaults]]. While she trusts the adventurers who frequent the mountain, she holds them firmly accountable for the safety of the workers under her charge.

## Leadership Style
A capable and hands-on organizer, Brenna works alongside [[Grimhold]] engineers and reformed saboteurs to maintain, repair, and reinforce the mountain's surface fortress, ensuring it remains a viable base of operations.
