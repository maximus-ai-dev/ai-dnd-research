The **Iron Warlords** were a militaristic human civilization that inhabited the [[Starmetal Hills]] region centuries prior to the current era.

## Architecture and Culture
Their architecture is characterized by brutal pragmatism and a focus on defense. Corridors are narrow with low ceilings, intersections form chokepoints, and defensive features like arrow slits are common. Their constructions include large-scale logistical facilities such as barracks, armories, and the [[Salt-Vaults]] designed to support a garrison indefinitely. Their culture was highly organized, with a focus on martial readiness and survival.

## Presence in the Asymmetrical Mountain
The Iron Warlords built a significant layer within the mountain, including the [[Salt-Vaults]] and garrison quarters. Their construction in the [[Grand Stairwell]] was a retrofit into the older [[Military Empire]]'s existing architecture.
