The **Resonance Chamber** is a vast subterranean chamber located deep below [[Pump Station East]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Significance
The chamber is characterized by its singing crystal columns and a pool of pure water. At its center is a harmonic crystal sphere that maintains dimensional stability within the mountain. The chamber serves as the harmonic anchor that [[The Twisted]] were created to protect from the 'thinking fire' of the [[Dream-Fire]] anomaly.
