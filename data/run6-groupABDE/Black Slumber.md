The **Black Slumber** is a seal or state of containment referenced in the scrolls of the [[Secretive Brotherhood]]'s [[Grand Archive]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Nature and History
According to Brotherhood records, the construction of the Asymmetrical Mountain and its subterranean complexes was intrinsically linked to maintaining this seal. Their contingency plans indicate the seal is now failing. Insights gained at the [[Chamber of the First Anchor]] suggest the Black Slumber is not itself a malevolent creature, but rather a contained possibility or state that must not be allowed to awaken.

## Current Status
Its exact nature remains one of the mountain's deepest mysteries.
