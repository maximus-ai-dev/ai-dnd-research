**Korathan's scaling runes** are an ancient [[Giant]] runic technique for repairing, maintaining, and amplifying containment architecture.

## Discovery
This knowledge was discovered within [[Korathan]]'s tomb by [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] and transcribed into his [[Architect's Codex]]. The techniques represent a "technology transfer" from Giant-scale engineering to human-scale application.

## Function and Application
The runes allow for the reactivation and reinforcement of damaged [[containment rune]] arrays, the inscription of new, smaller runes that interface with the larger Giant system, and the construction of specialized devices like the [[Capstone Resonator]]. They were used to reactivate the suppression field in the [[Warden's Post]] and are fundamental to ongoing efforts to reinforce the failing containment seal.
