A **Giant Monitoring Crystal** is a large crystalline device used to monitor containment integrity and energy levels within the [[Giant]] foundation of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Function
The crystal pulses with light in response to energy fluctuations, mapping signals and anomalous readings throughout the Giant architecture. It can detect spikes in psychic radiation, such as those emitted by [[Dreamstone]] veins, and assists in navigating the complex containment systems.

## Primary Location
A primary Monitoring Crystal is established at the forward camp within the Giant foundation level, serving as a central hub for environmental analysis and expedition planning.
