The **Dwarven Master Forge Key** is a complex iron key recovered from the core mechanism of the destroyed [[Drowned Juggernaut]].

## Description and Function
The key is an intricate piece of dwarven engineering, designed to operate the master control systems of the [[Cold Forge]]. When used in conjunction with a functional cooling system, the key unlocks the forge's full advanced capacity, enabling sophisticated metallurgical work, including the forging of adamantine. Prior to the reactivation of the mountain's pump infrastructure, the key was rendered unusable.
