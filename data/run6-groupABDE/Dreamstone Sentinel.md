A **Dreamstone Sentinel** is a crystalline construct formed from raw [[Dreamstone]] to defend an exposed Dreamstone vein from disturbance.

## Description
The Sentinel is a roughly humanoid construct approximately ten feet tall, composed of raw Dreamstone that has self-organized into a mobile, glowing form. It hovers above the ground, unaffected by local gravity distortions, and its crystalline body pulses with shifting colors. It possesses no true consciousness; it functions as a crystallized energy defense mechanism that activates in response to proximity, akin to a living pressure plate.

## Abilities and Vulnerabilities
The Sentinel can deliver powerful crystalline strikes that inflict both force and psychic damage. It can generate localized gravity pulses to reverse gravity for affected creatures and unleash psychic bursts that cause hallucinations and stun targets. While within thirty feet of an exposed Dreamstone vein, it regenerates health rapidly. It is vulnerable to thunder damage, which shatters its structure, and radiant damage bypasses its regeneration. It is resistant to fire, cold, lightning, and nonmagical weapons.
