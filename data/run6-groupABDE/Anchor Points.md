**Anchor Points** are four massive iron rings set into the walls of the [[Caldera]] at equidistant points around its circumference.

## Function and Design
They are critical structural components of the Giant containment system, designed as focal points where the containment field's energy is concentrated. Activating all four Anchor Points, typically through the application of [[Korathan's scaling runes]], strengthens the containment field to its maximum rated output.

## Significance
This reinforcement is essential for maintaining the integrity of the translation layer that manages the emissions from the [[Anomaly]].
