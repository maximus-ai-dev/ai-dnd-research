The **Architect's Council** was the governing body of master builders within the ancient [[Giant]] civilization.

## Function and History
The council oversaw the civilization's greatest construction projects, including the containment of the [[First World Sleeper]] beneath the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. After discovering the entity, the council debated its fate, ultimately choosing a strategy of containment over attempted destruction.

## Notable Actions
The council commissioned Master Runecarver [[Korathan]] to design and build the containment vessel and later sealed him within his tomb, [[The Maker's Rest]], following his entry into "the long watch."
