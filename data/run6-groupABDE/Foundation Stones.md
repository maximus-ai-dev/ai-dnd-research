The **Foundation Stones** are seven crystalline anchors constructed by the [[Giants]] to physically ground the psychic barrier containing the entity known as the [[Old Hunger]].

## Function and Current State
The stones serve as the critical infrastructure for the ancient containment system. This system is now partially failed, with one of the stones, identified as [[Stone Theta]], already dark and inactive. The failure of these stones threatens the integrity of the entire containment surrounding the Old Hunger.
