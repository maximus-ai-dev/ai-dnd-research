**Korathan** is the Master Runecarver of the [[Giant]] [[Architect's Council]] and the chief architect of the containment system for the [[First World Sleeper]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Role and History
Korathan spent three centuries designing and constructing the monumental containment architecture deep within the mountain. Upon the system's completion, Korathan voluntarily entered a state of suspended animation—"the long watch"—within a sealed tomb known as [[The Maker's Rest]]. This act served as a final contingency, preserving the architect to be awakened should the containment ever require fundamental repair.

## Legacy
Korathan's tomb contains the master runecarver's personal knowledge, including the critical scaling techniques that allow containment runes to function at any size. This knowledge was deliberately preserved as a technology transfer for future, smaller caretakers. His silhouette was briefly visible within the dissolving [[Avatar of the Slumber]] as the containment was secured, symbolizing the completion of his ancient work by a new generation.
