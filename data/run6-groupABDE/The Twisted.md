**The Twisted** are a faction of corrupted dwarves inhabiting the deep layers of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
The Twisted have undergone severe physical and mental degradation, repurposing ancient dwarven infrastructure with grotesque organic technology. Their modifications fuse [[Black Organic Resin]] with dwarven iron and stone, creating living, growing structures.

## Activities and Infrastructure
The Twisted maintain and modify the mountain's ancient systems, including the [[Dwarven Minecart Network]] and water management infrastructure. They utilize [[Dreamstone]] radiation as an energy source and have constructed bio-mechanical hybrids, such as the [[Drowned Juggernaut]], to further their goals. Their engineering schematics indicate a complex plan involving the redirection of containment energy. They originated from the dwarven volunteers known as the [[Stubborn Deep]].
