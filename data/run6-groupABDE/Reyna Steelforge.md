**Reyna Steelforge** is the pragmatic leader of the [[Prospectors' Guild]] in the frontier town of [[Grimhold]] and the primary administrative authority for expeditions into the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Appearance and Demeanor
Steelforge possesses a severe, practical appearance, with hands often stained from work. She speaks in short, declarative sentences, a habit formed from decades of frontier experience.

## Role and Activities
As head of the Prospectors' Guild, she regulates mining claims in the [[Starmetal Hills]], posts bounties, and maintains a network of scholarly contacts. She exhibits intense academic interest in ancient discoveries, particularly [[Giant]]-era construction. After learning the true nature of the mountain's containment, her focus shifted from pure research to active logistical support for efforts to reinforce the failing systems.

## Later Involvement
Following the successful deployment of the [[Dreamstone Seal]], Steelforge oversaw the transformation of the mountain's surface fortress into a citadel of learning and guardianship, ensuring the continued watch over the secured containment.
