**Reality-Anchoring Tools** are a set of alchemical implements crafted from [[Dreamstone]] designed to stabilize local reality.

## Properties
The tools create subtle zones of stability by their mere presence, counteracting gravity distortions and spatial warps caused by concentrated Dreamstone radiation. They appear to drink light from their surroundings, creating pockets of perfect stillness and predictable physics.

## Use
These tools are essential for safe mining operations and navigation in areas of severe reality distortion within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. They represent a practical application of Dreamstone's unique properties, turning the substance's reality-warping energy against itself to create areas of control and safety.
