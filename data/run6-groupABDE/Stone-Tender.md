**Stone-Tenders** are crystalline constructs that serve as the hands and sensory organs of the [[Psychic Resonance Entity]] within the [[Salt-Vaults]].

## Behavior and Function
They communicate through binary clicking sounds and patrol the vaults to monitor structural and magical integrity. Stone-Tenders provided instruction for repair work and reacted defensively to the breach of the interstitial space, positioning themselves to guard the food silos. They have also been observed repairing stress fractures and acting as translators for [[The Twisted]].
