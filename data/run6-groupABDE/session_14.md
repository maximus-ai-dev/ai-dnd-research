# Session 14: The Runecarver's Tomb

*Chronicle of the Adapted Wardens' descent into the Maker's Rest, where time itself holds its breath*

---

The forward camp in the [[Giant]] foundation level had become something like home. For weeks, the party had used this monitoring alcove—the size of a small cathedral—as their base of operations. The air hummed with the containment field's low-frequency vibration, a constant reminder of the [[First World Sleeper]] dreaming somewhere below. Walls were covered in [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]]'s notes, [[Cora Flint]]'s schematics, and [[Garrick Kade]]'s rough maps of [[The Root]] layer.

They had rested. A trip back to [[Grimhold]] for supplies—fresh rations, lamp oil, parchment, and a few days of real sleep in actual beds at [[The Second Wind Inn]]. [[Reyna Steelforge]] had listened to their report about the deeper entity with grim focus. "If the Sleeper was the barrier," she said, "and the barrier is failing... then we need to understand the barrier's design better than its builders did."

Now they stood before the [[Giant blueprint tablet]], its luminous runes casting pale blue light across the stone floor. Mercer had been studying it for hours, cross-referencing with containment schematics from the [[Grand Archive]]. His finger traced a symbol near the outer ring's deepest section—a rune unlike the others. Not a structural notation, not a harmonic frequency. A name.

"The Maker's Rest," Mercer said quietly. "[[Korathan]], Master Runecarver of the [[Architect's Council]]. The Giant who designed the containment vessel. According to the tablet, they buried him with his work. Not just his body—his knowledge. The scaling techniques that let containment runes function at any size."

He looked up, his eyes intense. "If we're going to repair the containment anchor, if we're going to build anything that can hold back what's below... we need those techniques. The tomb is here." He tapped the map. "Deep in the outer ring. Sealed by the Architect's Council after Korathan's... vigil."

The word hung in the air. Vigil, not death.

## The Obsidian Door

The journey to the tomb entrance took several hours through corridors they'd come to know—the massive inspection circuits, maintenance passages with tools the size of trees, silent monitoring alcoves where ancient crystals still tracked the containment field's strength. The scale never stopped being humbling. They were insects in a machine built for gods.

And then they saw it.

The door was forty feet tall and twenty feet wide, carved from a single slab of polished obsidian. Its surface was covered in flowing Giant script—not the functional rune sequences they'd seen throughout the foundation, but narrative text. A story carved in stone. The air was still, cold, and carried the scent of ozone and ancient dust. A massive lever mechanism was built into the door frame, fifteen feet above the floor, clearly designed for a Giant's hand.

Mercer approached, his spectral mind floating beside him as he studied the text. "It's an epitaph," he murmured. "Korathan spent three centuries building the containment. When it was complete... he entered the long watch."

Cora began searching along the base for maintenance access, while Garrick activated his [[Giant's Might]], growing to nearly ten feet tall to better examine the lever. Mercer deciphered the deeper meaning: "Visitors must demonstrate understanding of containment principles. The lever isn't a simple mechanical release—it's a test."

The first attempt failed spectacularly. Mercer's magical projection flickered under the tomb's shielding, Cora's hastily-made stencil proved imprecise, and Garrick's fighter hands were too clumsy for delicate rune-tracing. The amber glow in the rune grooves faded completely as the mechanism reset.

Mercer read the newly illuminated text, his face pale. "Three attempts granted to those who approach with honest intent. Two remain. Fail again, and the Maker's Rest becomes eternal."

## Craftsmanship and Understanding

They planned carefully. Garrick's insight proved crucial: "Korathan was a runecarver—a craftsman. He wouldn't respect magical shortcuts." Mercer's spectral mind measured the grooves—one inch wide, half an inch deep, three concentric circles with twelve connection points each. Cora realized the truth: "They're not just drawing lines. They're completing a circuit. All three runes need to be traced at once."

Working with her [[Tool Expertise]], Cora shaped a crystalline research rod into a triple-tipped stylus, three points arranged at perfect 120-degree angles. They practiced on the floor, Garrick's hands growing surprisingly steady as he traced Mercer's glowing patterns.

The second attempt began with Garrick growing to Large size again, the crystalline stylus humming in his hand. He traced the patterns perfectly—dimensional alignment spiral, harmonic scaling waves, sympathetic connection lines. The runes glowed with completed light... but didn't harmonize.

Mercer realized the missing element: "The sympathetic connection requires acknowledgment. Before you pull, state your purpose. Not aloud to us—to the tomb."

Garrick spoke, his voice rumbling through the chamber. "I'm Garrick Kade... We're not here for treasure or glory. We're here to fix what's broken. Korathan, if you're listening... we need your knowledge. Not to steal it. To use it. To continue the watch."

The three rune glows synchronized into unified harmonic resonance. Garrick pulled the lever, and the obsidian door slid sideways with a grinding sound unheard in millennia.

## Trials of Scale

Beyond lay a corridor thirty feet wide, forty feet high, its walls carved with illustrations of Korathan's life. Immediately, traps activated—pressure plates dropped to reveal blade channels, while massive stone crushers extended and retracted thirty feet above.

Cora mapped the pattern: safe tiles corresponded to construction panels in Korathan's life, triggered tiles to moments of uncertainty. But the safe tiles were spaced for Giant strides, not human steps. Cora engineered a rope bridge anchored in the scaffolding carvings—a crafted solution Korathan would respect.

Next came a gas chamber, nozzles twenty-five feet above ready to release magical-reactive vapor. Mercer understood: "This tests containment principles. The gas reacts to uncontrolled magical energy." They suppressed their infused items and spells to calibrated levels, demonstrating mastery rather than absence. The opalescent gas descended, forming delicate containment fields around their equipment but not harming them as they passed through with disciplined control.

A staircase of three-foot-tall steps awaited, every third one shimmering with gravity reversal runes. Using their [[Reality Anchor]] tools from the Dreamstone forging, they created a ten-foot bubble of stable physics, carrying normal reality through altered physical laws.

## The Final Choice

The corridor split into three passages marked with Giant runes: Strength, Endurance, Knowledge. Garrick tested each with thrown pitons. Strength triggered a crushing ceiling trap. Knowledge activated a deadly puzzle floor. Endurance remained undisturbed.

Mercer understood: "Endurance was Korathan's principle. The long watch." They chose correctly.

A fifteen-foot stone construct guardian blocked the final approach, inscribed with "Test of Recognition." Mercer spoke formal Giant, presenting the blueprint transcription: "We approach the Maker's Rest with recognition of purpose... We are inheritors of the long watch."

The construct's runes glowed blue. It stepped aside, bowing its head in respect.

## The Maker's Rest

The burial chamber was circular, sixty feet across with a domed ceiling. Walls were covered in Korathan's personal runes—smaller, denser, more intricate than any they'd seen. At the center, on a raised stone platform, lay [[Korathan]].

Twenty-two feet of Giant, intact in ceremonial armor, hands folded over a runic chisel that glowed with amber light. Not dead. Not alive. In the long watch—suspended animation maintained by the chamber's runes.

For four hours, they worked in reverent silence. Mercer transcribed the scaling runes with his wizardly quill, capturing not just shapes but concepts. Cora examined the runic chisel, marveling at its harmonic resonator design. Garrick kept watch at the entrance, humbled by the dedication before him.

As Mercer completed the final transcription, the chamber's runes illuminated in sequence. Korathan's stasis field projected a message—not words, but images and impressions:

*The Discovery*: Korathan finding the Sleeper, a vast presence leaking reality-warping radiation.
*The Decision*: The Architect's Council choosing containment over destruction.
*The Warning*: The containment designed for 100,000 years. They were at the halfway point—50,000 years passed, 50,000 remaining. The weakening was on schedule.
*The Gift*: The scaling runes placed deliberately for smaller caretakers. The tomb was a technology transfer.

The message ended. The runes dimmed.

## Inheritors of the Watch

The journey back felt different. Traps remained dormant. The construct guardian bowed as they passed. The obsidian door sealed behind them with finality.

At their forward camp, the familiar space felt changed. Mercer's notes, Cora's schematics, Garrick's maps—they were no longer just exploration records. They were the beginning of their own long watch.

Mercer unrolled the transcription, scaling runes glowing softly. "With these, we can repair the failing anchor. We can scale containment principles to our size, to our time."

Cora opened her ledger to sketches of the runic chisel. "I can create proper tools. Not improvised solutions, but crafted ones."

Garrick looked toward [[The Root]] layer, where the failed anchor [[Stone Theta]] awaited repair. "Then let's get to work. Korathan kept the watch for fifty thousand years. Time for us to do our part."

They were no longer intruders in a dead Giant's tomb. They were inheritors of a promise fifty thousand years in the making. The containment wasn't failing unexpectedly—it was reaching its designed midpoint. And now they had the language to speak to it, the tools to repair it, and the weight of the watch settling on their shoulders.

Korathan's vigil continued. Theirs was just beginning.
