The **Drowned Chokepoint** is a massive, domed cavern located at the deepest navigable level of the [[Dwarven Layer]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
The cavern is approximately one hundred feet across and fifty feet high, serving as the central convergence point for the entire dwarven water management system. Massive ten-foot diameter pipes enter from all directions, draining into a network of channels that radiate from a central elevated platform. The chamber was submerged for centuries until the reactivation of the mountain's pumps.

## Significance
The Chokepoint is located directly above the [[Giant Capstone]]. [[The Twisted]] used the flooded intersection as a hidden staging area and workshop, where they constructed and modified the [[Drowned Juggernaut]]. It was the site of a major battle that resulted in the Juggernaut's destruction and the recalibration of the mountain's filtration systems.
