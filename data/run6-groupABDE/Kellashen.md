**Kellashen** is an intelligent [[behir]] that has claimed the eastern sector of the [[Dwarven Minecart Network]] as its territory.

## Behavior and Territory
Kellashen exhibits a degree of territorial intelligence and has been observed attempting to contain threats emerging from the deeper mountain. It allowed explorers to pass through its domain and led them to the destroyed [[Eastern Transit Hub]], revealing its ongoing conflict with the dangers originating from the [[Drill Site]].
