**Black Organic Resin** is a biological-mineral composite material extensively used by [[The Twisted]] within the deep layers of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Properties
The resin is hard as stone, resistant to conventional tools, and possesses a distinctive iridescent sheen—a telltale sign of prolonged [[Dreamstone]] radiation exposure. It appears to be grown rather than manufactured and can exhibit regenerative properties under certain conditions.

## Usage and Origin
The Twisted employ the resin as a versatile tool for sabotage, sealing, and construction. It was used to fuse shut critical valves in [[Pump Station East]]. Analysis suggests it is a product of advanced Twisted bioengineering, seamlessly integrating biological materials with mechanical and architectural systems.
