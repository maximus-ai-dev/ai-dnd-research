The **Giant Blueprint Tablet** is a heavy stone artifact recovered from the [[Military Empire]] layer of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
The tablet is approximately two feet square, its surface carved with a detailed diagram inscribed in [[Giant]] script. It depicts a vertical cross-section of the entire mountain, illustrating all five architectural strata and the complex containment architecture surrounding a primordial anomaly at the bedrock level.

## Significance
The tablet is the most comprehensive structural record of the mountain's ancient construction. It has been instrumental in understanding the relationships between the different civilizations' layers, the purpose of the containment systems, and the location of critical features like the [[Giant Capstone]]. A transcription of its contents was provided to [[Reyna Steelforge]] of the [[Prospectors' Guild]].
