The **Hall of Conquests** is a vast trophy hall located within the [[Military Empire]] layer of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Function
The hall is a monumental space filled with Imperial relics and trophies from conquered civilizations. It houses a secondary [[harmonic regulator]], a critical apparatus for maintaining the structural integrity and temporal containment fields within the mountain complex.

## Significance
The hall is a key location for understanding the [[Military Empire]]'s activities. The [[Giant blueprint tablet]] was discovered here, and it is the location where explorers first encountered and communicated with [[Archivist-Controller Kaelen]], who revealed the Empire's project to harness the [[First World Sleeper]].
