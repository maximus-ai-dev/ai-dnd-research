The **Thieves Woods** is a location in the distant land of [[Emberfell]], associated with the pasts of both [[Garrick Kade]] and [[Kregg]].

## Significance
It is implied to be a place of exile, violence, and criminal activity. For Garrick, it represents a part of his history he left behind, a crucible that shaped his identity as a guardian who consciously rejected the path of cruelty embraced by others like Kregg. The Thieves Woods symbolizes the cycle of violence and the choices that define an individual's path.
