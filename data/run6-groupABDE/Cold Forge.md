The **Cold Forge** is the primary industrial forge complex within the upper [[Dwarven Layer]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], originally belonging to the dwarven settlement of [[Kazad-Khrom]].

## Description
The forge is a cathedral-like chamber featuring a central hearth twenty feet in diameter, surrounded by six massive anvils. It was uniquely powered by a bound [[Fire Elemental]], [[Ignathar]], rather than conventional fuel. The complex includes adjacent workshops, material stores, and a network of cooling channels.

## History and Significance
Constructed over a millennium ago, the Cold Forge was the production heart of Kazad-Khrom. It fell dormant when the dwarves abandoned the mountain but was reactivated following a bargain with Ignathar. The portable forge assembly used to begin work on the [[Dreamstone Seal]] was salvaged from here. Following Ignathar's release, the forge's fate—whether it continues to burn or cools to embers—is tied to the elemental's departure.
