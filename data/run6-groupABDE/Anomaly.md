The **Anomaly**, formally known as the **First World Sleeper**, is a primordial, reality-warping void entity imprisoned deep beneath the [[Starmetal Hills]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Containment
The entity was sealed away in ancient times by [[Giant]] engineering, utilizing a massive containment architecture centered on the [[Giant Capstone]]. This prison has been failing for centuries, allowing the Sleeper's influence to slowly leak out in the form of a constant psychic broadcast that corrupts life and warps reality.

## Influence and Goal
The Anomaly's radiation, or void energy, possesses transformative and corrosive properties. It can reanimate and corrupt ancient constructs like the [[Reanimated Warden]], mutate organic matter into [[The Twisted]], and cause physical transformations in the surrounding stone. Its ultimate goal appears to be a full awakening and breach of its prison.
