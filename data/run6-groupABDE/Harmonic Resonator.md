A **Harmonic Resonator** is a crystalline focus crafted from [[Dreamstone]] that analyzes and interacts with containment field harmonics.

## Function
The resonator shifts through colors in complex patterns, preserving encoded data about the mountain's containment systems. It pulses in time with the mountain's rhythms, allowing for real-time analysis of energy flows and reality distortions. It stabilizes spellcasting and other precision work against environmental interference.

## Composition
Forged from raw Dreamstone, the resonator's lattice structure is tuned to resonate with the mathematical frequencies of the [[Giant]] containment field, making it a powerful tool for arcane study and environmental navigation.
