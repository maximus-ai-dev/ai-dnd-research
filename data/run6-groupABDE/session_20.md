# Session 20: The Final Boundary

The Asymmetrical Mountain hummed with a question.

For three days, the fortress on the peak had been a hive of preparation. [[Cora Flint]] stood at the [[Cold Forge]], her hands resting on the anvil that had shaped the [[Dreamstone Seal]]. [[Garrick Kade]] patrolled reinforced gates, his eyes drifting to the door leading down into the depths. [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] sat in the [[Brotherhood]] Archives, his Codex open beside Commander Valerius's logbook—two records of the same mountain, two answers to the same question.

The mountain hummed. The Sleeper dreamed. And the Dreamstone Seal waited fifty feet from the sphere's boundary at the [[Observation Platform]], ready for deployment.

The final approach began now.

They stood at the platform, looking out across the zero-gravity [[Caldera]]. The four [[Anchor Points]] glowed faintly on distant walls. The sphere pulsed with steady light at the chamber's center. The [[Deep Things]] drifted in silent orbits.

Mercer's research was clear: activate the anchors first, establish the suppression field, *then* approach with the Seal. The party stands at the Observation Platform, looking out across the zero-gravity Caldera. The four containment anchor points glow faintly on the distant walls—massive iron rings at the cardinal positions, waiting to be activated. The sphere pulses with steady light at the Caldera's center, its boundary smooth and undisturbed. The Deep Things drift in slow, silent orbits.

The party is at the Observation Platform, 50 feet from the sphere's boundary. They are within range for my Boundary Wave.

A [[Boundary Wave]] hit them without warning—a psychic tsunami that slammed into their minds. Cora staggered, blood trickling from her nose, her clinical calculations scattering. Mercer's Codex trembled in his hands, ancient pages stained crimson. Garrick roared through the pain, his body swelling to Large size as he activated Giant's Might.

"Anchor points can wait!" he bellowed, closing half the distance to the boundary. "This thing needs to learn what happens when you mess with us!"

The sphere's boundary ripples violently.

Void energy condensed at its surface. The Deep Things scattered. The anchor points glowed with warning rather than activation. At the sphere's surface, a shape begins to coalesce. Darkness and light twist together, forming something vast and terrible. It emerges from the boundary like a figure stepping through a curtain—fifty feet tall, or appears to be. Its body is crystallized void energy, transparent in places, opaque in others, with the First World visible inside it like light through stained glass. Its form is a composite: part Giant, part dwarf, part human—features of all three blended into something that is none of them.

*This is why we needed the suppression field,* Cora thought, frozen in stunning paralysis. *This is exactly what Valerius warned about.*

The Avatar of the Slumber has manifested. And it stands between Garrick and the sphere's boundary, its form still solidifying, its presence radiating a broadcast so intense that the very air crackles with psychic pressure.

Garrick fought with brute force, his maul swinging in furious arcs that shattered crystalline void energy. He took psychic blow after psychic blow, blood streaming from his nose and ears, but he kept standing, kept fighting, kept roaring defiance.

Cora fought with calculation. Her [[Flaming Sphere]] created barriers. Her healing words pulled Mercer back from death's door. Her ledger tallied costs even as she shouted orders: "Garrick, fall back! Mercer, get to the north anchor! This is basic containment protocol!"

The party must reach each one and activate it using the scaling runes.

The suppression field is at 25% capacity. The suppression field is at 50% capacity. The suppression field is at 75% capacity.

With each anchor activated, the Avatar weakened. Its connection to the sphere strained. Its attacks lost potency. The void tendrils holding Mercer loosened. The mountain's hum shifted from threatening vibration to purposeful resonance.

100% suppression field active. "Boundary defenses negligible! Deploy the Seal now!"

Cora has reached the Dreamstone Seal and is moving it toward the sphere's boundary—fifty feet away.

She pushed the Dreamstone Seal across the zero-gravity space, the adamantine casing gliding smoothly. Garrick stood between her and the Avatar, a wall of muscle and rage, intercepting every weakened attack. Mercer watched from his knees, blood-stained Codex trembling in his hands.

The moment arrived.

Cora Flint: Intelligence Check to deploy Dreamstone Seal — 31 vs DC 16 (DC 18 -2 for masterwork quality) — **CRITICAL SUCCESS** Seal deploys perfectly.

The Dreamstone Seal slots into place at the sphere's boundary with a resonant *thrum* that echoes through the entire Caldera.

The effect was immediate and profound.

The boundary smoothed. The pulsing calmed to a steady glow. The broadcast dropped to its lowest level since they'd entered the mountain. The containment field reads at 97% rated capacity—higher than any reading the party has ever seen. Higher than the Giants' baseline measurements on the blueprint tablet.

The Avatar of the Slumber flickers one last time—its crystallized void energy unraveling peacefully, dissolving into wisps that are absorbed by the now-stabilized boundary.

Its crystallized void energy unraveled peacefully—not violently, but with acceptance. For one moment, Korathan's silhouette is visible within the fading form—the Giant architect, watching from within the dream. Then it is gone.

The immune response has terminated. The Seal proves the party belongs. The fight ends.

**Epilogue**

Six months later, the Asymmetrical Mountain stood as a citadel of learning and guardianship. The hum that once threatened now promised protection.

Cora Flint stood in "Flint & Associates: Alchemical Solutions & Containment Consulting" in [[Grimhold]]. Her ledger showed a balance that would have made her former debt collectors weep. The tools that forged the Dreamstone Seal were displayed on one wall. Her expedition ledger lay open on another, the final entry reading: *Containment secured at 97%. Debts paid in full. Never broke again.* She had built something that would outlast civilizations, and she had the solvent, sustainable business to prove it.

Garrick Kade stood at the highest point of the surface fortress, looking out over mountains he once crossed as a penniless exile. He ran "Kade's Keep Security" from a stone office, training [[Wardstone Wardens]], patrolling the perimeter. [[Reyna Steelforge]] had offered him command of the mountain's security forces. He took it—not for the gold, but because this place finally felt like home. A home [[Kregg]] and the [[Emberfell Rejects]] could never reach. A home worth defending.

Professor Thaddeus Mercer stood in the newly established [[Mercer Institute for Planar Studies]], housed in the restored Brotherhood Archives deep within the mountain. His paper, "Containment Architecture of the Asymmetrical Mountain: Empirical Validation of First World Sleeper Theory," sat on his desk, freshly printed by the University of Laternia Press. The dedication page read: *To Garrick Kade and Cora Flint, who proved that scholarship sometimes requires getting blood on the pages.* The monitoring crystal glowed steadily, displaying real-time containment data. His spectral mind transcribed notes from Commander Valerius's complete logs.

The mountain hummed with peaceful purpose. The containment held at 97%. The Sleeper dreamed on, its dreams translated by walls of stone and rune and Dreamstone—built by Giants, maintained by dwarves, reinforced by humans, extended by three people who took a bounty for fifty gold pieces and ended up carrying the weight of a mountain on their shoulders.

Korathan slept. The Sleeper dreamed. The watch continued.

The Asymmetrical Mountain would stand for a hundred thousand years more. Maybe two hundred thousand. Maybe longer, if someone came along who could build a better Seal.
