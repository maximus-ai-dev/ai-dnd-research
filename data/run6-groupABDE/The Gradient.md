The **Gradient** is a disorienting transition zone located below [[The Threshold]], leading into the zero-gravity environment of the [[Caldera]].

## Nature and Navigation
Within this zone, gravity does not merely weaken but actively rotates, shifting the perceived direction of "down" by significant degrees. This rotation is not random but follows an intentional, spiraling path designed by the Giants, requiring navigators to transition from walking to climbing along walls and ceilings.

## Hazards and Safety
The area poses a significant physical challenge, as a loss of footing can result in a dangerous fall relative to the new gravitational orientation. The deployment of a stabilizing device like the [[Reality Anchor]] is typically required for safe passage through the Gradient.
