The **First World** is the original, living reality from which the [[First World Sleeper]] originates.

## Nature and Physics
It is a reality governed by fundamentally different laws, where consciousness is a primary force rather than gravity or electromagnetism. Descriptions glimpsed through the Sleeper's sphere suggest a realm of impossible beauty where stone sings, light has tangible weight, and form is mutable.

## Relationship to the Current Universe
The First World is incompatible with the current physical universe. The Giants' containment architecture does not imprison the Sleeper but rather walls off its native reality to prevent a catastrophic merging of these incompatible states. The Sleeper's broadcast is an emanation of this other reality into our own.
