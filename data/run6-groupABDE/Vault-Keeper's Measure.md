The **Vault-Keeper's Measure** is a precisely calibrated lead weight, disguised as a ceramic jar, found within the [[Salt-Vaults]].

## Function and Mechanism
It is part of a dwarven security mechanism designed by the [[Iron Warlords]]. When placed on the correct pressure plate within a trapped corridor, its exact weight depresses the plate and deactivates adjacent traps, revealing a safe path through the defensive maze.
