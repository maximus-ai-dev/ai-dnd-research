**Thrain Ironheart** was the last runesmith of the dwarves of [[Kazad-Khrom]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Legacy and Actions
As recorded in a recovered journal, Ironheart was present during the final days of the dwarven occupation. He is noted as the individual who deliberately severed the binding of the [[Fire Elemental]] [[Ignathar]], freeing it from service as an act of mercy before the dwarven retreat. His journal also contains critical notes on the early signs of corruption reaching the forge level and the deep drill project undertaken by the [[Stubborn Deep]].
