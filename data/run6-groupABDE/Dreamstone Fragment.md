A **Dreamstone Fragment** is a piece of mysterious crystalline material intrinsically linked to the psychic influence of the [[First World Sleeper]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Properties and Significance
The fragment radiates potent psychic energy and is a physical manifestation of dream-drift contamination. Its crystalline structure is believed to hold insights into the nature of the mountain's dreams and the Sleeper's corrupting influence.

## Current Status
The fragment is currently in the possession of [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]], who intends to study its unique crystalline lattice under the controlled conditions of the [[Cold Forge]].
