The **Adaptive Quarantine** is a [[Giant]]-era containment and research facility located within the foundational layers of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Function and Design
Constructed by the Giants, the facility was designed to contain and study a powerful, adaptive intelligence known as the [[Dreaming Resonance]]. Its architecture features advanced crystal monitoring chambers capable of scanning intruders, learning their patterns, and adapting its defensive measures accordingly.

## Historical Significance
The quarantine represents one of the earliest known containment structures built around the primordial anomalies deep within the mountain. Its existence predates the later works of the [[Military Empire]] and the [[Secretive Brotherhood]], establishing the Giants as the first civilization to attempt systematic study and imprisonment of the mountain's inherent dangers.
