The **Dreamstone Seal** is a masterwork containment device forged to permanently reinforce the boundary surrounding the [[First World Sleeper]] within the [[Caldera]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Design and Construction
Based on the original [[Giant]] containment architecture designed by [[Korathan]], the Seal's core is a lattice of [[Dreamstone]], a crystalline material naturally resonant with the Sleeper's psychic frequency. This core is inscribed with a complete containment rune matrix using a replica of [[Korathan's Chisel]] and housed within a protective adamantine casing. Its design was scaled for human crafting using the principles of the scaling runes documented in the [[Architect's Codex]].

## Function
When deployed at the sphere's boundary, the Dreamstone Seal integrates with the existing containment architecture, acting as a refined translation layer between realities. It does not alter the Sleeper's nature but significantly strengthens the barrier, extending the containment's effective lifespan by millennia.

## Deployment and Legacy
The Seal was successfully deployed at the Sleeper's boundary, stabilizing the containment field at 97% of its rated capacity—a higher efficiency than the original Giant measurements. Upon integration, it became a permanent part of the mountain's containment system, securing the Asymmetrical Mountain for another hundred thousand years.
