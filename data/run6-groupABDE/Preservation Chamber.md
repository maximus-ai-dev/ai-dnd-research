The **Preservation Chamber** is a hidden room within the East Wing of the [[Grand Archive]] of the [[Secretive Brotherhood]].

## Location and Access
The chamber is located behind a false wall at the rear of the East Wing, detectable by slightly different stonework. It is sealed with an arcane lock that requires a specific command phrase or key to open.

## Contents and Function
The chamber is protected by a stasis or transmutation field that perfectly preserved its contents against decay. Within it, the Brotherhood stored their most valuable and critical research. Contents included sealed spellbooks (both practical and theoretical), research logs detailing their studies into the Deep Resonance, and the personal logbook of [[Commander Valerius]].
