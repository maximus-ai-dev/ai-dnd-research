**Petra** is one of the three [[Secretive Brotherhood]] [[Wardens]] preserved in stasis within the [[Salt-Vaults]].

## Role and Demeanor
Trained and disciplined, she is quick to act and slow to trust, defaulting to established security protocols when confronted with the unfamiliar. Her actions are guided by a strict adherence to her standing orders, making her a potential source of escalation in tense situations, though she is not inherently hostile.

## Equipment
Petra is equipped with Brotherhood-issue ring mail and a hand crossbow.
