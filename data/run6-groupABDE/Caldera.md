The **Caldera** is a vast, spherical chamber located in the deepest reaches of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], serving as the heart of the containment system for the [[First World Sleeper]].

## Environment
The chamber exists in a permanent state of zero gravity. At its center floats the translucent sphere containing the Sleeper. The air hums with the entity's constant psychic broadcast. [[Deep Things]]—crystallized fragments of void energy—drift in slow orbits throughout the space. The walls are inscribed with massive, ancient containment runes of [[Giant]] design.

## Structures and Features
Key features include the [[Observation Platform]], a Giant-era monitoring station built into the chamber wall, and four [[Anchor Points]], massive iron rings that form the structural foundation of the containment field's suppression grid.

## Strategic Significance
The Caldera is the ultimate destination for containment efforts. It was the necessary location for forging and deploying the [[Dreamstone Seal]], as the device required calibration within the Sleeper's broadcast zone. The final confrontation with the [[Avatar of the Slumber]] and the successful deployment of the Seal occurred here.
