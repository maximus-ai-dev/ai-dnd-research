A **Sabotage Device** is a specialized explosive weapon employed by [[Twisted]] Engineers.

## Design and Function
The device is a resin-based thermal charge that, once placed, detonates after a short delay. It is designed to superheat the air within a confined space, creating a devastating area-of-effect blast combining fire and necrotic energy.

## Tactical Use
Engineers deploy these devices to control the battlefield, creating zones of denial or catching groups of opponents in the blast. The devices represent the Twisted's capacity for tactical ingenuity and their use of their unique organic technology for offensive purposes.
