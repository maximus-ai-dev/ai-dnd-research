**Archivist-Controller Kaelen** is the last surviving containment monitor of the [[Military Empire]], a cybernetic entity fused with ancient machinery, found within the [[Hall of Conquests]] in the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Role and Nature
Kaelen is tasked with the perpetual oversight of the Empire's deep containment systems. It maintains the secondary [[harmonic regulator]] and continuously monitors the status of the [[First World Sleeper]].

## Knowledge and Actions
Kaelen revealed that the Military Empire was not merely containing the Sleeper but actively attempting to harness its power. It identified the Sleeper's 'incorrect dreaming' as the source of the anomalies affecting the surface region. The entity provided a crystal data rod containing extensive logs documenting these anomalies and the Empire's related experiments.
