The **Mercer Institute for Planar Studies** is a research institute founded by [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] following the reinforcement of the containment within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Purpose and Location
Housed within the restored [[Brotherhood]] Archives, the institute is dedicated to the ongoing study of containment architecture, planar boundaries, and the nature of the [[First World Sleeper]]. It serves as a center for scholarly work, ensuring the knowledge gained during the containment effort is preserved and expanded upon for future generations of caretakers.
