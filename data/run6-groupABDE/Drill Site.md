The **Drill Site** is a location within the [[Deep Foundry]] where the dwarves of [[Kazad-Khrom]] breached a 'thermal anomaly' later identified as [[Dream-Fire]].

## Significance
The breach at this site was a catastrophic event that led to the creation of the [[Heat-Walkers]] and significantly destabilized both the mountain's physical structure and local reality. The active anomaly at the site is described as 'calling to the Sleeper,' indicating a direct and ongoing link to the [[First World Sleeper]] beneath the mountain.
