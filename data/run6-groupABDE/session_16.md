# Session 16: The Warden's Price

**Three days after the destruction of the Twisted staging ground.**

The surface fortress had settled into an uneasy rhythm. Miners and engineers worked in shifts, reinforcing the upper levels while the party rested in the relative safety of the reclaimed watchtower. For three days, there had been only silence from below—no tremors, no broadcast echoes, no signs of [[The Twisted]] activity. The [[Monitoring Crystal]] sat on [[Cora Flint]]'s workbench in the [[forward camp]], its steady hum a reassuring constant.

Then, on the morning of the fourth day, the silence broke.

The crystal didn't just hum—it *screamed*. A sudden, piercing resonance frequency spiked so violently that a glass beaker on Cora's workbench shattered. The crystal's light flared from steady blue to blinding white, then pulsed with jagged crimson streaks. Throughout the [[Giant foundation]], the [[containment runes]] flickered—not the gentle dimming of low power, but a stuttering failure that made the very stone shudder.

Cora was already at the crystal, her hands steady despite the adrenaline. The readings were catastrophic. The containment field wasn't just weakened—it was in freefall. And the breach location wasn't at the bore hole they'd sealed, or at the [[Drowned Juggernaut]]'s scar. It was in the southwest quadrant, roughly six hundred feet from the forward camp, in territory they'd never explored. The Twisted had a backup plan. A third drill, hidden deeper, boring from a direction their monitoring didn't cover.

And something had come through.

---

The psychic wave hit them as they raced toward the new breach. [[Garrick Kade]] staggered, dark visions of the void flashing behind his eyes—the darkness below the [[Giant Capstone]], the shapes within it, closer than before. Cora and [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]], protected by their [[Dreamstone Circlet]]s, held firm against the assault.

"Southwest quadrant, six hundred feet," Cora reported, her voice clinical. "They had a third drill we didn't know about. We have approximately twenty-three minutes before the field drops below critical threshold."

Mercer's quill flew across his transcription of the [[Giant blueprint tablet]]. "That places it in what the Giants labeled 'Auxiliary Containment Chamber Beta'—a secondary failsafe location. The Twisted didn't just drill randomly; they targeted a structural weak point."

They moved fast, the corridors showing escalating damage with every step. Containment runes that glowed steadily hours before were now cracked and dark. Stone walls bore fresh fractures. Most disturbing of all: the [[Black Organic Resin]] in the walls, long dormant since the amplifiers' destruction, now glowed with a faint, sickly iridescence. The void energy was feeding the remnant Twisted infrastructure, bringing it back to life.

They navigated radiation surge zones, gravity fractures, and disorienting echo manifestations—psychic imprints of Giants, dwarves, and [[Secretive Brotherhood]] members flickering into existence before fading. The air grew warmer, sweeter—thick with the scent of ozone and something organic, like blooming flowers in a tomb.

They reached the [[Warden's Post]].

The chamber was enormous—eighty feet in diameter with a domed ceiling sixty feet high. At its center lay an open sarcophagus, empty, its stone lid shattered on the floor. At the far wall, a ragged hole forty feet across glowed with void energy—the breach. The containment runes around it weren't just cracked; they were *shattered*.

And standing at the edge of the breach, facing them, was the [[Reanimated Warden]].

It was the largest creature they had ever seen. A [[Giant]] skeleton, forty feet tall, held together not by ligaments but by threads of void energy that laced through the bones like dark tendons. Its skull was crowned with corrupted [[Dreamstone]] growths—crystalline formations that glowed with the same iridescent light as the void below. Its hands were the size of minecarts. Its eye sockets burned with the broadcast's frequency.

It spoke—not in any language, but in the broadcast frequency itself, the words forming directly in their minds:

***THE SEAL IS BROKEN. THE WATCHER WAKES. THE DEEP THINGS RISE.***

Then it raised one enormous fist and brought it down on the chamber floor with enough force to crack the stone for thirty feet in every direction. The impact shook the entire chamber, sending tremors through their bones.

The fist came down on Garrick. He planted his feet and roared against the force, but ribs cracked under the impact. Void energy burned through his armor. He stayed standing, but blood trickled from his mouth.

"Still standing!" he bellowed.

---

What followed was a brutal dance of death and resurrection.

Garrick grew to Large size with his [[Giant's Might]], his maul cracking the Warden's ancient bones. Mercer attempted [[Banishment]], but the Warden used its first [[Legendary Resistance]] to shrug off the spell. Cora's acid arrows sizzled against void-energy tendons but failed to reach the Dreamstone crown.

The Warden turned its attention to Mercer, recognizing the arcane threat. A [[Colossal Slam]] crushed the wizard against the chamber wall, reducing him to a gasping, bloodied heap. Cora's [[Healing Word]] stabilized him, but the damage was done.

Mercer, through pain and determination, completed his [[Wall of Force]] spell. An invisible, indestructible hemispherical dome snapped into existence around the Warden and the breach, trapping the skeletal giant within. They had ten minutes of breathing room.

They used that time to reactivate the chamber's ancient suppression runes—four arrays on the north, south, east, and west walls. Using [[Korathan's scaling runes]], Mercer and Cora worked with desperate speed as Garrick stood guard. One by one, the arrays flared to life, creating overlapping zones of suppressed void energy.

But the Warden wasn't idle. Trapped but undeterred, it unleashed [[Broadcast Scream]] after [[Broadcast Scream]], the psychic energy passing through the Wall of Force barrier as if it weren't there.

The screams were catastrophic.

Garrick fell first, his mind shattered by psychic assault, his body collapsing at -21 hit points—dead. Mercer followed, killed instantly by damage exceeding his maximum health. Cora stood alone, the only conscious member of the party remaining, staring at her dead companions as the Warden regenerated to over 500 hit points.

She made the cold, clinical calculation: retreat was the only viable option.

---

They carried Garrick's body back to the [[forward camp]], Mercer barely alive beside her. At the surface fortress, [[Reyna Steelforge]] met them, her eyes taking in their battered condition.

"What happened down there?" she asked, her voice low.

Cora gave the report: "Third breach. Reanimated Giant guardian, forty feet tall, drawing power from the void. It killed Garrick. We retreated."

But retreat wasn't surrender. They had an idea—a focused disruption device using the [[Dreamstone power cell]], the Monitoring Crystal, and Korathan's scaling runes. A crude weapon that could sever the Warden's connection to the breach.

They built it in two desperate hours. Garrick was resurrected with [[Revivify]], returning at 1 hit point, his memory of death fresh and painful. Mercer worked through resurrection shock, his academic detachment stripped away by the cold certainty of mortality.

Then they went back down.

---

The second engagement was even more brutal than the first.

Garrick charged, taunting the Warden, drawing its attention to the suppression zones. Mercer's [[Manifest Mind]] carried the disruption device into position. Cora targeted the Dreamstone crown with acid arrows.

The device activated, creating harmonic interference that disrupted the void tether. For precious moments, the Warden stopped regenerating. But it recognized the threat and turned its colossal fists toward the apparatus.

Garrick fought with the fury of a man who had already died once. His maul connected with the Warden's femur in a critical hit that cracked ancient bone from hip to knee. But the Warden's fist came down on the device, shattering it in a shower of sparks and twisted metal.

The void tether reconnected.

Then Garrick died again, crushed beneath a [[Colossal Slam]] that reduced him to -21 hit points once more.

Cora and Mercer fought on alone. Mercer's [[Disintegrate]] ray struck the already-damaged Dreamstone crown. The Warden had no more Legendary Resistances. The green energy lanced through corrupted crystalline growths, and the crown shattered in an explosion of iridescent light and psychic feedback.

The void tether was severed completely.

But the Warden's final blow was already descending. Mercer tried to dodge behind a column, but the fist smashed through stone and flesh alike. The wizard was reduced to -19 hit points—dead instantly.

Cora stood alone.

Her final acid arrow ate into the Warden's already-cracked femur. Mercer's [[Animate Objects]] spell, cast even as he died, sent a swarm of twisted metal and shattered stone hammering at the giant's joints.

With a sound like a mountain cracking, the Reanimated Warden's left leg collapsed. The forty-foot-tall skeletal giant toppled sideways, crashing into the chamber wall with earth-shaking force. As it fell, the remaining void-energy tendons snapped completely, and the skeleton came apart—pelvis separating from spine, ribs scattering like fallen trees, skull rolling free to come to rest facing the breach it was meant to guard.

The Reanimated Warden was destroyed.

---

Cora stood alone in the chamber. Garrick lay dead near the center, his body broken. Mercer lay dead near the shattered column, his spellbook fallen from lifeless hands. The Warden's bones were scattered everywhere.

The breach remained open, void energy flowing out, but without the crown to channel it, the energy dissipated harmlessly. The crystallized stone—a transformation they'd noticed too late—had spread to cover two-thirds of the chamber floor, giving the space an eerie, alien beauty.

The Monitoring Crystal would show the containment field readings had stabilized—not improved, but no longer plummeting. The immediate threat was gone.

But the breach remained.

And the cost...

Cora moved first to Garrick, checking for signs of life she knew weren't there. Then to Mercer. Both dead. Both beyond [[Healing Word]] or simple magic. She had the diamond components for Revivify—one set, worth 300 gold. She could bring one back within the next nine minutes. The other would need [[Raise Dead]] within ten days, if she could recover the body and afford the 1,000 gold in diamonds.

The chamber was quiet except for the soft hum of the breach and the faint crackle of the still-spreading crystallization. Cora stood between two dead companions, surrounded by the bones of a Giant, facing an open void to an ancient [[Anomaly]].

She had minutes to decide who to save, hours to deal with the breach, and the weight of knowing that even in victory, they had paid the highest price.

The mountain continued its transformation around her, stone becoming something else, something alien. And somewhere deep below, in the darkness beneath the capstone, the shapes moved closer.
