The **Reality Anchor** is a crafted device designed to stabilize localized physics in areas of severe reality distortion, such as the zero-gravity environment of the [[Caldera]].

## Function
When deployed, the Reality Anchor creates a spherical bubble of normalized, consistent gravity with a fifteen-foot radius, centered on itself. Within this field, "down" is consistently oriented toward the device, allowing for secure footing, navigation, and tethering. It effectively negates anomalous gravitational conditions like rotation or weightlessness.

## Construction and Use
Built by [[Cora Flint]], the Anchor was critical for the party's operations within the Caldera. It provided a stable work platform at their staging position on the [[Observation Platform]] during the forging of the [[Dreamstone Seal]], countering the gravity fluctuations and tremors caused by the sphere's reactive broadcast.
