The **Secretive Brotherhood** was a cabal of arcane scholars who operated within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] following the [[Iron Warlords]] era.

## History and Activities
The Brotherhood established a hidden sanctum, the [[Grand Archive]], within the mountain's ruins. Their primary work involved cataloguing the Warlords' military doctrine, studying giant runes, and researching a pervasive phenomenon they termed the "Deep Resonance."

## Structure and Demise
The Brotherhood was hierarchically organized with ranks including Initiate, Scholar, Archivist, and Commander. They abandoned the [[Grand Archive]] approximately two centuries ago, leaving behind their guardian creation, [[Amalgamation]], and a contingent of [[Wardens]] under a preservation spell. Their research indicates they studied a pre-existing containment network within the mountain. According to [[Warden Aldric]], they sealed the Archives because 'the books whispered' memories that weren't theirs.
