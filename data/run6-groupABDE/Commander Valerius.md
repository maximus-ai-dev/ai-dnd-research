**Commander Valerius** was the high-ranking officer of the [[Secretive Brotherhood]] in command of the [[Grand Archive]] outpost within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Discovery and Logbook
His preserved body was found in the Archive's Central Hall, showing no visible wounds. A personal logbook recovered from his person contained the Brotherhood's final research into the 'Deep Resonance' and a detailed record of the archive's last days. The log detailed the resonance's intensification and the discovery of a passage leading to older, non-human construction below a restricted vault.

## Final Days and Legacy
Commander Valerius's final entry indicated he remained at his post as the Brotherhood evacuated. His research later proved invaluable in understanding ritual observances and containment protocols within the deeper [[Military Empire]] layer. His work provided a crucial historical link that informed later efforts to reinforce the Sleeper's containment.
