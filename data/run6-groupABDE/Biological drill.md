The **biological drill** was a unique [[Twisted]] construct designed to breach the [[Giant Capstone]].

## Description
The drill was a living construct approximately fifty feet long, resembling an enormous boring worm. Its body was composed of layered [[Black Organic Resin]] reinforced with salvaged metal. The cutting head featured rotating teeth made of crystallized [[Dreamstone]].

## Operation and Mechanism
Unlike mechanical drills, this construct was grown and possessed a degree of biological autonomy. It fed on the rock it consumed, adding the material to its own mass and growing larger as it bored deeper. It was connected to and powered by the staging ground's [[Organic Power Hub]].

## Objective and Fate
The drill was boring into the Giant Capstone from below, targeting a structural weak point. It was destroyed when its Dreamstone teeth were vaporized by powerful magic, halting the breach attempt approximately thirty feet into the stone.
