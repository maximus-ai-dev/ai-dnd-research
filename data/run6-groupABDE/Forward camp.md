The **Forward Camp** is an operational base established within the [[Giant foundation]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Location and Setup
Situated in the upper reaches of the foundation, the camp serves as a staging area for deeper expeditions. It has been fortified and equipped with resources accumulated from prior explorations.

## Key Features
The camp contains a modified monitoring station, built from a salvaged [[Twisted]] communication node, which allows for intelligence gathering on Twisted activities. The perimeter is secured with magical alarms, and the site provides a relative safe haven from the pervasive [[Dreamstone]] radiation of the deeper tunnels.

## Role
The camp functions as a logistical hub, planning center, and fallback point for operations targeting the Twisted threat deep within the mountain.
