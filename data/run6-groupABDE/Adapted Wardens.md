The **Adapted Wardens** are a faction of former [[Brotherhood Wardens]] who have physically and psychologically adapted to the psychic broadcast of the [[First World Sleeper]] over centuries, now serving as self-proclaimed 'Keepers of the Rhythm'.

## History and Transformation
Originally tasked with maintaining the Sleeper's containment, these wardens were exposed to its constant psychic emissions for over two centuries. This prolonged exposure caused a biological and psychological alignment with the Sleeper's broadcast cycles. They are now permanently stationed within a maintenance annex along the void corridor of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Beliefs and Actions
The Adapted Wardens misinterpret the Sleeper's broadcast as a natural rhythm that must be preserved. This belief leads them to sabotage reactivation efforts targeting ancient dampener systems, viewing such interventions as a disruption to a delicate balance they are sworn to protect.
