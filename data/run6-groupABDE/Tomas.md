**Tomas** is a former bandit now working with the exploration party and being trained by [[Garrick Kade]].

## Role and Activities
Tomas assists with operations at the surface fortress of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. He aided Garrick Kade in locating and collapsing the ventilation shaft entrance to the [[Adaptive Quarantine]], demonstrating a willingness to integrate into the workforce and contribute to containment efforts.
