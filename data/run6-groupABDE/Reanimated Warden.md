The **Reanimated Warden** is a corrupted Giant guardian, a forty-foot-tall skeletal construct reanimated and controlled by void energy from a breach in the [[Giant Capstone]]. It was stationed in the [[Warden's Post]], a guardian chamber in the foundation level of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
Originally a Giant sentinel entombed in a containment sarcophagus as a last-resort defender, the Warden was designed to be reanimated by the containment system to combat any breach of the seal. The void energy from the third breach drilled by [[The Twisted]] reanimated the guardian but also corrupted it, overwriting its original programming. The creature is held together by threads of void energy that act as dark tendons, and its skull is crowned with corrupted [[Dreamstone]] growths that glow with iridescent void light. Its eye sockets burn with the broadcast frequency of the [[First World Sleeper]].

## Capabilities and Vulnerabilities
The Reanimated Warden is a formidable combatant, capable of devastating physical attacks and a potent psychic [[Broadcast Scream]]. It is tethered to the void breach, drawing regenerative power from its proximity. Its key vulnerabilities include distance from the breach, which weakens it; the containment runes of the Warden's Post, which can suppress void energy; and its corrupted Dreamstone crown, which acts as a control node. Dreamstone-forged weapons are particularly effective against it.

## Fate
The Reanimated Warden was destroyed in the Warden's Post by adventurers from the surface after a protracted battle. The destruction of its Dreamstone crown severed its void tether, causing the massive skeleton to collapse.
