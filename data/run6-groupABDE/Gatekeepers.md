The **Gatekeepers** are an organization dedicated to monitoring planar thin points, ley-line anchors, and containing anomalous entities across the world.

## Activity at the Asymmetrical Mountain
A detachment of Gatekeepers, led by [[Lyra]], arrived at the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] after detecting the presence of nested dreaming entities within. The detachment entered into a formal research partnership with the primary exploration party operating at the site.
