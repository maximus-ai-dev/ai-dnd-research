**Professor Alaric** is a scholar from the [[Laternian Historical Preservation Society]] who led an expedition to breach the [[Adaptive Quarantine]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Expedition and Fate
Alaric and his apprentice triggered the awakening of a [[Dreaming Resonance]] fragment within the quarantine. The encounter resulted in severe psychic contamination for the expedition members, and their published findings later drew significant and potentially dangerous attention to the site.
