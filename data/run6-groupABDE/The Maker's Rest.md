**The Maker's Rest** is the tomb of the [[Giant]] architect [[Korathan]], located deep within the outer ring of the [[Giant]] foundation level beneath the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
The tomb is sealed by the [[Architect's Council]] and is accessed through a massive obsidian door inscribed with Korathan's epitaph. Its interior is a series of descending corridors and chambers scaled for [[Giant]] inhabitants, filled with intricate carvings depicting Korathan's life and work. The tomb is protected by a sophisticated gauntlet of traps and puzzles designed to test intruders' understanding of containment principles.

## Primary Chamber
The burial chamber is a circular room with a domed ceiling, its walls covered in Korathan's personal scaling runes. At its center lies Korathan in a state of suspended animation, maintained by the chamber's runic stasis field.
