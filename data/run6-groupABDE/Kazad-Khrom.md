**Kazad-Khrom** was a lost dwarven empire renowned for its masterful stonework and intricate geometric patterns.

## Legacy and Architecture
Examples of its distinctive architecture are found within the [[Dwarven Layer]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. The empire was responsible for constructing key facilities such as the [[Cold Forge]], the [[Dwarven Minecart Network]], and the [[Memory Vault]]. Its manufacturing arm, the [[Kazad-Khrom Forgeworks]], produced much of the equipment found throughout the mountain's dwarven strata.
