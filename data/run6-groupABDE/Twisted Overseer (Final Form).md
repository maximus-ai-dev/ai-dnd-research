The **Twisted Overseer** is a powerful, corrupted dwarven commander serving the [[First World Sleeper]]. It represents the pinnacle of the [[Twisted]] military command structure encountered by the adventurers.

## Description
Clad in master-crafted, corrupted plate armor and wielding a warhammer imbued with necrotic energy, the Overseer is a formidable frontline commander. Its crystalline eyes allow it to survey the battlefield and coordinate its forces with chilling efficiency.

## Tactics and Abilities
The Overseer possesses a deep understanding of military tactics, honed over millennia of service. It is capable of issuing commands that enhance the combat prowess of nearby Twisted and can unleash a devastating pulse of corrupting energy. Having studied its opponents from previous encounters, it adapts its strategy to target key party members, prioritizing saboteurs, frontline combatants, and spellcasters for disruption.

## Campaign Role
The Overseer served as the commanding officer of the [[Twisted]] forces at a fortified staging ground within the [[Giant foundation]]. Its primary objective was to oversee a biological drilling operation aimed at breaching the [[Giant Capstone]]. The creature fights with relentless focus until destroyed or driven from the field, showing no inclination for negotiation or surrender.
