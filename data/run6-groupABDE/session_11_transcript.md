**Now: The Breach**

The capstone sits in the lowest chamber of the fortress, patched but scarred from the Twisted's drilling attempts. Cora and the Grimhold engineers have spent the last week carefully cutting a controlled doorway through it—following seams in the rune work that the Giant blueprint tablet revealed, passages that allow physical access without compromising the containment field.

Passing through feels like walking through a waterfall of warm electricity. Your hair rises. Your skin tingles. Below the capstone: a landing.

The scale hits you immediately.

The platform is fifty feet square, carved from a single block of dark basalt that Mercer identifies as Giant-era stone, quarried from somewhere far below the mountain's current foundation. The ceiling is forty feet above—so high your torchlight barely reaches it. The walls are carved with continuous rune sequences, not decorative but functional, covering every surface in patterns so dense they look like text.

A corridor stretches into darkness in both directions, thirty feet wide—wide enough for three giants to walk abreast. Steps leading down are two and a half feet tall, each one a climb for a human. Door handles are at head height. Everything is built for beings twice your height and five times your mass.

To your left, the corridor curves gently. To your right, it extends into blackness. The air is still, cold, and carries the faint, metallic scent of Dreamstone. The only sound is the hum of the containment runes—a deep, subsonic vibration you feel in your bones rather than hear.

**Garrick** assesses the space tactically and finds it terrifying. No cover. No chokepoints. Everything is open and enormous. If something lives here, you cannot hide from it.

**Mercer** runs a hand along the runes, his fingers tracing patterns that haven't been touched in millennia. "The Inspection Circuit," he murmurs, consulting the blueprint tablet. "A perimeter walkway for monitoring the containment from outside the primary cage."

Above you, the capstone's breach glows like a square of daylight. Below, the Giant foundation stretches into darkness.
