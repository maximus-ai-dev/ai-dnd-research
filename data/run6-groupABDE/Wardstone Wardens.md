The **Wardstone Wardens** are a faction known for advocating a forceful containment approach to perceived existential threats.

## Philosophy
According to allied scholars, the Wardstone Wardens would view an awakening primordial entity as a threat to be contained by direct force, contrasting with more research-oriented approaches. Following the securing of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], members of this faction were among those integrated into the citadel's security forces, patrolling the perimeter under new command.
