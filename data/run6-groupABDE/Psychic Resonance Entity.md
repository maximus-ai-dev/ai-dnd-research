The **Psychic Resonance Entity** is a coalescence of consciousness fragments with psychic and extraplanar properties that breached containment from the lower levels of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Nature and Origin
The entity is a psychic phenomenon, a coalescence of consciousness fragments. It breached containment from the lower levels of the mountain, emerging into the [[West Wing]] of the [[Grand Archive]].

## Abilities and Containment
The entity possesses strong psychic and extraplanar properties. It was disrupted by a powerful sonic field, after which the corridor it occupied was sealed to prevent further incursion.
