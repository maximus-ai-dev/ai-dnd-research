The **Dreaming Resonance** is an alien, adaptive intelligence contained by the [[Giants]] within the crystal chambers of the [[Adaptive Quarantine]] in the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Properties and Behavior
This intelligence exhibits a profound ability to scan, learn from, and adapt to intruders. A fragment encountered in a monitoring chamber demonstrated this capability by scanning a mind and rapidly learning and mirroring combat patterns.

## Containment History
The Resonance represents one of the ancient, primordial threats sealed within the mountain's foundational layers. It was studied and contained by the Giants long before the arrival of later civilizations such as the [[Military Empire]] or the [[Secretive Brotherhood]].
