**Ignathar** is a [[Fire Elemental]] originally bound within the [[Cold Forge]] of the [[Dwarven Layer]] in the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Nature and History
Ignathar manifests as a being of pure, sentient flame and communicates in the Primordial tongue. It was bound over a millennium ago by the runesmiths of the dwarven settlement [[Kazad-Khrom]] to power their primary industrial forge. Following the dwarven retreat, it remained dormant until recently reignited.

## Bargain and Departure
The elemental agreed to assist adventurers in exchange for a promise of eventual freedom. This promise was fulfilled during the forging of the [[Dreamstone Seal]], when the party opened the [[Fire Gate]] to access a controlled conduit to the [[Plane of Fire]]. After providing the precise elemental heat required to complete the Seal, Ignathar departed through the Fire Gate, finally free after its long imprisonment.
