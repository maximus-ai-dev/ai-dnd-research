The **Grand Stairwell** is a 120-foot descent within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] that connects the [[Secretive Brotherhood]]'s sanctum to the [[Military Empire]] layer below.

## Description and Construction
The upper sixty feet consist of [[Iron Warlords]] construction, retrofitted into an existing opening. The lower sixty feet are original [[Military Empire]] work, featuring wider steps, higher ceilings, and carved relief panels. The stairwell terminates at a landing before an iron-banded door leading to the Imperial Lower Palace.

## Structural State
While the stairwell itself is structurally sound, the landing at its base shows ancient hairline cracks radiating from the center, indicating significant stress from the settling of the immense layers below.
