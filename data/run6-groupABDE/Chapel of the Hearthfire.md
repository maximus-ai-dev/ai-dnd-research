The **Chapel of the Hearthfire** is a simple stone chapel located in the frontier town of [[Grimhold]].

## Description and Purpose
The chapel serves as a place of worship, solace, and healing for the townsfolk. It is a modest, sturdy structure that offers a quiet refuge from the hardships and dangers of frontier life.

## Caretaker
The chapel is tended by [[Brother Aldwin]], the town's healer, who provides care for physical ailments and spiritual unease alike, ministering to those affected by the strange influences emanating from the wilderness.
