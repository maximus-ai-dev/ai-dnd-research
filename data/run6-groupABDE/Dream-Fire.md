**Dream-Fire** is a conceptual energy source that exists in the liminal space between reality and imagination, originally referenced in ancient [[Giant]] lore.

## Nature and Effects
It is the sentient thermal anomaly that the dwarves of [[Kazad-Khrom]] accidentally breached at their [[Drill Site]]. Dream-Fire possesses the ability to transform machinery and call to the [[First World Sleeper]]. Its influence is directly responsible for the creation of the [[Heat-Walkers]] and is a major contributor to the destabilization of reality within the deeper layers of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].
