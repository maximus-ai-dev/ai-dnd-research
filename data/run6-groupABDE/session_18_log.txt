--- DM ---
The Observation Platform hangs in the void, a bubble of air and gravity anchored to the Caldera wall by ancient Giant engineering. For three days, the party has rested here — not sleeping in the traditional sense, but in a state of watchful meditation. The Deep Things drift in their slow orbits around the sphere, their crystalline forms catching the faint light of the containment runes. Cora has spent the days calibrating the Reality Anchor, her artificer's tools floating in the zero-gravity as she adjusts harmonic frequencies. Garrick stands watch at the platform's edge, his maul magnetically secured to the floor, his eyes on the sphere fifty feet away — close enough to see its surface ripple with slow, organic motion. Mercer's spectral mind — the manifestation of his awakened spellbook — drifts near the containment wall, recording observations in shimmering script that only he can read. They have rested. They have resupplied from the Cold Forge above. They have prepared.

Now comes the hard part.

The Dreamstone Circlets hum against their temples — Cora's creation, forged from the mountain's own psychic ore. They have protected the party from the Sleeper's broadcast at this distance. But the mission requires getting closer — within fifty feet of the sphere's surface, close enough to deploy the Dreamstone Seal when it is built. And the circlets have limits.

Cora secures the final tether line, triple-checking the knots. "The Reality Anchor will keep us oriented," she says, her voice carrying strangely in the thin air. "But past fifty feet from the wall, the broadcast intensity increases exponentially. The circlets will resonate. They might fail."

Garrick tests his own tether, the line connecting him to Cora, to Mercer, to the Anchor. "And if they fail?"

Mercer's spectral mind drifts back to his body, the ghostly quill still writing. "Then we experience the broadcast directly. Not filtered. Not translated. The Sleeper's dreaming, raw."

The sphere pulses. A wave of golden light expands from its surface, washing through the Caldera. The Deep Things shift in their orbits, turning crystalline faces toward the party. Waiting.

Cora nods to the others. "Ready?"

The party launches from the Observation Platform, roped together, the Reality Anchor providing navigational stability. They drift toward the sphere — past the sixty-foot mark, past fifty-five. At fifty feet from the wall, the Dreamstone Circlets begin to vibrate — a physical trembling that Cora recognizes as resonance overload. The hum becomes a shriek in their minds, then—

**Silence.**

The circlets have not broken. They have been overwhelmed. The broadcast at this distance is simply too strong for the shielding to contain.

**DC 18 Wisdom save (all characters) upon circlet failure:** This is not a combat save — it is a transition. On success, the character adjusts to the unshielded broadcast gradually. They feel the pressure but maintain coherence. On failure, the transition is jarring — the character experiences a 1d4 round disorientation as the broadcast floods their unprotected mind.

Beyond the circlet's effective range, the Caldera transforms. The party's perception shifts — not hallucination, but augmented reality. They see the Caldera as it is **and** as the Sleeper dreams it simultaneously:

- The containment runes on the distant walls appear as bars of golden light — a cage, seen from inside
- The sphere pulses with dream-logic: it is simultaneously close and infinitely far, small and impossibly large
- The Deep Things resolve into something almost recognizable — shapes that suggest faces, hands, memories, from a time before memory existed
- Sound becomes visible. The broadcast manifests as ripples in the air — concentric waves spreading from the sphere in all directions, passing through the party like tidal surges

The Reality Anchor holds them steady, tethered to floating debris. The sphere hangs before them, its surface fifty feet away. And at the edge of each character's vision, something familiar waits.

**What do you do?**
