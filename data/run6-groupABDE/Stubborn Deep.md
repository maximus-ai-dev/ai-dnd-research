The **Stubborn Deep** was the original name for the dwarves of [[Kazad-Khrom]] who volunteered to stay behind in the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] to contain the rising corruption.

## History and Fate
According to the journal of [[Thrain Ironheart]], these dwarves attempted to control the corruption by matching their bodies to it, a desperate act that ultimately led to their transformation into [[The Twisted]].
