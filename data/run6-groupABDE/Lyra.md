**Lyra** is a member of the [[Gatekeepers]] who specializes in monitoring planar anomalies and ley-line anchors.

## Activity at the Asymmetrical Mountain
She arrived at the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] with [[Reyna Steelforge]], confirmed the presence of nested dreaming entities within, and formed a research partnership with the exploration party to study the containment failures and the [[First World Sleeper]].
