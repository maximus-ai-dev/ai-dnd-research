The **West Wing** is one of the three primary sections of the [[Secretive Brotherhood]]'s [[Grand Archive]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Restriction
Accessed from the Central Hall, the West Wing was designated as a restricted area, accessible to Commander-level personnel only. The Brotherhood [[Wardens]] were never informed of the reason for this restriction.

## Contents and Discovery
Brotherhood research indicates the West Wing contained a restricted vault. When this vault was opened by [[Commander Valerius]], it was found to contain a passage leading downward. This passage was not of Brotherhood or [[Iron Warlords]] construction, but was older, with proportions not designed by human hands. The Commander resealed the vault after a scholar sent to investigate failed to return. This passage is believed to connect to the lower levels of the mountain, specifically to an area known as the Relic Basements.
