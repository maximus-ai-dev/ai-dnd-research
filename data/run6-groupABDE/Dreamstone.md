**Dreamstone** is a crystalline substance intrinsically linked to the psychic energy of the [[First World Sleeper]].

## Properties
Dreamstone radiates potent psychic energy, which can induce hallucinations, mental fatigue, and physical transformation. Its energy manifests visually as iridescent stains, glowing motes, and crystalline growths. The radiation is often described as having a palpable, metallic-sweet sensation. The substance exhibits corruptive properties, capable of transmuting both organic and inorganic matter.

## Presence and Use
Traces of Dreamstone radiation permeate the deep layers of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], particularly within the [[Dwarven Layer]] and near the [[Giant Capstone]]. The [[Twisted]] utilize it extensively as a power source for their bio-mechanical creations. It was also used by ancient Giants, forming the refined core of certain powerful runic tools.

## Recent Applications
The Twisted employed crystallized Dreamstone to form the cutting teeth of a biological drill designed to bore through the Giant Capstone. These teeth were vaporized, halting the drilling operation.
