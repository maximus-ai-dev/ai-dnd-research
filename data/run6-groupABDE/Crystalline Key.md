The **Crystalline Key** is an artifact discovered within a sealed bronze case in the [[Secretive Brotherhood]]'s [[Grand Archive]] in the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Function
According to Brotherhood scrolls, the key is a crucial component of a contingency plan designed to address a failing seal. It is specifically crafted to open the [[Chamber of the First Anchor]], enabling the ritual that can bind a [[Warden]]'s consciousness to reinforce the ancient containment.
