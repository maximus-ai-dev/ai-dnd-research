**Amplifier Gamma** was the largest and most powerful of the three organic broadcast towers constructed by the [[Twisted]]. It was located due south in the [[Giant]] foundation level, near the threshold of the inner void within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Function
Gamma was a massive column approximately fifteen feet in diameter, directly tapping into the raw psychic energy rising from the void. Its proximity to the broadcast source made it the primary node in the amplifier network. The air around it shimmered with visible broadcast energy.

## Destruction and Aftermath
The destruction of Amplifier Gamma triggered a catastrophic chain reaction, sending a powerful feedback pulse through the entire Twisted resin conduit network. This event caused the organic infrastructure to fail, severing Twisted forces from their communication network and significantly reducing the psychic broadcast pressure on the Giant level.
