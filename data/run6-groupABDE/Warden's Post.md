The **Warden's Post** is a vast guardian station located in the southwest quadrant of the [[Giant foundation]] level beneath the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
Designed as a permanent station for a Giant sentinel, the chamber is roughly eighty feet in diameter with a domed ceiling sixty feet high. Its architecture is integrated with the containment system, featuring arrays of [[containment runes]] on its walls. At the chamber's center rests an open containment sarcophagus, originally meant to house a guardian. The far wall of the chamber was the site of a third breach drilled by [[The Twisted]], a ragged forty-foot hole in the capstone floor from which void energy flows.

## Function
According to [[Giant]] blueprints, the Warden's Post was one of several emergency defender stations. The containment system was designed to automatically reanimate the entombed guardians within if the primary seal was breached, turning them into autonomous combat units. The chamber's rune arrays are part of a local suppression field meant to weaken void-corrupted entities.
