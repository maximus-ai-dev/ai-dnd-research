The **Salt-Vaults**, also known as the Salting Halls, are a series of massive preservation chambers located beneath the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## History and Construction
Constructed centuries ago by the [[Iron Warlords]], the vaults were designed for the industrial-scale storage of salted meats, dried grains, pickled vegetables, and rock salt to sustain a large garrison through a prolonged siege.

## Description and Architecture
The architecture is brutal and functional, featuring wide corridors, heavy sealing doors, and stone shelving carved directly into the mountain walls. The vaults were later occupied and modified by other factions, including the [[Secretive Brotherhood]], who constructed concealed passages within the structure.

## Contents and Significance
Despite significant pest infestation over time, the vaults contained preserved food stores in sealed stone crocks, bins of rock salt, grain in stone silos, and a cache of antitoxin. This provided a critical, sustainable food supply for the current inhabitants of the mountain fortress. A preparation kitchen within the vaults complex serves as a key access point to the deeper Brotherhood sanctum.
