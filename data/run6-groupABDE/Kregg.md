**Kregg** was the heavyset enforcer and leader of the [[Emberfell Rejects]].

## Background and Demise
An exile from [[Emberfell]], Kregg led his band through cruelty and intimidation from a base on the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. His actions drew a bounty from the [[Prospectors' Guild]], leading to his defeat. Kregg serves as a symbolic contrast to [[Garrick Kade]], representing the path of violence and desperation that was overcome.
