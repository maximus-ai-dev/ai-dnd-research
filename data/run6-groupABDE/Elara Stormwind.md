**Elara Stormwind** is a retired mercenary and the proprietor of the [[Griffon's Hold]] equipment shop in [[Grimhold]].

## Appearance and Personality
Stormwind is a warm and chatty individual, marked by the scars of her past profession. Her demeanor stands in contrast to the more austere figures of the frontier, as she is known for sharing stories and offering fair deals to her customers.

## Services and Knowledge
She sells quality adventuring and prospecting supplies at reasonable prices. Leveraging her extensive knowledge of the local trails, she provides rough maps and practical advice to those heading into the [[Starmetal Hills]] and toward the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. She is the sister of [[Kaelin]].
