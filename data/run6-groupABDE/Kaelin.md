**Kaelin** is a retired member of the Griffon Company and the sister of [[Elara Stormwind]].

## Description and Knowledge
Kaelin bears extensive scars from her past service. Like her sister, she possesses considerable knowledge of the dangers on the northern frontier.

## Role in Grimhold
Kaelin warns adventurers about the dangers of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], citing previous failed bounty hunters who ventured toward it. She provides a salve to help resist the unsettling whispers associated with the mountain's influence.
