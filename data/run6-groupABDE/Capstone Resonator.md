The **Capstone Resonator** is a theoretical containment device designed to seal breaches in the [[Giant Capstone]].

## Design and Function
As conceived by [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]], the resonator would utilize [[Korathan's scaling runes]], a [[Dreamstone Power Cell]], a [[Monitoring Crystal]], and raw [[Dreamstone]] to create a focused containment pulse. The pulse is designed to suppress void energy, damage void-corrupted creatures, stun them, and temporarily seal any breach within its radius.

## Purpose
The device represents the culmination of the party's accumulated knowledge and technology from Act III of their exploration, designed specifically to counteract catastrophic breaches and the powerful entities that emerge from them, such as the [[Reanimated Warden]].
