The **Giant foundation** is a deep layer of ancient, monumental architecture within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Purpose and Construction
Built by Giants in a remote age, this foundation serves as a containment structure. Its primary feature is the [[Giant Capstone]], a massive stone seal designed to imprison the [[First World Sleeper]] below. The architecture incorporates powerful [[containment runes]] and is constructed on a scale far beyond human or dwarven engineering.

## Current State
The foundation has degraded over millennia. Its corridors and chambers are now infiltrated by the [[Twisted]], who have established fortified positions and bio-engineered infrastructure within it. Significant areas are saturated with [[Dreamstone]] radiation, creating hazardous environmental conditions.

## Key Locations
A major [[Twisted]] staging ground was discovered and destroyed within the foundation. This installation housed a biological drill aimed at breaching the capstone from below.
