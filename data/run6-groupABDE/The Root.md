**The Root** is the deepest known layer of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], located below the inner void.

## Description and Significance
The Root is a vast cavern containing the seven [[Foundation Stones]], where reality has been severely warped by the dreams of the [[Old Hunger]]. It is the site of the primary containment for this ancient entity.

## Access
Access to The Root requires a descent of approximately 3000 feet through the void aperture at the bottom of the mountain's foundation layers.
