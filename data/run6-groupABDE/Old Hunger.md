The **Old Hunger** is an entity older than the [[First World Sleeper]], imprisoned by the [[Giants]] deep beneath the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Nature and Threat
The entity's psychic corrosion has been weakening its containment for millennia. According to the Sleeper, the 'Old Hunger' began stirring because the rhythmic barrier maintained by the [[Foundation Stones]] had weakened, and it had been actively corrupting one of those anchors, [[Stone Theta]].
