**Sewer Plague** is a disease carried by the mutated giant rats inhabiting the [[Salt-Vaults]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Transmission and Symptoms
Infection occurs through bites. Symptoms include fever, weakness, and painful swelling around wounds. If left untreated, the disease can be fatal.

## Treatment
Antitoxin, found stored within the Salt-Vaults, is an effective cure.
