**DM:** **SESSION 5: THE WEIGHT OF EMPIRES**

**Three months have passed.**

The Grand Stairwell is clear now — the Amalgamation's remains cleared away, the stonework inspected and reinforced where needed. A new door of salvaged timber and iron bands stands at the top, built by the workforce under Brenna's direction. The surface fortress is stable: walls patched, food from the Salt-Vaults properly stored, the Wardens at their posts. Winter came and went, a season of relative peace earned through blood and discovery.

Now it is spring. The snowmelt trickles down the mountain's flanks, and the air in the corridors carries a damp chill that smells of thawing earth and ancient stone. The party has spent the winter studying the Brotherhood's research, transcribing Commander Valerius's logbook, and preparing for what comes next. The First Anchor Chamber remains stable — the consensus you formed held. But six more anchors await, and the deeper layers of the mountain beckon.

A week ago, a Grimhold trader found the fortress easily now that the trail is cleared. He brought a letter from Reyna Steelforge, sealed with the Prospectors' Guild insignia. Her request is simple but significant: produce a map of the lower levels. Grimhold's historians believe the Military Empire — the deepest human layer, built directly atop the dwarven ruins — left behind trophy halls containing artifacts from every civilization they conquered, including the Giant era. If any Giant artifacts remain, they could tell you everything about what lies below the dwarven layer. And about what the Giants were trying to contain.

Warden Aldric, consulted before the descent, stands at the top of the Grand Stairwell with you. His expression is grim. "The Brotherhood never went deeper than the Archives," he says, his voice echoing in the stone passage. "They said the lower levels were someone else's problem. But if you're going down... be careful. The Empire built heavy. And what's beneath them is older."

The Grand Stairwell descends 120 feet into darkness. The upper 60 feet are Iron Warlords construction — serviceable, utilitarian, the torch brackets still sound. Below that, the architecture changes. The steps become wider, the ceilings higher, the stonework more elaborate. Military Empire builders favored grand entrances: carved relief panels on the walls show soldiers in parade formation, officials in ceremonial dress, maps of territories long since vanished beneath ocean.

At the base of the stairwell, a landing with a single iron-banded door. Above the door, carved in Military Empire script: *THE IMPERIAL LOWER PALACE. LET ALL WHO ENTER SERVE THE EMPIRE'S MEMORY.*

The air here is different. The Iron Warlords' layer smelled of dust and old stone. The Brotherhood's sanctum smelled of preservation and ink. This smells of damp and old iron and something that takes a moment to identify: the faint metallic sweetness of very old blood, so distant it might be imagination.

**Garrick** can see the stairwell is sound — someone built this to last. The Iron Warlords' section above was retrofitted into an existing opening; this Grand Stairwell predates them by centuries.

**Cora** notices the landing floor has hairline cracks radiating from the center. Not fresh — ancient. But the pattern suggests the floor is being stressed from below. Something under it is settling, or was.

**Professor Mercer** can read the script above the door as archaic Common — the Empire's memory awaits.

The door stands before you. Beyond it lies the Military Empire layer, the weight of their construction pressing down on dwarven vaults that have already begun to fail. Your goal: map the Relic Basements. Find Giant artifacts. Locate — but do not open — the Sealed Crypt. And do not trigger a structural collapse that could bring thirty people's worth of fortress down on your heads.
