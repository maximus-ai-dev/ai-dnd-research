The **Giant Capstone** is a critical architectural seal located beneath the [[Drowned Chokepoint]] in the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
The capstone is a forty-foot diameter section of darker, denser stone inscribed with potent Giant-era [[containment runes]]. It functions as the final barrier and filtration membrane between the [[Dwarven Layer]] and the [[Giant]] foundation levels below, sitting directly above the prison of the [[First World Sleeper]].

## Current State
The capstone was the target of the [[Drowned Juggernaut]]'s drilling, which left a three-foot-deep groove in its surface before the machine was destroyed. The runes remain active, glowing with containment magic, and were instrumental in weakening the Juggernaut during the final battle. It represents the threshold to the deeper, largely unexplored levels of the mountain.
