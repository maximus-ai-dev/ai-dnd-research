The **Architect's Codex** is an ancient tome that contains the complete technical and philosophical foundation of the Giant containment architecture within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. It is written in the Giant language and represents the pinnacle of their arcane engineering knowledge.

## Contents
The Codex provides detailed schematics, operational protocols, and repair rituals for the entire containment system designed to hold the [[First World Sleeper]]. This includes diagrams of structural zones like [[The Threshold]] and [[The Gradient]], specifications for key features such as the [[Observation Platform]] and [[Anchor Points]], and the complete theoretical framework for the containment runes.

## Significance and Use
The Codex is an indispensable reference for understanding and interacting with the mountain's deepest secrets. [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] has studied it extensively, using its principles to design the [[Dreamstone Seal]] and to guide the precise inscription of the scaled-down containment rune matrix onto the Seal's [[Dreamstone]] core during its forging.
