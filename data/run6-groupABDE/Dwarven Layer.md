The **Dwarven Layer** is an architectural stratum within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], constructed by dwarves between the human-built strata above and the ancient [[Giant]] foundations below.

## Description
This layer features classic [[Kazad-Khrom]] architectural designs, characterized by sturdy stone corridors, grand halls, and highly functional industrial spaces. Key facilities within this layer include the [[Memory Vault]], the [[Cold Forge]], and the [[Dwarven Minecart Network]].

## Current State
The lower sections of the Dwarven Layer were submerged for centuries until the reactivation of [[Pump Station East]]. The draining water has revealed extensive modifications by [[The Twisted]], including the [[Drowned Chokepoint]], a critical water management junction located directly above the [[Giant Capstone]].
