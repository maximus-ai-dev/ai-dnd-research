**Varek** is a retired mercenary from the Griffon Company who was afflicted by the mountain's influence.

## Current State and Care
Varek resides at the [[Chapel of the Hearthfire]] in [[Grimhold]], where he is receiving care from [[Brother Aldwin]] for his psychic affliction.

## Knowledge and Contribution
During his affliction, Varek provided a detailed drawing and account of a chamber within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] where the stone itself seemed alive, offering a crucial clue to the mountain's unnatural nature and the influence of the [[First World Sleeper]].
