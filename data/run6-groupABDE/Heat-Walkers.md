**Heat-Walkers** are dwarven constructs that have been transformed by the [[Dream-Fire]] anomaly at the [[Drill Site]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Nature and Behavior
They exhibit sentient and tactical behavior, coordinated by a hive intelligence. Their transformation grants them abilities linked to the dream-energy that corrupted them.

## Habitat
Heat-Walkers are found within the [[Dwarven Minecart Network]], particularly around the [[Eastern Transit Hub]] and [[Pump Station East]], where they pose a significant threat to intruders.
