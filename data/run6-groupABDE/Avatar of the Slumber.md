The **Avatar of the Slumber** is a powerful defensive projection generated by the containment boundary surrounding the [[First World Sleeper]]. It manifests as a crystallized void entity when incompatible objects, such as the [[Dreamstone Seal]], approach the Sleeper's sphere within the [[Caldera]].

## Nature and Manifestation
The Avatar is not the Sleeper itself, but an immune response—a dream given form and force. It appears as a towering figure of crystallized void energy, fluctuating in perceived size and composed of a composite form drawn from the subconscious of those who approach: elements of [[Giant]], dwarf, and human features blended into an alien whole. Its body is partially transparent, with glimpses of the [[First World]] visible within.

## Abilities and Purpose
The Avatar's sole purpose is to prevent any foreign object from integrating with the containment boundary. It possesses immense physical and psychic power, capable of unleashing waves of boundary energy, grappling foes with void energy, and temporarily transporting adversaries into the Sleeper's dreamscape. It is tethered directly to the sphere's boundary, drawing power from it.

## Weaknesses and Defeat
The Avatar is vulnerable to radiant energy and weapons forged from [[Dreamstone]]. Its connection to the sphere can be weakened by activating the Caldera's [[Anchor Points]], which generate a suppression field. The Avatar is ultimately defeated not by destruction, but by the successful deployment of the Dreamstone Seal, which reinforces the boundary and severs the Avatar's connection to its power source, causing it to dissolve peacefully.
