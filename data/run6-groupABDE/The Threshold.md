The **Threshold** is a fifty-foot transition zone located directly beneath the capstone of the [[Caldera]].

## Description and Function
It marks the first stage of the descent into the void, where the physics of the normal world begin to weaken. The zone is characterized by a gradual reduction in gravitational force and is lined with densely packed, overlapping containment runes that burn with intense golden light. Giant-sized handholds carved into the curved walls serve as ledges and platforms for smaller beings navigating the area.

## Significance
The Threshold represents the primary barrier of the Giant containment system, where its power is most concentrated before giving way to the more severe distortions below.
