The **Chamber of the First Anchor** is a ritual chamber located below the [[Grand Archive]] of the [[Secretive Brotherhood]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Function and Significance
According to Brotherhood scrolls, this chamber is central to a contingency plan for a failing seal. The [[Crystalline Key]] opens the chamber, where a [[Warden]]'s consciousness can be ritually bound to the mountain's foundational architecture. This act is intended to reinforce a failing seal, presumably that of the [[Black Slumber]], by anchoring a sentient will to the containment matrix.
