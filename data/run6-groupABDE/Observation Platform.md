The **Observation Platform** is a Giant-era structure built into the wall of the [[Caldera]], designed for the long-term monitoring of the [[First World Sleeper]].

## Design and Features
The platform is equipped with crystalline viewing panels that provide magnified views of the central sphere. It features preserved Giant tools and monitoring equipment and is positioned amidst dense clusters of containment runes on the Caldera wall, which offer a degree of shelter from the Sleeper's intense psychic broadcast.

## Strategic Role
The platform serves as one of the four locations for the [[Anchor Points]]—massive iron rings that concentrate the overall containment field. It functions as a critical forward operating base within the Caldera, providing a stable point from which to launch operations toward the sphere. The party used it as their staging area to forge the [[Dreamstone Seal]].
