**Rolf** is a quiet and competent watchman and supervisor within the workforce led by [[Brenna]] at the fortress in the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Demeanor and Actions
He is not given to panic and operates with methodical precision. During repair work in the [[Salt-Vaults]] preparation kitchen, Rolf's mattock accidentally breached a hollow section of wall, revealing the interstitial space containing the [[Secretive Brotherhood]] [[Wardens]]. He reported the incident calmly and took responsibility for the breach.
