**Amplifier Beta** was the second, more sophisticated organic broadcast tower constructed by the [[Twisted]] within the [[Giant]] foundation level of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description and Function
Situated in the southeast quadrant, Amplifier Beta was built into a repurposed Giant monitoring alcove. It underwent multiple modifications, featuring a reinforced resin casing and a complex root network. Its design parasitized the alcove's original resonance instruments to significantly boost the [[First World Sleeper]]'s psychic broadcast.

## Destruction
The amplifier was destroyed using a combination of alchemical dissolution and arcane overload. Its collapse triggered a localized psychic shockwave, but the containment of the blast was largely successful.
