**Pump Station East** is a major dwarven engineering installation within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. It is a critical component of the mountain's water management infrastructure, designed to drain the lower [[Dwarven Layer]] using geothermal pressure differentials.

## Location and Function
The station is accessed via the northern branch of the [[Dwarven Minecart Network]]. It consists of a large circular chamber housing three massive pump assemblies. When operational, the pumps redirect water from the mountain's flooded lower levels through drainage conduits that exit on the eastern slopes of the [[Starmetal Hills]].

## Recent History
The station was found to be offline and systematically sabotaged, with its mechanisms sealed, severed, or disassembled. This sabotage was later identified as the work of [[The Twisted]]. The station was successfully repaired and reactivated, permanently lowering the water level in the lower Dwarven Layer and exposing previously submerged areas like the [[Drowned Chokepoint]].
