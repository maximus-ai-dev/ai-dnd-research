The **Prospectors' Guild** is the governing body for mining claims and frontier labor in the town of [[Grimhold]].

## Function and Leadership
The guild regulates mining activities in the [[Starmetal Hills]], posts bounties on threats to commerce, and acts as a de facto administrative authority for the region. It is led by the pragmatic [[Reyna Steelforge]]. The guild hall is a sturdy building marked by a sign featuring a crossed pickaxe and hammer.

## Current Concerns
The guild monitors and reports on environmental contamination spreading from the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] into the surrounding hills and provides logistical and scholarly support for expeditions to the ancient site.
