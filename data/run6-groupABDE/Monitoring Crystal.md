The **Monitoring Crystal** is a crystalline arcane device used to monitor the integrity of the [[containment field]] and detect breaches within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Function
Resonating with the containment architecture's frequency, the crystal provides real-time readings on field strength and pinpoints the location of any failures or breaches. A sudden spike in its resonance frequency serves as a critical alarm for catastrophic containment events.

## History
The crystal was a key tool for the party operating from the [[forward camp]], allowing them to detect the third breach in the [[Warden's Post]]. It was also identified as a potential component for constructing a [[Capstone Resonator]], a device designed to produce a powerful containment pulse.
