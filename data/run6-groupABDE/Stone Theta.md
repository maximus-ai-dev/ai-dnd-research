**Stone Theta** is one of the seven [[Foundation Stones]], crystalline anchors built by the [[Giants]] to ground the psychic barrier containing the [[First World Sleeper]]. It is located within [[The Root]], the deepest layer of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Current Status
Stone Theta has failed and is corrupted. It is dark and webbed with corrupting glyphs, representing a critical breach in the ancient containment system. The acquisition of scaling runes from [[Korathan]]'s tomb provides the necessary knowledge to attempt its repair.
