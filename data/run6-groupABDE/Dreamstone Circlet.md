A **Dreamstone Circlet** is a band of crystalline [[Dreamstone]] crafted into a wearable item that provides significant psychic shielding.

## Properties
The circlet grants the wearer advantage on Wisdom saving throws against psychic effects and resistance to psychic damage. It absorbs ambient psychic energy, creating a bubble of mental clarity and offers crucial protection against the constant broadcast of the [[First World Sleeper]]. Its surface is etched with micro-fine, naturally occurring patterns and hums with a steady, calming frequency.

## Crafting and Importance
Dreamstone Circlets are essential equipment for prolonged exploration of the deeper levels of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]]. They are forged from raw Dreamstone at extreme temperatures, a process that requires significant energy expenditure from a powerful forge source, such as the [[Fire Elemental]] [[Ignathar]].
