**Grimhold** is a frontier mining town located in the shadow of the Sentinel Mountains on the continent of [[Laternia]]. It serves as the primary settlement and supply hub for the northern frontier and the nearest point of civilization to the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Geography and Layout
Built on rocky terrain, Grimhold's streets are lined with timber buildings. Key locations include the [[Second Wind Inn]], the [[Prospectors' Guild]] hall, and the [[Chapel of the Hearthfire]]. The town center features an ancient standing stone of unknown origin.

## Economy and Society
The town's economy is driven by mining operations in the nearby [[Starmetal Hills]]. It has a lived-in, austere atmosphere typical of frontier settlements. Grimhold regularly serves as a resupply and planning base for expeditions into the mountain and supports the ongoing operations at the secured surface citadel.
