A **harmonic regulator** is an Imperial apparatus designed to stabilize large-scale structures and maintain specialized containment fields.

## Function and Design
The regulator uses bound storm energy to counteract geological stresses and preserve a time-dilation field within its area of effect. This technology was employed by the [[Military Empire]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]] to manage the containment of the [[First World Sleeper]].

## Known Regulators
A primary regulator existed deep within the complex, which later failed. A secondary regulator is located within the [[Hall of Conquests]]. Stabilizing this secondary unit is critical to preserving the integrity of the Sleeper's containment after the primary unit's failure.
