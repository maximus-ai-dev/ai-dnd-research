**Emberfell** is a distant land or faction known for its aggressive commercial interests and exploitative practices.

## Reputation and Influence
According to [[Lyra]], commercial interests from Emberfell would likely seek to exploit an awakening primordial entity, such as the one within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]], as a resource. The [[Emberfell Rejects]] bandits were exiles and failed criminals from this land.

## Recent Communications
A rider was dispatched to Emberfell to deliver a warning to the [[Laternian Historical Preservation Society]] regarding the dangers contained within the [[Adaptive Quarantine]].
