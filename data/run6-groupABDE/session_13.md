# Session 13: The Fleshy Amplifiers

**Chronicler's Note:** *The following account documents the party's sabotage mission against the Twisted amplifier network, a turning point in the containment war that revealed deeper, more ancient threats sleeping beneath the mountain's foundations.*

---

The air in the forward camp tasted different after three days of rest—cleaner, sharper, with the metallic tang of ozone from the steadily glowing containment runes. [[Dreamstone Circlets]] hummed softly against temples, a constant reminder of the psychic shield they provided against the mountain's dreaming heart. [[Cora Flint]] had cataloged crystalline research rods, [[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]] had transcribed the last anomaly logs, and [[Garrick Kade]] had sharpened his maul until the edge could split stone. They had returned briefly to [[Grimhold]] for supplies and delivered their report to [[Reyna Steelforge]], whose expression grew graver with each detail about the [[Dreamstone]] vein and the [[Dreamstone Sentinel]].

Now they stood before the [[Giant Monitoring Crystal]], its pulsing schematic overlay revealing three organic broadcast towers—"fleshy amplifiers"—grown into the outer ring of the [[Giant]] foundation level. The Twisted had parasitized the containment runes, siphoning energy to boost [[The Sleeper]]'s psychic emanation upward through the mountain. Left unchecked, they would push the corrupting broadcast through containment and into the human layers above.

Mercer's cross-referencing of Twisted schematics with the [[Giant blueprint tablet]] revealed the amplifiers' locations: **Alpha** in the northwest quadrant, a prototype; **Beta** southeast, modified multiple times; **Gamma** due south, largest and most heavily guarded near the threshold of the inner void. Approximately twenty-five [[The Twisted]] occupied the three sites—a force they could not defeat head-on.

"They're organic structures grown into stone," Cora explained, her fingers tracing bioluminescent vein patterns on the tablet. "They draw energy from containment runes through root-like tendrils. Sever those roots cleanly, and the amplifier dies. Crude attacks trigger broadcast bursts that alert every Twisted in the area."

The air carried the sweet-rot scent of organic corruption. They were behind enemy lines now—this wasn't exploration or open combat, but stealth, sabotage, and precision destruction.

## Surgical Strike on Alpha

Mercer's spectral mind scouted ahead, drifting through stone walls to observe [[Amplifier Alpha]]. The chamber contained a ten-foot diameter column of black resin threaded with pulsing bioluminescent veins, guarded by two [[Twisted Overseer|Twisted Sentries]] facing outward. A parallel maintenance corridor offered approach without crossing their line of sight.

"Twelve minutes until patrol," Mercer reported. "We have a window."

Cora prepared an Experimental Elixir for enhanced dexterity as they slipped from the forward camp, moving silently through Giant corridors where walls pulsed with faint bioluminescence—the Twisted communication network, alive and watching. They reached the maintenance corridor as a patrol of three Twisted Laborers shuffled past, backs turned.

Positioned behind Alpha's chamber, they observed the amplifier's base. Mercer's spectral mind examined the root structure from within the stone itself. "Three primary conduits with secondary feeders," he whispered. "The roots penetrate along Giant containment ley lines. There are inscriptions—Giant containment maintenance markers. The Twisted grew their roots directly through original diagnostic runes."

Cora applied acid solution to secondary feeders with surgical precision, the organic material dissolving with a soft hiss. Mercer used *Mage Hand* to maintain tension on primary conduits, mimicking natural failure. The amplifier shuddered, its veins flickering erratically. One sentry shifted weight, sensing disturbance.

On Mercer's count, Cora applied acid to all three primary conduits simultaneously. [[Amplifier Alpha]] collapsed inward with a soft, wet crumpling, its psychic hum ceasing abruptly. The two sentries turned, confused, touching their temples as if feeling a sudden absence.

Then the walls pulsed—the resin conduits dimming slightly in a system-wide response to the power drop. The Twisted communication network had registered Alpha's loss.

## Pursuit and Evasion

Footsteps approached from Beta's direction—fast, purposeful. Someone was investigating the fluctuation.

"Garrick, take the sentries—quietly," Cora hissed. "Mercer, distraction toward Beta."

Mercer's *Minor Illusion* fizzled, psychic residue interfering with precise magic. "The amplifier's collapse is disrupting illusion work!" Instead, he cast *Silence* around the archway as Garrick dispatched the confused sentries with soundless efficiency.

They retreated through the maintenance corridor as the investigator reached Alpha's chamber, finding the collapsed amplifier and missing sentries. The resin conduits pulsed with rapid, erratic light patterns—alarm signals racing through organic infrastructure.

A service tunnel offered escape, its Giant-sized rails suggesting a minecart network. As a search party closed in behind them, Mercer examined the rails with sudden intensity. "Wait—these aren't ordinary tracks. Look at the etching: Giant containment glyphs for kinetic dampening and directional guidance."

His spectral mind scouted ahead, revealing three minecarts on a loading platform with a control panel. "We can use the magical propulsion to reach Beta faster," he realized. Cora disabled the spur's guidance to stop pursuit while Mercer created an illusion of collapsed debris at the tunnel entrance.

The cart accelerated with silent magical propulsion, carrying them southeast toward [[Amplifier Beta]]. The ninety-second ride ended at a larger platform where fresh resin conduits pulsed along walls—this area was still in active use.

## Beta's Enhanced Defenses

Mercer's spectral mind scouted upward, revealing upgraded security: four sentries instead of two, with two patrolling perimeter circuits. The resin conduits pulsed with rapid alarm patterns—the Twisted knew Alpha had been destroyed and were on high alert.

"The moment both perimeter sentries are farthest from the alcove entrance," Mercer calculated, "we have approximately fifteen seconds when the amplifier column blocks the stationary sentries' view."

On his signal, Garrick moved with surprising quiet for his size, intercepting the perimeter sentries with brutal efficiency. Simultaneously, Mercer cast *Hold Person* on the two stationary sentries at the entrance, freezing them in place.

Cora slipped past the paralyzed sentries into the monitoring alcove. [[Amplifier Beta]] loomed larger than Alpha, with thicker resin casing and complex root networks spreading across floor and walls. She applied conductive paste to primary containment conduits, then realized Mercer's assessment had been incomplete.

"The casing has insulation," she whispered urgently. "I need to create an access point."

The paralyzed sentries began to twitch—*Hold Person* was wearing off. From down the corridor came the sound of approaching Twisted reinforcements.

"Garrick, keep them off me for fifteen seconds!" Cora abandoned precision, pouring remaining acid into a carved opening. "Mercer, Melf's Acid Arrow at the junction!"

Mercer's spell struck with surgical accuracy, following a stress fracture in the resin casing where original Giant containment glyphs had been incompletely overwritten. The acid reached energy channels as Garrick held the entrance, activating his Giant's Might to swell to Large size.

"Now for the overload!" Mercer declared, casting *Chain Lightning* through his Awakened Spellbook. The force energy traveled through conductive paste into Beta's core.

The amplifier emitted a deafening psychic shriek felt rather than heard, its casing cracking like eggshell before collapsing inward into black sludge. The four Twisted reinforcements staggered as the psychic shockwave hit them, but kept coming.

## The Inner Void Corridor

They retreated to the minecart platform, but the magical guidance system Cora had disabled also prevented departure. "The system might be locked out due to network-wide alarm," she reported as Twisted descended the stairs.

Mercer pointed to a narrow, downward-sloping tunnel that drank light from its surroundings. "The inner void corridor. It bypasses patrols completely."

The passage was unlike anything they'd experienced—walls perfectly smooth with geometric precision that made eyes want to slide away, air cold and thin with psychic pressure that pushed against minds despite the Dreamstone Circlets. Stress-dissipation runes glowed faintly at intervals, Giant engineering meant to withstand immense psychic forces.

After two hundred feet, the corridor opened into a vast circular chamber—the threshold of the inner void. A fifty-foot aperture in the floor dropped away into absolute darkness, from which rose a column of visible psychic energy: a shimmering, twisting ribbon of solidified thought, the raw broadcast unfiltered by amplifiers.

And at the aperture's edge stood [[Amplifier Gamma]].

## Gamma's Last Stand

The amplifier was enormous—fifteen feet in diameter, its black resin casing threaded with bioluminescent veins pulsing in time with the rising broadcast. Around it stood the [[Twisted Overseer]] from the pump station, four sentries, and four laborers. The Overseer recognized them—comprehension in its corrupted eyes. It had been waiting.

It raised a hand, and Twisted forces moved into formation. The Overseer stepped forward, warhammer ready.

Cora's mind raced through calculations. "Direct assault is suicide. We need to disrupt the energy source, not fight the guards." She proposed a resonance catalyst applied to Gamma's connection with the void energy.

But Mercer's analysis revealed deeper truth. "Gamma isn't just drawing power—it's acting as a regulator valve for the entire containment system. Look at the energy flow patterns!" His spectral mind identified emergency dampeners that would activate if Gamma failed. "We need to disable those first, then use *Telekinesis* to bend the energy column back into Gamma's core."

Garrick created chaos, charging the Overseer to draw attention while Cora applied catalyst to dampener conduits. Mercer cast *Telekinesis*, bending the psychic energy column with immense strain. The amplifier shuddered, veins flaring with sudden intensity.

The Overseer realized their plan. "NO!" it roared—the first intelligible word from any Twisted. It charged Mercer, but Garrick intercepted, maul meeting warhammer in a shower of sparks.

Cora poured acid onto main root conduits as Gamma reached critical mass. The Overseer's warhammer descended toward Mercer, but Cora's Flash of Genius gave him the insight to drop just in time. Garrick caught the follow-up strike, holding the line as Gamma convulsed.

Mercer maintained concentration despite the assault, ensuring perfect energy redirection. "Now—everyone shield your minds!"

## Cataclysmic Overload

[[Amplifier Gamma]]'s destruction was cataclysmic. The column shattered outward in a storm of black resin and blue-white energy, followed by a visible psychic shockwave that rippled reality. The Twisted forces collapsed as their degraded systems couldn't handle the raw psychic energy. The Overseer staggered back, warhammer dropping from nerveless fingers. Its expression showed comprehension—not just fear, but understanding that they weren't mere intruders, but a threat to everything the Twisted had worked for.

Then the chain reaction began.

With Gamma destroyed—the primary node—a feedback pulse raced through every resin conduit on the Giant level. The organic infrastructure went dark in a wave radiating outward. The Twisted communication network collapsed.

The Overseer dropped to its knees, making a keening sound of loss. It had been cut off from the signal it served for millennia. The remaining Twisted scattered toward the inner void, disoriented and disconnected. The Overseer rose last, looking at Cora specifically with pure comprehension before walking into darkness.

The chamber fell startlingly quiet. The psychic hum dropped to manageable levels. Mercer checked the Monitoring Crystal. "Containment field readings are improving. The broadcast on the Giant level has dropped by approximately sixty percent."

But his expression grew serious. "The readings deeper in—toward the inner void—have not changed. Without amplifiers filtering its signal, more of the Sleeper's raw dreaming is concentrated inward. The pressure is building below."

## Unintended Consequences

Returning to the forward camp, they found corridors dark and silent, resin conduits inert and decaying. The containment runes glowed stronger, but Mercer's analysis revealed troubling data. "The amplifiers weren't just boosting the signal upward—they were acting as pressure release valves. Without them, energy concentrates at the source. We have approximately forty-eight hours before pressure builds to critical levels."

He spread the Giant blueprint tablet. "The original containment design included pressure regulation systems—harmonic dampeners and emergency venting conduits. The Giants didn't just contain the Sleeper; they managed its energy output."

As they prepared to reactivate these systems, the decaying resin conduits suddenly pulsed with a new signal: three slow flashes, repeating. Not Twisted patterns—mathematical, structured.

Mercer's spectral mind traced it to a maintenance annex in the void corridor. "There's someone in there. Not Twisted. Not Giant. Humanoid, but changed. Wearing modified [[Brotherhood Wardens|Brotherhood Warden]] armor."

## The Adapted Wardens

Investigating, they found three figures around a modified Giant monitoring console. Their armor was heavily modified with extra plating and crystalline nodes—crude psychic shielding. Their skin was pale, almost translucent, with dark veins visible beneath. Their eyes glowed with faint blue light. Adapted.

"You should not have come here," the tall one said, its voice raspy and strained. "The balance is delicate. Your actions threaten everything."

They claimed the pressure buildup was natural, cyclical—"the Dream breathing." The dampeners had been disabled for a reason. The Giants understood: in final days, they let pressure build, preparing for something.

"The rhythm wasn't just communication," the tall Warden explained. "It was a barrier. A psychic wall. And by guiding the release... we may have weakened it."

Mercer realized the truth: "Layered containment. The Giants didn't just contain a dreaming entity—they used its dreaming as a psychic barrier against something worse."

The Sleeper's communication confirmed it: *The rhythm was protection. The focusing is change. Something wakes in the deep. Not me. Other. Older.*

With the message came an image: seven crystalline structures—[[Foundation Stones]]—buried deep in the mountain's heart. Six glowed steadily. One was dark, cracked, its resonance silent. Physical anchors for the psychic barrier.

"The barrier has a physical component," Mercer said, voice hollow. "One anchor has failed. That's what allowed the stirring."

The adapted Wardens—who called themselves Keepers of the Rhythm—provided schematics and data from their 247 years of observation. The failed anchor, [[Stone Theta]], showed corruption patterns that formed glyphs—not Giant, not any recognizable language, but structured. A message. A signature.

"The old hunger knows you come," the Sleeper warned. *It prepares. It remembers the builders. It hates.*

## Descent into the Root

With reinforced Dreamstone Circlets—the Sleeper's gift of invested dreaming power—they descended through the void aperture, carried downward by the psychic energy column. Three thousand feet below, they emerged in the [[The Root|Root]]—the deepest layer of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

The cavern was vast, filled with crystalline formations glowing with sickly green light. Reality itself warped here: surfaces flowed like liquid, froze into impossible geometries. And in the center stood Stone Theta—fifty feet of pure crystal, dark and webbed with black corruption that pulsed with malevolent life.

Forms moved in the shadows around it: creatures of corrupted crystal and warped stone, multi-limbed scuttling things, towering shape-shifting constructs, floating spheres trailing dark energy. The old hunger's defenses, shaped from warped reality over millennia.

One floating sphere turned, its surface resolving into something like an eye. It conceptualized them, the weight of its attention a physical pressure. A pulse of green light raced through corruption veins in the floor, alerting the others.

The guardians began to converge—not rushing, not panicking, but with the deliberate patience of things that had waited since before recorded history for this moment.

They stood at the edge of the Root cavern, Stone Theta corrupted in the distance, surrounded by defenses shaped from warped reality. The mission to repair the anchor and restore the barrier had reached its most critical point.

But first, they had to survive what had been waiting for them.

**Chronicler's Epilogue:** *The party's sabotage succeeded beyond expectations, dismantling the Twisted amplifier network and reducing broadcast pressure by sixty percent. But in doing so, they uncovered deeper truths: the Sleeper was not the primary threat, but a warden dreaming a barrier against something older and hungrier. Now they stand in the deepest layer of the mountain, facing manifestations of an intelligence that has been patiently corrupting its prison for millennia. The battle for Stone Theta—and perhaps for the mountain itself—is about to begin.*
