The **Brotherhood** is an ancient and secretive order that maintained the containment of the [[First World Sleeper]] within the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## History and Role
Operating from their outpost in the [[Grand Archive]], the Brotherhood studied the "Deep Resonance" and performed ritual observances to stabilize the containment systems inherited from earlier civilizations. Their commander, [[Commander Valerius]], remained at his post until the end, leaving behind crucial research logs.

## Legacy
The Brotherhood's archives, containing records of their centuries-long watch, were restored following the reinforcement of the containment. Their work provided a critical link in understanding the mountain's layered history and the ongoing responsibility of safeguarding the Sleeper.
