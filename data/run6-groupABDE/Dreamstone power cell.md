A **Dreamstone Power Cell** is a powerful energy source composed of crystallized [[Dreamstone]], recovered from the wreckage of the [[Drowned Juggernaut]].

## Description
The cell is a fist-sized sphere encased in a protective lead shell. It contains enormous reserves of psychic and arcane energy, which were originally channeled to power the Juggernaut's systems.

## Potential Uses
The stable but potent cell has two primary applications. It can be installed into defensive systems to power magical alarms and barriers. Alternatively, it can be studied to gain a deeper understanding of the [[First World Sleeper]] and its influence, though such study carries significant risks of psychic feedback and disturbing nightmares. It was also identified as the core energy source required to construct a [[Capstone Resonator]].
