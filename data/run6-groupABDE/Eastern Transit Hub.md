The **Eastern Transit Hub** is a major junction within the [[Dwarven Minecart Network]] in the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
Located east of the [[Cold Forge]], this cavernous chamber was designed as a primary transfer point where multiple rail lines converged at loading docks. The hub features iron crane systems, complex switch mechanisms, and extensive storage bays.

## Current Status
The hub is heavily damaged, with overturned carts, toppled cranes, and debris blocking the rails. The area shows signs of prolonged occupation by a large predator, evidenced by deep claw marks, lightning scorches, and scattered bones.
