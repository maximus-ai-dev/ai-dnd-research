A **Broadcast Scream** is a potent psychic attack emitted at the resonant frequency of the [[First World Sleeper]].

## Effects
The scream manifests as a cone of concentrated psychic energy that inflicts severe psychic damage. Beyond physical harm, it forces the broadcast's corrupting influence directly into the minds of targets, often causing fear and powerful hallucinations. Those who fail to resist perceive the void as a welcoming place and feel compelled to move toward its source.

## Notable Use
The [[Reanimated Warden]] utilized this ability, a reflection of the Sleeper's influence channeled through its corrupted form. The attack bypasses most physical barriers, as it is a direct psychic assault.
