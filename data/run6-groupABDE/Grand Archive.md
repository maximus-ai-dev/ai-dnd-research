The **Grand Archive** is a sealed repository of knowledge belonging to the [[Secretive Brotherhood]], accessed via a concealed corridor adjacent to the preparation kitchen of the [[Salt-Vaults]].

## Description and Security
The archive door is sealed with an arcane lock bearing the Brotherhood's closed-eye symbol and a warning inscription. According to records, its contents are guarded by a powerful bound construct known as the [[Amalgamation]].

## Contents
The archive contains scrolls detailing the Brotherhood's knowledge of the [[Black Slumber]], the function of the [[Crystalline Key]], and the purpose of the [[Chamber of the First Anchor]].
