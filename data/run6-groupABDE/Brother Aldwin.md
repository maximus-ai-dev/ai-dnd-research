**Brother Aldwin** is the gentle and earnest healer who tends the [[Chapel of the Hearthfire]] in the frontier town of [[Grimhold]].

## Role and Demeanor
Aldwin provides both spiritual guidance and medicinal care to the townsfolk and travelers. He is a kind man, though his manner often carries a note of weariness and deep concern for those affected by the harsh realities of frontier life.

## Knowledge and Concerns
Aldwin is aware of strange reports from prospectors returning from the [[Starmetal Hills]], including accounts of bad dreams, pervasive unease, and whispers emanating from old mine shafts. He treats these ailments under the general diagnosis of 'mountain fever,' but privately worries their cause may be more profound and unsettling. He currently provides care for the afflicted retired mercenary, [[Varek]].
