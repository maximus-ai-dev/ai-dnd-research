# Session 15: The Staging Ground Assault

*From the personal chronicles of an observer attached to the Emberfell Rejects, recovered from the forward camp after their assault on the Twisted staging ground. The handwriting shifts between precise notation and hurried scrawl, suggesting composition during and immediately after the events described.*

---

## The Weight of Intelligence

The air in the [[forward camp]] held the peculiar stillness that follows weeks of intense work—not peace, but the calm before the storm. For two weeks, [[Cora Flint]] had labored over her modifications to the Twisted communication node, transforming a parasitic amplifier into a delicate monitoring station. Crystals tapped into residual signals flowing through the mountain's resin network, and what they revealed was worse than anyone had feared.

The [[Twisted]] hadn't retreated. They'd regrouped.

Eight hundred feet below, where the [[Giant foundation]] met bedrock, they'd established a fortified staging ground with thirty to forty of their kind, including command structures and specialized workers. At its heart: a second drill. Not a machine like the [[Drowned Juggernaut]], but something *grown*—biological, with [[Dreamstone]] teeth feeding on the rock it consumed. Already thirty feet into the fifty-foot thickness of the [[Giant Capstone]], it was a countdown to catastrophe.

Cora's monitoring identified a single vulnerability: a damaged resin conduit creating a thirty-foot gap in the Twisted's perimeter signal coverage. A narrow window—twenty-four hours before repairs closed it—but a way in. A chance to strike before the drill reached the capstone's weak point.

The party gathered around the monitoring station, supplies organized for military operation: [[Dreamstone Circlet]]s for psychic shielding, the [[Giant Monitoring Crystal]] for radiation tracking, [[Korathan]]'s scaling runes ready for containment manipulation. The air hummed with the low, persistent thrum of the [[Sleeper]]'s dreaming—weaker since the amplifiers fell, but still present, like a heartbeat in the stone.

"Right," Cora said, ledger in hand. "We have a twenty-four hour window and thirty to forty hostiles in a fortified position. Let's not waste either."

[[Garrick Kade]] hefted his maul, the Dreamstone Circlet already on his forehead. "Point. Got it. Single file through the gap, straight to the drill. No stopping for patrols." His eyes hardened. "Once we're in, we hit the command structure first. Take out their leadership."

[[Professor Thaddeus Mercer]]'s quill danced across his journal. "Twenty-two minutes," he said without looking up. "The patrol rotation interval is precisely twenty-two minutes, with a three-minute variance window." He closed his journal with a snap. "Garrick, your approach is tactically sound but archaeologically reckless. That drill is a biological marvel. If we destroy it without documenting its construction, we lose invaluable data."

He conjured his spectral mind—a shimmering, book-shaped apparition. "I'll send this ahead through the gap. It can scout without triggering psychic alarms." The mind drifted toward the corridor entrance. "One more thing. If that drill reaches the weak point... this isn't just about stopping the Twisted. It's about preventing a catastrophic structural failure that could bring the entire mountain down on whatever's sealed below."

The decision was made. The descent began.

## Through the Gap

The corridor sloped downward, worked stone giving way to older bedrock. The air grew colder, and shimmering motes of psychic energy drifted through the air like dust in sunlight—the first signs of radiation. Cora's Monitoring Crystal hummed softly, readings climbing from "light" toward "moderate."

After two hundred feet, the environmental shift became palpable. Dreamstone radiation pressed against their mental defenses. Strange, half-formed thoughts brushed against consciousness: fragments of ancient dreams, geometric patterns, the sensation of immense weight pressing from below. Their Dreamstone Circlets hummed in response, providing just enough protection to push through.

Ahead, the spectral mind paused at the edge of the communication gap. Through Mercer's connection, the party saw what it saw: a thirty-foot section where the resin conduit was cracked and dark, its organic material dried and brittle. Beyond it, the conduit resumed, pulsing with faint internal light. The gap was exactly as described—a blind spot.

But something else was visible: on the far side, a Twisted patrol was just turning a corner. Two Laborers, one Sentry. Moving away. They would return in approximately nineteen minutes.

"Constitution save against the radiation—I'm fine," Cora said, her eyes tracking the patrol's movement. "That's our window."

Garrick led the crossing, movements deliberate and silent. Each footstep placed with care, his maul held low to avoid contact with the ceiling. Cora followed, eyes fixed on her crystal. Mercer brought up the rear, his spectral mind already scouting ahead.

The crossing took forty-five seconds of tense, silent movement. As they regrouped on the far side, Cora's Monitoring Crystal emitted an urgent chime. They'd entered the heavy exposure zone surrounding the staging ground perimeter.

Psychic pressure intensified, a wave of disorienting force that buckled knees and blurred vision. All three failed their saves simultaneously.

**Cora took 14 psychic damage** as geometric patterns erupted across her vision—walls breathing, stone rippling like water. **Garrick took 11**, phantom sensations crawling across his skin: being buried alive, stone growing through his flesh. **Mercer took 13**, worst of all as his connection to the spectral mind became a feedback loop, the corridor doubling and tripling in his perception.

Hallucinations lasted precious seconds where they were vulnerable, exposed. Through the haze, Garrick's training kept him moving. He saw the junction guard eighty feet ahead—or thought he did. The image wavered. But muscle memory took over. He began moving forward, handaxe ready.

Cora's chemical mixture slipped from trembling fingers, the vial shattering. Mercer attempted a Minor Illusion containment breach alarm, but it emerged distorted—a discordant, wavering shriek that echoed down the corridor.

At the staging ground entrance, the Twisted Sentry's crystalline eye snapped toward the sound. The junction guard turned, peering into the gloom.

They'd drawn attention rather than diverting it.

## Breach and Battle

The alarm was raised. A resonant hum vibrated through organic conduits. From side passages, the patrol began doubling back—their return accelerated by the alert.

Garrick exploded forward, Haste magic making distance vanish. His body swelled to Large size as he activated Giant's Might, muscles bulging. His maul connected with the junction guard in a cataclysmic impact that echoed through the corridor. A critical hit—the Twisted's armor shattered like glass, its body hurled backward to slam into the wall fifteen feet away. Dead before its final reflexive swing even whistled through the air.

Mercer's Web spell blossomed across the passage, thick strands erupting from resin conduits. The patrol Sentry weaved through, but four Laborers plowed into the webbing, becoming entangled. Cora's Ray of Frost struck the advancing Entrance Sentry, ice crystals forming across its chitinous plating.

But the Overseer's commanding hum grew louder from deeper within the staging ground. Through the spectral mind's connection, Mercer received his first clear view: a massive cavern expanded by Twisted bio-engineering. Organic alcoves (barracks), a separate chamber (armory), a nexus of resin conduits (the [[Organic Power Hub]]). And at its heart—the drill.

Exactly as intelligence suggested: a biological construct fifty feet long, shaped like an enormous boring worm, with a rotating head of crystallized Dreamstone teeth. Active. Grinding.

Standing near it, directing reinforcements: the **[[Twisted Overseer (Final Form)]]**. Master-crafted corrupted plate armor, warhammer glowing with necrotic energy, crystalline eyes fixed on the entrance.

The two Engineers facing Garrick fought smart. One engaged directly while the other placed something on the floor—a timed explosive resin charge that began to pulse with ominous light. A [[Sabotage Device]]. Three rounds until detonation.

## Race Against Destruction

"Garrick, that explosive detonates in three rounds," Cora called, her Monitoring Crystal screaming radiation warnings. "Engineer placing it is priority one."

Mercer's spectral mind analyzed the device. "Twenty-six on Arcana. It's a resin-based thermal charge—designed to superheat air in confined space. Extremely dangerous in this radiation-saturated environment."

Garrick charged, maul swinging. "Twenty-six to hit! Thirteen damage!" The Engineer staggered but continued working. "Action Surge!" A second blow finished it, but the device continued its ominous pulsing—two rounds remaining.

Mercer cast Slow, temporal energy washing over the Overseer and approaching reinforcements. Their movements became sluggish, halved. Cora's Flaming Sphere ignited near the barracks entrance, creating a wall of fire.

Round by round, they fought through the Twisted defenses. Garrick trading blows with the Overseer, necrotic energy seeping through his cracked armor. Cora fighting through Command Corruption assaults that left her frightened, trembling, but still calculating. Mercer maintaining multiple concentrations—Slow, spectral mind, analysis.

The drill ground forward: thirty-three feet, thirty-four, thirty-five. The bore hole began showing a translucent quality, shapes moving within the stone—something ascending.

Mercer's Disintegrate spell streaked toward the Dreamstone teeth. The drill mechanism failed its Dexterity save. Green energy vaporized the cutting head in a flash. The biological drill shuddered, went still.

But the translucent column continued to rise. Thirty-six feet. Thirty-seven. Shapes moving with renewed urgency.

## Revelation and Retreat

Mercer's spectral mind drifted to the bore hole edge, pages turning rapidly. "Fascinating," he murmured, academic awe cutting through chaos. "That translucent column isn't a breach—it's a reality-distortion field. The shapes within are crystallized dream fragments, not physical entities."

He projected the analysis: mathematical sequences matching containment harmonics. "They're not trying to escape—they're reinforcing the capstone from below. The Sleeper is attempting to repair the damage the drill caused."

The Overseer staggered, not from physical damage but cognitive dissonance. Millennia of service to a misunderstood purpose—its drilling damaging the containment it was meant to maintain. Its warhammer lowered slightly, movements becoming jerky, uncoordinated. Command Corruption assaults faltered, psychic energy dissipating before fully forming.

The Overseer was breaking—mentally.

"Garrick, disengage and fall back," Cora ordered, clinical even as she fought through fear. "You're too wounded to finish this."

Her Stinking Cloud enveloped the remaining Twisted, followed by Mercer's Web spell. The Engineer and Laborers retched, tangled, incapacitated.

They retreated—clean, disciplined. Garrick first, wounds partially healed but serious. Cora following, maintaining concentration. Mercer bringing up the rear, spectral mind maintaining the data projection to the broken Overseer even as they withdrew.

They passed through the staging ground entrance, back into the corridor. The communication gap still open ahead—repair cycle disrupted by the Power Hub's destruction. The organic infrastructure was dead. For the first time on the Giant level, the walls were just walls.

But from behind them, from the bore hole, the Sleeper's single sustained note followed them up. A deep, resonant sound vibrating through containment runes, through stone. Not gratitude. Not anger. Awareness.

The Sleeper had felt the drill's destruction. It was awake. Slightly more awake than it was yesterday.

## Aftermath

The forward camp welcomed them with exhausted quiet. Cora's healing magic knit Garrick's wounds, the necrotic chill slowly receding. Mercer's Alarm spell took effect around the perimeter. His spectral mind dissolved, data safely stored alongside Korathan's runes and the Giant blueprint transcription.

Garrick took first watch, maul across his knees, eyes fixed on the dark corridor leading down. The Sleeper's note had faded to a distant vibration, but it was still there—a constant reminder.

Cora prepared Death Ward on each of them before sleep, hands moving with automatic precision. Final notations in her ledger: mission accomplished, costs tallied, intelligence gathered.

Mercer used Master Scrivener to create a Teleport scroll, magical ink glowing softly. He tucked it into his spellbook, then lay down, mind already organizing findings into chapters, footnotes, appendices.

The camp fell silent except for the Alarm spell's hum and Garrick's steady breathing. The mountain was quiet. The staging ground lay ruined below. The drill was rubble. The Sleeper's dream fragments continued their work, reinforcing the capstone from below.

But as Garrick watched, he noticed something new: the [[containment runes]] on the walls glowed slightly brighter than before. Not the urgent flare of a breach, but a steady, increased luminescence. The Sleeper's increased awareness was manifesting in the containment field itself.

He didn't wake the others. They needed the rest. But he made a mental note to mention it in the morning.

The war hadn't ended. It had just entered a new phase.
