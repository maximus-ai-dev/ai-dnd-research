The **Drowned Juggernaut** was a corrupted dwarven mining automaton encountered in the [[Drowned Chokepoint]] of the [[Asymmetrical Mountain]].

## Description
The Juggernaut stood approximately eighteen feet tall, constructed from dwarven iron and stone in a roughly humanoid form. Its arms terminated in massive, rotating drill assemblies, and its torso contained a furnace. [[The Twisted]] extensively modified it with [[Black Organic Resin]] and a pulsing biological control node that served as its brain. It was powered by [[Dreamstone]] energy channeled through organic cables.

## Purpose and Demise
The machine was designed to drill through the [[Giant Capstone]] and breach the containment below. It was destroyed in the Drowned Chokepoint by a coordinated assault that severed its power cables, strategically flooded the chamber, and activated the capstone's containment runes. Its wreckage yielded several key items, including a [[Dreamstone power cell]] and the [[Dwarven Master Forge Key]].
