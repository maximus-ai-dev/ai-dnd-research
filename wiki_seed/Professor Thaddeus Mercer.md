# Professor Thaddeus Mercer

**Race:** Human
**Class:** Wizard (Order of Scribes)
**Role:** Party scholar, magical support, knowledge repository

Professor Thaddeus Mercer is an academic wizard who treats every adventure as fieldwork for his research. He cannot resist examining ancient architecture, reading inscriptions, or theorizing about unknown phenomena. His curiosity frequently overrides his self-preservation instinct.

## Personality

- Examines ancient architecture, ruins, and artifacts from a distance before approaching
- Theorizes about unknown phenomena, often at length
- Retreats behind Garrick when facing physical threats, casting from range
- Cannot resist knowledge — books, inscriptions, tablets override self-preservation
- Lectures the party on historical context during strategy discussions
- Disagrees vocally when Garrick wants to smash something that might be academically valuable

## Combat Style

Mercer fights from the back line using control and damage spells. He relies on Garrick as a physical shield and Cora for healing. His spell selection prioritizes utility and information-gathering, with combat spells as backup. He occasionally forgets he is in danger while examining something fascinating.

## Key Relationships

- **Garrick Kade:** Appreciates Garrick's protection, exasperated by his anti-intellectual approach. Refers to Garrick's insights as "characteristically astute for someone with his practical background."
- **Cora Flint:** Respects her engineering mind, considers her too focused on profit. They collaborate well on technical problems.
