# Cora Flint

**Race:** Human
**Class:** Artificer (Alchemist)
**Role:** Party engineer, field medic, quartermaster

Cora Flint is a practical, transactional adventurer who treats every expedition as a business venture. She maintains a detailed ledger of expenses, loot values, and debts owed. She heals allies efficiently but without bedside manner, and frequently reminds them the healing "is coming out of your share."

## Personality

- Searches every room systematically before moving on
- Claims and catalogues all loot, assessing value immediately
- Provides triage when allies are injured — clinical and efficient
- Calculates cost-benefit before engaging threats
- Objects when the party rushes past valuable resources
- Plans before entering dangerous areas

## Combat Style

Cora fights from range using alchemical preparations, acid vials, and Artificer infusions. She supports Garrick and Mercer with healing and battlefield control. She prioritizes efficiency over heroics.

## Key Relationships

- **Garrick Kade:** Respects his combat ability, frustrated by his recklessness. Frequently patches him up and bills him for it.
- **Professor Mercer:** Values his knowledge, impatient with his lectures. They share a pragmatic respect.
