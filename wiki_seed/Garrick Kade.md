# Garrick Kade

**Race:** Human
**Class:** Fighter (Champion)
**Role:** Party frontline, primary combatant, door-kicker

Garrick Kade is an aggressive, direct fighter who leads with action rather than deliberation. He takes point in every formation, charges threats before the party finishes planning, and refuses to retreat from any fight. His instinct is always to hit first and assess later.

## Personality

- Takes point position in every formation without being asked
- Charges into danger when others hesitate
- Refuses to retreat unless physically dragged away
- Pragmatic and blunt in conversation
- Protective of allies despite his aggressive style
- Impatient with lengthy planning or academic discussion

## Combat Style

Garrick fights in melee with a maul, using Giant's Might and Action Surge for devastating burst damage. He wears chain mail and relies on HP and aggression rather than finesse. He targets the biggest threat first and hits it until it stops moving.

## Key Relationships

- **Cora Flint:** Respects her competence, tolerates her ledger-keeping. Knows she'll patch him up even while complaining about cost.
- **Professor Mercer:** Protective of the physically frail wizard, frustrated by his tendency to stop and examine things mid-danger. They disagree frequently but Garrick trusts Mercer's judgment on magical matters.
