# Asymmetrical Mountain

A massive mountain in the Starmetal Hills with an unusual lopsided profile. The mountain contains multiple layers of civilization built on top of each other over millennia:

- **Surface/Town level:** Grimhold mining town at the base
- **Iron Warlord layer:** Military Empire fortifications, salt-vaults, armories
- **Dwarven layer:** Kazad-Khrom forges, minecart network, pump stations
- **Giant foundation level:** Ancient Giant construction, the capstone, monitoring systems
- **Deep level:** The First World boundary, the Sleeper's containment

The mountain is both a dungeon and a history — each layer represents a different civilization that tried to contain what lies beneath. The party descends deeper through the campaign, uncovering each layer's secrets and failures.
