# Grimhold

A small mining town in the Starmetal Hills, built at the base of the Asymmetrical Mountain. Grimhold serves as the party's base of operations throughout the campaign. The town has a general store, the Second Wind Inn, and a small population of miners, prospectors, and frontier folk.

Grimhold is not the party's stronghold — the stronghold is the fortified position the party establishes inside the mountain itself.
